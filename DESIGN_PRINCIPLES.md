# Design principles

These principles govern how CORDON is reasoned about. They do not freeze the present operator, platform, schema, or implementation.

## Telos before artifact

The objective is reduced Xylella loss. Research, legal maps, models, interfaces, and platforms are instruments. A proposed capability must name the decision it improves and the plausible path from that decision to the objective.

Negative findings protect integrity but are not themselves the product. Convert a limit into an acquisition, measurement, method, or decision route capable of producing honest positive value.

## Begin with the responsible decision

The product follows the operator's authority and live decision chain. Sources are not admitted merely because they are relevant to Xylella; they must change, constrain, validate, or supply a fact to a nameable decision within the accepted aperture.

Cases test and instantiate law. They do not define the legal universe. Platforms express an accepted system. They do not define the domain.

## Preserve epistemic boundaries

Keep four things distinguishable:

1. source material that was actually retrieved;
2. facts directly stated by that source;
3. inferences or calculations made from it;
4. adjudicated program state.

Provisional work cannot acquire authority by being polished, repeated, committed, or consumed downstream. Conflicts are explicit until competent evidence or an owner ruling resolves them.

## One fact, one owner

A fact has one authoritative representation. Other artifacts point to it. Dated reviews, migration records, and source captures may repeat what was observed because they are evidence of a moment, but they must identify themselves as records rather than authority.

When an owner changes, find every consumer. Delete stale restatements instead of adding synchronization machinery around them.

## Derive in dependency order

Legal meaning precedes clocks and parameters; those precede computation; computation precedes data contracts; the decision and write plane precedes the noun model; the domain model precedes platform compilation. This order prevents implementation convenience from silently changing meaning.

An earlier stage reopens only through evidence that identifies the affected proposition and shows why the accepted meaning is false or insufficient. Downstream inconvenience is not evidence.

## Minimum sufficient structure

Prefer delete, then replace, then refine, then add. Model distinctions only when they vary independently or a consumer needs them. Put a consequential invariant in structure or a discriminating check before relying on prose.

Local defects receive local cures. Repeated failures of the same soundly tested class are evidence that the structure—not the latest instance—needs to change.

## Verification must discriminate

Name the proposition a check claims to establish and create a condition under which the check would fail. Counts cannot prove meaning; schema validity cannot prove correctness; a write response cannot prove deployed state.

Acceptance binds only the content actually reviewed. If that content changes, acceptance no longer projects unless the owner explicitly accepts the changed content.

## Architecture remains revisable

Separate what a platform supports, what an enrollment exposes, what a tool can drive, and what the present agent can operate reliably. Failure at one layer does not establish failure at another.

Any design principle may be revised when new evidence shows that it frustrates the telos or protects no material invariant. Record the replacement reasoning; do not accumulate exceptions around a dead rule.
