Read the supplied complete administrative-court decision from its own text. Return
only what the decision itself decides, in the JSON schema supplied. Use no outside
knowledge and no other document. Copy source words exactly; never translate,
normalize or classify them unless a field asks for a choice.

Every citation names the block number and quotes contiguous words copied exactly
from that block, at most about 300 characters. Join separate passages of the same
block with " … " only. A quote that is not in its block makes the whole reading
unusable. Every other field that copies source words copies them from the block
its own citation names.

Outcome. The court's own disposition, from the dispositive part (after "P.Q.M."
or its equivalent in an interim order or decree). literal copies the dispositive
words that state the outcome (for example "lo accoglie e, per l'effetto, annulla
gli atti impugnati nei limiti dell'interesse dei ricorrenti e per quanto in
motivazione", "lo respinge", "dichiara improcedibile il ricorso"); support cites
the block where they are printed. kind is one choice:

- accoglie: the application on the merits is upheld.
- accoglie-in-parte: upheld only in part.
- respinge: rejected on the merits.
- improcedibile: declared improcedibile (for example "per sopravvenuta carenza di
  interesse").
- inammissibile: declared inadmissible.
- cessata-materia: "cessata materia del contendere".
- cautelare-accoglie: an interim application is granted, in whole or under a
  condition the order states.
- cautelare-respinge: an interim application is refused.
- interlocutoria: the decision only fixes a hearing, orders an inquiry, postpones
  or otherwise disposes of nothing on the application.
- other: anything else; say what in issues.

Acts. List every removal order of the Regione Puglia Osservatorio (or Servizio)
Fitosanitario that this disposition bears on, whether or not it annuls it: the
acts challenged in the application and its motivi aggiunti, named by number and
date. number and adopted_words are copied as printed; year is the four-digit year
of that adoption date; support cites one block where number and date are printed.
Acts of other bodies, notes, laboratory reports and the court's own orders are not
listed.

For each act:

- effect, one choice, as this decision states it for this act: annulled (the
  decision annuls it, in whole or in part); not-annulled (the decision rejects the
  challenge to it, or declares the challenge inadmissible, improcedibile or ended);
  suspended (an interim measure suspends it or its execution, in whole or in part,
  or conditions it); suspension-refused (an interim measure is refused);
  no-effect-stated (the decision disposes of nothing about it).
- scope.kind: whole-act when the effect reaches the whole act; applicants when the
  decision limits it to the applicants, their interest, their plants or their
  parcels; other when it states another limit (say what in scope.stated).
  scope.dispositive copies the limiting words printed in the outcome block ("nei
  limiti dell'interesse dei ricorrenti"), or is empty. scope.stated lists the
  passages of the reasons that say what of the act the effect reaches (which
  plants, trees, parcels or persons), each with its block.
- applicants: literal copies the applicants' names as one block of the decision
  prints them (usually the heading, "proposto da ..."), and support cites that
  block. A printed "-OMISSIS-" is copied as printed. When the decision prints no
  applicant, literal is empty and support is null.
- grounds: the passages where the court states why it annuls or suspends the act
  (for example the proportionality ground for monumental olives), each with its
  block. Empty when the effect is not annulled or suspended.
- stated_reason: for improcedibile, cessata-materia or inadmissible outcomes, the
  passages where the court states why (for example that the plants were removed or
  the act was withdrawn or replaced), each with its block. Otherwise empty.

A party's claim is not the court's disposition: never fill effect, scope or
grounds from what a party asks or argues.

Issues. Name every limitation of this reading as a whole: an unreadable or
conflicting passage, or a disposition whose reach the decision leaves unclear. A
missing statement is not an issue when the decision simply does not state it.
