Read the supplied complete administrative-court decision from its own text. Return
only what the decision itself states, in the JSON schema supplied. Use no outside
knowledge and no other document. Copy source words exactly; never translate,
normalize or classify them unless a field asks for a choice.

Every citation names the block number and quotes contiguous words copied exactly
from that block, at most about 300 characters. Join separate passages of the same
block with " … " only. A quote that is not in its block makes the whole reading
unusable. Every other field that copies source words copies them from the block
its own citation names.

Orders. List every removal order of the Regione Puglia Osservatorio (or Servizio)
Fitosanitario that the decision names by its number and date (for example
"determinazione dirigenziale n. 137 dell'11.11.2021", "DDS n. 2 del 25/01/2023").
number and adopted_words are copied as printed; year is the four-digit year of
that adoption date. Name each order once, citing one block where its number and
date are printed. Acts of other bodies, laboratory reports and notes are not
orders.

Events. List every dated event the decision states about one listed order. One
entry is one event of one kind:

- recipient-pec-delivery: the order, or a communication of it, was sent or
  delivered by PEC to a named person.
- municipal-publication-start / municipal-publication-end: the first or last day
  of the order's posting on a municipal albo pretorio. A stated interval ("dal 12
  al 19 novembre 2021") gives one start and one end entry from the same quote. A
  single posting day with no end gives a start entry only.
- owner-request: a named owner or holder asked the administration for something
  about the order (review, autotutela, alternative measures, access to records).
- administrative-reply: the administration answered such a request or wrote to a
  named person about the order (a note, a rejection), with its date.

For each event: order_index (from 0, into the orders list); recipient, the named
person the event concerns as printed, or an empty string for a municipal
posting; municipality as printed for a posting, otherwise an empty string;
date_words, the date exactly as printed for this event; date, the same day as
YYYY-MM-DD; attribution, court when the decision states the event as its own
account of the facts, party when it reports what a party claims, pleads or
challenges (for example text inside the list of challenged acts or a party's
arguments); support, the block and quote.

A date the decision does not print for the event is not supplied: omit the event.
Do not add events of other kinds. Hearings, filings and the court's own orders
are not events of the listed kinds.

Issues. Name every limitation of this reading as a whole: an unreadable or
conflicting passage. A missing statement is not an issue when the decision simply
does not state it.
