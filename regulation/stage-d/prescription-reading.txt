Read the supplied complete administrative act from its own text. Return only what
the act itself states, in the JSON schema supplied. Use no outside knowledge and
no other act. Copy source words exactly; never translate, normalize or classify
them unless a field asks for a choice.

Every citation names the physical page and quotes contiguous words copied exactly
from that page's supplied text, at most about 300 characters. Join separate
passages of the same page with " … " only. A quote that is not on its page makes
the whole reading unusable. Every other field that copies source words copies
them from the pages its own citations name.

Identity. The issuing office as printed, the act number and the adoption date as
the act states them (not a publication, protocol, proposal or signature date),
and the title. Mark authority puglia-osservatorio only when the issuing office is
the Regione Puglia Sezione (or Servizio) Osservatorio Fitosanitario.

Prescribed work. List each removal work (estirpazione, abbattimento, eradicazione
or equivalent) that the act's operative part (the points the act adopts, after
"DETERMINA" or equivalent) prescribes to recipients, one entry per distinct
population of plants or land: the recipients as printed (for example "il
proprietario/conduttore"), where the act lists them or their positions as printed
(cohort, for example "Allegato 1/C", or an empty string), the work and the
population as printed. by_reference is the other act whose prescription this
work applies, with its number and year as printed, when the act prescribes the
work by reference to it (for example "come riportato nella DDS 188/2024"); null
when this act states the work itself. An act that prescribes no removal to recipients (for
example one that only delimits areas, only corrects another act's annex without
prescribing work, or only directs an agency) has no entries.

Enforcement clauses. List every clause that states what follows if the
recipients do not begin or do not perform the prescribed removal. A recital, a
premise or a quoted rule of another act is not an operative clause; if the only
statement is outside the operative part, return it with part "recital". A
voluntary-removal schedule with no consequence is not an enforcement clause. For
each clause:

- work_indices: the indices (from 0) of the prescribed work entries it enforces.
- commencement_work and commencement_population: what the recipient must begin
  or perform by the term and the plants it concerns, as this clause prints them.
- term: the period this clause gives the recipient, as printed. number is the
  number exactly as printed (digits or words). unit_word is the unit word exactly
  as printed after it (for example "giorni"), including any qualifier printed
  with it (for example "giorni lavorativi"). literal is the complete term phrase
  as printed. null when the clause states no period.
- anchor: the words stating from when the term runs, as printed, and its kind:
  notification when it runs from notification or notice to the recipient; other
  when it runs from anything else (publication, adoption, a date, the
  recipient's own communication); none when the clause states no starting point.
- consequence: what the clause states follows, as printed. commitment is commits
  when the Osservatorio states that it will direct it (for example "disporrà"),
  permits when it states only that it may, other otherwise.
- coercive_population: literal is the words naming what is to be removed
  coercively, as printed. resolved is the population that the same clause names
  and those words refer to: a back-reference such as "delle suddette piante"
  refers to the population the same clause names before it. Copy that
  population's words from the clause. Use an empty string when the clause states
  no coercive removal, or when the words refer to nothing in the same clause.
- executor: who carries out the coercive removal, as printed; null when not
  stated.
- limits: only what prevents reading this clause's own words: unreadable,
  incomplete or conflicting text in the clause itself. An empty list otherwise.
  A reading choice you made is not a limit.

Relationships. List every other act this act states it corrects, supplements,
completes, replaces, revokes or suspends, with its number and year as printed and
the payload it affects. Citation of an act as legal basis is not a relationship.

Issues. Name every limitation of this reading as a whole: a page without a native
text layer that may hold operative content, an unreadable or conflicting passage
outside the enforcement clauses. A missing statement is not an issue when the act
simply does not state it.
