# Stage D input map

This is the front door to Stage D. It starts with facts required by accepted A–C,
then identifies the source that can supply them. `contracts.json` owns the exact
input kinds. `SPEC.md` owns admission, including temporal reach.

| A–C needs to know | Real source | What one record means | What D must establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| What was observed, where and when, including positive, negative and other results | Regional campaign workbooks, the CKAN CSV and the SIT monitoring point layers | One published observation: a sample, visual inspection or assessment, on one day, with the result its publisher printed | Every published observation, all hosts and all result states, through the ordinary reader `cordon_d.monitoring`. Field meaning below. The publisher's surveillance follows containment and is not an inventory of all infected plants, so the release inventory never completes the detection record. An observation is not an inspection unit. Reach: `SPEC.md`, observation-dated. |
| What the laboratory actually reported | The official report linked from the monitoring record, and the annexes and corrections that report declares | One report rendition with its source-located sample results and its declared document relationships | Follow every referenced report and join its rows to observations in both directions. A join needs the observation's own report route, a source-supported identifier and uniqueness in both directions. Separate reports can each attach to one observation. A correction needs its own source relationship and affected scope; recency and filenames do not supply it, and a whole-report replacement does not change every column. Corrections and distinct copies are kept. Without its predecessor a correction cannot show what it changed. A report row is not the Service's official confirmation; laboratory designation, status and custody come from their own records where the confirmation branch needs them. A failed route or an unread region is not a negative. A blank fragment is not a missing assay. Reach: `SPEC.md`, observation-dated by sampling date. |
| Which legally adopted area contained the location on the event date | The adopting regional act, and the geometry it defines the zone by: official administrative boundaries, cadastral geometry, the located positions of the infected plants it names, or the Region's published layer where that layer carries the act's geometry | One adopted zone in one legal version | The `adopted-geography` input: area polygon, CRS, positional error and shared boundary, bound to the A version in force. The zone is constructed as the act defines it: named administrative units from official boundaries; listed cadastral units from cadastral geometry; a radius or band the act states around named infected plants, from their located positions at the act's width in accepted B; the Region's published layer where it carries that act's geometry. Maps are renderings. A zone name printed beside an observation is not this geometry. Reach: `SPEC.md`, by A's interval. |
| Which plants or surfaces fall inside C's distance and survey calculations | Monitoring observations, PuntiStampa, land-use or host-bearing surfaces, parcels and the other population records the calculation names | An observation, published point or polygon, parcel, grid cell or host-bearing surface, according to its own source | The population each source actually represents. Positives are not all plants. An observation or sample identifier is not a plant. Reach: `SPEC.md`, identity over time. |
| Which cadastral parcel contains or intersects a relevant location | Agenzia delle Entrate and SIT cadastral geometry | One parcel geometry with its cadastral reference | The reached parcel population, with the source identifier preserved. A parcel string on an observation is not a parcel. Ownership is never inferred from geometry or from that string. Reach: `SPEC.md`, identity over time. |
| Whether the Osservatorio issued a removal measure and which plants or parcels it covered | The regional removal determination and its incorporated annexes | One adopted act, plus the subject rows that act defines | Identity, operative clause with its stated term, coercive population and executor, branch, annex incorporation and correction relationships, through one general reader. An annex association is not the operative act, not land standing and not proof of withdrawal. A laboratory replacement does not correct or withdraw an earlier administrative target. Reach: `SPEC.md`, act-dated with live effect. |
| Whether a legally consequential notice, delivery, publication, receipt or response happened | The act's own text for its declared route; the competent municipality's albo pretorio; BURP and the regional sites the plans name; the Osservatorio's communication record and municipal notification attempts; PEC to ARIF and the Prefettura; ARIF's election record or the owner's PEC | One event at one time concerning one document, one sender and one recipient under one route; or one publication with its start, continuity and end | The source that holds the event on the route accepted A selects for the act and recipient. Sending, delivery, publication, recipient effect and response stay distinct. Publication proves the publication duty, not recipient effect, silence, refusal, breach or cost liability. An immediate-effect or cautionary clause makes a measure operative; it is not notification. An election column on an observation is not an owner election. Reach: `SPEC.md`, follows the order it concerns. |
| Who has the consequential relationship to the affected land | The determination's incorporated annex; later acts that correct listed owners; the Osservatorio's matter and the cadastral and owner data it holds; a competent public-asset register for public land | One addressee position in one act version, or one stated ownership, occupation, management or other legally relevant relationship | The annex states the position the act published, not that the named person held the land. An effective correction replaces the listed position for the parcels it names. Current standing beyond the latest act needs the operator's matter or a competent register; parcel maps and public-land catalogues are candidates. A holder string on an observation is not standing. Reach: `SPEC.md`, follows the order it concerns. |
| Whether an affected plant has protected status | The regional monumental-tree register and the matter's exact plant evidence | One registered or provisional protected-plant occurrence | Match the affected plant and read the status in force at the event date from that plant's own history. A protection flag printed beside an observation is not a register entry. Reach: `SPEC.md`, identity over time. |
| Whether protected status requires another permission for this removal | The exact PPTR or local rule and the competent authority's decision reached by that plant and intervention | One applicable protection rule or one issued decision | Only for an affected protected plant. The landscape-proceeding catalogue is not the population. A landscape or hydrogeological flag printed beside an observation is a lead, not the rule or the decision. Reach: `SPEC.md`, identity over time. |
| Who was assigned, what field work occurred, whether removal was completed and what lawful cost resulted | The Osservatorio's and the executing body's casefile and field records | One assignment, assessment, treatment, removal, inspection, completion or cost event | The first-party record for the matter. A public manual cannot fill an absent event. A coercive-direction or priority act is not completed removal. A removal label, date, decree reference or removed-plant layer printed beside an observation is a lead, not a removal occurrence. Reach: `SPEC.md`, act-dated with live effect. |
| When the vector flies in an adopted zone, and which vectors tested positive | The Osservatorio's vector-monitoring tables, composites and circolari (2022–2024) and the CNR-IPSP transmissions to ARIF and the Osservatorio under DGR 1593/2024 (2025 onward), published in the SIT `vettori/` folders and named on the Emergenza Xylella portal; the Osservatorio's vector-measure acts that print monitoring results; the vector findings its held acts recite | One place (a site with its code, agro, printed coordinates, altitude and crop, or an area as printed), one survey window, one method, one species and one stage, with its result as printed. A transmission is identified by its publisher, date, own protocol where printed, and each crop series it carries with that series' round and window | Every in-reach round of every series and every circolare, found by following the publisher; file names are never composed. Round numbers run per series, so a round number alone identifies nothing, and a round a later record names but no listing holds stays visible as unnamed. A missing round, a zero count, a juvenile-only table, a survey calendar, a treatment deadline or an order's urgency is never absence of adults; a count without a test result is not a negative. Two publications of one observation are related and never added. An area aggregate is never spread to sites and an area name is never a zone: a record joins a zone only where its publisher places it, through the agro it prints or a statement tying it to the zone; printed coordinates with no stated positional error place nothing by themselves. For `vector-biology`, per zone version and detection date: the next-season onset bound, the end of the earliest adult window that begins after the detection on a record placed in the zone the consumer names, with its source cell, or its cause; a lower bound only where an official statement says adults were absent from the whole zone; and the unnamed rounds that could fall before the bound. For `survey-performance`: the vector-positive days placed in each area version at both ends of their printed window, and every other positive with its cause. D compares no removal instant with a bound and sets no completeness flag. Reach: `SPEC.md`, observation-dated. |
| Which days count for a working-day clock | Italian holiday law and enacted one-off changes | One national calendar rule | Only the years reached by accepted B clocks. |

## Monitoring observations

One record is one published observation. Every original field is retained.
Publications of one observation are related, never added: counts across releases
and views are not additive.

| Published fields | What one value means |
|---|---|
| `ID`, `ID_CAMPIONE` | The observation reference. From 2018 it identifies one observation across its publications. In the 2013–2017 SIT views it is a daily counter: a value one view gives several rows on one day is not an identifier, and a value used once in each of two views still correlates them, which cannot be told from coincidence. No identifier here identifies a physical plant. |
| `OBJECTID`, `ID_GIORNALIERO`, `NUMERO_ORDINE`, `IDANDROID` | The publisher's other identifiers, under their own names. They are never merged into the reference and never compared across views. `CODICE_CAMPIONAMENTO` names a campaign and is not an identity. |
| `DATA`, `DATA_RILEVAMENTO`, `DATA_CAMPIONE`, `DATA_PRELIVEO`, `DATA_RILIEVO` | The observation or sampling day. Excel midnight is date storage. ArcGIS UTC epochs become the Puglia calendar day; the early campaign's 23:00 UTC values belong to the following local day. Campaign names are not date boundaries. Report, protocol and removal dates stay separate. |
| `TIPOLOGIA`, and the named inspection and assessment views | Samples, visual inspections and assessments, kept apart. An observation without an analytical result is not a negative. |
| `RISULTATO` | The publisher's label: positive, negative, doubtful, pending and other labels. A blank is unpublished. A duplicate label restates the positive it accompanies and is not a positive by itself. The label is not the laboratory diagnosis. Publications that disagree on any field are exposed and none is preferred; only a result disagreement withholds an agreed result. |
| `SPECIE`, `CULTIVAR`, `SUBSPECIE` | Recorded host, cultivar and sample-level subspecies. A view's subspecies title is not a sample-level identification. |
| `SINTOMO`, `SINTOMI` | Visible drying symptoms. `Presente` and `Assente` are presence and absence; an unexplained code stays unknown. Drying symptoms are not a diagnosis. |
| Native geometry and spatial reference; `LONGITUDINE`, `LATITUDINE` | The published location. The frame belongs to the pair: SIT geometry is EPSG:32633 as stated; campaign and printed degree columns state no datum and are read as EPSG:4326, established from the publisher's own redundancy and re-checked as `SPEC.md` says. No source states positional error. Where a record states its place twice, the geometry is taken. Two publications are one place within one centimetre on the ground. A printed pair can carry transposed axes that only another publication of the same observation exposes. |
| `COMUNE`, `COMUNE_COD`, `PROVINCIA` | The observation's own administrative location. `COMUNE_COD` is the ISTAT code. `COD_COMUNE` is the cadastral municipality code and belongs to the parcel row. |
| `DOCUMENTO_CONFERMA`, `LNK_DOCUMENTO_SELGE` | The literal route to the laboratory report. |
| Zone, parcel, protection, holder, election, removal and decree fields | The literal the monitoring publisher printed, for the row that owns the fact. A transcription is not the fact. |

## Materials that are not standalone input gaps

- The BURP URL census locates regional acts. It is not a decision input.
- The SIT service catalogue is a discovery surface. A service enters only when a
  row above requires it.
- Municipal archives enter only for a required publication or notice event.
- Historical removal records enter only when an accepted present or prospective
  input needs that history.
- Public and state land data enter only for standing or permission on affected land.
- PPTR, municipal plans and landscape proceedings enter only for an affected plant
  whose accepted branch requires that protection or permission.
- Large-file backup and repository preservation are engineering concerns, not
  evidence inputs.
- "Difficult source tables", "copy relationships" and "adoption lineage" describe
  work a row may require. They are not source families or reasons to widen the
  aperture.
