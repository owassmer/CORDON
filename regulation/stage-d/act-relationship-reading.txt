Read the supplied administrative act from its own text. Return only what the act
itself states, in the JSON schema supplied. Use no outside knowledge and no other
act. Copy source words exactly; never translate, normalize or classify them
unless a field asks for a choice.

The text is supplied as numbered blocks, one block per page of the act. Every
citation names the block and quotes contiguous words copied exactly from that
block, at most about 300 characters. Join separate passages of the same block
with " … " only. A quote that is not in its block makes the whole reading
unusable. Every other field that copies source words copies them from the blocks
its own citations name.

Identity. The issuing office as printed, the act number and the adoption date as
the act states them (not a publication, protocol, proposal or signature date),
and the title. Mark authority puglia-osservatorio only when the issuing office is
the Regione Puglia Sezione (or Servizio) Osservatorio Fitosanitario.

Relationships. List every other act this act states that it corrects,
supplements, completes, replaces, revokes, suspends or withdraws, as a whole or
in part. withdraws is a statement that what the other act prescribes, or part of
it, will not be carried out or no longer applies, when the act does not call it
a revocation (for example "non si procederà all'estirpazione ..."). For each
act named:

- relationship: the choice above that the act's words state.
- extent: whole when the change reaches the whole other act, part when the act
  states that it reaches only part of it.
- number and year: the other act's number and year as printed. date_words: its
  date as printed, or an empty string when none is printed.
- affected_payload: the words stating what is changed, as printed.
- part: operative when the statement is in this act's operative part (the
  points it adopts, after "DETERMINA" or equivalent); recital when it is only in
  the premises or narrative, including a restatement of a change another act
  made.

When one statement names several acts, list one entry for each act with the
same payload. Citation of an act as a legal basis, a premise or a mention
without a stated change is not a relationship.

Issues. Name every limitation of this reading: an unreadable or conflicting
passage, or a block that may hold operative content but has no readable text. A
missing statement is not an issue when the act simply does not state it.
