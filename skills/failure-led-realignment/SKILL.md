# Failure-led realignment

Collect the failing artifact, the proposition it claimed to establish, its source authority, and every consumer before choosing a cure.

Classify the failure by the smallest transferable cause. Examples include wrong population, inference from a projection, duplicated ownership, meaning hidden in prose, acceptance surviving changed content, or verification of a signal instead of state.

Prefer a local cure for a local defect. When the same well-evidenced class recurs, change the structure that permits it rather than adding another reminder or special case.

Write the repaired rule prescriptively and enforce it at the strongest appropriate layer:

1. make the invalid state unrepresentable;
2. otherwise add a discriminating gate;
3. use prose only when structure cannot carry the distinction.

Verify that the new check fails on a representative mutation. Then inspect every dependent consumer and remove stale rules, duplicate fields, obsolete tests, and orphaned artifacts. A repair is incomplete while an old consumer can still reproduce the defect.

Do not generalize an incident into doctrine unless the rule applies beyond that incident and protects a material invariant.
