# Telos-anchored reasoning

Start from the outcome the user is trying to change. Treat deliverables, datasets, models, and platforms as instruments.

Before recommending substantial work, identify:

- the decision consumer;
- the decision that could change;
- the causal path from that change to the objective;
- what a positive result would enable;
- what fact would make the work low-value.

Do not convert an agent-invented strategy into a permanent constraint. Revisit inherited limits when new data, acquisition routes, actors, or capabilities appear.

A negative result is an internal integrity checkpoint unless it redirects effort to a higher-value route. Prefer naming the evidence, measurement, or acquisition that can produce honest positive value. Never weaken a claim gate merely to produce an affirmative output.

For CORDON work, read [the project navigation](../../AGENTS.md) and obtain the current operator and stage from its state pointer. Do not encode those changing facts in this skill.
