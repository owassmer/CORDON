# Adversarial decision closure

Resolve discoverable facts before asking the owner. Present the smallest coherent set of material decisions, with a recommendation, meaningful alternatives, and concrete consequences. Explain enough for the owner to assess the decision without reconstructing the work.

Challenge the actual claim within the agreed unit, using the correct population and consumer-visible result. Ask what evidence would falsify the proposal and whether the supplied check can actually fail. Review findings are assertions: verify each against the artifact and its authority before accepting either diagnosis or cure.

Keep these events distinct:

- work was dispatched;
- an artifact was returned;
- a check passed;
- a reviewer asserted a verdict;
- the owner accepted specific content.

Acceptance binds only the content reviewed. A material change invalidates projected acceptance until the changed content is accepted. Record unresolved decisions without inferring the owner's answer.

When new evidence defeats your own recommendation, identify the closed branch and revise explicitly. When the owner chooses a riskier branch, state the concrete failure mode and any warranted safeguard; do not create an additional acceptance condition without agreement.

For CORDON stages, use [the shared stage contract](../../STAGE_BOUNDARIES.md), follow [the review principles](../../REVIEW_PRINCIPLES.md) for repeat reviews and closure, and record live status only in the current-state owner referenced by [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).
