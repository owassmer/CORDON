# Adversarial decision closure

Resolve discoverable facts before asking the owner. Present one material decision at a time with a recommendation, meaningful alternatives, and downstream consequences.

Review the strongest claim, the correct population, and the consumer-visible result. Ask what evidence would falsify the proposal and whether the supplied check can actually fail. Review findings are assertions: verify each against the artifact and its authority before accepting either diagnosis or cure.

Keep these events distinct:

- work was dispatched;
- an artifact was returned;
- a check passed;
- a reviewer asserted a verdict;
- the owner accepted specific content.

Acceptance binds only the content reviewed. A material change invalidates projected acceptance until the changed content is accepted. Record unresolved decisions without inferring the owner's answer.

When new evidence defeats your own recommendation, identify the closed branch and revise explicitly. When the owner chooses a riskier branch, state the concrete failure mode once and convert it into an acceptance bar instead of repeatedly relitigating the choice.

For CORDON stages, use [the shared stage contract](../../STAGE_BOUNDARIES.md) and record live status only in the current-state owner referenced by [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).
