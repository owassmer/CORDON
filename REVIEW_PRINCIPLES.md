# Review principles

Review protects the objective and the operator's decision, not the artifact or process. Obtain current alignment from `AGENTS.md` and the applicable contract from `STAGE_BOUNDARIES.md`. This document owns review coverage and adjudication, not stage substance or acceptance.

## Five facets

The partition is MECE in primary responsibility, not in files read or evidence followed. Assign each finding to its smallest material cause; preserve cross-facet implications without counting it twice. Reviewers may investigate beyond their starting lens.

| Facet | Owning question | Distinct responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Purpose and aperture | Is this the right work for the decision and objective? | The chosen decision chain and its population, authority and stage boundaries, read from current state—not the operator's whole remit. Include necessary dependencies and exceptions; other actors enter only through their direct effect on that chain. |
| 2. Authoritative meaning | Does intended meaning faithfully follow its authority? | Primary evidence and accepted upstream semantics: conditions, quantities, actors, exceptions, time, and fact versus inference or adjudication. Later stages cannot rewrite earlier meaning. |
| 3. Composition, consistency and completeness | Do the necessary parts form a coherent, complete and consistently expressed system? | Coverage against the in-aperture authoritative universe; schema shapes, field meanings, naming, units and contracts across owners and consumers; relationships, version seams and independently variable states. Sound parts must also compose correctly. |
| 4. Realization and assurance | Does the actual result satisfy the claim, and can the evidence distinguish failure? | Consumer-visible behavior, recomputation, boundaries, failure paths, permissions, integrity, discriminating tests, destination reads and exact-content acceptance. A green signal is not the result. |
| 5. Minimality and ownership | Can full necessary meaning survive in a slimmer representation? | An explicit lean adversary: unnecessary schema, scope or workflow expansion; redundant state, multiple sources of truth, abstractions, documentation, tests and ceremony. Challenge proposed cures as well as implementation. |

Facet 1 decides what belongs; facet 5 challenges unnecessary machinery representing or protecting it. Facet 2 checks meaning against authority; facet 3 checks coverage, consistency and composition; facet 4 checks realization and verification. Consistency preserves shared contracts, not uniform shapes for genuinely different concepts. A defect and its missing regression test are not two findings.

Across A–G, the stage contract changes what can be demanded, not the partition. Mathematical boundaries, evidence joins, atomic Actions, fact ownership and deployment permissions enter when their stage owns them—not earlier.

## Independent review, joint adjudication

For consequential new behavior, architecture, irreversible change or an owner-reserved decision:

1. Name the coherent unit, snapshot, claim, consumer and negative scope. Share generalized alignment and applicable rulings, not expected findings or verdicts.
2. Assign independent subagents across all five facets, explicitly including the lean adversary. Record coverage; grouping or waves may accommodate capacity without changing the unit. Reviewers choose their investigations. Prior findings support regression, not an exhaustive script for thought.
3. Collect all reports before adjudication. Do not exchange interim conclusions between reviewers or remediate from the first return.
4. Adjudicate together. Verify consequential positive and negative assertions against authority and consumers; reproduce counterexamples. Resolve duplicates and conflicts. Distinguish defects, assurance limits, rejected assertions and genuine owner decisions. Reject bloated cures even when the diagnosis is valid.
5. Repair owners and affected consumers together; verify failures and material regressions. Before presenting content for acceptance, obtain convergent independent review across all five facets on the resulting coherent whole. Targeted repair checks do not substitute for that pass. Collect and jointly adjudicate each round; resolve material findings and review the resulting whole again until convergence. A convergence pass requires adequate stated coverage and no remaining material findings after adjudication; it does not require inventing certainty beyond the stage contract. Reviewer verdict, passed check and owner acceptance remain distinct.

Routine reconciliation stays direct. Findings do not authorize broader work or another approval ritual. Repeat review only for changed meaning or a named remaining risk, subject to user direction. This constitution does not authorize a fanout or acceptance.

## Shared dispatch brief

Use this same brief for every facet. Supply only the review-unit record and assigned facet separately; the record identifies the snapshot, claim, consumer, boundaries and reason for review. Preserve these inputs in the dated record. Amend the common brief explicitly when justified, not through ad hoc per-reviewer paraphrases. The five facet definitions above supply the assignments.

> Review independently under AGENTS.md, these review principles, the applicable STAGE_BOUNDARIES.md contracts and state/CURRENT.json. Read the supplied review-unit record and use relevant repository skills. Obtain the objective, chosen decision chain, population, authority and stage boundaries from their owners; the operator's whole remit is not the aperture.
>
> Test the supplied claim against authoritative meaning and actual consumers. Existing representation does not establish relevance; absence does not establish a defect. For a proposed inclusion or exclusion, explain the practical decision consequence in context. Necessary dependencies can be performed by other actors or affect a prospective decision without a held instance. A real source obligation is not by itself a requirement to operate the surrounding workflow.
>
> Choose your investigations and methods. Your facet is a primary responsibility, not a restriction on following relevant evidence. Prior reviews and primary recommendations are assertions to evaluate, not conclusions to inherit. Challenge additions, deletions and retained meaning on the same basis. Preserve necessary distinctions with the least structure; missing evidence must not become an invented legal condition.
>
> Work read-only and do not consult peer reports. Return PASS within stated coverage or FINDINGS, with coverage and limits. For each finding identify the affected claim, evidence, practical consequence and smallest faithful remedy. Distinguish a material defect from an optional improvement or a concern outside the unit. No finding count or verdict is expected. Do not change state, dispatch agents, or presume adjudication or owner acceptance.

## Lean enforcement

A consequential review is incomplete until its existing dated record shows the unit, all five facets' coverage, evidence limits, joint adjudication and remaining decisions. A compact table and supported findings suffice—no parallel ledger, scoring system, quota or workflow schema. Missing coverage is addressed or explicitly bounded, never counted as passed.

Enforce demonstrable invariants in structure or discriminating checks. Repository checks verify this constitution's presence and reference, not the quality of reasoning. The primary adjudicator demonstrates coverage in the review record; the stage owner accepts content.

Delete before adding, without erasing necessary distinctions. Caution, provenance and review must not become the product. Revise facets for demonstrated blind spots; keep incidents and stage-specific checklists in dated records.
