01 /

One decision system.
Three kinds of work.

A / Legal meaning

What follows—and why?

A connects circumstances to legal consequences: applicable authority, actors, triggers, branches, exceptions, evidence requirements and effects.

It is a versioned decision map, not a pile of summaries. A particular source can establish authority, narrow a population or modify a case without creating a separate operator workflow.

B / Time & quantity

What must be measured?

B extracts the temporal and quantitative meaning already supplied by A: clocks, anchors, ordering, recurrence, distances, thresholds and satisfaction conditions.

It is a qualified specification, not a bag of numbers. “48 hours” is incomplete without “from what event, for which plants, and satisfying what condition?”

C / Computation

What is the result here?

C will turn A’s predicates and B’s specifications into deterministic calculations over supplied inputs: dates, elapsed time, spatial sets and condition evaluations.

It is evaluation, not new law. It neither invents missing meaning nor performs the operator’s legally reserved decision.

The stages describe how we derive the instrument.
They are not three consecutive events in an outbreak.

A is connected, not simply sequential

A rule may rely on another rule’s authority or result; an exception can narrow a population; two obligations can coexist. An arrow can mean “depends on,” not “happens next.”

B retains meaning, not just magnitude

A wait is not a deadline. A minimum is not an exact value. A clock’s completion is the fact satisfying that clock—not the whole operation it helps enable. Open terms stay open where the source gives no number.

  • B owns clocks, parameters and expression dispositions. A disposition explains whether an expression supplies a new item, reuses one, is non-computational or is deferred. It is not another operational duty.
02 /

Start at the operator’s desk.

A positive finding arrives. The Osservatorio needs a faithful answer to “What now?”—not a tour through every law or administrative function.

What finding?

Which plant, which result, which competent official conclusion?

Which governing context?

Event-time law, adopted geography, applicable regime and authority.

Which plants and measures?

Relevant populations, treatment, sampling and genuine exceptions.

What is due?

Notice, election, sequencing, time windows and applicable case terms.

What has happened?

Readiness, commencement, physical execution and official proof.

What if execution stalls?

Applicable substitute or coercive authority and its actual conditions.

These questions interact. Evidence can change a branch; a corrected recipient relation can change who is notified; a retention exception can change the removal population while leaving treatment relevant. This is an orientation map, not a six-step mandatory procedure.

Authority is not an extra queue

EU, national, regional and case-specific meaning are read together. A regional obligation may implement a Union duty; that does not automatically mean two operations. The other actor’s authority, action or result enters where it changes this chain—not through importing their whole remit.

Two kinds of time

Legal time: which version or adopted area governed the relevant event?

Operational time: when was notice effective, treatment executed or work begun?

Old law may matter to an unfinished obligation today. Historical completeness for its own sake does not.

Outside this chosen product chain: the separate planting/replanting authorization workflow, funding and indemnities, the administrative-penalty chain, and full laboratory or staffing administration. An excluded workflow’s relevant legal consequence can still matter to an included decision.

  • CURRENT → aperture owns the chosen chain and exclusions; operator competence is context, not the entire product boundary.
  • Design principles explain shared performance, other actors, evidence boundaries and minimum sufficient structure.
  • Jurisdiction A: IT-DLGS-19-2021:Art.28(3) and IT-DLGS-19-2021:Art.32 families illustrate official finding and enforcement authority. Read their exact applicable versions, not this summary as a legal instruction.

04 /

What is due?
First identify what the clock measures.

Illustrative timing story. Assume a plant remains in the operative removal population, the relevant treatment has actually been executed, and the applicable treatment duties are otherwise satisfied. The example isolates the regional wait; it is not a complete removal authorization.

A / Meaning

The applicable treatment/removal sequence concerns plants still due for removal after exceptions. Treating a lawfully retained plant does not give permission to remove it.

B / Specification

Kind: not-before.
Anchor: executed vector treatment.
Magnitude: at least 48 elapsed hours.
Satisfaction: this waiting condition is met—not that removal occurred.

C / Illustrative computation

From a supplied treatment timestamp, evaluate the elapsed interval. Report whether this prerequisite is satisfied while leaving other prerequisites and actual execution as separate conclusions.

Ready to perform an operation ≠ the operation performed.

Now change the kind of clock

A retained case-specific ten-day noncommencement term starts from legally sufficient notification and is satisfied by concrete commencement. It is not the same clock as completing removal after an owner’s election. Its expiry alone does not execute a coercive decision or create a universal ten-day grace period.

“Immediately” stays legally meaningful. It is not converted into an invented number of hours. Likewise, biological windows and retained source conflicts cannot be replaced by a convenient calendar shortcut. In the treatment example, the November–March wording is not blanket permission to skip controlling treatment requirements.

What is established?
A measurement is not an official finding.

Illustrative evidence story. A technically valid analytical result is supplied with its assay identity. These numerical examples explain the existing classification meaning, not a diagnostic service or a decision to remove a plant.

A / Meaning

Separate the assay’s analytical class, the individual plant identity, applicable confirmation requirements, and the competent official finding. A separate routing-validity issue need not erase confirmation evidence.

06 /

Change one fact.
Notice what stays true.

These are the distinctions that keep sophistication useful. Each protects a different decision consequence; none is a reason to build an adjacent administrative system.

Commencement / completion

“Work started.”
“All required work finished.”

Starting can satisfy a commencement condition without completing the prescribed operation. A later completion question remains.

Do not collapse these into one “done” status.

Execution / proof

“Trees were removed.”
“The official record is missing.”

Missing proof limits what can be established from the record. It does not prove physical nonexecution; an evidentiary duty may nevertheless remain outstanding.

Missing evidence is not affirmative evidence of failure.

More than one authority / one performance

“Both rules apply.”
“This operation satisfies both.”

Do not manufacture duplicate work from duplicate source references. Preserve any independently additional duration, population or condition.

Shared performance is not permission to erase a stricter requirement.

Retention / treatment

“This plant is lawfully retained.”
“A treatment duty still applies.”

An exception to removal does not automatically remove other phytosanitary duties. Conversely, treating the retained plant does not revive a removal permission.

Exceptions change the conclusions they actually govern.

Historical / presently consequential

“This is an old prescription.”
“Its unfinished obligation still matters.”

Earlier meaning may govern a live consequence. By contrast, reconstructing superseded programme compliance with no operative consequence does not advance this instrument.

History enters through consequence, not age or archival completeness.

Consuming status / running administration

“This laboratory’s task status changed.”
“Its recurring audit programme must be managed.”

The first can affect which official evidence the chain may use. That does not automatically bring the second workflow into CORDON.

A real legal duty is not automatically a product capability.

Source conflict / operational permission

“The source contains this number or window.”
“The operator may act on it unconditionally.”

A retained conflict is evidence of the conflict. Its value cannot silently override controlling meaning or become permission to omit a required measure.

Preserving a statement is not endorsing every possible use of it.

One unresolved input / the whole decision

“This historical area boundary is unknown.”
“Nothing useful can be concluded.”

The gap blocks the membership conclusion requiring that geometry. Unrelated supported calculations need not stop, and required reachable evidence remains acquisition work.

Localize uncertainty without hiding it or spreading it.

07 /

What we have.
What C will add.

The A/B handoff

A source-grounded, versioned account of the relevant legal decisions, plus their temporal and quantitative specifications. It preserves necessary distinctions and explicitly limits unsupported conclusions.

It is not a running decision service, live case database, comprehensive historical legal profile or certification that every external workflow has been performed.

The C contribution

Deterministic formulas, calendar/elapsed-time calculations, spatial operations and predicate evaluation, with boundary and missing-input behavior that preserve the supplied meaning.

For now, assume inputs can be supplied. Their acquisition and evidence contracts belong to D; operational Actions to E; semantic nouns and platform compilation to F/G.

Computation supports a decision; it does not manufacture authority. A calculated deadline is not a notification event. A calculated affected set is not an executed removal. A classified result is not the operator’s official finding. C makes the accepted distinctions usable rather than dissolving them.

A explains what the world legally means.
B specifies the relevant time and quantity.
C will calculate faithfully within that meaning.

All three serve the same practical objective: help the operator take the right next step against Xylella, with neither unnecessary removal nor avoidable delay caused by a distorted decision.
