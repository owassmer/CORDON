### Findings (quoted)

**Method in actu, regional.** DDS 45/2025 lines 820–829: "Per pianificare la sorveglianza e applicare il metodo Ribess + sono definiti: Area/zona; popolazione target; unità epidemiologiche; fattori di rischio; sensibilità del metodo. I parametri individuati sono elaborati attraverso l’applicazione dello strumento statistico RiBESS + con i livelli di confidenza e prevalenza indicati nel Reg. UE 2020/1201 per ogni area/zona oggetto di monitoraggio, in alcuni casi (alto rischio) sono applicati criteri più stringenti. Dall’applicazione dello strumento statistico RiBESS + deriva il n° piante da campionare." Lines 838–847: the epidemiological unit is "le piante appartenenti alle specie specificate a maggior rischio … e di conseguenza gli ettari di superficie investiti a oliveto, frutteto, ecc." DGR 1075 line 983: "L'applicazione del Ribess+ prevede una metodologia ad uno step con la quale si definisce il numero di piante da campionare nell'area interessata." DGR 538/2021 lines 445–448: "RiBESS+ con un'applicazione a due step. Il primo step … numero di piante da campionare nell’ Unita Epidemiologica identificata (macromaglia da 100 ettari) e il secondo teso a definire il numero di macromaglie da monitorare" with "Livello di confidenza a livello di U.E. 0,50" (line 461). The two-step form persists in DGR 343/1866 (REGIONAL.md lines 562, 700) and becomes one-step in DGR 1075.

**Design values in actu.** DGR 1075 Tabella 2 (lines 1247–1309): confidence 0,95 (olive, vine, fruit), 0,9 (other areas), 0,99999 (total); prevalence 0,0005 (olive), 0,0009 (vine, fruit), 0,007 (other), 0,0001 (total); plants per hectare 300 / 1500 / 625 / 300; high-risk share 0,0167 / 0,0192 / 0,0323 / 0,0133. These equal the PNI 2026 RiPEST rows (U138 = 0.98330913690173727 medium olive; AD138 = 5E-4; AC138 = 0.95). DGR 538 Tab. 5 (Monopoli AD): 0,90 / 0,0004 olive; 14 plants/ha high-risk vs 7 base (lines 584–587). B stores the PNI values with `confidence_bound: exact`, the EU targets with `confidence_bound: floor`.

**Sensitivity values held.** PNI 2026 row 138–146: O = 0.7 (sampling effectiveness), P = 0.78 (diagnostic sensitivity) for every Puglia stratum; DM 2026 lines 891–893: "Sensibilità del metodo: probabilità di ottenere un risultato positivo campionando una pianta sicuramente infetta. È data dal prodotto della sensibilità di laboratorio e della sensibilità di campo". The 2022 plan's 0.55 reference value is read in NATIONAL.md line 389 as "expressly a reference value that may be changed", and line 524: "The 2022 `0.55` reference sensitivity is no longer stated. … No old default should be carried into the current plan by temporal inertia." No Puglia act states a per-hectare or per-pool sensitivity; DDS 45 lists "sensibilità del metodo" as an input without a value.

**Pooling in actu.** DDS 45 line 1011–1016: symptomatic plants sampled singly; pools from asymptomatic plants; "Le piante da campionare devono essere della specie cui è attribuita la maglia". DDS 31/2022 lines 639–651: 14 plants per maglia → 2 pools of 7, "scelte casualmente … ad es. lungo le diagonali". DGR 538 line 473: "in ciascun ettaro si campionano 7 piante che costituiranno 1 campione pool". PNI workbook distinguishes samples (X) from tests (AA): e.g. 5537 samples / 791 tests (olive medium). The C draft (`SPEC.md` line 103–105): "These identities follow the operative inspection unit, not the number of assays."

Biological explanation, software assumptions, recommended methods and reference sensitivity values remain supporting design inputs unless adopted as mandatory source-native parameters. RiBESS/RiPEST calculates or supports sample size. It does not define legal population, allocate samples, select a branch or establish completion." ### Findings (quoted)

**Cq bands in actu (DDS 45/2025, lines 1500–1508).** "In Puglia, per uniformare i risultati dei diversi laboratori e tenendo conto del risultato del ring test effettuato a ottobre 2024, i laboratori effettuano le analisi molecolari applicando: PCR in tempo reale sulla base di Harper et al., 2010 (e erratum 2013). … Cq< 32: “Positivo” / Cq>32 e < 35: “Dubbio” da sottoporre ad ulteriori verifiche / Cq> 35: nessun valore, “Negativo”." The act calls these "criteri generali di riferimento" (line 1504). Exact 32 and exact 35 are not assigned by the source; B and C carry them as unresolved (B note: "Boundary values 32 and 35 are unresolved in A (AUTHORITY_INTERPRETATION_REQUIRED)"; C `diagnostic.py` emits `"Cq = 32"` and `"Cq = 35"` as separate predicates).

**Doubtful route in actu (DDS 45/2025, lines 1509–1518).** Same sample re-extracted and re-run with Harper; if still doubtful on a pool, singles tested; if a single is still doubtful, sample to CNR-IPSP for aliquots; if any aliquot is still doubtful, "ne viene data comunicazione al SFR e l’esito caricato sul database. In tal caso la pianta dovrà essere ricampionata."

**Predecessor bands (DDS 31/2022, lines 1050–1063).** "Cq< 32: “Positivo” / Cq>32: “Dubbio” … / Cq=nessun valore, “Negativo”". No 35 upper bound; the value 35 appears only inside the repeat route: "il campione generi valori di “Cq” compresi fra 32 e 35, si procede dapprima ad una nuova estrazione del campione come “campione pool”". DDS 31 also permitted Ouyang 2013 as the repeat assay ("che ha una sensibilità diagnostica comparabile a quella di Harper"), and for zona indenne/cuscinetto pools routed to CNR-IPSP for "un‘analisi molecolare … differente da quella utilizzata dal laboratorio di primo livello" (line 1071–1073). DDS 45 drops the different-assay requirement and instead routes singles to CNR for subspecies typing (Dupas 2019 qPCR; MLST in zona indenne).

**Lab return times in actu (DDS 45/2025, lines 1564–1569).** "Le analisi sono effettuate, registrate nel database e comunicate all’Osservatorio, entro massimo: 7 giorni lavorativi dal ricevimento dei campioni, per i campioni risultati positivi / 10 gg lavorativi … per i campioni risultati negativi. I campioni positivi devono essere inviati con sollecitudine al CNR-IPSP che effettua l’analisi e comunica il risultato entro massimo tre giorni lavorativi dal ricevimento del campione." B encodes these as working-day clocks anchored at receipt. The 2026-09-04 review (R7.2) corrected the DDS45 "prompt transfer" completion from delivery to dispatch, keeping receipt as the CNR clock anchor.

**Data field conventions (CAMP workbooks, recomputed 2026-09-08 from `CAMP_2024.xlsx` and `CAMP_2025.xlsx`, read-only).** 2024: 119,729 rows; `RISULTATO` ∈ {NEGATIVO 118,610; POSITIVO 1,113; NaN 6}; `SINTOMO` ∈ {ASSENTE 116,753; PRESENTE 1,590; NaN 1,386}; `SUBSPECIE` ∈ {NaN 118,616; MULTIPLEX 617; FASTIDIOSA 339; PAUCA 157}; `TIPOLOGIA` ∈ {CAMPIONE 71,923; NaN 47,806}. 2025: 121,860 rows; `RISULTATO` ∈ {NEGATIVO 121,464; POSITIVO 396}; `SINTOMO` ∈ {ASSENTE 120,712; PRESENTE 1,136; NaN 12}; `SUBSPECIE` ∈ {NaN 121,464; PAUCA 340; MULTIPLEX 36; FASTIDIOSA 20}; `TIPOLOGIA` all CAMPIONE. ### Findings (quoted)

**No held source states flight-season dates as law.** The EU texts use "flight season of the vector" without dates (authoring-eu.json 5(1)(d) quote; B windows). In presenza di inverni miti, alcuni esemplari adulti possono sopravvivere fino alla primavera successiva." (lines 466–468). ### Findings (quoted)

**Buffer origin — the canonical example.** Canon: `B-PAR-EU-4(2)(a)-eradication-buffer-2.5km` scope "width outward from the infected-zone boundary, eradication branch" (floor, ≥2.5 km); `B-PAR-EU-4(2)-sub1-infected-zone-50m` "minimum radius around each plant found infected" (floor). Contradicting implementation: `W1/scripts/verify_containment_locally.py` lines 110–112 "point_m = to_metric(…Point…); ring_circle = point_m.buffer(INFECTED_ZONE_RADIUS_M); buffer_circle = point_m.buffer(ERADICATION_BUFFER_RADIUS_M)" with docstring "The transform buffers a confirmed positive to the regulation's 2.5 km eradication radius in EPSG:32633 and intersects it against surveyed parcels." — the 2,500 m disk is drawn from the plant, reaching 2,500 m not 2,550 m. The same repository's later `verify_zone_math.py` (commit `2e7cd9b`, "Project the clocks and parameters, then check the geometry by computing it") asserts the opposite: "The buffer width is measured from the infected zone's boundary, so a single plant's eradication buffer reaches 2 550 m and not 2 500 m" and "The infected zone for several plants is the union of their buffers, and union-then-buffer differs from buffer-then-union wherever circles overlap" (lines 7–10; note its own comment at line 66–69 shows the areas are identical for a convex union and diverge only for non-convex unions). Neither script is authority; the C draft (`spatial.py` docstring) states "Legal geometry is supplied as adopted, never inferred from a numeric minimum" and `minimum_enclosure()` only tests an adopted shape for containment and clearance.

**Validation against an official annex — what was and was not validated.** (a) 50 m ring parcel set: `check_ring_parity.py` compares the platform's computed zone rows with DET 3/2026 Allegato 1/B (5 parcels). The skill (`SKILL.md`) records the outcome as "5/5 for Bitonto 29/{117,137,145,146,247}". I found no review file in `W1/reviews/` that prints the parity run output; `w3-cycle3-domain.md` line 347 confirms the host parcel citation "A893:29:145 host: DET 3/2026 Allegato 1/B: BITONTO foglio 29 particella 145" and `verify_containment_locally.py` line 147 prints "annex ring parcels reproduced : n/5". The parity claim therefore rests on the skill note and script, not on a preserved run log. The parcel test used was `ring_circle.intersects(geom_m)` on a plant-centred 50 m disk in EPSG:32633 against SIT Catasto (Sept 2021 Sigmater) polygons — i.e. **any intersection counts**, matching the acts' "anche parzialmente" clause. (b) Zone-layer identity: `tier0-cycle2-domain.md` lines 161–186 validated the ST1 layers against DDS 236/2025 foglio tables (EXACT MATCH) using a >1 m² intersection threshold with the SIT foglio layer. (c) No 2.5 km / 5 km buffer construction was ever validated against an official annex in these repositories; the only 2.5 km object compared was the SIT-published Cuscinetto layer, taken as adopted geometry.

Each item names the artifacts, quotes the disagreement, and states which side (if any) is current authority. Nothing here is an adoption recommendation.

### C1. Buffer origin — plant vs infected-zone boundary (Family 5)
- `W1/scripts/verify_containment_locally.py` (commit `9c6ccee`, 2026-08-25; implementation, superseded): "buffers a confirmed positive to the regulation's 2.5 km eradication radius in EPSG:32633" — `point_m.buffer(2500)` from the plant.
- `W1/scripts/verify_zone_math.py` (commit `2e7cd9b`, 2026-08-26; implementation, historical): "a single plant's eradication buffer reaches 2 550 m and not 2 500 m"; union-then-buffer.
- `SK/stage-a-trajectory…` line 28 (historical): "per-plant shortcut still wrong LEGAL model (erases IZ/BZ identity, diverges when adopted geometry differs from statutory minimum)."
- Canon: `B-PAR-EU-4(2)(a)-eradication-buffer-2.5km` scope "width outward from the infected-zone boundary"; C draft never constructs a legal buffer (`spatial.py`: "Legal geometry is supplied as adopted").
- Gap: no 2.5 km/5 km construction was ever validated against an official annex; only the SIT cuscinetto layers were compared with act foglio tables (`tier0-cycle2-domain.md`).

### C2. 400 m band origin differs by source (Family 5)
- EU Art. 10 / 15(4): "the first 400 m surrounding the infected zones" (`B-PAR-EU-10-sub2-400m-higher-risk`, `B-PAR-EU-15(4)-400m-higher-risk`); EU 5(1)(c) v2: "surrounding the infected plants"; EU 2(4a): vector radius "at least 400 m".
- DDS 45 containment: "area larga 400 m attorno alla pianta risultata infetta, partendo dal perimetro dell’area di raggio 50 m" (line 1310) → 50–450 m band from the plant; DDS 45 indenne/cuscinetto: "area di larghezza pari a 400 m attorno alla zona infetta, tutti gli ettari" (line 1318) → band from the zone.
- Canon preserves all as separate parameters (`B-PAR-DDS45-post-finding-containment-outer-band-400m` vs `…-pest-free-buffer-outer-band-400m`); recovery handoff line 287 states the distinction. Not a contradiction in canon; a trap for any implementation that unifies "400 m".

### C3. Partial-parcel rule — most-restrictive label vs membership relation (Family 5)
- `W1/DOMAIN_DECISIONS.md` D-10 (historical, no authority): "most-restrictive-regime on partial intersects; method literal `local_shapely_pip_most_restrictive_intersect`"; §D-12 then says predicates must read "zone membership … never from the resolved label" — the former build contradicted itself between D-10's single label and D-12's set.
- Deleted `SPECS/S3-eligibility.md` A1 (`git show 69c1180^`): `zone_as_of(centroid(A), today)` — centroid-based, contradicting D-10's intersect rule within the same build.
- Canon/draft: `REG-PUGLIA-U181-DIR-2021-00069:partial-parcel-rule:v1` effect "Apply source-defined mandatory measures to the parcel administrative relation without classifying every plant infected"; `SPEC.md` "A partial-parcel relation does not expand infected-plant membership"; `spatial.partial_parcel` = area overlap > 0, edge contact excluded.

### C4. Parity claim "5/5" without a preserved run log (Family 5)
- `SK/../SKILL.md` asserts "5/5 for Bitonto 29/{117,137,145,146,247}"; `check_ring_parity.py` and `verify_containment_locally.py` implement the test; no `W1/reviews/*` file prints the parity output (searched `5/5|parity`; hits are the Alberobello/Bitonto annex transcriptions only). Status: historical claim, unverified in the archive.

### C5. Notice: "assume valore di notifica" vs Law 241 Art. 21-bis vs MAIA Art. 20(3) (Family 4)
- Orders 2024–2025: "decorso il settimo giorno dalla data di pubblicazione assume valore di notifica" (DDS 147/2024 line 111–113; DDS 173/2025 line 120–122). Orders 2019–2020: "decorso il tredicesimo giorno dalla data di invio della Pec assume valore di notifica" (HIST-DDS-51-2019 line 201–203) — different anchor (PEC send date + 3 + 10) and period.
- MAIA Art. 20(3) (`PUG-DPGR22-A20.txt`): albo telematico "per dieci giorni lavorativi … L'avvenuta pubblicazione, comunque, non esonera dall'obbligo della notifica".
- `SK/civic-data-universe-probes.md` line 31: "TAR Bari 1640/2019: albo-pretorio-only notification upheld" (secondary note; judgment not held in the corpus).
- Canon: four Law 241 Art. 21-bis routes emit `ACT_EFFECTIVE_AGAINST_RECIPIENT` (commit `2ada1e8`); `PUG-DGR1075-2025:§4.5:recipient-notice-route:v1` effect "Use personal communication where possible; use mass publicity only when its act-specific predicates are proven"; B anchors election/silence clocks on "end of the publication period" and noncommencement on "legally sufficient notification". The act text's "assume valore di notifica" is retained as a source claim, not as the operative effectiveness rule.

### C6. Internal clock conflict inside the same orders (Family 4)
- `W1/…/REGIONAL.md` lines 1420–1438 (historical reading of the 2021 orders): election through day 14 + 10 days execution "versus: forced-removal trigger at day 10 from notice … The source does not reconcile the two anchors." The same structure persists in 2024–2025 orders (3-day election after 7-day publication + 10 days execution vs "concreto avvio … entro massimo 10 giorni dall’avvenuta notifica"). Canon keeps both (`B-CLK-DGR1075-owner-execution` etc. and the 18 `notification-noncommencement` clocks); `SPEC.md` line 49–51 treats noncommencement as a separate case-qualified condition.

### C7. Counting units and the missing roll-over rule (Family 4)
- Sources use "giorni naturali e consecutivi" (albo), plain "giorni" (election/execution), "giorni lavorativi" (internal handoffs, lab return, D.lgs 19 Arts. 29/31). No held Osservatorio or Puglia source states event-day exclusion, weekend/holiday roll-over, or the calendar to use. `stage-c-derivation-2026-09-08.md` line 121–127 records the pending proposal; the C draft parameterises it (`deadline(..., roll_forward, minimum_working_days)`), fixtures use weekend {Sat, Sun} + one holiday without adjudicating.

### C8. Cq boundaries — versioned bands, exact values unresolved, data carries no Cq (Family 2)
- DDS 31/2022: "Cq>32: Dubbio … Cq=nessun valore, Negativo" (no 35); DDS 45/2025: "Cq>32 e < 35: Dubbio; Cq> 35: nessun valore, Negativo". Exact 32 and 35 unassigned by both; B notes `AUTHORITY_INTERPRETATION_REQUIRED`; C draft emits `Cq = 32`/`Cq = 35` predicates.
- Official records held (CAMP workbooks, SIT monitoring layer) carry `RISULTATO` labels only; no Cq, assay, or pool/single flag. The classifier has no held data to run on; D-6 (historical) and canon agree a positive label is evidence, not delimitation.
- DDS 31 lines 1053–1055 add: "tali indicazioni sono generiche e vanno adattate alle risultanze ottenute per ogni singolo saggio real time PCR" — the bands are stated as guidance, not a mechanical rule; DDS 45 repeats "criteri generali di riferimento".

### C9. Lab return clocks — DDS 31 vs DDS 45 completion boundary (Family 2)
- DDS 31 (line 1082–1085 region; canonical `first-level-analysis-registration-return:v1`): 5 wd covers analysis + registration, communication "a seguire"; DDS 45: 7/10 wd cover analysis, registration and communication. The 2026-09-04 review R6.7 sustained the distinction; not to be merged.

### C10. RiBESS two-step (DGR 538/343/1866) vs one-step (DGR 1075) and the tool's approximations (Family 1)
- Versioned method change, not a contradiction; `SPEC.md` line 90–91: "The historical DGR1866 two-stage method and the DGR1075 one-stage method are distinct operative contexts, not interchangeable defaults."
- Tool vs exact: RiBESS 0.1.2 returns 86 where exact needs 87 (N=100, D=2, s=.9, C=.95). Whether the instrument reproduces the tool or the exact kernel is the pending method question (derivation record lines 118–120).
- PNI "Popolazione infinita" (row 146) vs DGR 1075 finite hectare populations: two population models in the same programme year.

### C11. Sensitivity values (Family 1)
- 2022 national plan: 0.55 reference (NATIONAL.md line 389); PNI 2026: 0.7 × 0.78 = 0.546; DGR 1075 tables state no sensitivity. Prior reading: "No old default should be carried into the current plan by temporal inertia." No Puglia act binds a value.
