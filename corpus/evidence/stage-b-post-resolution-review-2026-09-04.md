The primary agent read all nine A atoms for this instrument and the complete relevant vector passage in `regulation/jurisdiction/national/DM-348260-2026-Xylella-plan.txt`, lines 1476–1500. Counterexample: a valid eradication area after 2026-08-06 activates B's investigation but makes its producer not applicable. A later consumer must suppress the duty or bypass A. Neither is coherent.

Recommended repair: admit the source-supported eradication meaning into the appropriate A atom and rebind B and its expression disposition. Preserve the separate containment geography and approved annual cadence. This is a narrow source-supported reopening, not permission to broaden the containment producer indiscriminately.

### R2 — Medium: global seat binding conflates Union responsibility with domestic execution

The B convention `seat_binding` maps the Member State concerned to the Osservatorio globally. Both annual Article 22(3) reporting clocks use `MEMBER_STATE_CONCERNED`. But A's `IT-DLGS-19-2021:Art.27(6):transmit-prior-year-results:v1` and its admitted source `IT-DLGS19-A27-20210313.txt`, paragraph 6, assign Commission/Member-State transmission to the Central Plant Health Service. B's own disposition for this national atom recognizes the national actor.

Counterexample: the region has transmitted its results to the central service but Union reporting is not complete. The approved completion interpretation correctly keeps these events separate, while the global role convention still misattributes the reporting duty operationally.

Recommended repair: preserve the Union-level legal obligor, derive the domestic actor from the applicable A allocation, and remove or narrow the universal seat assertion. Do not turn this into regional responsibility for Rome's workflow. Respect the domestic provision's temporal coverage; the 2021 allocation must not simply be backdated over the entire original reporting version.

### R4 — Medium: an unqualified note turns a retention condition into an apparent sampling duty

`B-CLK-EU-7(1)(e)-v2-immediate-sampling` says the two-year lookback is the only alternative to immediate sampling. The primary agent checked its complete A/B context and [Article 7(1)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02020R1201-20251124): the provision defines a removal population; immediate negative testing and the stated deferral can avoid that route. Immediate removal remains available without first satisfying the testing/lookback route.

Counterexample: a point-(e) plant is immediately removed without prior testing or qualifying lookback evidence. The note implies no lawful alternative existed, although removal satisfies the relevant removal requirement. B's expiry consequence already preserves the removal population, so the finding is the unqualified exclusivity claim, not proof the entire conditional timing row must be deleted.

Recommended repair: scope the interaction to avoiding removal through the testing/deferral route. Do not add sampling as a prerequisite to compliant removal, or confuse this with Article 5's separately stated reduction conditions.
