## §1 Which regime am I in?

The rules that decide whether your trees can be felled, what you may replant, and what you may move **differ by regime**. The map changed four times in 2024–2026. New eradication foci keep minting growers who learned the wrong (Salento) rules. Establish your regime first.

### 1.1 Decision tree

Use the official parcel lookup on **www.emergenzaxylella.it** (application referenced in DDS 18 del 14/03/2024). Do not rely on memory or on last year's map.

```
Is your parcel inside the pauca-ST53 "ex Salento" area delimitata?  (map: DDS 82 dell'11/05/2026)
├─ YES → which zone?
│   ├─ Zona infetta (all of Lecce + Brindisi provinces, parts of Taranto/Bari — Annex III Part A,
│   │   Reg. (UE) 2020/1201 as amended by 2024/1320)
│   │   → CONTAINMENT regime, Arts. 12–17. Felling: infected plants only (Art. 13(1)). §3.2
│   ├─ Zona infetta — 2-km containment strip (the northern band where containment measures are
│   │   actively applied; cut from 5 km to 2 km by Reg. (UE) 2024/2507 Art. 15(2)(a),
│   │   enacted by DDS 158 del 18/11/2024)
│   │   → CONTAINMENT regime + special bans: no Art. 13(2) derogation, no LR 4/2017 art. 8
│   │     co. 7-bis alternative measures (DDS 18/2024); no resistant-olive replanting (§4.1);
│   │     no Art. 23 movement authorizations (§4.4)
│   └─ Zona cuscinetto (5-km belt, Ionian to Adriatic)
│       → surveillance zone; planting/movement restrictions apply; heightened sampling
└─ NO → is your parcel inside a separate ERADICATION area delimitata? (register: §1.2)
    ├─ YES → ERADICATION regime, Arts. 7–11. Felling: infected + symptomatic/suspect + ALL
    │   same-species plants within the 50-m zona infetta regardless of health (Art. 7(1)). §3.1
    └─ NO → zona indenne. Region-wide vector-control obligations still apply (§2).
```

**HIGH-STAKES.** A farm in northern Puglia can change regime overnight when a new focus is found (Bitonto went from zona indenne surveillance to Art. 7 eradication prescriptions between October 2025 and January 2026 — DDS 3 del 12/01/2026). Salento rules do not transfer to eradication foci, and vice versa.

### 1.3 How to read a prescrizione — and the 7-day window

Removal orders (prescrizioni di estirpazione) are **notified by publication on the comune's albo pretorio for 7 days** (Piano d'azione §4.5; example: DET 9 del 28/01/2025, Crispiano). Owners receive **no certified letter**. Publication on the albo pretorio, or on regione.puglia.it for ranking acts (stated explicitly in DDS 53 del 30/03/2026), has full legal value of notification and starts the clock.

Practical reading order of a prescrizione: (1) which area delimitata and which regime it invokes (Art. 7 eradication vs Art. 13 containment); (2) the allegato listing fogli/particelle and proprietari — check whether your parcel appears even partially; (3) the measures prescribed per letter of Art. 7(1) (see §3.1); (4) exclusions (non-susceptible species; historic-value plants); (5) deadlines. Obligatory inspector access: D.lgs 19 del 02/02/2021, art. 22.

If you intend to challenge an order, that is lawyer territory (TAR); this playbook explains only the generic mechanism. **Monitor your comune's albo pretorio weekly during campaign season.**

## §2 2026 obligations calendar

### 2.1 Juvenile-stage vector control (soil works) — all Puglia, all zones

Legal basis: **DDS n. 39 dell'11/03/2026** (prescription), extended by **DDS n. 79 del 28/04/2026** (proroga after rain; verified on emergenzaxylella.it and Regione Puglia press, 29 Apr 2026).

| Comuni (altitudine media, ISTAT) | Original window (DDS 39/2026) | Extended deadline (DDS 79/2026) |
|---|---|---|
| < 200 m s.l.m. | 25 Mar – 30 Apr 2026 | **15 May 2026** |
| > 200 m s.l.m. | 10 Apr – 15 May 2026 | **30 May 2026** |

**What counts as compliant work** (DDS 39/2026): lavorazioni superficiali del terreno — aratura, fresatura, erpicatura, or trinciatura (trinciatura only with sward ≤ 10 cm). Where machines cannot go (declivi, bordi strada, banchine, rotatorie): pirodiserbo or steam; herbicide only where physical means are impossible, favouring low-impact products. In parcels with herbaceous crops (cereals, forage, field vegetables, pasture) where olive, almond, or other Prunoideae are present: work **under the canopy only**.

**Who is obligated:** proprietari and conduttori of agricultural land, **and** proprietari/gestori (public and private) of uncultivated surfaces, aree a verde pubblico, road edges, canals, and demanio (DDS 39/2026). Public bodies may delegate execution to farmers. Exempt areas: aree protette (l. 394/91) except olive groves, orchards, and vineyards inside them; boschi; pinete; giardini privati.

**CAP interaction:** the mandatory tillage derogates the grass-cover (inerbimento) obligation for holdings under **ecoschema 2** (DDS 39/2026). **Enforcement:** Carabinieri Forestali run compliance checks. Regional support: €5M to comuni (Piano di rigenerazione + regional budget; Olivo e Olio, Mar 2026).

**HIGH-STAKES.** These deadlines move every year and mid-year (2026 had a proroga). Quoting last year's dates causes fines. Check the current determina each spring.

## §3 Felling: what triggers removal in *my* zone

**The single most consequential zone difference.** Getting this wrong — telling a Bisceglie/Bitonto grower the Salento rule, or vice versa — costs trees.

### 3.1 Eradication areas (Arts. 7–11) — the 50-m rule applies

Within the 50-m zona infetta around an infected plant, the prescrizione orders removal of (Art. 7(1), as itemized in DDS 3 del 12/01/2026):

- (a) the plant(s) found infected;
- (b) all plants with symptoms indicating possible infection or suspected of being infected;
- (c) **all plants of the same species as the infected plant, regardless of their health status**;
- (d) plants of other species found infected elsewhere in the area delimitata;
- (e) other specified plants **not** immediately sampled and tested and found free of the pest.

Reliefs and exclusions:

- **M5 testing relief** (Reg. (UE) 2024/2507): specified plants of *other* species that test negative need not be removed; plants of species not found infected in that area delimitata in the last 2 years may skip immediate testing.
- **Non-susceptible species are not felled:** agrumi, pesco, albicocco, susino are excluded from eradication in pauca-ST53 foci (DDS 3/2026).
- **Historic/monumental derogation:** officially recognized piante di valore storico that test not infected can be spared under Art. 7(3) (applied in DDS 3/2026), subject to annual testing and vector treatment.

### 3.2 Containment (the Salento zona infetta, Arts. 12–17) — infected plants only

**Only plants found infected are removed** (Art. 13(1)), immediately or at latest before the next vector flight season. There is no 50-m same-species removal in containment.

In the **2-km containment strip** two escape routes are closed (DDS 18/2024): the Art. 13(2) scientific derogation does not apply, and the LR 4/2017 art. 8 co. 7-bis "alternative measures" are excluded. An infected tree in the strip is removed.

### 3.3 Facts that hold in every zone

- **A treated tree that tests positive is legally infected.** No available treatment eliminates *Xylella fastidiosa* from a diseased plant in the field (EFSA Statement 2016, EFSA Journal 4456; EFSA PLH Panel 2019, EFSA Journal 5666). Zinc-copper-citric-acid protocols reduce symptoms in trials (Scortichini et al. 2018, Phytopathol. Mediterr. 57(1):48–72; Pathogens 2021, 10(1):85) but treated trees remain PCR-positive. Where removal is triggered by positivity — eradication foci, the 2-km strip — a treated tree is still felled. Selling coexistence protocols as protection from removal orders is false.
- **Indemnities:** income-loss indemnities in the zona infetta were extended from 3 to 5 years (Olivo e Olio, May 2024). See §5.
- Removal orders are notified via the albo pretorio, 7-day publication (§1.3).

## §4 Replanting

### 4.1 Legal frame (Art. 18, Reg. (UE) 2020/1201)

Planting specified plants **in the zona infetta** is allowed only under Art. 18:

- **Art. 18(b):** resistant/tolerant varieties, or species tested clean for 2 years — **but this is excluded in the 2-km containment strip** (DDS 18/2024). Inside the strip, replanting resistant olives is banned. Check the strip boundary on the DDS 82/2026 map before ordering plants.
- **Art. 18(c):** in eradication zones, same-species replanting only where tested clean per the conditions of the article.

### 4.2 The authorized list (DDS 48 del 03/05/2024)

For the zona infetta (ex containment strip), Puglia authorizes: olive **Leccino, FS-17/Favolosa, Lecciana (resistant), Leccio del Corno (tolerant)**; citrus, peach, plum, apricot (non-susceptible); almond, cherry (low susceptibility); rosemary, cistus, myrtle, alaternus, laurel, phillyrea, pelargonium.

### 4.3 Cultivar table with evidence grades

| Cultivar | Legal status (DDS 48/2024) | Published evidence | Supply / system fit | Field-conversion evidence (local, observational) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Leccino** | resistant | two decades of published resistance evidence; the default choice | huge nursery availability; unsuited to super-high-density (SHD), fits intensive | **PENDING internal gate** |
| **FS-17 / Favolosa** | resistant | oil ~18%, bearing yr 2–3; best cumulated yield of the resistant set under SHD (Agronomy 2022, 12(12):3157, doi:10.3390/agronomy12123157) | good availability | **PENDING internal gate** |
| **Lecciana** (Arbosana × Leccino) | resistant (authorized May 2024) | oil >19% fw, bearing yr 3, >1,200 trees/ha, ~50% lower production cost, high polyphenols (Agronomy 2021, 11(11):2154, doi:10.3390/agronomy11112154); own field titre data vs Leccino still thin | **proprietary to Agromillora; no third-party licenses; ~1M plants/yr — availability is the constraint** | **PENDING internal gate** |
| **Leccio del Corno** | **TOLERANT, not resistant** | infection rates "molto elevata … prossima a … varietà suscettibili", without overt desiccation (Servizio Fitosanitario dossier via DDS 48/2024) — a materially different risk profile: the tree survives but hosts inoculum | available | **PENDING internal gate** |

- **The "field-conversion evidence" column is empty by design.** A separate internal analysis of repeat-tested official monitoring coordinates must first pass its positive control — Leccino must emerge field-resistant, matching the published record — before any local field numbers appear here. Until that gate passes, no field-performance claim is made. Even after, field conversion rates are observational and confounded with location, management, and survey timing; they are not an inoculation trial and do not certify resistance.
- **Clone warning (Leccino):** resistance varies by clone. In a 2025 inoculation study of 7 Leccino clones, 16-month infection ranged 57–100% and four clones had 16–40% dead plants (SSSUP/CNR 2025; repo-verified). Specify clone and source with your nursery, not just cultivar.
- **Horizon:** ~100 genotypes screened over 5–6 years produced nothing better than Leccino/FS-17 (Boscia, EFSA 2021); Lecciana took ~26 years from cross to authorization. No new cultivar realistically beats this set before ~2035.
- **Grafting** monumental/secolari trees with Leccino/FS-17 is regionally promoted (€5M measure, 80–100% aid; LR 14/2007 census). Take-rate is assessed at 18–24 months, first production ~3 years (AIPP/AMO technical meeting, Monopoli, Feb 2026). Evidence level: practitioner + trial plots; no published landscape-scale survival/yield statistics. Uptake capped by the small budget (AIPP/AMO, Feb 2026).
- **SHD conversion economics:** no published Apulian €/ha or NPV study located — **GAP**. Any number would be a model and must be labeled as such.

### 4.4 Movement and passports (Arts. 19–27)

- Specified plants move out of / within demarcated areas only via authorized insect-proof sites with testing; passports carry the **"Buffer zone – XYLEFA"** marking per M5 (Reg. (UE) 2024/2507).
- **Art. 23 movement authorizations: none are granted in containment areas; in eradication areas, only officially non-susceptible species** (DDS 48/2024 Allegato A).
- Buy only passported stock. Nursery plants are the canonical long-jump pathway for this bacterium (the Mallorca introduction traced to almond cuttings; repo-verified).
- Dormant *Vitis* from demarcated areas requires hot-water treatment (§6).

### 5.2 The AGEA audit — one sober paragraph

An AGEA control report (reported Gazzetta del Mezzogiorno, 21 Nov 2025; FruitJournal, 24 Nov 2025) found that of €122M paid in 2016–2022, €18.82M went to beneficiaries with "no plants present", 51% of those (~2,500 firms) closed or with deceased holders, and only ~65% (€67.5M) of sums map to positions with a positive plant-count change; AGEA states the €5M-funded automated control system was never built. The Region rebuts that late-paid 2019 indemnities legitimately reached since-deceased-but-entitled beneficiaries, that replant aid is not parcel-bound, and that 5% ex-post checks follow the norm (Nardone, via FruitJournal). Practical consequence for honest applicants: expect **tighter documentation demands** from 2026 on. Keep planting invoices, delivery notes, passports, and georeferenced photos.

## §6 Wine-estate annex

For estates whose portfolio includes vineyards (exemplar: a ~20 ha wine + oil + tourism estate near Ostuni).

- **The pauca ST53 epidemic does not infect *Vitis*.** Your vines are not at risk from the olive epidemic around them. The grapevine threat is a different subspecies: *X. fastidiosa* subsp. *fastidiosa* **ST1** (the Pierce's disease genotype), present near the Bari table-grape district — ~50+ km from Ostuni.
- **ST1 status:** 2024 campaign: 339 infected plants (212 almond, 119 vine); 2025: 20 (13 vine, 7 almond); >50,000 samples, >30 ha of vineyard removed (repo-verified). The 2025–26 campaign **closed 28 Feb 2026 with "no expansion"** declared (Regione Puglia press). Key rules in the ST1 area: 400-m almond-removal provisions (DET 22/2025; DDS 11 del 12/02/2026); voluntary vine removal ≤ 400 m indemnified (DGR 994/2024). Epidemiology: almond→vine via *Neophilaenus campestris*, vine-to-vine via *Philaenus spumarius* (repo-verified).
- **Propagation-material hygiene is your one real control point.** Buy vine material only with plant passports. Dormant *Vitis* moving out of a demarcated area requires **hot-water treatment at 50 °C for 45 minutes** (Art. 22, Reg. (UE) 2020/1201). Demand documentation of the treatment, not just the passport.
- **Your olive and tourism value streams remain fully exposed** — the estate's tillage obligation (§2.1) applies to all estate land regardless of crop or zone, its oil line depends on the cultivar decisions in §4, and its landscape capital depends on the felling rules in §3.
- **Complacency warning.** "No expansion" is a campaign declaration, not a revocation of the demarcated area. The historical failure mode after apparent containment is the quiet return of the pathogen while attention moves elsewhere (S4 review). Watch the ST1 demarcation until formally revoked, and the vector-monitoring bulletins (Dati Monitoraggio Vettori, emergenzaxylella.it). Keep or remove marginal almonds near vineyards as a considered decision, not by default.
