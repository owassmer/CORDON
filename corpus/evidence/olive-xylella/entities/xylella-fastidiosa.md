# Xylella fastidiosa

Xylem-limited Gram-negative bacterium (Xanthomonadaceae). Causes Pierce’s disease of grape, citrus variegated chlorosis, and in Apulia [[oqds]].

## Apulian agent

- Subsp. **pauca**, sequence type **ST53** (historically CoDiRO).
- First EU detection: olive, southern Puglia, October 2013.
- Spreads inside the tree by degrading pit membranes and forming biofilm. The plant answers with tyloses. Water transport fails. See [[resistance-not-immunity]].

## Other subspecies now in Bari province (2024–25)

- subsp. **fastidiosa** ST1 near Noicattaro / Rutigliano (table grape).
- subsp. **multiplex** ST26 on the Murge.

Same legal species, different crops and risk. CORDON’s 90 days stay on olive / ST53 unless CNR asks otherwise.

## Vector here

[[philaenus-spumarius]], not the California sharpshooters.
