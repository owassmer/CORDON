# Leccino

The first olive cultivar shown to be field-resistant (not immune) to [[xylella-fastidiosa]] ST53. Partner cultivar: FS-17 / Favolosa. Later authorized: Lecciana, Leccio del Corno.

## What the phenotype is

Infected, lower bacterial load, slower internal spread, milder dieback, longer incubation. That also lowers vector acquisition. See [[resistance-not-immunity]].

## Genetics

La Notte / Saponari / Giampetruzzi / Saldarelli 2024: of 61 Leccino offspring in the high-inoculum zone, **67%** scored highly resistant, resistant, or tolerant, vs 32% from Cellina di Nardò and 49% from Ogliarola. Four open-pollinated seedlings confirmed by artificial inoculation. Genotype **S105** was transcriptomically more resilient than S215 and S234.

Giampetruzzi 2016: 659 DEGs in infected Leccino vs 447 in Ogliarola.

No screened cultivar has beaten Leccino / FS-17.
