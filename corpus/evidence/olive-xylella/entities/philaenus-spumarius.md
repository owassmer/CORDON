# Philaenus spumarius

Meadow spittlebug. The efficient European vector of [[xylella-fastidiosa]] on olive.

Xylem feeder. Nymphs live in herbaceous cover (controlled by tillage); adults move onto olive from spring to autumn (controlled, imperfectly, by insecticides in demarcated areas).

Boscia (Jan 2026) attributes partial Salento recovery to empirically fewer spittlebugs and therefore fewer re-inoculations of the same tree — not to a cure. No density time series is cited. Treat as observation, not a measured decline.

California sharpshooters that drive Pierce’s disease are not established here. That is why ST1 near Bari grapes is worrying but not automatically a California-scale grape epidemic.
