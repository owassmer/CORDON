# Resistance Decoder v0 — from PDFs we hold

Not a finished Artifact B. Gene IDs below are **Arabidopsis orthologues named in Giampetruzzi 2016**, plus La Notte 2024 genotype-level facts. Olive locus IDs were in Additional file 3 (not extracted here). Do not synthesize anything.

## What the papers show

### Giampetruzzi et al. 2016 BMC Genomics
- Leccino infected vs healthy: **659 DEGs** (586 down, 73 up).
- Ogliarola: **447 DEGs**.
- Leccino classes most moved: cell wall 11.39%, stress 7.78%, signaling 7.08%. 39.58% unassigned (olive genome poorly annotated in 2016).
- Cell wall: **82 DEGs down** (2–8 fold). Orthologues:
  - FLA11 / FLA12 — at5g03170, at5g60490 (cellulose deposition, wall strength)
  - PMEs — at5g53370
  - PMEIs — at3g49220, at5g09760
  - pectin lyases — at5g19730, at1g60590, at1g48100, at1g10640, at5g48900, at3g61490
  - polygalacturonase — at1g70370
  - laccases — at2g40370, at2g30210 (paper: at2g3021), at2g38080, at5g01190, at5g60020 (up to 7× down)
- Up: LRR-RLK / LRR-RLP (up to 4×): at5g49290, at1g74190, at1g74180, at3g23120, at5g27060 (RLKs); at1g07390, at1g74170, at3g53240 (RLPs).
- Interpretation in-paper: Leccino *perceives* Xfp (RLK/RLP) and *remodels* the wall; the wall-down signature matches Xf-resistant Ponkan mandarin.

### La Notte / Saponari / Giampetruzzi / Saldarelli 2024
- 171 field genotypes → 139 unique SSR. Leccino progeny n=61: **67% HR/R/T** vs Cellina 32%, Ogliarola 49%.
- Three naturally infected Leccino-bearing genotypes: **17,227 / 13,031 / 4,513** genes altered (photosynthesis, cell wall, primary/secondary metabolism).
- **S105** more resilient (fewer expression changes) than S215 and S234. Use S105 as the “quiet transcriptome” reference, not generic Leccino.

### Pavan et al. 2021
- 23 PRPs cluster K1 with Leccino + FS-17.
- Other PRPs sit outside that cluster.
- Field: **partial resistance in Frantoio and Nocellara Messinese**.
- This already breaks “only two cultivars.”

### Surano et al. 2022
- gs and Ψstem discriminate Cellina vs Leccino/FS-17 under infection. Hydraulics are a *phenotyping tool*, not just a mechanism.

### BeXyl (press, Jan 2026 — paper not released)
- Greenhouse: Leccino, **Frantoio, Gordal** lower load vs Arbequina/Arbosana.
- 300–500 seedlings/year; Arbosana×Leccino hybrids in field; Mallorca 26-cv × 3 subspecies.
