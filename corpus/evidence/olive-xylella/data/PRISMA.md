# PRISMA

Italian hyperspectral mission (ASI). ~30 m VNIR/SWIR + 5 m panchromatic.

Catalogue is searchable without an account. Download needs free registration at https://www.prisma-i.it / the CNM access system.

Role: orchard-block spectral discovery after Experiment 1, not single-tree diagnosis.

Human gate: Owen registers. Does not block F7 or the Crecco overlap count.
