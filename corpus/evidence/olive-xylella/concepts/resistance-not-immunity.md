# Resistance is not immunity

A “resistant” olive still gets [[xylella-fastidiosa]]. The useful traits:

1. Yes, it is infected.
2. Longer incubation.
3. Milder, more limited canopy dieback.
4. Lower bacterial concentration.
5. Limited within-tree spread → fewer successful vector acquisitions.

## Mechanisms with evidence

- **Pit membranes:** ST53 walks through degraded pits. Cellina degrades; [[leccino]] much less (Montilon 2022).
- **Hydraulics:** Leccino cavitates less and refills faster (Sabella 2019; Surano 2022).
- **Transcriptome / cell wall:** 659 vs 447 DEGs, Leccino vs Ogliarola (Giampetruzzi 2016).
- **Heritability:** 67% of Leccino offspring R/T (La Notte 2024). S105 is the quiet transcriptome.
