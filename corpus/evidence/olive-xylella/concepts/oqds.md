# OQDS

Disease of olive caused by [[xylella-fastidiosa]] subsp. pauca ST53, vectored by [[philaenus-spumarius]].

Leaf scorch starting at the canopy top, twig and branch dieback, then tree death. Physiology of infected trees resembles drought (Surano 2022). That is why [[previsual-detection]] must beat a drought-only baseline.

## Scale (do not collapse)

- Agromillora / Agrimeca Nov 2025: ~8,000 km², >10 million trees dead.
- Popular figure: ~21 million infected. Upper campaign estimate, not a census.
- Schneider PNAS 2020: Italy €1.9–5.2bn / 50y if no replant; €0.6–1.6bn with resistant replant.

## Status 2025–26

*pauca* front slowed; seven Bari-outskirt olives eradicated Mar 2025. Salento shows some recovery coincident with fewer observed vectors. New subspecies (ST1, ST26) appeared in Bari province.
