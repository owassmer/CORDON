# Assumption reopen — live, 15 Aug 2026

Written while three background briefs are still running. Every lock below is a CORDON self-authoring, not a law of nature. Evidence can kill it.

## Lock: NDVI is dead / we have no drought control

**Status: wounded, not dead.**

On the 100-point 12 Aug 2021 join:
- NDVI does not meet the pre-registered bar (δ −0.12, p=0.29).
- NDMI does (δ −0.31, p=0.008).
- **NDMI residualized on NDVI gets stronger** (δ −0.38, p=0.0005). Greenness control does not explain the moisture gap; it unmasks it.
- NDVI-nearest-neighbor match (median |ΔNDVI|=0.012): NDMI still drier in positives (δ −0.31, p=0.006).

What this is not: a meteorological drought layer, irrigation map, or multi-date stack. What it is: first-order “maybe it’s just browner” is already a poor explanation.

**Keep:** NDVI-only ablation in any nowcast.
**Drop:** the claim that we cannot say anything about drought until we have ERA5.
**Next falsifier:** a non-August date, and NDMI vs a real water-balance layer. A +10-day replicate (22 Aug) already holds (NDMI δ −0.33, p=0.004).

Sample composition (challenge to ourselves): all 100 points are Brindisi. Positives are 24 Ogliarola salentina + 23 “Altro”; almost no Leccino. The NDMI gap is a *susceptible-cultivar front* signal. It may shrink on Leccino groves. That is a feature for breeding screens, a bug if we sell it as a universal detector.

## Lock: Leccino / FS-17 are the ceiling

**Status: already false in papers we hold.**

Pavan et al. 2021 (PDF on disk): field evaluation of cultivars related to putatively resistant plants defined **partial resistance in Frantoio and Nocellara Messinese**. 23 PRPs clustered with Leccino/FS-17; others did not.

BeXyl greenhouse work reported Jan 2026 (Olive Oil Times, not yet the paper): **Leccino, Frantoio, and Gordal** lower load/symptoms vs Arbequina/Arbosana (high infection under *pauca*). Also: 1M+ variant calls, 300–500 seedlings/year, Arbosana×Leccino hybrids in the field, 372-tree / 26-cultivar Mallorca collection under all three subspecies.

La Notte 2024: S105 is more transcriptomically resilient than S215/S234 — resistance is not one Leccino clone.

**Keep:** Leccino/FS-17 remain the legal replant core and the best-documented pair.
**Drop:** “no cultivar beat them, stop looking.” Frantoio is now a second documented seam. Gordal is a greenhouse claim awaiting the BeXyl paper.

## Lock: no field-transferable treatment

**Status: EFSA 2016 still binds on *clearance*. Symptom and transmission tools are a different question we under-weighted.**

EFSA 2016 PDF (on disk): “do not eliminate the pathogen”; “premature to draw conclusions” on long-term symptom control. That sentence is about sterilization, not about “nothing works.”

Still live after 2016 (to be filled by the intervention delegate, already visible):
- Dentamet endotherapy (Girelli 2022) — metabolomic, not eradication.
- BIOVEXO biopesticides — 2025 press: symptom mitigation, product-not-yet.
- BeXyl **thermal treatment of nursery cuttings** — works on almond/cherry/ornamentals; olive cuttings die or fail to root. Nursery sanitation is a different product than a grove cure. We should not have folded it into “no treatment.”
- Vector tillage + adult insecticides: already the legal cordon, and PNAS priced slowing the front.

**Keep:** we will not claim a cure.
**Drop:** treating every non-cure as out of scope. Nursery heat + vector + resistant replant is the actual European strategy. CORDON can attach to any of those computationally (nowcast the cordon; mark seedlings; score nursery lots).
