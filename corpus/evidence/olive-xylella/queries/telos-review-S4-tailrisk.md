# Telos review — S4: stop the next fires (tail risk)

Adversarial research review, 16 Aug 2026. Objective metric: expected reduction of projected economic loss (probability × impact), tail only. Sources cited inline; unverified items marked.

## A) € mechanism and magnitude — verified from primary sources

**Schneider et al. 2020 PNAS (10.1073/pnas.1912206117), read from the paper, not the abstract.** 50-y present-value grower impact of *pauca* if introduced and spreading (RR05–RR95 spread quantiles):
- Italy: €1.86–5.17B no-replant; €0.59–1.57B with resistant replant. Slowing spread 5.18→1.1 km/y saves €0.5–1.3B. (Matches repo anchor.)
- **Spain: €0.71–16.86B no-replant; €0.39–4.98B with replant.** The "multi-billion" press figure is the RR95 no-replant tail: **€16.86B**, the largest single number in the paper. Benefit of resistant cultivars to Spain: €0.32–11.88B.
- **Greece: €0.21–1.94B no-replant; €0.09–0.58B with replant.**
- Critical conditioning the repo must not drop: Spain/Greece figures are **averages over 1,000 random introduction points, conditional on introduction**. They are not expected losses; multiply by P(introduction & establishment). Climatic suitability: 84.6–99.1% (ES), 99.2–99.8% (GR) of production area suitable. Also: Italian losses are partially *transfers* — Spanish/Greek growers gain €0.9–5.6B from price rises in the no-replant scenarios, so EU-level net loss < sum of national losses. The repo has never stated this.

**Pierce's disease in Europe: there is NO published € projection.** Verified proxies:
- California PD burden: >$100M/y (Tumber et al. 2014, cited in Giménez-Romero et al. 2022, Commun Biol 5:1389, 10.1038/s42003-022-04358-w).
- Giménez-Romero 2022: PD could establish in **22.3% of EU vineyard area**; highest risk indices (0.70–0.88) include **Apulia**, Balearics, Crete, Cyprus, Peloponnese. Sequels: Sci Rep 2024 (10.1038/s41598-024-59947-y) — risk rises sharply above +2 °C, Italian PDO vineyard area at risk up to 82.7% at +4 °C; Sci Rep 2025 (10.1038/s41598-025-13994-1) — high-res climate raises EU vineyard-at-risk from 21.8% to 41.2%.
- Exposure anchor for the Italian tail (industry data, not peer-reviewed): Italian table grape ≈ €640M/y at origin, export €973M (2025 campaign); Puglia 23,400 of ~41,000 ha and 55–80% of volume depending on source; Bari province ≈ ¼ of national output; in Noicattaro 20,000 of 26,000 residents work in the chain (Ismea via quotidiano.net 2025; Coldiretti via uvadatavola.com; freshplaza.it 2024). The 2024 ST1 demarcated area sits **inside** this district.
- Honest sizing of the Italy-PD tail: district gross value ~€0.3–0.5B/y × multi-year disruption if established ⇒ plausibly €1–5B undiscounted over decades, but the 2024–26 eradication evidence (below) has driven P(establishment from this outbreak) well down. The unmanaged tail now lives more in climate-driven risk (IFISC maps) than in Triggiano.

## B) State of the art and actors, 2024–2026 (all primary)

**ST1 (Pierce's genotype), Bari province — timeline:**
- Vector-first detection: Xf-positive *P. spumarius* in Triggiano Oct 2022 (2/79) and autumn 2023 (13/474); host survey Jan 2024 found 6 infected almonds → first Xff ST1 in Italy (EPPO RS 2024/038; Surano et al./Saponari group, Eur J Plant Pathol 2024, 10.1007/s10658-024-02945-7). Note the 15-month lag from first positive vector to demarcation.
- Demarcation: DDS 8 (21/02/2024) Triggiano; updates DDS 12, 45, 94/2024 — 50 m infected zones + 2.5 km buffer, eradication per art. 7–11 Reg. (EU) 2020/1201 (BURP DET_94_24_7_2024).
- Scale: >38,000 plants sampled by 18/07/2024; 404 infected incl. 324 ST1, 45 multiplex ST26, 9 pauca ST53 → separate demarcated areas for ST26 (Noicattaro-Triggiano; Capurso) and ST53 (Bari), DDS 92–93 (22–23/07/2024).
- 2024 total in the ST1 area: 339 infected = 212 almond, 119 vine, 7 cherry, 1 polygala (DET 22, 15/02/2025) — **exactly matching the 339 FASTIDIOSA rows in CAMP_2024.xlsx on this disk**. All asymptomatic at sampling; infected vines mostly on outer rows near infected almonds.
- Epidemiology already established by the actors (CIHEAM Bari vector monitoring + CNR-IPSP): bimodal transmission — primary almond→vine via *Neophilaenus campestris*, secondary vine-to-vine via *P. spumarius*; hence removal of almonds within 400 m of infected zones (DET 22/2025; DDS 11, 12/02/2026 prescribes removal of marginal/abandoned almonds via ARIF, explicit goal: revoke the demarcated area).
- Outcome: **>50,000 samples analyzed and >30 ha of vineyard removed over two years; 2026 monitoring closed 28 Feb 2026 with "no expansion"** (Regione Puglia press release; assessor Paolicelli, Osservatorio head Infantino). Compensation regime: DGR 994/2024; voluntary vine removal within 400 m indemnified (DET 22/2025).
- EU frame: Reg. (EU) 2020/1201 consolidated (M1–M5); 2024/1320 ratified the enlarged pauca containment zone (Alberobello, Castellana, Monopoli, Polignano, part of Putignano); **2024/2507 (26/09/2024)** cut the containment survey belt 5→2 km, added "Buffer zone – XYLEFA" plant-passport marking and road/port/airport checks — the nursery-trade control instrument (EUR-Lex).

**Multiplex ST26:** on-disk 2024: 617 positives (605 almond) centred on the **Murge** — Santeramo 385, Altamura 131, Cassano 41 — plus the Noicattaro/Triggiano/Capurso pockets; N–S span 61 km, E–W 36 km. Demarcated areas exist only for the Bari-belt pockets (DDS 93/2024); regional action plan 2024–2026 (agrometeopuglia.it) governs the rest.

**Other regions:** Spain-Alicante: multiplex ST6 since 6/2017; demarcated ~140–170k ha, 80 municipalities; 5,540 positives (90% almond); 240,000–315,166 trees destroyed; **€35M spent**; ~140 staff (BeXyl interview with Vicente Dalmau, Generalitat Valenciana; EPPO RS 7343; Phytoma 2023). Balearics: PD established on grape; almond leaf scorch on ~78% of ~1M almonds; introduced ~1993 via **almond cuttings from California** — the canonical nursery-pathway case (EPPO RS 6672; Moralejo/Gomila cited in Giménez-Romero 2022). Portugal: multiplex + fastidiosa in Porto metro (first EPPO-region citrus record), Lisbon, Fundão; under eradication (EPPO RS 2023/015; DGAV despachos 77-79/G/2024, 12/G/2025); no PD-in-vineyard record found. Greece: no Xf outbreak record found in searches (EPPO GR page not directly pulled — **unverified**); its exposure is the Schneider conditional number plus the highest-in-EU PD climate trajectory (63% of area at risk at +4 °C, Sci Rep 2024).

## C) Brutal verdict on CORDON

1. **The filtering itself was correct; the framing was the failure.** Dropping non-pauca rows from an olive-label regression is sound. Writing them off as "poison" (CAMP_XLSX.md §3) and never opening S4 while 339 Pierce's-genotype trees stood 2.3 km from Noicattaro's centre (computed today from CAMP_2024: min distance of ST1 positives to Noicattaro centre 2.3 km, Rutigliano 5.1 km; 2 positives inside Noicattaro comune) is a strategic-attention failure the S0 review already conceded.
2. **But the counterfactual € cost of that blindness is small, and honesty requires saying so.** Regione Puglia, CNR-IPSP, and CIHEAM Bari generate these records; they had the vector data, cadastral parcels, and delimitation shapefiles CORDON lacks, and they produced the decisive epidemiology (bimodal transmission, 400 m almond rule) and executed removal fast enough that the 2026 campaign closed with no expansion and ST1 counts falling 339→20 (on-disk 2025 book). Nothing a two-person computational team could have computed in 2024–25 from these workbooks would plausibly have changed responder allocation on ST1. Estimated foregone Δ€ from not analyzing: near zero for ST1.
3. **The one thing the unopened data actually contains that is not in any public document found:** the surveillance asymmetry. Computed today: samples in the ST1 belt comuni 43,336 (2024) → 14,078 (2025); samples in the Murge multiplex comuni **34,443 (2024) → 1,966 (2025), a 94% collapse**, while multiplex still spans ~61×36 km and 2025 fastidiosa positives went vine-majority (13 vine vs 7 almond) for the first time. Whether the Murge drawdown is deliberate (post-delimitation triage) or a gap is exactly the question a consumer would want answered. Caveats: workbook may not cover the full 2025–26 campaign; comune-level effort is a proxy for area-level effort. This is the only genuinely novel, on-disk, S4-relevant finding — and CORDON never looked.
4. The 2025 Gargano cluster (260 olive-pauca in Cagnano Varano) is an S1/S3 spread problem, not an S4 subspecies tail; CAMP_XLSX.md already flags it correctly.

## D) Top 3 actions by expected Δ€ (2-person computational team, data on disk)

1. **Murge multiplex surveillance-gap brief (2–3 weeks).** Quantify per-comune effort and positivity 2024 vs 2025, map the 617+36 multiplex points against the DDS 93/2024 demarcations and almond/cherry/vineyard land cover, flag undersampled sectors and the Ginosa outliers (~60 km west). Consumer, named: Osservatorio Fitosanitario Regione Puglia (dirigente Salvatore Infantino; gestionefitosanitaria@pec.rupar.puglia.it), secondarily EFSA survey-guidance team. Expected Δ€: low single-digit M€ (small probability of redirecting 2026–27 survey allocation × Murge almond/cherry value and the option value of catching a multiplex-to-new-host jump early); highest of the three because it is the only claim nobody else has published.
2. **ST1 eradication performance dossier (3–4 weeks).** Positivity-per-effort by campaign, spatial hull 2024→2025→(2026 press outcome), host shift almond→vine, distance-to-vineyard-core series — an open, reproducible record that Europe's first Pierce's-genotype outbreak in a spittlebug landscape was contained. Consumers, named: DGAV Portugal and Generalitat Valenciana/MAPA Spain (both currently running Xff eradications and needing evidence the effort pays), EFSA PLH, BeXyl. Expected Δ€: indirect but real — sustaining eradication funding elsewhere is the cheapest tail insurance in the whole loss function; CNR will publish their own version, so the marginal value is speed and openness.
3. **PD-Italy exposure note (1–2 weeks, joint with #2).** No € figure exists for PD in Europe (§A); staple the IFISC risk maps to the verified district economics (€640M/y sector at origin; Bari ≈ ¼ of national volume; €973M export) into a first-order value-at-risk table by warming scenario. Consumers, named: the table-grape industry bodies already convening on this district (Le Terre dell'Uva network / Regina di Puglia, Coldiretti Puglia, MIVA vine-nursery association) and the Giménez-Romero group (IFISC), who have the model but not the € layer. Expected Δ€: small directly; its function is keeping vector control and abandoned-almond removal funded in the district after the demarcated area is revoked — the moment risk complacency historically returns.

## E) Kill list

- **Any ST1/grape "nowcast" or remote-sensing product.** 20 positives left, all managed, outcome already "no expansion"; a satellite adds nothing to a 50 m felling radius. Zero.
- **Re-modelling Spain/Greece olive impact.** Schneider 2020 + EFSA own this; a reproduction changes no decision. Zero.
- **Re-deriving PD climate-risk maps.** IFISC (Giménez-Romero/Matías) publishes annually at higher skill; consume, don't compete. Zero.
- **Nursery-trade network modelling.** Plant-passport/EUROPHYT movement data is not public; without it this is decoration. The control already tightened via 2024/2507. Zero until data access exists.
- **Treating Gargano as S4.** It is pauca on olive — S1/S3 territory.
- **Any wet or field intervention on Xff/ST26.** Quarantine pest; out of scope by law and by CORDON's own contract.

**Bottom line:** the S4 tail inside Puglia is currently being won by the actors without CORDON, and the honest expected Δ€ of repo action is modest. The single defensible S4 investment is the Murge surveillance-gap brief (#1): it is the only S4 fact on this disk that is new, checkable, and addressed to a named consumer who can move resources. Spain's €16.86B and Greece's €1.94B tails are real but reachable from this desk only through action #2's indirect channel.
