## 2. Where the report overreaches — prudent-judgment corrections

**2.1 The full OS is a destination, not a build.** A living digital twin ingesting six data classes with five modules and six revenue lines is a multi-year, multi-partner program. A two-person computational team that tries to stand it up at once ships nothing. The OS is adopted as the north star; execution is by wedge (§4).

**2.2 Parametric insurance triggers contradict our own measurements.** A trigger needs a signal that is identifiable, un-gameable, and stable. We proved: the 2022–25 front rate is unidentifiable because the survey frame moved (`FRONT_RATE.md`); sampling intensity collapsed −94% on the Murge in one year (`murge-surveillance-gap.md`); S2 vigor decline is cause-agnostic (drought/pruning/Verticillium confound, S8-A5). Any trigger built on these tods pays on survey behavior, not on disease. S7 killed parametric insurance for exactly this reason. Salvage: the honest adjacent product is **compensation-acceleration analytics for public actors** (documenting loss timing/extent to shorten the indemnity queue), not private parametric contracts. Revisit only if a partner brings vector-trap and ground-truth density we do not have.

**2.3 The marketplace monetizes scarcity we cannot create.** Certified stock is supply-capped (Lecciana proprietary to Agromillora, ~1M plants/yr; S2/S9). A marketplace with transaction fees on rationed goods, in a post-scandal environment, is politically toxic and adds no plants. Salvage: a **stock registry + aggregated demand signal** (which coop needs how many of what, when) is useful to nurseries and bodies and costs little; fees come later, if ever.

**2.4 "Honesty is table stakes, not a moat" — wrong in this specific market.** After €18.8M paid to phantom plantings, 51% of flagged firms closed or deceased, and a GdF protocol extended to the Piano, the region's binding scarcity is *auditability*. An eligibility engine whose every output cites the decree and article it derives from is not garnish — it is the feature that lets a coop batch-submit without absorbing fraud risk, and the feature a public body can adopt without embarrassment. Our red-line discipline (S8) converts directly into compliance-grade product DNA. The report undervalues its own best asset.

**2.5 The kill lists still bind.** Farmer-paid SaaS, per-tree satellite scores, arrival dates, "Xylella-free" certification, dose prescriptions, injunction legal advice remain killed (S7-E, S8-E, S9-E). The OS reframe does not resurrect them; it routes around them (multi-sided monetization, bodies and institutions pay).

**2.6 Verification verdicts (from `telos-pivot-verification.md`, 16 Aug 2026).** All three project names are real; none is what the report sells.

- **FIXYLL**: real ASI project (contract 2023-36-I.0, Planetek + DTA + UNAPROL) — but it **ended 18 Jun 2025**, has no commercial product, and is not a documented REDoX successor. Its service list ("verifica delle prescrizioni regionali", explant validation) is the first primary evidence that satellite compliance-verification **for authorities** is being prototyped. Wedge-3 consequence: the conversation is with Planetek/DTA about a post-project gap, and the compliance buyer there is the Osservatorio/ARIF, not producers.
- **OMIBREED**: MASAF-funded (DM 664766/2022) CNR-IPSP marker groundwork; no registered-variety output promised; olive MAS "ancora difficilmente applicabile". **REACH-XY**: verified, €15M MUR umbrella (2023–2027, CNR-led, 16 partners incl. genomics-assisted selection and therapeutics WPs) — a **duplication risk** that reinforces keeping decoder-style work as credibility material, not a program line.
- **Coltiva Italia**: a near-final **bill**, not a call — €1.14B total, ~€300M Piano olivicolo 2026–2030, national Xylella commissario to 2028; Camera approved 6 Aug 2026, Senato final vote pending; money spendable early **2027**. Playbook watch item; promise nothing before 2027.
- **"€10M/yr monitoring"**: misleading. DGR 1593/2024 budgets €10.62M for 2024 only, then **€2.77M (2025) and €1.95M (2026)** — a documented ~80% monitoring-budget cut that independently corroborates the Murge sampling collapse and belongs in that brief as a citable anchor.
- **€30M AGEA**: real (MASAF/MEF decree Feb 2025: 100% aid, €15k/ha cap, containment strip excluded; funds at AGEA since ~Jan 2026) and **still not open as of 16 Aug 2026** — ~18 months of "imminent". Exactly the deadline-watch content Wedge 1 monetizes.
- **Salento recovery**: published, citable (Agronomy 14(9):2003; Plants 13(16):2186; Plants 13(7):930) — resilient groves rebuild crowns and yield 5–15 kg/tree while remaining Xf-positive at 10^4–10^7 CFU/mL. Wedge 2 gains a peer-reviewed foundation; the mandatory caveat is that resilient trees remain inoculum reservoirs. The report's Boscia attribution and "officially under-used" framing are unsupported.
- **New verified anchors**: >€600M public funds spent / >4M trees felled in 13 years (Il Sole 24 Ore 2026); FS-17 adult-plant scarcity (Catalano/Olivo e Olio); ARIF Restinco free channel rationed at 100 plants/request.

## 3. What survives of the current product line

**3.1 The briefs were never the endgame — they are the OS's content and credibility layer.** Playbook v0.1 = the regulatory-state module, already drafted and decree-verified. Exposure engine = the parcel-history module. Cultivar-conversion engine = the seed of the participatory phenotyping network. Front-rate and Murge analyses = the surveillance-intelligence module. Nothing is discarded; everything is re-slotted from "product" to "module". The correction to S7 is on business model, not on content: the same artifacts, recurring instead of one-off, embedded in a system instead of sold as PDFs.

**3.2 The Frantoio field finding and the decoder are the scientific entry ticket.** The report says CNR/BeXyl need feedback loops from commercial orchards and high-throughput phenotyping of recovery trees. We hold: 16,771 repeat-tested coordinates, 845 conversions, the first field-scale Frantoio signal (RR 0.348, p 0.005), a real-locus-ID decoder, and a conversion engine that self-validates. That is exactly the "structured inputs rather than anecdotes" the report asks for — already built.

## 4. The pivot, sequenced — three wedges toward the OS

**Wedge 1 — Eligibility & collective-application engine (build first).**
The one module that attacks the verified binding constraint (money friction) with assets already on disk (regulatory matrix, decree register, CAMP parcels) and has named buyers with existing distribution (coops/OPs/CAAs — licensed to them, so we tool the dossier work rather than practice it, which respects the S9-E kill line on consultancy). Timing is live: the €30M AGEA bandi was imminent as of Apr 2026, and DDS 120/2026-style silent deadlines are exactly what an engine catches. Prototype: parcel in → zone status, applicable measures, eligibility conditions, deadline calendar, pre-filled dossier skeleton out — every field citing its decree. Pilot on the next open call with one coop.

**Wedge 2 — Participatory phenotyping & recovery network (design now, cheap).**
Protocol + data schema that turns coop members' resistant plantings and Salento recovery observations into structured longitudinal records, pooled with our conversion cohort. Consumers: BeXyl/CNR (registration evidence), nurseries (performance claims), the cultivar table gate (new outcomes). This is the outreach vehicle to CNR-IPSP — arriving with data and a protocol, not a pitch.

**Wedge 3 — Partnership layer (converse, don't build).**
Planetek/FIXYLL own "see"; we own "decide under legal and financial constraints". The offer: our regulatory/economic engine as the decision layer on their imagery; their imagery as the monitoring module we do not rebuild. Contact only after the verification file confirms who FIXYLL is and after Wedge 1 has a demo.

Deferred: parametric triggers (§2.2), marketplace fees (§2.3), heritage-tree services (blocked on LR 14/2007 registry — accesso civico request stays queued), Osservatorio public contracts (needs a working engine first).

## 5. Business-model consequence

From one-off briefs to a recurring coordination layer: annual per-body subscription (playbook + parcel briefs + deadline watch) + per-dossier engine fee paid by the body + later licensing (platforms, institutions). Recurring revenue is a property of the *cadence already in the domain* — campaigns are annual, calls scroll continuously, decrees change mid-season. Freshness was already our thesis (S9-D: "stale guidance is where growers get hurt"); the OS makes freshness the subscription.
