# Telos pivot — claim-by-claim verification

Fact-check of the 16 Aug 2026 investor/stakeholder report's novel claims. Verified 2026-08-16 against primary sources (ASI transparency portal, Planetek/DTA project pages, CNR institute pages, MASAF, Regione Puglia, MDPI/Nature papers, dated Italian press). Repo baselines from `queries/telos-review-S{2,7,9}*.md` are not re-verified. Grades: VERIFIED / PARTIALLY VERIFIED / UNVERIFIED / CONTRADICTED.

## Summary — what this verification changes (≤15 lines)

1. All three project names are real; none is what the report sells. FIXYLL is a finished (Jun 2025) 18-month ASI demo with UNAPROL, not a live commercial compliance product and not a documented REDoX successor. OMIBREED is MASAF-funded marker groundwork, ~10+ years from registered varieties. REACH-XY is the €15M MUR/CNR umbrella (2023–2027) — treat it as a duplication risk for any decoder-style work, reinforcing the S2 kill list.
2. FIXYLL's "verifica delle prescrizioni regionali / convalida espianti" service list is the first primary-source evidence that satellite **compliance-verification for authorities** is being prototyped — relevant to CORDON positioning, since the buyer there is the Osservatorio/ARIF, not producers.
3. Coltiva Italia is a near-final bill, not a call: €300M Piano olivicolo 2026–2030, national Xylella commissario (to end-2028, €3M), money spendable early 2027. Playbook §5 should watch it but promise nothing before 2027.
4. The "~€10M/yr monitoring" figure is a one-year artifact: DGR 1593/2024 budgets €10.62M (2024) then €2.77M (2025) and €1.95M (2026). Anyone citing a €10M run-rate overstates 2025–2026 monitoring ~4–5×.
5. €30M AGEA line: still not open as of 16 Aug 2026; new pins for the tracker — MASAF/MEF criteria decree (Feb 2025), 100%/€15k-ha cap, containment-strip exclusion, funds at AGEA since ~Jan 2026. The repo's "announced Apr 2026" understates how long it has been imminent (~18 months).
6. Salento resilience is published (Agronomy 14(9):2003; Plants 13(16):2186; Plants 13(7):930) — citable in the playbook with the hard caveat that resilient trees remain Xf-positive inoculum reservoirs; the report's Boscia attribution and "officially under-used" framing are unsupported.
7. Nothing found contradicts the repo's verified money/queue picture; the report adds names and colour, not numbers. New usable anchors: >€600M spent / >4M trees felled (Il Sole 24 Ore 2026), FS-17 adult-plant scarcity (Catalano/Olivo e Olio), ARIF Restinco 100-plant free channel.
