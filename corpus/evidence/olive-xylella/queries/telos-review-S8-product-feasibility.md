# Telos review — S8: product feasibility and honesty (farm-level Exposure & Action Report)

Reviewer: independent adversarial product/research review, 16 Aug 2026. Scope: which claims the on-disk data can honestly support for a paying producer (exemplar: Cantine Amalberga, Ostuni), and the legal/licensing basis for commercial use. Every license claim below verified against the primary page/text this session and quoted. Sources on disk: `nowcast/ARTIFACT_A.md`, `nowcast/NEGATIVE.md`, `nowcast/EXPERIMENT1.md`, `data/CAMP_XLSX.md`, `data/CRECCO.md`, `data/ORTOFOTO.md`, `data/DAY1.md`, `queries/telos-review-S0-self.md`, `queries/telos-review-S1-spread.md`, `queries/detection-surveillance-challenge.md`.

## A. Claim-by-claim evidence grading

**A1. Distance/direction of the official positive front to the farm, 12 campaigns — SUPPORTABLE WITH CAVEATS.**
Data basis: 12 workbooks 2013–2025, ~1.3M tree-level records with coordinates and `RISULTATO`; the campaign chronology in `data/CAMP_XLSX.md` shows the front is computable and legible (Trepuzzi 2015 → Oria/Francavilla 2017 → Carovigno/Ostuni 2018–21 → Fasano/Monopoli 2022–23 → Crispiano/Massafra 2024). Computation is trivial and the map series is real value.
Caveats the report must carry: (1) this is the *official detection* front, which follows the survey frame — Lecce exited the frame in 2018, 2018–19 had zero samples east of lon 17.80, demarcation polygons walk north yearly, and the 2024–25 test budget partly shifted to ST1/multiplex; (2) the Region's own download page states monitoring aims to *precede* the bacterium, so the true infection boundary can sit ahead of the detected one; (3) 2024–25 rows must be filtered olive + pauca via `SUBSPECIE`; (4) 2025 Cagnano Varano is a separate Gargano focus, not front movement.
Exemplar honesty: Ostuni logged 1,516 olive positives in 2021 alone. For this client the front is not approaching — it passed through 2018–2021. The report must say "you are inside/behind the official positive front" and pivot from anticipation to management. A product that sells Ostuni an "approaching front" is lying about the direction of time.

**A2. Positivity and testing intensity within 1/3/5/10 km rings — SUPPORTABLE.** The strongest claim in the product. Numerator (positives) and denominator (samples tested) both exist per campaign at exact coordinates; testing intensity is itself decision-relevant (the 2018 Lecce exit and 2022–23 starve are visible facts a producer cannot get elsewhere).
Mandatory caveat text: "Sampling is risk-stratified by design (7–14 samples/ha by stratum; 400 m rings around prior positives are oversampled). Ring positivity is a property of the official survey, not an estimate of local prevalence. Zero tests in a ring means no information, not no risk." Testing-intensity panels must carry equal visual weight with positivity panels — a positivity number without its denominator is the product's most likely dishonest failure mode.

**A3. Documented negative→positive conversion pressure nearby — SUPPORTABLE WITH CAVEATS.**
Data basis: repeat-tested exact coordinates with documented conversions — 98/86/42/12 converters in four adjacent campaign pairs (`telos-review-S0-self.md` §6). Bounded infection windows in official data; no published Puglia analysis uses them (S1 review §C).
Caveats: (1) coordinate identity ≈ tree identity is an assumption (GPS jitter, replanting); (2) survivorship — positives are felled by law, so converters exit the panel and conversion is right-censored; (3) repeat testing concentrates where the survey chose to look (around prior positives), so conversion pressure is only measurable in already-hot neighborhoods; (4) many farm windows will contain zero repeat-tested coordinates. The report must show the repeat-test denominator and render sparse windows as "insufficient repeat testing", never as "low conversion pressure".

**A4. Cultivar-specific field outcomes from repeat-tested coordinates — MISLEADING IF SOLD TODAY.**
The analysis does not exist yet. S1 §C and S2 §D list it as an unbuilt deliverable ("a cultivar × conversion-rate table with CIs"). What exists is a converter count. Known obstacles: inspector-recorded cultivar is noisy; cultivar×geography collinearity (Cellina/Ogliarola dominate Salento — conversion differences confound with front-arrival timing); the one matched cohort ever built locked cultivar on only 457/1,679 pairs (`EXPERIMENT1.md`).
Upgrade path: run the analysis with location/campaign controls and require the positive control — Leccino must emerge field-resistant, matching two decades of published evidence — before any client sees a cultivar table. Until then this claim cannot be sold. Even after, the caveat: "field conversion rates are observational and confounded with location, management, and survey timing; they are not an inoculation trial and do not certify resistance."

**A5. Block-scale Sentinel-2 canopy-decline monitoring (Hornero et al. 2020) — SUPPORTABLE WITH CAVEATS, gated.**
Literature basis verified this session: Hornero, Hernández-Clemente, North, Beck, Boscia, Navas-Cortés, Zarco-Tejada, *Remote Sensing of Environment* 236:111480 (2020), doi:10.1016/j.rse.2019.111480 — multi-date S2 ARVI/OSAVI tracked orchard-level disease severity and incidence, r²>0.7 (p<0.001), validated on >3,000 trees / 16 orchards; "when seasonal understory changes were accounted for using modelling methods, the error of DI prediction was reduced 3-fold" (abstract, DIGITAL.CSIC record).
Repo constraints: the repo's own A2 measured median 20 m crown fraction 0.349 — pixels are ~2/3 soil/understory, so understory handling is not optional; and the repo has implemented neither the 3D-RTM correction nor any local validation. Sellable formulation: multi-date block-level ARVI/OSAVI trend vs the block's own baseline, labeled **"canopy-condition trend — cause-agnostic"**. Mandatory caveat: "Canopy decline at block scale can reflect drought, Verticillium, pruning, harvest, irrigation change, or Xylella; this panel monitors established damage, it does not detect or diagnose infection, and it says nothing about individual trees." Gate before shipping: validate against the Crecco-box incidence labels; if local performance is not in Hornero's league, the module stays out.

**A6. Arrival-time estimates from front-velocity fits — MISLEADING as sold; a scenario band is the only honest form.**
No empirical front rate exists after April 2018 (Kottelenberg et al. 2021: 10.0 km/y, 95% CI 7.5–12.5, 2013–2018; White-group ABC: 5.7–7.2 km/y through 2020; S1 review §A–B). The repo's own design-adjusted estimate (S1 action D1) is unbuilt. The front is anisotropic; LDD jumps of ~20 km (Bari San Giorgio 2024) put single-farm arrival at ±years; the 2022–23 count collapse is an unresolved mixture of epidemiology and survey redesign. And for the exemplar the question is moot (front already passed Ostuni).
A point estimate ("Xylella reaches your farm in 2029") cannot be sold. Permissible only: a published-rate scenario band with the explicit sentence "no empirical front-speed estimate exists for any period after April 2018; these are extrapolations of pre-2018/2020 rates", and only for farms genuinely ahead of the front.

## B. Red lines (binding product constraints)

1. **No tree-level previsual claims from Sentinel-2, native or super-resolved. Ever.** This repo closed four pre-registered designs negative on the same matched official-PCR cohort: A0 (absolute paired NDMI, 1,679 pairs, cluster sign-flip p≥0.13 all lags), A1 (temporal anomaly, pre-registered feature wrong-signed, p 0.73), A2 (crown-fraction dilution, null after confound control), A7 (2.5 m DiffFuSR super-resolution, crown-masked p≥0.22, high-cf wrong-signed, bicubic control identical) — `nowcast/NEGATIVE.md`, `nowcast/ARTIFACT_A.md`. Hornero 2020's introduction independently scopes satellites out of tree-level early detection. Any marketing sentence implying "our satellite finds infected trees before symptoms" is contradicted by the vendor's own experiments. That is not overclaiming; it is selling a result the seller proved false.
2. **Survivorship bias is structural, not a footnote.** Positives are felled by injunction. Panels truncate; "no recent positives nearby" can mean removal, not remission; cultivar and conversion analyses skew toward survivors. Every longitudinal panel in the report must state whether felling could produce the pattern shown.
3. **Survey-design shifts forbid naive trends.** Lecce exit 2018; 2022–23 frame shift and positive collapse; 2024–25 subspecies and budget shift; polygons and risk strata move yearly. A count decline must never be presented as a risk decline. Every time-series panel carries a design annotation.
4. **Labels are official diagnostic outcomes at sampling date; symptom flags are weak.** The Region's own download page (fetched 16 Aug 2026): "l'indicazione di 'sintomo presente' … non è sinonimo di sintomi di OQDS". `SUBSPECIE` filtering is mandatory for 2024–25.
5. **No prevalence claims inside the infected zone.** The Region states it plainly: "i dati di monitoraggio non possono fornire indicazioni sulla frequenza del batterio nella zona infetta, in quanto la maggior parte della sua superficie non è più oggetto di sorveglianza." The product must repeat this, especially to clients behind the front.

## C. Licensing and legal basis (all verified against primary sources this session)

- **Regione Puglia CKAN monitoring dataset** (`dati.puglia.it`, package `dati-monitoraggio-xylella-fastidiosa`, API `package_show` fetched 16 Aug 2026): `"license_id": "CC-BY-4.0"`, `"license_title": "Creative Commons Attribution 4.0"`, `"license_url": "https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/"`; resource `license_type` = w3id.org vocabulary `A21_CCBY40`. Not IODL. Commercial use and derivatives permitted with attribution ("Regione Puglia — Sezione Osservatorio fitosanitario"). Covers the CKAN CSV (2020–mid-2023) only.
- **emergenzaxylella.it campaign workbooks (the core asset).** The download page (`/Download/mon_xf`, fetched this session; files hosted at `cartografia.sit.puglia.it/doc/xylella/download_monit_pauca/CAMP_*.xlsx`) states interpretation limits but **no license**. Legal basis is Italy's open-by-default rule, CAD art. 52(2), d.lgs. 82/2005 (docs.italia.it consolidated text): "I dati e i documenti che i soggetti … pubblicano, con qualsiasi modalità, senza l'espressa adozione di una licenza …, si intendono rilasciati come dati di tipo aperto", and CAD art. 1(1)(l-ter) defines open data as usable "da parte di chiunque, anche per finalità commerciali". Supporting intent: the same Osservatorio publishes the CKAN twin under CC BY 4.0, and DGR 422/2022 sets CC-BY 4.0 as the regional open-data license (historically IODL 2.0 per DGR 2183/2012). Verdict: commercial reuse is lawful; it rests on a statutory default rather than an express grant, so attribute prominently and send the Osservatorio a one-line confirmation note. Not a launch blocker.
- **Copernicus Sentinel-2** (Legal notice on the use of Copernicus Sentinel Data and Service Information, sentinels.copernicus.eu, fetched this session): "free, full and open access", expressly granting "(a) reproduction; (b) distribution; (c) communication to the public; (d) adaptation, modification and combination with other data". Commercial use included; no warranty; required credit "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data [Year]". Clean.
- **Crecco OQDS-Insight** (figshare 28191245 v5, API fetched this session): `"license": {"name": "CC BY 4.0", "url": "https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/"}`. **CC BY, not CC BY-NC** — commercial use of this layer is permitted with citation (Crecco et al. 2025, doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.28191245.v5). Two caveats: (1) the 2,225 labels are photointerpretation ("identified most likely as either infected or removed"), never to be presented as diagnostic data; (2) the released rasters derive from commercial WorldView-2 imagery — the CC BY tag is the depositors' assertion, not a Maxar warranty. Use points and rasters internally for validation; do not redistribute the rasters as an imagery product.
- **Puglia orthophotos** (repertorio.sit.puglia.it metadata): Ortofoto 2006 and CTR records state "Limitazione d'uso: Italian Open Data License (IODL 2.0)"; IODL 2.0 (dati.gov.it) permits copying, distribution, and derivative works — including commercial mashups — with attribution only. Per-vintage metadata for 2015_IR/2019/2022/2023 was not individually opened; verify the specific record before shipping orthophoto chips in a paid report.
- **Selling analysis of official diagnostic data to private parties.** Lawful: the data are open data published for reuse including commercial (above; PSI/Open Data framework, d.lgs. 36/2006). Three real constraints: (1) **personal data** — CAD art. 52(2) excludes personal data from open-by-default; the workbooks carry no names, and the product must not re-identify (no cadastral-owner joins, no neighbor naming); (2) **no representation of official status** — only the Osservatorio Fitosanitario determines phytosanitary status and demarcation; the report is private analysis of public data and must say so on page 1; (3) **negligent-misstatement liability** — a paying client acting on a wrong "low exposure" panel is the main legal exposure; the caveat texts in §A are the defense, plus E&O insurance before scaling.

## D. v1 product spec — the minimal honest report

Per-farm PDF/web report, ~10 pages. Cadence: annual, keyed to the campaign workbook release; interim errata only if a new official positive appears within 10 km. Sections:
1. **What this is** — private analysis of public official monitoring data; not a diagnosis, not a certification, not the Osservatorio; licenses and attributions.
2. **Where you stand** — farm boundary vs official olive-pauca detections, 2013–2025 map series; distance/bearing of nearest positives per campaign; comune demarcation status from official acts. Design annotations on every panel (§B3).
3. **Testing and positivity around you** — 1/3/5/10 km rings × campaign: samples, positives, positivity; testing intensity co-equal with positivity; "no information" rendering for untested ring-years.
4. **Conversion pressure** — repeat-tested coordinates within 5 km: stable negatives, documented negative→positive conversions, with denominators and the felling/survivorship note.
5. **Actions** — official obligations only: mandatory vector-control (tillage) windows, suspect-symptom reporting duty, links to official downloads and the authorized resistant cultivars (Leccino, FS-17, Lecciana, Leccio del Corno — the Region's own planting list). No bespoke agronomy.
6. **Data and limits** — the §A caveat texts and the Region's own interpretation notes, translated.
Stays out of v1: the satellite canopy module (until the Crecco-box validation gate in A5 passes — earliest v1.1), cultivar outcome tables (until the A4 analysis passes its positive control), arrival-time numbers, all vine/ST1 content. If a wine estate asks about vines, the honest one-liner: ST53 pauca is not a grapevine pathogen; the separate ST1 (Pierce's) focus is in the Bari area under eradication, ~50+ km from Ostuni — monitored, not modeled, here.

## E. Kill list — dishonest, unlicensed, or liability-creating

1. **Satellite early-warning for individual trees** — the vendor's own four nulls prove it false. Dishonest at the level of consumer fraud.
2. **Per-tree health scores or maps from any satellite input** — same nulls, plus 20 m pixels are ~2/3 soil (A2).
3. **Arrival-date point predictions** — no post-2018 empirical rate exists; LDD makes farm-level dates meaningless.
4. **"Xylella-free" certification, or anything a buyer/insurer could read as phytosanitary status** — regulatory function of the Osservatorio; misrepresentation plus unbounded liability.
5. **Prevalence claims inside the infected zone** — the Region's own page forbids the inference.
6. **Cultivar resistance rankings before the controlled analysis with a Leccino positive control exists** — currently a count, not a result.
7. **Vine-risk fear-selling to wineries from pauca data** — wrong subspecies, wrong host.
8. **Redistributing Crecco WV2 rasters or orthophoto mosaics as an imagery layer** — third-party (Maxar) provenance risk; unverified per-vintage orthophoto terms.
9. **Neighbor- or owner-level risk lists** — personal-data exclusion from open-by-default; defamation and community-conflict exposure.
10. **Calibrated-sounding infection probabilities ("your block: 23% by 2028")** — no validated model on disk; false precision is the liability-creating version of A6.
11. **NDMI "water stress = Xylella" storytelling** — A0 null; Cagnarini 2025 shows infected-tree hydraulics are not a monotone "drier = sicker".
