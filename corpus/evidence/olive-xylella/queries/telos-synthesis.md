## Convergent facts the reviews establish

1. **The paying customer is the intermediary body, not the farmer.** ~160k olive farms exist; ~16k are businesses; buying power sits in ~50 bodies (746 mills, coops/OPs like APROL Lecce 28,749 members and Oleificio Coop Ostuni 386 members, 3 oil DOP consortia, AMO Puglia, polo antixylella, GALs). Demonstrated software WTP tops out ~€300/yr and only for weekly-actionable pest alerts. Xylella generates 2–4 discrete decisions per decade → one-off briefs at €1–10k per body, sold through consortia. (S7)
2. **The sellable core is official-data geography + regulatory/financial synthesis, not satellites.** Ring positivity with testing-intensity denominators is the strongest claim (S8-A2). The zone-conditional legal matrix, the live money/deadline tracker, and the change-log are the genuinely underserved content (S9-D). Producer-facing remote sensing is killed by both S9 and the repo's own four nulls.
3. **The data shadow is the gap.** Post-front comuni lose all official signal at decision time: Ostuni logged 1,516 olive positives in 2021 (top comune) and has zero sample rows in 2023–2025. Confirmed independently by S7 and by the exposure engine. No incumbent (Elaisian, xFarm, Agricolus, REDoX, Regione) fills it.
4. **Licensing is clean for commercial use.** Workbooks: open-by-default under CAD art. 52(2) incl. commercial (attribute "Regione Puglia — Sezione Osservatorio fitosanitario"; send a one-line confirmation note). CKAN twin: CC-BY-4.0. Copernicus: free incl. commercial. Crecco: CC BY 4.0 (points/labels usable; do not redistribute WV2 rasters). (S8-C)
5. **The €1.3–3.6B replant lever is gated by money/administration, not knowledge** (€222M demand vs €80M; €17M cash out; €18.8M phantom plantings; ~3,758 of ~9,186 applications processed by DDS 53/2026). Better gene tables change none of it. (S2, S9-B)
6. **No empirical front rate exists after April 2018** (Kottelenberg 2021: 10.0 km/yr, 2013–2018). Arrival-time point estimates are unsellable; only labeled scenario bands are honest. (S1, S8-A6)
