# Telos review — S9: the producer playbook

Adversarial research review, 16 Aug 2026. Customer = the Apulian producer (olive estate, mill, cooperative, wine estate; exemplar Cantine Amalberga, Ostuni). Question: which decision-relevant 2026 facts can a 2-person computational team package *additively*, versus what consortia/agronomists already deliver? All items verified against the cited primary source this session unless marked **UNVERIFIED** or **repo-verified** (from `queries/telos-review-S{1,2,4}` / skill fact banks). **HIGH-STAKES** = stale/wrong statement can get a grower fined or a tree wrongly felled.

## A) Obligations by zone

**Legal stack.** Base regime: Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1201, amended by 2021/1688 (M1), 2021/2130 (M2), 2023/1706 (M3 — rewrote the Annex I host / Annex II specified-plant lists; Puglia's action plan now cites host lists "di cui al Reg. 2023/1706"), 2024/1320 (M4 — ratified enlarged pauca containment zone: Alberobello, Castellana Grotte, Monopoli, Polignano a Mare, part of Putignano), 2024/2507 (M5 — the big operational amendment, applied 26 Sep 2024/1 Jul 2025), 2025/2231 (M6, 3 Nov 2025 — Annex III Part B: Occitanie moved to containment; shows Annex III is live). Current consolidation: CELEX 02020R1201-20251124. Regional layer: Osservatorio Fitosanitario determinazioni (BURP) + Piano d'azione 2024–2026 (DGR 1593 del 25/11/2024, >€15M).

**Current Puglia zones (decrees).** One pauca-ST53 "ex Salento" demarcated area — zona infetta = all of Lecce + Brindisi provinces + parts of Taranto/Bari (Annex III Part A); inside it, a **zona di contenimento** now only **2 km** wide from the buffer border (cut from 5 km by 2024/2507 Art. 15(2)(a); enacted by DDS 158 del 18/11/2024); around it a **zona cuscinetto** 5 km wide, Ionian-to-Adriatic. Latest map update: **DDS n. 82 dell'11/05/2026**. Separate *eradication* demarcated areas (50 m infected zones + 2.5 km buffer, Arts. 7–11): ST1 Triggiano (DDS 8/2024 + 12, 45, 94/2024; DDS 11 del 12/02/2026), pauca-Bari (DDS 92/2024), multiplex Santeramo/Ginosa/Noicattaro-Triggiano/Capurso (DDS 29, 91, 93, 148, 198/2024; 106/2025), pauca-Minervino delle Murge (DDS 59 del 14/04/2025), pauca-Bisceglie (DDS 113 del 23/06/2025), plus early-2026 pauca foci at Bitonto (1), Valenzano (16), Modugno (5) (Olivo e Olio, Feb 2026 — determina numbers **UNVERIFIED**). Parcel-level zone lookup exists on www.emergenzaxylella.it (stated in DDS 18/2024). **HIGH-STAKES:** northern-Puglia farms can change regime overnight when a focus is found; Salento rules do not transfer (see felling below).

**Vector control, 2026 calendar (all Puglia, all zones).** Mandatory juvenile-stage control (soil works vs spittlebug nymphs): **DDS n. 39 dell'11/03/2026** — aratura/fresatura/erpicatura/trinciatura (trinciatura only with sward ≤10 cm); pirodiserbo/steam where machines can't go, herbicide only as last resort; obligations on owners *and* managers of uncultivated land, road edges, canals, public green. Windows: 25 Mar–30 Apr (comuni <200 m asl), 10 Apr–15 May (>200 m); **extended by DDS n. 79 del 28/04/2026 to 15 May / 30 May** after rain. Exemptions: woods, pine stands, protected areas (except olive/fruit/vine plots inside them), private gardens; in herbaceous crops with olive/almond/prunus present, work under-canopy only. Compliance checked by Carabinieri Forestali; the tillage obligation derogates the CAP ecoschema-2 grass-cover requirement; €5M regional support to comuni (RAI TGR Mar 2026). Adult-stage treatments: obligatory under Piano d'azione 2024–2026 §4.10.6 and Art. 8(2) / Art. 14 of 2020/1201 (2025 reference: DDS 47 del 26/03/2025 + circolare); the specific 2026 adult-treatment determina and the Puglia-authorized actives were **not located this session — GAP; a playbook must pin this per campaign year**. **HIGH-STAKES:** the deadlines move every year and mid-year (2026 proroga); quoting last year's dates = fines.

**Felling triggers.** Eradication ADs (Art. 7(1)): remove infected plants, symptomatic/suspect plants, **all plants of the same species as the infected one within the 50 m infected zone regardless of health status** (7(1)(c)), and other specified plants not immediately sampled+tested — but per M5, other-species specified plants that test negative need *not* be removed, and plants not found infected in that AD in the last 2 years may skip immediate testing. Historic/monumental trees can be spared under the Art. 7 derogation (annual test + vector treatment). Containment (Salento infected zone, Arts. 12–17): **only plants found infected are removed** (Art. 13(1)), immediately or before the next vector flight season; the Art. 13(2) scientific derogation and the LR 4/2017 art. 8 co. 7-bis "alternative measures" are **excluded in the 2-km containment strip** (DDS 18/2024). Removal orders are notified via **7-day publication on the comune's albo pretorio** (Piano d'azione §4.5; e.g. DET 9 del 28/01/2025, Crispiano) — owners get no certified letter. Inspector access is obligatory (D.lgs 19/2021 art. 22); pruning-residue movement out of demarcated areas is banned (DM 24 gennaio 2022 — verified via Lazio act citing it; Puglia enactment detail **UNVERIFIED**). **HIGH-STAKES:** the 50-m same-species rule applies only in eradication foci; telling a Bisceglie/Bitonto grower the Salento only-infected-trees rule (or vice versa) is exactly the kind of error that costs trees.

**Planting & movement.** Planting specified plants in the infected zone: only under Art. 18 — (b) resistant/tolerant varieties or 2-yr-tested-clean species, **excluded in the 2-km containment strip**; (c) same-species tested clean in eradication zones. Puglia's DDS 48 del 03/05/2024 authorizes in the infected zone (ex containment strip): olive Leccino, FS-17, **Lecciana (resistant)**, **Leccio del Corno (tolerant)**; citrus/peach/plum/apricot (immune); almond/cherry (low susceptibility); rosemary, cistus, myrtle, alaternus, laurel, phillyrea, pelargonium. Movement of specified plants out of/within demarcated areas: Arts. 19–27 (authorized insect-proof sites, testing, "Buffer zone – XYLEFA" passport marking per M5, road/port checks); Art. 23 movement authorizations: **none granted in containment areas; in eradication areas only officially immune species** (DDS 48/2024 Allegato A). Dormant Vitis out of a demarcated area requires hot-water treatment 50 °C/45 min (Art. 22). Buyers should demand plant passports; nursery stock is the canonical long-jump pathway (Mallorca via California almond cuttings — repo-verified).

## B) Money

**Art. 6 replant (Misura 2.B, DI n. 2484 del 06/03/2020, Piano rigenerazione €300M).** Demand vs money (repo-verified, Regione 22 Jul 2024): **9,186 requests needing €222M vs €80M** allotted (Fallucchi interrogation says 9,136 requests / only 1,341 actually paid — counts vary by source). Senato record 13 Mar 2025: €55M committed, 1,889 beneficiaries, 938,281 plants / 8,185.70 ha; **€17M actually disbursed**. Overall Piano at Jul 2024: €181M committed / €137M disbursed (76% / 58%). 2026 movement: **DDS n. 53 del 30/03/2026** scrolled the ranking — 403 new admissions (positions 3355→3758), **€7.4M** from residual economies; beneficiaries had 30 days to upload the delega on pma.regione.puglia.it or be archived; **DDS n. 120 del 28/07/2026** reopened that window for 10 days. So ~6 years in, roughly 3,758 of ~9,186 applications have even entered processing. **HIGH-STAKES (financially):** these portal deadlines silently kill applications.

**Scandal & consequences (verified multi-source).** AGEA report (Gazzetta del Mezzogiorno 21 Nov 2025; OlivoNews; FruitJournal 24 Nov 2025; Libero): of €122M paid 2016–2022, **€18.82M went to beneficiaries with "no plants present"; 51% of those (~2,500 firms) closed or with deceased holders**; only 65% (€67.5M) of sums map to positions with a *positive* plant-count change; AGEA says the Region never built the €5M-funded automated control system. Regione rebuttal (Nardone, via FruitJournal): late-paid 2019 indemnities to since-deceased-but-entitled beneficiaries; replant is not parcel-bound; 5% ex-post checks "per normativa". Follow-on: Senate interrogations (Melchiorre; Fallucchi/Naturale — **>€50M now under review** per sottosegretario La Pietra), planned national commissario straordinario (**UNVERIFIED** beyond press), and the 2026 Regione–Guardia di Finanza protocol extended to the Piano (Regione press, Apr 2026).

**Other lines a producer can actually watch in 2026.** (1) **€30M national riconversione/reimpianto fund — AGEA bandi announced "coming weeks" as of Apr 2026** (assessore Paolicelli): the single most watch-worthy new window. (2) Income-loss indemnities in the infected zone extended 3→5 years (Olivo e Olio, May 2024). (3) Monumental-tree grafting measure, €5M at 80–100% aid (repo-verified); AIPP/AMO say uptake was capped by the small budget (Feb 2026). (4) ST1 area: DGR 994/2024 compensation, voluntary vine removal ≤400 m indemnified (repo-verified). (5) CAP/CSR Puglia interactions: the only verified item is the ecoschema-2 tillage derogation; specific CSR investment measures for Xylella replant **GAP — verify before citing**. Realistic payment experience: commitments ≠ cash; advances/balances arrive years late (€17M cash on €55M committed as of Sep 2024); phantom-planting fallout will likely *tighten* documentation demands on honest applicants from 2026 on.

## C) Agronomic decisions with evidence

**Replant cultivar (all repo-verified + DDS 48/2024).** Leccino: resistant, huge nursery availability, unsuited to super-high-density (SHD), fits intensive; the default. FS-17/Favolosa: resistant, oil ~18%, bearing yr 2–3, best cumulated yield of the resistant set under SHD (Agronomy 2022, 12/12/3157). Lecciana (Arbosana×Leccino): authorized May 2024; oil >19% fw, bearing yr 3, >1,200 trees/ha, ~50% lower production cost, high polyphenols (Agronomy 2021, 11/11/2154); **proprietary to Agromillora, no third-party licenses, ~1M plants/yr — availability is the constraint**; its own field titre vs Leccino still thin. Leccio del Corno: authorized as **tolerant, not resistant** — infection rates "molto elevata … prossima a … varietà suscettibili" without overt desiccation (Servizio Fitosanitario dossier via DDS 48/2024 press) — a materially different risk profile the label "resistant list" hides. Leccino clonal variation is real: 7 clones inoculated, 16-mo infection 57–100%, four clones had 16–40% dead plants (SSSUP/CNR 2025 paper) — clone/source matters, not just cultivar. Nothing new beats these before ~2035 (Boscia EFSA 2021: ~100 genotypes screened, none beat Leccino/FS-17; Lecciana took ~26 yr cross-to-authorization).

**Grafting.** Top-working monumental/secolari trees with Leccino/FS-17 is regionally promoted (LR 14/2007 census; €5M measure; ResiXO): AIPP/AMO Monopoli technical meeting (Feb 2026, Leone) shows take-rate assessed at 18–24 months and first production ~3 yr, but **no published landscape-scale survival/yield statistics located — evidence level: practitioner + trial plots**. Honest framing: proven technique, thin public outcome data, subsidy-capped.

**Dentamet / Zn-Cu-citric acid: symptom management, not cure.** Scortichini et al. 2018 (Phytopathol. Mediterr. 57(1):48–72): 3-yr Veglie trial, 6 canopy sprays/yr (Apr–Oct, excl. Jul–Aug), reduced symptom severity and in-leaf Xf densities; most untreated trees died, all treated survived. Mid-term: Pathogens 2021 10(1):85 (41 trees, 3–4 yr, low Xf over seasons, trees still infected); >700 ha applying the protocol (Pathogens 2021 10(6):668). EFSA's position is unchanged and binding for expectations: treatments "do not eliminate Xylella … but can reduce disease symptoms" (EFSA statement 2016, EFSA Journal 4456) and "there is currently no method available to cure diseased plants in the field" (EFSA PLH 2019, EFSA Journal 5666; echoed on DG SANTE's Xylella page). A treated tree stays PCR-positive and legally infected — in an eradication AD it will still be felled; in the containment strip it will still be felled. **HIGH-STAKES:** selling coexistence protocols as protection from removal orders is false where removal is triggered by positivity, not symptoms.

**SHD conversion economics.** Verified inputs only: Lecciana SHD >1,200 trees/ha with ~50% lower production cost and yr-3 bearing (Agronomy 2021); FS-17 best SHD yield (Agronomy 2022); Lecciana supply capped ~1M plants/yr. Full €/ha and NPV vs traditional replant: no published Apulian study located — **GAP**; any playbook number would be a model, and must say so.

**Wine estates / ST1.** The pauca ST53 epidemic does not threaten Vitis; the grape threat is Xff ST1 (Pierce's genotype) near the Bari table-grape district. Status (repo-verified): 2024 = 339 infected (212 almond, 119 vine); 2025 = 20 (13 vine, 7 almond); >50,000 samples, >30 ha vineyard removed; almond→vine via *Neophilaenus campestris*, vine-to-vine via *P. spumarius*; 400-m almond-removal rule (DET 22/2025; DDS 11 del 12/02/2026); **2026 campaign closed 28 Feb 2026 with "no expansion"** (Regione press). What an Ostuni estate (deep in the pauca infected zone, ex-containment belt) should actually do: comply with tillage on all estate land (obligation is zone-independent); keep/remove marginal almonds thoughtfully; buy vine material only with passports (Art. 22 thermotherapy for dormant Vitis from demarcated areas); watch the ST1 demarcation until formally revoked, and vector monitoring bulletins (Dati Monitoraggio Vettori on emergenzaxylella.it). Complacency after revocation is the historical failure mode (S4 review).

## D) Verdict + packaging

**What is genuinely underserved (additive):** (1) **The zone-conditional legal matrix.** The rules genuinely differ by regime (50-m same-species felling vs infected-only; where replanting resistant olives is banned — the 2-km strip; where Art. 23 movement is impossible), the zone map changed 4× in 2024–2026, and new eradication foci keep minting growers who learned the wrong (Salento) rules. Nobody publishes this as one farm-facing document; it lives across ~15 BURP determinazioni, 6 EU amendments and a PDF action plan. (2) **The live money/deadline tracker.** Scorrimenti, 30-day/10-day portal deadlines, the €30M AGEA bandi, indemnity windows — consortia announce pieces reactively; a single dated, cited "state of the queue" page (updated monthly) is real value and pure information work. (3) **Change-log discipline**: "what changed since 2024 and what it means for you" (2-km strip, M5 testing relief in the 50-m radius, Lecciana/Leccio del Corno, XYLEFA passports, 2026 tillage proroga). Stale guidance is where growers get hurt; freshness is the product. **What is NOT additive:** the tillage obligation itself (every comune reposts it), "plant Leccino/FS-17", "Dentamet isn't a cure" (agronomists know), zone lookup (official web app exists).

**Producer Xylella Playbook 2026 — shippable in 2 weeks by this team:** ~25 pp, Italian-first, every fact cited to decree/article/DOI. §1 Which regime am I in (decision tree + decree register + how to read an albo-pretorio prescrizione, appeal window). §2 My obligations calendar (2026 dates incl. proroga; adult-treatment determina pinned once verified). §3 Felling: what triggers it in *my* zone, monumental derogation, indemnities. §4 Replant law + cultivar table with evidence grades (incl. Leccio del Corno tolerance caveat, Lecciana supply). §5 Money: queue state, deadlines, AGEA-scandal fallout, €30M watch. §6 Wine-estate annex (ST1 status, propagation-material hygiene). §7 Sources & update log. **Per-farm personalization from on-disk data (the moat):** for a farm's coordinates, the 12 CAMP workbooks (1.3M records, 2013–2025) yield what the official app does not show — positives within 400 m/1 km by year, subspecies and cultivar; local negative→positive conversion rates by cultivar (field resistance evidence at the buyer's doorstep); and **local sampling-effort trend** (is my area still being surveyed at all — cf. the 94% Murge collapse, repo-verified). That is a defensible, checkable per-farm brief no consortium produces. Caveat: this is regulatory/financial information synthesis — additive precisely because it is *not* agronomy.

## E) Kill list

- **Recommending plant-protection products, doses or spray schedules** (adulticides, Dentamet regimes). Label law, PAN, buffer rules, operator licenses — agronomist/DPI territory. We cite the obligation and the evidence; we never prescribe. Zero (negative) Δ.
- **Legal advice on individual felling injunctions/appeals (TAR).** Lawyers. We may explain the generic 7-day albo-pretorio mechanism only.
- **Per-farm SHD business plans / credit files.** Agronomi + commercialisti; CAP consultancy is a licensed market. We publish the cited inputs, not the plan.
- **Re-building a zone-lookup app.** emergenzaxylella.it already does parcel lookup; duplicating it is zero.
- **Generic "what is Xylella" education.** CNR, infoxylella, EFSA, consortia saturate this.
- **Predicting graduatoria scorrimenti or promising subsidy outcomes.** We report the queue; forecasting administrative acts invites blame without control.
- **Coexistence advocacy / Xf-causality revisionism** (Scortichini-line "3.21%" claims — rebutted by Boscia: 48.5% positivity among 2021-22 symptomatic trees, 68.7% in the ex-containment belt). Touching this politicized fight destroys the playbook's institutional usability.
- **Selling detection/remote-sensing to producers.** Four repo nulls + no producer decision hinges on it (S1/S2 reviews). Zero.
