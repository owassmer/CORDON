# Telos review — S2: cut severity / restore value in the infected zone

Adversarial reviewer, independent. Objective: reduce the projected € loss from *Xylella fastidiosa*. Judge research by expected Δ€ only. Every figure below is verified against a primary source or marked **UNVERIFIED**. Date: 2026-08-16.

## A. € mechanism and magnitude — verified from the paper

**The lever is the difference between Schneider's two economic scenarios: resistant replant feasible vs. not.** Verified verbatim from Schneider et al. 2020, *PNAS* 117(17):9250–9259 (pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1912206117, article text, Table 1 discussion):

- Italy, no replant (production ceases after die-off): **€1.86B (RR05) – €5.17B (RR95)** → rounds to €1.9–5.2B. ✓
- Italy, replant with resistant cultivars: **€0.59B – €1.57B** → rounds to €0.6–1.6B. ✓
- **"The benefit of resistant cultivars to Italy ranged between 1.27 billion to 3.60 billion Euros."** ✓ **This exact sentence is the S2 lever. €1.3–3.6B confirmed.**
- Investment lost to premature tree death: €0.21–0.61B **regardless of scenario** (rainfed-intensive 28% + rainfed-traditional 27% of it) — a floor that replant does *not* recover.
- Cross-check on the S1 lever: slowing spread 5.18→1.1 km/yr saves Italy **€1.29B (no replant) / €0.46B (replant)** — the repo's "€0.5–1.3B" is the Italy replant↔no-replant range, correct.

**Mechanism.** The model computes discounted foregone grower profits + lost investment. The replant scenario assumes infected orchards *are* replanted with a resistant cultivar and recover supply; the difference vs. the die-off scenario is the ceiling that replant can capture. **Critical caveat the number hides: €1.3–3.6B is the modelled maximum if replant happens at scale.** Schneider assumes feasibility, not uptake. The real-world captured fraction is set by capacity/money/policy (Section B), not by resistance biology. The gap between the €1.3–3.6B ceiling and what is actually being replanted is therefore a *policy/capacity* gap, not a *knowledge* gap.

**Scope flag (verified, out of S2 but decisive for allocation):** Schneider's Spain figures dwarf Italy's — slowing spread saves Spain **€7.12B**, Greece €0.87B. If Xf reaches Andalusian olive, the S2/S1 Italy numbers are a rounding error against the Iberian tail. Not this segment, but it bounds how much of a computational team's time S2 deserves.

## B. State of the art and actors — what actually limits replant TODAY

The binding constraint is **money, administration, nursery capacity, and grower incentives — not knowledge.** Numbers, all from official/primary sources:

**Legal + agronomic knowledge is already sufficient to replant now.**
- Legal replant set: Leccino, FS-17/Favolosa (EU 2017/2352; EFSA 2017;4772), plus **Lecciana** (Arbosana × Leccino; authorized Regione Puglia Decreto Servizio Fitosanitario **n.48, 3 May 2024**, Reg. EU 2020/1201) and **Leccio del Corno** (putatively resistant in Boscia EFSA 2021, MDPI *Agriculture* 2025 14/2/157). A grower can legally plant a resistant tree next winter.
- Field agronomy is characterized: FS-17 oil yield ~18%, bearing from yr 2–3, best cumulated yield of the resistant set under super-high-density (SHD) (MDPI *Agronomy* 2022 12/12/3157); Leccino unsuited to SHD (poor vigour/yield), fits intensive. Lecciana: oil >19% fw, bearing 3rd yr, >1,200 trees/ha, ~50% lower production cost vs best intensive, nutraceutical-grade polyphenols >450 mg/kg (MDPI *Agronomy* 2021 11/11/2154). **Lecciana's own Xf resistance was "under evaluation" at 2021 publication** and rests on its Leccino parent — UNVERIFIED that its field titre matches Leccino.

**Money is the binding constraint — demand vastly exceeds allocation.**
- Piano straordinario di rigenerazione olivicola della Puglia: **€300M total**, FSC funds, Decreto interministeriale n.2484 (6 Mar 2020), Legge 44/2019; 14 measures (regione.puglia.it; masaf.gov.it). ✓ (~€300M reported figure confirmed exactly.)
- Replant (Art.6, infected zone): dotazione **raised €40M → €80M**. Authoritative government status (**Senato, Resoconto stenografico seduta n.285, 13 Mar 2025**): commitment at 30 Sep 2024 = **€55M for 1,889 beneficiaries, 938,281 plants over 8,185.70 ha**; only **€11M advances + €6M balances = €17M actually disbursed.**
- **Oversubscription (Regione Puglia press, 22 Jul 2024):** Art.6 has **9,186 contribution requests needing €222M** against €80M available. Demand is ~2.8× the money. Overall Piano at that date: €181M committed / €137M disbursed (76% / 58%).
- Later data (Quotidiano di Puglia, 12 Feb 2025): of €300M, Regione received €240M; Art.6 €61.5M committed, €25M paid; +€30M unblocked Feb 2025. Copagri (PugliaPress, 4 Mar 2025): "9,000 applications, €55M committed, €16M advances, €6M balances… delays and opacity."
- **Governance failure (Gazzetta del Mezzogiorno, 21 Nov 2025, citing AGEA report):** of €122M paid since 2016, **€18.82M went to beneficiaries "with no plants present," 51% to closed/deceased firms (~2,500 businesses).** Money leaks before it becomes trees.

**Nursery capacity is a real bottleneck for the SHD cultivars.**
- Lecciana: ~2M plants distributed to 2020, ~1,200 ha planted *globally*; **only Agromillora subsidiaries propagate it — no third-party nursery licenses** (MDPI 2021). First Lecce-province planting was **3,500 trees at Galatina, Sep 2024** (RAI TGR Puglia). Throughput ~1M plants/yr, proprietary.
- Leccino/FS-17: widely propagated by many Italian nurseries (Iocoli, Spinelli, Russo…), less constrained. Coldiretti+Unaprol formed a "polo antixylella" to coordinate certified-plant supply — the industry itself names supply chain, not genetics, as the gap.

**Replanted vs. lost (scale check).** Funded replant ≈ **8,186 ha / 938k plants** (Sep 2024). Loss: >10M trees dead (Agromillora Nov 2025); Coldiretti cites ~200,000 ha / 21M trees contaminated. **Replant to date is ≈4% of contaminated area.** The ceiling is capacity-throttled, exactly as Section A predicted.

**Verdict on the central question:** the replant lever is limited by **CAPACITY / MONEY / POLICY / INCENTIVES**, not knowledge. Evidence: €222M demand vs €80M money; 58% disbursement after 4 yr; €18.8M to phantom plantings; proprietary SHD nursery stock; growers already knowing what to plant. A better gene table changes none of these.

## C. Brutal verdict on CORDON's decoder as a contribution to THIS lever

**Near-zero Δ€ on the replant lever, and largely duplication.**

1. **Wrong constraint.** The decoder (Artifact B, `data/decoder/v1-targets.md`) improves the *knowledge* margin — which OE9A locus, which pathway. Section B shows the €1.3–3.6B is gated by money/admin/nursery, not by which gene a breeder knows. Even a perfect target table leaves €222M demand chasing €80M, 58% disbursement, and phantom-planting fraud untouched. On this lever the decoder is a rounding error.

2. **Its only real home is the breeding pipeline — a 10–20+ year horizon, and CNR-internal.** Anchor: Lecciana was crossed **1998**, identified in commercial trials **2013**, characterized **2021**, Xf-authorized **2024** — **~26 years cross-to-authorization.** La Notte 2024 seedlings S105/S215 (symptomless at 24 months) are at the *start* of that pipeline; a released cultivar from them is realistically 2035–2045. Nothing on that clock touches Schneider's 50-year curve in time to matter more than replanting Leccino/FS-17 today does.

3. **It duplicates the actors who hold the phenotypes.** CNR-IPSP / CIHEAM / UniBari ran the RNA-seq, hold the counts, mapped to Farga v9, and *named the susceptibility genes themselves* (DMR6-like, JOX1 — La Notte 2024 main text). Artifact B is a faithful re-extraction of their own Supplementary Table S4 into a ranked list. For a CNR breeder it is near-duplication of internal knowledge, not new information. Marker/genomic-selection design needs their inoculated phenotypes as training labels; La Notte already showed SSRs poorly predict phenotype. Boscia (EFSA 2021) is blunt: ~100 genotypes screened 5–6 yr, **none beat Leccino/FS-17**, and "a significant number of resistant cultivars will probably require long time." Broad screening is a solved-to-be-slow problem, and it is theirs.

4. **The one additive angle is NOT the transcriptomics.** The 13-year tree-level monitoring series on disk (repeat-tested coordinates with cultivar) yields **cultivar-specific field conversion rates at landscape scale** — independent, field-scale resistance evidence at a sample size no inoculation trial can reach. That is worth **more** than the decoder because it speaks to the actual replant decision ("what goes in the ground") and could surface cheap, culturally-preferred field-resistant local cultivars (Frantoio, Nocellara, Leccio del Corno already flagged: Frontiers 2021 fpls.723879). It is still a knowledge-margin contribution — it does not unblock the money — but it is decision-relevant and genuinely novel from held data.

## D. Top 3 actions ranked by expected Δ€

Framing: a 2-person computational team cannot fell, spray, breed, or legislate. Its only Δ€ path is **information that redirects the money/plants of those who can.** Ranked by expected Δ€ on the €1.3–3.6B lever.

**1. Replant bottleneck / money-flow decision brief (S6-adjacent, highest Δ€).**
Assemble the public evidence — Senato 13 Mar 2025 record, AGEA phantom-planting report, regional attuazione tables, €222M-demand-vs-€80M-money oversubscription, 58% disbursement — into one quantified brief showing the binding constraint is *capital allocation and administration*, and estimate the Δhectares per marginal €10M and per month of disbursement acceleration. **Actor:** MASAF / Regione Puglia / Copagri-Coldiretti. **2–6 wk deliverable:** a cited brief + simple throughput model from public decrees and AGEA/regional data. **Why #1:** it targets where the €1.3–3.6B actually leaks; even a modest acceleration of uptake moves hundreds of €M. Our leverage is advisory, but the bottleneck is real and under-analyzed.

**2. Cultivar-specific field conversion-rate analysis from the 13-year series.**
Compute negative→positive conversion and survival by cultivar at exact coordinates from data already held (converters counted: 98/86/42/12 in four adjacent campaign pairs — see `telos-review-S0-self.md`). Rank field resistance of Leccino/FS-17/Cellina/Ogliarola and local candidates at thousands-of-trees scale. **Actor:** CNR/CIHEAM/Osservatorio + growers choosing replant stock. **2–6 wk deliverable:** a cultivar × conversion-rate table with CIs, plus candidate under-tested field-resistant cultivars. **Why #2:** independent field-scale resistance evidence, novel from held data, directly informs the replant choice; cannot unblock money.

**3. SHD-conversion economics + nursery-supply rate model.**
Model €/ha, time-to-breakeven, and 50-yr NPV of converting to Lecciana/FS-17 SHD vs traditional Leccino replant, rate-limited by nursery throughput (~1M proprietary Lecciana plants/yr). Identify the subsidy-targeting that maximizes recovered hectares per € and per nursery-plant. **Actor:** regional planners, growers, Agromillora, the "polo antixylella." **2–6 wk deliverable:** an economic + supply-constraint model from public agronomic (Agronomy 2021/2022) and price data. **Why #3:** helps steer the €80M to fastest-recovery configurations; smaller, more uncertain Δ€.

## E. Kill list (Δ€ ≈ 0 on this lever; stop or de-prioritize)

- **Decoder v2 pitched as a replant €-lever.** KILL. Duplicates La Notte Table S4; knowledge is not the binding constraint; its only home (breeding) is a 10–20 yr CNR-internal horizon.
- **Marker panel / KASP / genomic-selection design by us.** KILL. Needs CNR's inoculated phenotypes as labels; SSRs already shown non-predictive (La Notte 2024); entirely CNR/CIHEAM-internal.
- **New germplasm resistance screening.** KILL. Boscia EFSA 2021: ~100 genotypes, none beat Leccino/FS-17; "will require long time." Owned by CNR, slow by nature.
- **Grafting field-trial design (susceptible scion → resistant, or ResiXO-style).** KILL for a computational team — it is wet-lab/field; ResiXO (Regione Puglia) already runs it. The monumental-tree grafting subsidy exists (Piano, €5M, 80–100% aid); no computational gap.
- **Symptom-management sprays (Dentamet/Kalex Zn-Cu class) modelled as an S2 €-lever.** DE-PRIORITIZE. S-only bridge, not value-restoration; keeps a doomed canopy productive briefly, does not restore per-hectare value or unblock replant. Belongs in the intervention-landscape note, not here.
- **Any transcriptomics reanalysis framed as decision-relevant to next-decade replant.** KILL. Timescale mismatch with the 50-yr loss curve.
- **Do NOT** confuse activity with Δ€: the decoder is publishable and rigorous, and still moves the S2 number by essentially nothing versus getting the €80M spent and the nursery pipeline scaled.
