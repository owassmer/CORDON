## Governing repair principles

The corpus needs these rules enforced structurally:

1. An operative legal fact is distinct from whether evidence of it has been loaded.
2. Missing evidence does not become a false legal state.
3. Approval, opinion, adoption and recordkeeping duties remain independent propositions unless the retained operative text expressly makes them conditions of the downstream decision.
4. Duty activation, authority exercise and completed legal result are different events.
5. A semantic note cannot “repair” a contradictory live AST.
6. Copy-forward wording, register gaps and document identity problems do not earn conflicts or unknowns unless they block a named decision.
7. B may normalize only what the attached A quote actually says; it may not repair A by reinterpretation.
