## 1. EU → Italian national seam

### 1.1 Substantive branch authority

**Ruling:** Regulation 2020/1201 controls the substantive outbreak-response branches and their point-in-time plant, vector, geography, survey, removal, treatment, destruction and planting conditions.

D.lgs. 19/2021 and the national plans allocate Italian authority and supply procedure. They may impose a supported stricter protective requirement where Union law states a minimum or permits national implementation. They may not make containment available outside Article 12, remove an EU condition, rewrite Annex membership or substitute a national summary for the applicable EU version.

### 1.2 Institutional allocation

**Ruling:** The authority seam is:

```text
Central Plant Health Service
→ national coordination, Union liaison, national plans/programmes,
  committee operation, standards and central ordinances

National Plant Health Committee
→ approval/opinion on measures, plans, programmes and procedures

Regional Plant Health Service
→ territorial surveillance, official confirmation, investigation,
  demarcation, branch implementation, prescriptions, violations and enforcement

Emergency secretariat / territorial unit
→ coordination, implementation support and verification

reference and official laboratories
→ designated official or confirmatory evidence
```

Coordination, scientific support and verification do not transfer the reserved legal decision from the competent service.

### 1.2a Plant-health assistants

**Ruling:** D.lgs. 19/2021 Article 20 permits bounded official field activity only through the complete appointment and assignment chain:

```text
professionally qualified external technician
+ central appointment after committee opinion
+ national personnel-register entry and validated current status
+ no effective Article 24 cancellation
+ technical accountability to the territorial service
+ express assignment by the competent service manager
→ assigned official plant-protection activity
```

The assistant cannot prescribe official measures or issue phytosanitary certificates. The assistant may propose a measure or sanction to the inspector, but the competent authority or inspector owns the resulting decision. No plan, convention or procedure may enlarge those limits.

### 1.3 Suspicion, confirmation and area trigger

**Ruling:** The national sequence implements EU Articles 2 and 4 without collapsing its states:

```text
report or suspicion
→ immediate precautionary measures
→ official sample and valid official-laboratory diagnosis
→ required reference confirmation
→ official plant finding
→ investigation and demarcation decision
```

A suspected finding does not trigger Article 4 as though officially confirmed. A laboratory result is not official solely because the method is technically valid. Missing confirmation is unknown, not negative.

From 17 October 2024, an infected-vector finding uses Article 2(4a)'s immediate 400 m plant investigation. It does not automatically create an Article 4 area.

### 1.4 Demarcation and containment selection

**Ruling:** Eradication is the default substantive branch after a confirmed plant finding. Containment requires all of:

```text
applicable Annex III membership
+ exact infected-zone identity
+ competent territorial containment decision
+ the nationally required action-plan approval/adoption route where engaged
```

D.lgs. 19 Article 31(3)'s initial eradication wording governs the ordinary urgent response. It cannot override directly applicable EU Article 12. Article 31(5) expressly allows the later action plan to select eradication or containment. Committee approval, central adoption and the regional territorial act remain separate events.

A regional containment act before Annex III eligibility cannot obtain authority from Article 31 alone.

### 1.5 No-immediate-demarcation surveillance

**Ruling:** The national two-year follow-up is a valid stricter protective requirement.

Before 17 October 2024, it matches the then-effective EU minimum. After M5 reduces the EU minimum to one year, the still-operative 2022 national plan continues to require two years. The 2026 replacement plan knowingly retains two years under the national planning authority. A two-year survey is compatible with the EU floor and has continuing competent national authority.

Therefore:

```text
EU minimum from 17 Oct 2024: at least 1 year
Italian national requirement: at least 2 years
controlling Italian workload: at least 2 years
```

This does not change Article 5's other conditions or permit survey evidence to revoke an area automatically.

### 1.6 Containment geometries during the 2022-plan tail

**Ruling:** The source values describe two distinct, simultaneous geometries.

The national plan’s 5 km value is the Article 4 outward buffer zone. M5 changes Article 15(2)(a)’s inward surveillance band inside the infected zone from 5 km to 2 km. DDS 158 expressly adopts a 2 km containment-surveillance band and a separate 5 km buffer zone. No national/regional width conflict arises from these values.

The exact regional area act remains the operative territorial state. Portal geometry may reconcile the adopted state but cannot create or alter either legal geometry.

The 2026 replacement plan continues the post-M5 2 km inward surveillance design without changing the separate Article 4 buffer.

### 1.7 Owner, direct and substitute execution

**Ruling:** D.lgs. 19 Article 32 supplies the controlling default:

```text
valid prescription
→ owner/holder primary implementation and cost duty
→ complete conforming execution
OR proven omission/incompleteness/nonconformity
OR unresolved execution evidence
→ substitute execution and cost recovery only on the proven failure branch
```

A regional act may directly assign work to public personnel or a delegated body where an independent legal basis authorizes direct public execution for that measure. That is not substitute execution and does not require manufacturing owner default.

An owner election between self-execution and ARIF changes the selected executor. It does not make silence, refusal or breach true. Missing response data is unresolved evidence.

### 1.8 Notice, immediate effect and enforcement

**Ruling:** Law 241/1990 Article 21-bis controls effectiveness against recipients.

Personal communication is the default. Mass publicity is available only where personal communication is impossible or especially burdensome and the competent act establishes a suitable route. Generic plan language stating that seven-day publication constitutes notice does not prove those predicates for every act.

Urgent or cautionary immediate effect is a separate route. It may authorize immediate protective administration action. It does not by itself prove that a recipient received notice, remained silent, refused, became liable for substitute costs or committed a sanctionable violation.

The complete branches are:

```text
measure validity / urgent public effect
≠ recipient notice
≠ response default
≠ refusal
≠ final violation
```

### 1.9 Protected plants

**Ruling:** Three routes remain separate:

1. EU Article 7(3) retention of qualifying uninfected protected plants in eradication;
2. EU Article 13(2) scientific retention of infected plants at qualifying Article 15(2)(b) sites in containment;
3. D.lgs. 19 Article 33 case-specific removal of uninfected monumental or historic-interest plants after landscape authorization.

The general emergency derogation does not merge these routes. Apparent monumental characteristics do not establish official protected status.

### 1.10 Laboratory and diagnostic authority

**Ruling:** Official evidentiary effect requires:

```text
official control or official activity
+ official sample and custody chain
+ valid laboratory designation for territory/task/method
+ required accreditation at the event time
+ applicable Annex IV or authorized method
+ conforming report
+ required reference confirmation
```

The 2022 national plan supports the institutional national-reference role of CNR-IPSP. It does not establish a seat-specific ministerial designation for Bari. Regional designation can independently make CNR-IPSP Bari an official laboratory for a defined task; that is not the same as national-reference status.

Where a pest-free-area confirmation depends specifically on national-reference authority and the only claimed route is a seat-specific Bari designation, that narrower authority remains unresolved. A valid CREA-DC route or another supported institutional CNR-IPSP route may satisfy the requirement where the evidence shows it.

DTU 39 supplies technical procedure. It does not create coercive duties. Its diagnostic dispositions remain evidence states: “not detected” does not prove global absence.

### 1.11 National survey programme

**Ruling:** EU Articles 2, 10 and 15 create the recurring survey duties. D.lgs. 19 Article 27 and the annual national programme organize Italian design and execution.

The PNI 2026 workbooks are committee-approved and published survey-design material. The unseen numbered central-service instrument leaves formal instrument identity unresolved, not the workbook content. The workbooks cannot independently create area status, findings, breach or completeness.

### 1.12 Plan timing and scientific guidance

**Ruling:** A timing or numeric statement is binding only where the operative national instrument prescribes it for the relevant population and interval. Biological explanation, software assumptions, recommended methods and reference sensitivity values remain supporting design inputs unless adopted as mandatory source-native parameters.

RiBESS/RiPEST calculates or supports sample size. It does not define legal population, allocate samples, select a branch or establish completion.

### 1.13 Reporting and awareness

**Ruling:** National law implements distinct EU effects:

- regional system entry/draft within five working days after official confirmation;
- central official Commission notification within the following three working days;
- affected-operator information without delay;
- public-awareness method defined through the national authority structure;
- recurring annual reporting.

Reporting and awareness do not activate the finding, area or removal duty they describe.

### 1.14 Sanctions

**Ruling:** A source warning about possible sanctions is not a sanction decision.

The national sanction route requires the underlying active duty, evidence of the violation, contest/service, defence opportunity and competent decision. Coercive removal, substitute execution, cost recovery, administrative contest and final injunction remain distinct effects.

### 1.15 D.lgs. 19 Article 5 textual versions

**Ruling:** The 2023 Article 5(2) changes alter organizational text but do not change the admitted central-service substantive powers in Article 5(4).

The exact textual intervals remain provision versions. For the current Stage A aperture, their executable semantic change is `NO` unless a later proposition depends on one changed unit description or staff qualification. This prevents organizational wording from proliferating substantive authority states.

## 2. Italian national → Puglia seam

### 2.1 Regional authority

**Ruling:** Regione Puglia — Sezione Osservatorio Fitosanitario is the competent territorial plant-health service for the admitted mechanism.

It owns regional surveillance, the official-confirmation decision, investigation, area institution/revision/lifting, eradication-or-containment implementation, measure prescriptions, violations and enforcement. L.R. 4/2017 supplies regional implementation but remains subordinate to the effective EU branch and national authority procedure.

ARIF, InnovaPuglia, laboratories, Carabinieri, municipalities and scientific bodies support evidence or execution. None acquires the Osservatorio’s reserved decisions merely because a plan assigns work.

### 2.2 Regional plan and procedure succession

**Ruling:** Regional plans operate only for their effective intervals and only for legal effects they actually adopt:

```text
DGR 538/2021
→ DGR 343/2022
→ DDS 67/2022 surveillance supplement
→ DGR 1866/2022 + DGR 570/2023
→ DGR 1593/2024
→ DGR 1075/2025
```

DDS 45/2025 supplies the current surveillance-to-removal procedure from 26 March 2025. DDS 31/2022 remains the unseen historical procedure from 13 May 2022 through 25 March 2025.

A successor plan ends the predecessor’s plan-level rules. It does not repeal statutes, EU provisions, exact area acts, completed official findings or case effects that survive independently.

DGR 1075’s authorization for the Osservatorio to update implementation does not permit an unsigned portal, map or operational data change to amend legal meaning. A legally material change requires the competent source form.

### 2.3 DGR 1593 approval boundary

**Ruling:** DGR 1593 is a valid regional act, but the status of its materially updated text as the nationally approved/adopted action plan is unresolved.

The July 2024 committee approval predates M5 and DDS 158. “Shared” does not equal committee approval or central adoption. Therefore:

- propositions unchanged from the approved plan or independently grounded in EU law, D.lgs. 19, L.R. 4 or a separate competent determination retain that independent authority;
- materially new plan-only coercive propositions requiring Article 31 action-plan approval carry `AUTHORITY_INTERPRETATION_REQUIRED` unless another exact authority supports them;
- exact area and case acts are adjudicated on their own authority and cannot be invalidated wholesale because the plan-approval chain is incomplete.

### 2.4 Internal 2.5 km glossary

**Ruling:** The DGR 1593/DGR 1075 glossary value of 2.5 km does not control the containment edge.

The operative plan sections, M5 Article 15 and DDS 158 use 2 km for the inward surveillance band. The glossary is stale contextual text. The separate Article 4 buffer remains 5 km; the 2.5 km glossary creates no third legal route.

### 2.5 Regional crop exclusions

**Ruling:** Annex II membership controls the specified-plant predicate. A regional plan or procedure cannot amend it through a biological assertion.

#### Pauca ST53

Current Annex II includes `Citrus L.`, `Prunus L.`, `Asparagus acutifolius` and both named *Olea europaea* subspecies for pauca.

The categorical regional exclusion of citrus, peach, apricot and plum from surveillance and eradication conflicts with Annex II. The blanket no-sampling rule for asparagus also conflicts where the applicable EU survey population reaches that taxon.

A plant may avoid Article 7(1)(e) removal only through the effective EU evidence routes, including immediate negative testing or the current two-year no-infection route with continuing surveillance. Regional evidence cannot create a crop-wide exemption.

#### Fastidiosa ST1

Current Annex II includes named citrus species, `Prunus L.` and `Vitis L.` but does not include *Olea*.

The ST1 olive non-removal rule is not an override of Annex II. For an infected grape, almond or other plant, uninfected olive is not automatically in Article 7(1)(c), (d) or (e). Olive still enters if it independently satisfies another removal predicate, including being officially infected or suspected.

#### Multiplex ST26

Current Annex II includes `Olea L.`, `Prunus L.` and `Asparagus acutifolius` for multiplex.

The categorical regional multiplex olive exclusion and blanket asparagus no-sampling rule conflict with Annex II. They cannot control the reusable EU population.

### 2.6 Positive pools and doubtful results

**Ruling:** Positive pools must be decomposed before individual plant identity is established. Pool positivity does not make every pooled plant officially infected.

DDS 45’s `doubtful` Cq route is a specific regional procedure under Harper testing. DTU 39’s repeated-inconclusive route permits the territorial service to require further investigation. DDS 45 exercises that discretion by requiring aliquot review and resampling for its persistently doubtful route.

For Puglia from 26 March 2025:

```text
persistent DDS 45 doubtful result
→ further investigation / resampling
→ not an infected finding
→ not a negative or absent finding
```

The national “not detected” disposition is not projected onto a regional sample still inside the mandatory resampling route.

### 2.8 Monumental and Piana alternatives

**Ruling:** The regional Piana capitozzatura/grafting alternative is not a general lawful substitute for infected-plant removal.

- Article 7(3) does not reach Article 7(1)(a) infected plants.
- Article 13 requires removal of infected plants in containment.
- Article 13(2) permits retention only for scientific purposes at qualifying Article 15(2)(b) sites.

Therefore an infected monumental olive may use the Piana alternative only if an exact higher-law derogation applies and the competent authority authorizes that exact route. L.R. 4 or a plan cannot independently convert every infected monumental tree into an owner-choice branch.

The general Piana alternative is classified as a regional conflict. It does not enter the reusable lawful removal AST.

### 2.9 Voluntary 400 m vineyard route

**Ruling:** The vineyard route is an optional regional measure, not an Article 7 duty.

It may be represented as a regional `may` permission for the source-defined vineyard population. It creates no breach, silence/default, substitute execution or mandatory removal consequence. Aid eligibility, payment and replanting conditions remain outside this Stage A aperture.

Its higher-law relationship is an additional eradication-support measure, not semantic equivalence with Article 7’s infected-zone population.

### 2.10 Mandatory 400 m almond route

**Ruling:** Mandatory removal of source-defined marginal or abandoned almond trees is a separate eradication-support measure under EU Article 11 and D.lgs. 19 Article 6, implemented by the Osservatorio’s specific prescription and ARIF execution.

It is not Article 7 removal and does not expand the legal infected zone. Its valid activation requires:

```text
operative ST1 eradication area and infected-zone perimeter
+ tree within the exact adopted 400 m prescription/annex population
+ almond tree
+ marginal-or-abandoned characterization
+ competent Osservatorio prescription
+ valid measure effectiveness
```

ARIF is the direct assigned executor. Owner default is not an antecedent. Obstruction may route to the public-force process after the measure is effective.

The source does not support converting the 400 m survey band itself into removal authority. The authority comes from the separate DGR policy plus the Osservatorio prescription under the higher eradication-support powers.

### 2.11 Almond characterization

**Ruling:** DDS 11–13/2026 state one source-native holistic characterization. All five listed indicators are preserved as inputs to the competent field assessment:

1. little or no crown management, absent pruning and dry/dense branches;
2. spontaneous vegetation in the ground/under-crown area;
3. no irrigation system;
4. low quantity/quality productivity;
5. phytosanitary symptoms or damage.

The AST uses `all_of`. Qualitative satisfaction of each characteristic requires field evidence and authorized judgment. No vineyard compensation test is imported.

The geometry phrase is not converted into an invented outward-only formula in Stage A. The exact adopted prescription and annex determine the legal selection. Stage C may calculate geometry only from that accepted source meaning.

### 2.12 Notice and owner/ARIF routes

**Ruling:** Regional plan and order notice clauses inherit section 1.8.

### 2.13 Prefect/public-force request

**Ruling:** D.lgs. 19 Article 33 reserves the legal request for Prefect assistance to the competent territorial plant-health service.

ARIF, inspectors and assistants may document obstruction and transmit or initiate the operational request. The legally effective request is made by or attributable to the Osservatorio. Convention language does not independently delegate this reserved statutory power.

### 2.14 Delegation and sanctions

**Ruling:** ARIF may perform surveillance, sample delivery, prescribed removal, direct assigned work, substitute work and execution reporting within the effective convention and prescription.

It does not own findings, areas, prescriptions, derogations or violations. Carabinieri may inspect, document and contest within the agreement. The Osservatorio remains the regional sanction authority; contest is not final injunction.

For each result after the applicable Ordinance 10 effect, the laboratory must be both designated and accredited for the task/method. Earlier designation remains relevant but cannot substitute for missing event-time accreditation.

CNR-IPSP institutional reference status is supported nationally. A claim that the Bari seat itself holds ministerial national-reference designation remains narrow and unresolved as stated in section 1.10.

- eradication immediate removal resolves to Article 7, not Article 13;

The wrong citation remains a source conflict attached to the act.

Post-M5 orders that quote pre-M5 Article 7(1)(e) cannot override the current EU alternatives. Current EU law determines the reusable population. The order remains effective only for plants actually inside that current population.

### 3.6 Mixed and overlapping areas

### 3.7 Partial-parcel rule

**Ruling:** Where the source includes parcels even partially intersecting a legal zone, the whole parcel becomes subject to the source-defined parcel-level administrative relation. That does not make every plant on the parcel infected or place the entire parcel in the same geometric subzone.

The system must preserve:

```text
exact geometric intersection
+ source partial-parcel legal inclusion
+ plant-specific removal predicate
```

A survey-grid majority rule cannot substitute for this area relation.

### 3.8 Case resolution contract

**Ruling:** Each case resolves through:

```text
case decision time
+ effective EU provision and Annex versions
+ effective national plan/procedure
+ effective regional plan/procedure
+ exact or explicitly unresolved area-state version

+ genuine case delta
```

The case act does not become reusable law merely because it repeats a provision. Personal names, parcels, plants, samples, coordinates and beneficiaries remain instance payload.

### 3.9 Eradication cases

**Ruling:** An eradication case selects the complete effective Article 7 population, not only the infected plant named in the title.

It inherits Article 8 treatment, Article 9 destruction/root/wood routes, Article 10 continuing surveillance and Article 11 investigation/obstacle measures through exact reusable proposition dependencies. Those recurring duties need not be copied as direct links from every case.

A post-M5 case uses the post-M5 Article 7 version even if the order quotes older text.

### 3.10 Containment cases

**Ruling:** A containment case requires Annex III eligibility, an operative regional containment state and an official infected-plant finding.

Article 13 applies to infected plants found on the basis of Article 15(2) monitoring. The removal duty is not spatially confined to the minimum edge band once the Article 15(2) discovery basis is established. Wider or additional regional work requires its exact legal characterization; it does not become Article 15(2) monitoring merely because it occurs inside an Annex III infected zone.

The case inherits Article 14 treatment, Article 15 surveillance/finding routes, Article 16 destruction and Article 17 obstacle measures through reusable dependencies.

### 3.11 Corrective and supplemental cases

**Ruling:** Corrections preserve predecessor law unless they expressly change it.

- identity/ownership correction may extinguish one person’s duty and prescribe it to the correct current person;
- failed service cannot be treated as silence or refusal and requires effective re-prescription;
- annex-only corrections alter payload/geography evidence without creating a new removal rule;
- omitted-parcel supplements add the omitted party/parcel relation and its effective prescription;
- a correction title does not prove complete replacement where the operative text does not state the scope.

Predecessor links and changed legal effects are case deltas, not semantic-equivalence keys.

### 3.12 Staged removal

For current eradication, Article 8 makes the specific pre/during-removal sequence flight-season dependent while preserving broader all-stage treatment duties. A fixed month range does not establish flight state or compliance; event-time vector and appropriate-treatment evidence remain required.

### 3.15 Case notice and coercion

**Ruling:** Every case carries separate questions:

1. Is the measure substantively valid and immediately effective for protective action?

## 7. One-schema semantic ruling

The accepted EU Stage A schema can represent the adjudicated national and regional meanings without a parallel model.

### Stable identity

A stable proposition remains source-specific. It does not use an article-named or crop-named synthetic family as identity.

### Versions

A version changes when exact operative text/status changes. A semantic change is marked only when independently executable meaning changes. Plan succession, EU amendments and regional policy reversals receive exact intervals.

### AST

Every conditional proposition uses the accepted operators only:

```text
predicate
all_of
any_of
not
provision_ref
route_table
otherwise
```

True, false, exception and unresolved outcomes remain explicit. Unknown never becomes false. Effects, notice, clocks, evidence and remedies never activate the duty they describe.

### Relationships

- same-instrument legal hierarchy uses `parent_stable_provision_id`;
- executable proposition dependence uses `provision_ref`;
- cross-instrument authority uses `higher_authority_dependencies`;
- proven equivalent meaning uses `semantic_equivalence_key` without replacing source identity;
- area and case mappings use exact version identities or an explicit unresolved source state.

### Cases

Cases remain outside the reusable rule corpus except for source-specific operative deltas. The case map contains exact proposition versions, exact or unresolved area state, branch, evidence and genuine delta. Payload remains payload.

### Negative scope

Step 5 does not design clocks, calculations, geometry algorithms, data contracts, Actions, nouns or Ontology. It fixes the source meaning those stages will consume.
