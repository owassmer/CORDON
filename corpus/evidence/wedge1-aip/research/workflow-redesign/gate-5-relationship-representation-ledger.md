## Decision rule

The fact-ownership analysis below remains anti-collapse evidence. The blanket rule that every fact-bearing predicate requires an exact object-backed relationship is reopened. The current core admits object-backed relationships only when the cooperative manager needs independent traversal/history and no simpler representation preserves the decision or authorized Action.

Owen also accepts the semantic-uniformity consolidation rule: exact predicates may share one domain-named backing type only when endpoints, fact shape, lifecycle, authority boundary, Actions and security meaning are materially identical. A controlled kind may distinguish them. A material difference on any dimension requires a split.

For predicate 9, Owen accepts an authority-class split: `Proceeding Claimed Scope` owns filer-asserted formal scope and `Proceeding Authority-Determined Scope` owns competent-authority scope. Stages/versions remain within the applicable class. Both classes specialize by subject endpoint rather than using a subject-kind switch. The 20-type matrix is a maximum semantic inventory; deploy only endpoint classes required by current workflows and grain-sufficient evidence. Factless `concerns` links remain direct.

For predicate 14, Owen accepts two exact types. `Member Entitlement` links a beneficiary Decision-Capable Actor to the Public Programme or Measure and owns the durable support right. `Creditor Right` links a creditor Decision-Capable Actor to the debtor Organization and owns recognized payable debt. Holding, Intervention, Proceeding and Instrument references remain scope/basis links. Cash Settlement Transaction remains separate proof of cash.

For predicate 3, Owen accepts `Parcel Consent` and `Parcel Access Authorization` as separate exact types. Consent owns affirmative assent by the competent consenter; Access Authorization owns operative permission to enter or perform specified acts. Consent may ground authorization but never proves it. `Instrument-Grounded Parcel Permission` remains a distinct F3 effect when its normative permission independently survives.

For predicate 6 lineage, Owen accepts separate `Instrument Amendment`, `Instrument Supersession` and `Instrument Stay` types. They own alteration, displacement and temporary suspension respectively. Generic Instrument Lineage is rejected. Annulment, interpretation/construction and termination require selective reopening; a self-contained end date remains an Instrument property.

For predicate 12, Owen accepts `Intervention Responsibility` with controlled management, execution, warranty and aftercare kinds, plus separate `Intervention Inspection Authority` and `Intervention Acceptance Authority`. Responsibility owns affirmative performance/coordination/remedy duties. Inspection owns examination competence. Acceptance owns binding route-specific decision power. Inspection never implies acceptance.

For predicate 16, Owen accepts five exact types: `Lot Allocation`, `Lot Custody`, `Lot Movement Control`, `Lot Warranty Responsibility` and `Lot Return Responsibility`. Possession never implies movement authority; warranty never implies return handling. Plant Material Movement events consume or change these relationships but do not own them.

For predicate 6 Programme effects, Owen accepts separate `Programme Constitution` and `Programme Funding Authority` types. Existence and authorized money have different fact shape and lifecycle. Ordinary openings/windows remain on their Governing Instruments plus a Programme context link unless an independent Programme Operative Window relationship later passes deletion and six-dimensional tests.

This ledger applies one deletion-first test to each semantic relationship:

1. **Delete the relationship.** If nothing decision-relevant is lost, do not create a link.
2. **Try a direct link.** If the relationship is meaningful and owns no independent facts, keep one bidirectional direct link with natural names on both sides.
3. **Try the endpoints.** If basis, effective period, role, powers, duties, scope, quantity, amount, allocation, authority, or history cannot safely belong to either endpoint, the relationship needs one independently owned fact class and is an **OBJECT-BACKED RELATIONSHIP CANDIDATE**.
4. **Classify before promoting.** An occurrence/outcome, observation, derived overlay/computation, endpoint classification, co-reference, or transitive inference is **NOT A LINK**. It may create, evidence, change, or derive a relationship without becoming that relationship.
5. **Atomize before naming.** A slash or “or” in an accepted review label does not authorize a generic family-level object. Create only the exact domain predicate that real evidence instantiates.

Current Palantir guidance permits one object-backed semantic relationship to expose both (a) a direct endpoint-to-endpoint traversal for workflows that only need the connection and (b) detailed traversal through the backing fact object for workflows that need its metadata. These are two views of **one semantic relationship and one fact owner**, not a second direct-link truth. Any “direct view” named below means that platform-supported projection, never an independently stored duplicate link.

## Decision vocabulary

- **DIRECT LINK** — meaningful endpoint connection; no independently owned facts.
- **OBJECT-BACKED RELATIONSHIP CANDIDATE** — deletion loses relationship facts that neither endpoint can own; the named fact class is the sole truth owner.
- **CONDITIONAL** — the accepted review label contains both a factless direct case and an independently fact-bearing case, or depends on the conditionally admitted Individual Plant endpoint. The row states the exact split.
- **NOT A LINK** — event/outcome, observation, derivation, classification, identity/provenance, or transitive shortcut.

“Fact class” below is a semantic candidate for the intermediary relationship object. It does not select properties, physical identity keys, source datasets, cardinality, Actions, Functions, Interfaces, surfaces, or implementation.

## Minimal representation verdict

- **No one of the 16 accepted fact-bearing predicates is safely demoted wholesale to a direct link.** Predicates 8 and 9 are conditional because the authorities explicitly preserve factless participant/subject pointers as direct context; predicate 10 is conditional because Individual Plant identity is conditional.
- The remaining accepted predicate instances require exact, domain-named object-backed relationship candidates when real evidence establishes their independently owned facts.
- F7 Member-Specific Entitlement or Creditor Right is predicate 14 and requires an independently owned right fact class.
- Direct contextual links are admitted only at the factless boundary stated above.
- A platform object-backed relationship may expose concise direct traversal and detailed traversal through its backing object. That is the preferred way to serve both navigational and detail-heavy workflows without duplicating truth.
- Generic family-level objects such as `Actor Assignment`, `Instrument Effect`, `Intervention Scope`, `Material Relationship`, `Proceeding Relationship`, or `Entitlement Relationship` are rejected. The exact predicate and independently recognized grain determine the fact-class noun.
- Evidence-based consolidation remains allowed after that test. The result must still be one coherent domain relationship rather than a family-level container.
