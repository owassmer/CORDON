# Programme / instrument universe — Puglia olive cooperative operator

**Current-state cut:** 23 August 2026. **Scope:** routes that are open, imminently opening, or closed but still producing rankings, concessions, variants, claims, liquidation or payment acts. This is a beneficiary-side recon, not a list of programmes that once existed. It treats an act's existence, funder, current procedural state, and existence of an open money line as four separate claims.

## Executive verdict

A Salento cooperative has four different jobs, not one “Xylella application”: (1) coordinate members already inside the Article 6 or SRD01.01B execution chains; (2) submit the cooperative's own mill, storage, energy and commercialization investments through SRD13 or large-project finance; (3) use recognized-OP status and an OP/AOP programme to fund shared services and investments; and (4) prepare projects now for the open CSR generalist/innovation calls and the next annual/periodic ISMEA windows. The only late-August 2026 routes in this recon with a verified open application line are CSR **SRD01.05**, **SRG01**, **SRD15.2**, and the ISMEA tractor-safety call. **SRE01, SRD01.06 and SRG03 are officially “Prossima apertura.”** Article 6, SRD01.01A/B, SRD13, ISMEA Investe, Più Impresa and Generazione Terra are closed to new applications but have live downstream proceedings.

The central currency finding is negative but actionable: **no act after DDS 120/2026 was located for Article 6** in the live regional measure page, rigenerazione news feed, BURP-indexed PDFs, or exact-phrase searches. The 403 applications admitted by DDS 53/2026 therefore remain at the **delegation/ARIF-instruction boundary**, except for any applications independently reaching a concession act that has not yet been published. DDS 92 and DDS 110 are the 26th and 27th concessions, but both pre-date DDS 120 and neither act says it is the concession outcome of the DDS 53 cohort. No 2026 Article 6 anticipation, saldo, liquidation or payment-order act was located; the latest published liquidation series found is 2025.

The national MASAF €30 million route is **legally designed and funded but not operative for applicants**. MASAF says applicants “potranno presentare domanda secondo i termini e le modalità stabilite dalla Regione Puglia”; the regional portal still contains no avviso. Treat it as announced/imminent, not open.

# Class 3 first — model-memory claims quarantined

These statements were plausible before fetching but are not evidence and are **not used as operative advice**.

1. “The €30 million national Xylella line is open because the ministerial criteria decree exists.” **Rejected.** A criteria decree is not a regional avviso or an open SIAN/EIP window.
2. “DDS 92/2026 or DDS 110/2026 must be concessions from the DDS 53/2026 scorrimento.” **Unproved.** Timing and programme identity are insufficient; no cohort bridge appears in the fetched publication text.
3. “ARIF has published 2025–2026 batch liquidation determinations under DGR 994/2024.” **Not established.** The operative route and application interface are live, but no indexed ARIF liquidation act was retrieved.
4. “All Piano di rigenerazione measures still have applicant-facing money.” **False.** Several are implementation/procurement or legacy compensation lines; others have exhausted or reallocated allocations.
5. “Any agricultural cooperative can use the olive OP programme.” **False.** The beneficiary must be a recognized OP/AOP; ordinary cooperative status alone is not enough.
6. “SRD06 is a general Xylella restoration route.” **Unproved and unsafe.** The visible 2026 implementation is packaged with the cherry-sector SRD01.04 call, not an all-olive Xylella window.
7. “MiniPIA is an agricultural primary-production route.” **Not established.** Puglia Sviluppo instruments can matter to a legally separate transformation/commercial subsidiary, but eligibility by ATECO/activity and State-aid section must be checked before treating them as farm finance.

# Class 2 — bounded inferences

1. **DDS 53 chain state.** Because DDS 120 only reopens the delegation step, and because no later instruction-outcome/concession act was found, the safest state for the DDS 53 cohort is `window closed → delegation completeness reconciliation pending → ARIF technical-administrative instruction not publicly resolved`. This is an inference from the absence of a successor in four live source surfaces, not proof that ARIF has done no internal work.
2. **Article 6 post-DDS-120 result.** The authoritative regional measure page's newest item is dated 29 July 2026 and is DDS 120. Exact searches for August 2026 concessions, istruttoria outcomes and liquidations return no successor. This supports “no published successor located as of cut-off,” not “no successor exists.”
3. **ARIF payment opacity.** DGR 994/2024 delegates grant administration, SIAN State-aid registration and payment to ARIF, while ARIF's public page confirms it is responsible for instruction and liquidation. The failure to retrieve 2025–2026 liquidation acts is therefore a publication/discovery gap at the precise place where beneficiary-level payment truth should be visible.
4. **Cooperative coordination.** Article 6 collective applications and the monumental-olive route use a lead cooperative/OP with member adhesion applications; CSR SRD01.01A/B primarily concede support to the individual applicant, even when cooperative membership improves coordination. OP/AOP programmes are the strongest durable collective route because the recognized organization owns the programme and can delegate interventions to an AOP without double counting.
5. **Piano implementation.** The Piano is not dormant: Article 6 concessions, Article 15 nursery liquidations, Article 18 communication selection, Article 8 ranking and municipal-vector-payment acts are live. But it is a portfolio of uneven administrative chains, not a single open fund.

## 25. Olive OP/AOP sectoral operational programmes (PAC 2023–2027)

Source for recognition universe: [MASAF recognized OP/AOP list](https://www.masaf.gov.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeBLOB.php/L/IT/IDPagina/6063), updated 08-04-2026.

## 26. ISMEA Investe 2026 — subsidized mortgage finance (FAG)

**Status:** 2025 edition window closed 27-02-2026; chronological instruction live. **Applicant:** young- or woman-controlled agricultural SME; takeover or expansion; project up to €1.5m. **Mechanics:** up to 35% grant + up to 60% zero-interest loan under current programme design. **Status:** closed 19-06-2026; applications in instruction (ISMEA reported 256 applications). **Applicant:** under-41 farm entrepreneur or eligible startup. **Mechanics:** ISMEA acquires land and assigns it under reserved-title sale; up to €1.5m (€500k certain startups), two-year pre-amortization, up to 30 years; startup installation relief up to €100k.

## 30. ISMEA ordinary direct/counter/portfolio guarantees

**Status:** standing credit instrument; accessed through bank/confidi rather than a Xylella call. **Applicant:** agricultural/fishery SME. **Mechanics:** direct surety, co-guarantee, counter-guarantee or portfolio guarantee; normal credit merits apply. ## 34. ARIF nursery/plant allocation

**Status:** standing request route; not a verified free commercial-olive programme. The previously reported Restinco resistant-olive ration (up to 100 plants for non-economic actors) was not reverified on a current official call page, so it is not presented as an open commercial farm route. ## 35. Rural microcredit

**Status:** standing, bank-mediated credit; not Xylella aid. **Applicant:** very small agricultural firm or qualifying cooperative. Rural microfinance can serve agricultural microenterprises, including through BCC Terra d'Otranto. Source: [Ente Nazionale Microcredito — Microcredito rurale](https://www.microcredito.gov.it/in-corso/microcredito-rurale.html), which includes agricultural microenterprises and lists Puglia banks.
