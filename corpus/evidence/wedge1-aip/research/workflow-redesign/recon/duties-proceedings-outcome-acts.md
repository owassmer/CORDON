# Puglia Xylella compulsory-duty proceedings and financial outcome-act chains

**Current-state cut:** 23 August 2026 (EDT).  
**Scope:** public acts and proceedings that can change an operator's compulsory duty, plus public 2025–2026 acts that liquidate or order payment of a Xylella-related amount. This is a read-only source recon. It does not infer that a payment reached a bank merely because an act authorizes payment.

## Evidence classes and method

### Class 3 — model-memory hypotheses (shown first; not used as evidence)

The starting hypotheses were: eradication duties normally run from official positive result → demarcation → parcel-level prescription/notification → voluntary or ARIF execution → inspection/enforcement; containment removes only officially infected plants; vector duties run from annual plan → juvenile/adult determina → municipal republication or ordinance → control/sanction; money runs from claim → ARIF instruction → regional concession → regional liquidation/payment authorization → treasury mandate. These hypotheses only shaped searches. No Class 3 statement supports a finding below.

### Class 1 — fetched primary or issuing-authority evidence

Class 1 includes BURP PDFs, Regione Puglia PDFs and news pages, ARIF pages, municipal acts/pages, Consiglio regionale material, and the official national plant-protection portal. Every material fetched fact below carries a URL and a verbatim fragment. Judicial texts were sought first in Giustizia Amministrativa/OpenGA; where the document-discovery interface did not expose a stable judgment URL, the judgment's official metadata/full-text mirror at Doctrine is identified as a secondary access route and treated as Class 2.

### Class 2 — fetched corroboration and local grounding

Class 2 includes municipal mirrors of regional PDFs, court-text aggregators, press reports used only to prove execution, and the two local reference files supplied for this task. The local register is evidence, not a universe. Its high-value rule was followed: recent recitals, especially DET 2/2026 and later acts, are the canonical cross-check for which institution/update act belongs to which area.

### Search discipline and universe limit

There is no prescribed list that proves completeness. I recursively followed recital references and searched by: (1) act number/date and UOR 181 code; (2) operative phrases such as `prescrizione di misure di eradicazione`, `liquidazione e pagamento`, and `domande di pagamento`; (3) every named focolaio/comune; (4) BURP stable-document patterns; (5) municipal mirrors; and (6) judicial case numbers and party/subject terms. A negative is dated and records at least two strategies. Unknown-unknown risk remains for unindexed albi, treasury mandates not exposed as documents, recovery/audit acts, and new proceeding types whose title omits “Xylella”.

## A5. Judicial state in 2026

### Decisions touching operator duties

1. **TAR Bari, Sez. III, judgment 384, 25 Mar 2026, RG 185/2024 — Monopoli monumental olives.** The court treats the later DDS 18/2024 as superseding the original 50 m-wide order, making part of the challenge improcedibile, but rejects the attempt to avoid removal of infected trees in containment. The full-text access route records: “**le misure alternative non sono consentite per le piante infette nelle zone di contenimento**” and says failure to execute the original order contributed to spread. [Doctrine access](https://www.doctrine.it/decisions/ittardoxcze8gn1qcpad) **Duty effect:** the stay/interim history did not mature into a right to keep infected trees; the current infected-tree removal duty stands.
2. **TAR Bari, Sez. III, judgment 383, 25 Mar 2026.** Search-indexed official metadata identifies a challenge to DDS 119/2023 ordering 37 infected plants in Monopoli. [Doctrine access](https://www.doctrine.it/decisions/ittar6yf2wg0326van8) The page was login-walled during extraction, so no dispositive beyond indexed metadata is asserted here.
3. **TAR Bari, Sez. III, judgment 387, 25 Mar 2026, RG 1238/2023.** Search indexing places it in the same Xylella/monumental-olive litigation family. [Doctrine access](https://www.doctrine.it/decisions/ittarxpsiid2uwamlbf) Full extraction was login-walled, so this report does not overstate its holding.
4. **TAR Lecce, Sez. III, judgments 459 (20 Mar), 496 and 497 (31 Mar 2026).** These are damages claims alleging late/inadequate public containment, not stays of current operator orders. No. 459 says the administrations acted “**tempestivamente e adeguatamente**” and rejects damages; [459 text](https://www.doctrine.it/decisions/ittargg6p0w94bvp1c6). No. 496 follows TAR Lecce 1439/2025 and says the claim is “**manifestamente infondato e va respinto**”; [496 text](https://www.doctrine.it/decisions/ittarn8qwfw77i3o3fb7). No. 497 likewise rejects the alleged late/inadequate activation; [497 text](https://www.doctrine.it/decisions/ittar35ps8avzoqcvd). **Duty effect:** none stays a 2026 eradication/vector duty; they strengthen the pro-administration baseline.
5. Additional 2026 Doctrine-indexed Xylella results include TAR Lecce 501 (31 Mar) and 641 (27 Apr), but extraction was login-walled. They are logged as unresolved, not silently omitted.

## Bottom-line verdict

The **duty side is presently richest in the ST1 fastidiosa belt and Bitonto/Modugno**, where 2026 acts expose not just legal geography but owner-facing prescription, notification, ARIF execution choice, protected-host logic, 400 m preventive removal and indemnity/no-indemnity classification. Across the other foci, the institution and 2025 prescription acts remain public, but several 2026 execution/successor edges are visible only as reports or are not locatable. DDS 39→79 is the current juvenile-vector chain; no 2026 adult-treatment determina was located.

The **outcome side has strong 2025 regional liquidation/payment acts but no located 2026 liquidation**. DDS 167/2025 is the best authenticated external-outcome demonstration because its public chain is almost end-to-end and has a named public beneficiary. DDS 216/2025 is the best single private-claim example. Article 6 acts provide rich, repeatable batch events, but public reconstruction stops short of raw claims and treasury mandate/settlement. DEL 994/2024 does not yet yield a publicly reconstructible concrete ARIF indemnity liquidation chain in 2025–2026.
