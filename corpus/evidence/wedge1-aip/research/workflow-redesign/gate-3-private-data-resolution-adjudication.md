## Executive verdict

The brief preserves the 13-noun verdict and correctly expands the evidence considered by Gate 3 Rule 6.

Its central correction is accepted:

> A noun’s real grain can be established by a real authority, actor, physical process or operative instrument available through public, operational, judicial, procurement, scientific or permissionless observation. It does not always require a private cooperative record or outreach.

This changes the disposition of the earlier private-evidence blockers. Public and permissionless evidence can establish the noun classes and general grain of mandates, contracts, guarantees, work orders and other operative instruments. Actual private terms, current commitments, responsibility assignments, live availability, settlement and member-specific aftercare remain unresolved facts or relationships. They no longer block the 13-noun set.

The brief does not change the accepted operator model and does not justify additional nouns.

## Six prior private-gap adjudications

### 1. Cooperative mandate and cohort mechanics

**Merit:** Public Article 6 rules, cooperative/OP statutes, actual and model collective agreements, recognition rules, concession acts and disputes establish that mandates and governed agreements are real operative instruments with independent identity, parties, scope, duration, amendment and termination concepts.

**Gate 3 consequence:** Private Agreement does not need a separate noun. `Governing Instrument` can cover a real mandate or cooperative agreement.

**Remaining gap:** No public analogue proves the actual current member mandate, selection deliberation, service price, cost allocation, withdrawal/default terms or current cohort for a target cooperative. Those remain Gate 4 relationship and later instance facts.

### 2. CAA case handling

**Merit:** The existing CAA research already uses 30 substantive public artifacts. They establish mandate validation, farm-file repair, release, protocol, anomaly handling, evidence custody, correction, control and follow-up.

**Gate 3 consequence:** Public Proceeding, Agricultural Holding and Governing Instrument grains are supported. A CAA Dossier noun remains unnecessary.

**Remaining gap:** Local assignment, communications, worklist, cycle-time, error distribution and undocumented workaround are not established. They affect operations and surface design later, not the noun set.

### 3. Contracting and scarce capacity

**Merit:** Public procurement, specifications, awards, CIG/CUP records, RUOP/operator registers, catalogues and public prices establish contracts, work scopes, qualifications, delivery windows, penalties and resource categories.

**Gate 3 consequence:** Governing Instrument, Intervention, Organization and Plant Trade Unit / Lot grains are supported. Capacity Reservation remains a relationship/event, not a noun.

**Remaining gap:** Public contract value or maximum envelope does not prove current usable capacity. Live stock, calendars, reservations, management bandwidth, finance and allocation overrides remain unknown unless currently observed.

### 4. Field acceptance and defects

**Merit:** Public verbali, collaudo and regular-execution structures, technical-control acts, specifications, passport rules, warranties, penalties and disputes establish the distinction among execution, official acceptance, public funded-work acceptance, private acceptance, defects and cure.

**Gate 3 consequence:** Intervention, Governing Instrument and Public Proceeding grains are supported. Acceptance and defect remain events/outcomes or Gate 4 relationships.

**Remaining gap:** Public-work clauses are analogues, not proof of private cooperative contracting terms or an actual member defect outcome.

### 5. Bank settlement and payment repair

**Merit:** The existing payment deep dive already reconstructs a real invalid-IBAN claim through technical admission, exclusion, PEC correction, repeated clearances, liquidation and payment order. Public guarantee forms and recovery rules establish guarantees as operative instruments.

**Gate 3 consequence:** Public Proceeding and Governing Instrument cover claims, guarantees and public decisions. Settlement is an event/outcome, not another noun.

**Remaining gap:** Payment order still does not prove bank credit. Final settlement remains unknown without treasury, bank, beneficiary or equivalent authoritative confirmation.

### 6. Aftercare and survival

**Merit:** Scientific cohorts, trials, planting records, imagery, orthophotos and crown-level data establish real biological observations at supported plant, land or cohort grains. They can test biological change without synthetic records.

**Gate 3 consequence:** Individual Plant and Intervention remain the necessary nouns. Survival, defect, replacement and cure remain observations/events/outcomes. No Survival Cohort noun is justified.

**Remaining gap:** Remote sensing cannot establish official plant identity, causal defect, contractual responsibility, member-specific maintenance or a completed cure unless the supported grain and authority permit it.

## Corrected Rule 6

A noun candidate passes evidence clearance when real public, operational, judicial, procurement, scientific, permissionless or directly supplied evidence establishes that the real authority, actor, physical process or operative instrument distinguishes and preserves the instance.

Evidence sufficiency is grain-specific:

- a public or permissionless source can establish a noun class and general identity grain;
- a real public instance can establish that instance;
- an analogue can establish possible structure but not a target actor’s facts;
- an observation establishes only the grain it can actually resolve;
- an unknown private fact remains unknown; and
- no source row, template, proxy or analytical grouping creates a noun by itself.

## Gate 3 impact

1. Keep the 13 candidates.
2. Accept Official Area as an authority-defined noun when its identity is preserved by the real authority/process.
3. Replace `Public Legal or Administrative Instrument` with candidate `Governing Instrument`, bounded to real operative instances.
4. Replace `Plant Lot` with candidate `Plant Trade Unit / Lot`, using actual governing terminology per instance.
5. Remove Private Agreement, Work Order, Financing/Guarantee Arrangement and Aftercare Protocol from the blocked-noun list; they are possible Governing Instrument instances, not separate nouns.
6. Keep Capacity Reservation, Execution Cohort, CAA Dossier and Survival Cohort rejected.
7. Move direct private evidence from a blanket Gate 3 blocker to a grain-specific requirement. It remains mandatory before asserting current private instances or accepting later relationships that depend on actual private terms.
8. Do not add provenance classes, demo architecture, source schemas, notional records or relationship design.

## Bottom line

The analyst’s strongest contribution is not a new dataset or noun. It is a correction to evidence sufficiency:

> Permissionless real-world evidence can establish noun grain even when it cannot establish a target cooperative’s current private facts.

This lets Gate 3 remain ambitious and real without narrowing CORDON around unavailable private records. It does not permit analogues, proxies or templates to masquerade as current operator truth.
