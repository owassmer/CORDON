# Institutional unknown unknowns in Puglia Xylella recovery and collective operations

Status: workflow evidence. The named objects in “Workflow redesign impact” are unaccepted candidate terminology, not an approved product or Ontology model.

## Scope and method

This is an actor-and-proceeding-first sweep. It does not inventory datasets or restate a Land/Funding/Applications/Field Work/Payments flow. It asks which institution can create, block, reverse, split, or reopen an operator decision, and what evidence moves the matter between institutions.

The findings distinguish three levels:

- **Proved for a named Xylella proceeding.** The cited act supplies the actor, trigger, state, evidence, or consequence.
- **Conditionally relevant.** The branch affects a cooperative only when it performs the stated role, such as importing plants, operating as a recognised OP, or using a high-density irrigated design.
- **Rejected or bounded.** The search did not establish a decision right or an operator-facing route. These branches must not be turned into product rules without new primary evidence.

The 2020 Article 6 replant notice and its later implementation acts are treated as one demonstrated proceeding, not as universal terms for every later Xylella measure. Thresholds, competent offices, deadlines, aid regimes, and permitted cultivars must remain act-versioned.

## Novel findings

### 1. Delegation creates a two-level decision chain, not an interchangeable “regional authority”

The Observatory is the competent plant-health authority. ARIF is an **organismo delegato** for monitoring, prevention, removal, communications, and aid instruction. The Observatory must define procedures and priorities, designate laboratories, run second-level checks on surveillance and removal, and can audit ARIF or withdraw all or part of the delegation if tasks are not performed adequately or deficiencies are not corrected promptly.[S2]

The same arrangement gives Carabinieri Forestali a separate control role for plant movement and mandatory vector-control measures. ARIF may use qualified economic operators for forced removals under procurement law, while remaining responsible for the delegated execution and reporting.[S2]

**Operator relevance proved.** A cooperative must not send every objection or evidence package to ARIF merely because ARIF contacted it. The legal measure, its reconsideration, and delegation oversight belong to the Observatory; operational scheduling and indemnity instruction may belong to ARIF. A contractor is not a new decision authority.

**New states and handoffs:**

`official_result → Observatory_measure → ARIF_execution_order → scheduled/voluntary/forced_execution → Observatory_second_level_check → delegated_activity_report`

Add `delegation_active | deficient | corrective_action | partially_withdrawn | withdrawn`. If delegation changes, open operational tasks must be reassigned without changing the underlying legal measure.

### 2. Publication, not inbox receipt, can start the destruction clock

A 2024 containment order provides a concrete clock: seven consecutive days at the municipal albo; PEC to a proprietor/holder if available; a choice between voluntary removal and ARIF during publication and no later than three days after it ends; voluntary or ARIF removal within ten days; silence routes directly to ARIF removal.[S3] A TAR Puglia decision held that failure to notify a holder personally did not itself invalidate the order because municipal albo publication was sufficient under the special Xylella rule.[S4]

The order is immediately enforceable and separately states that a judicial administrative appeal or extraordinary appeal may be brought within the legal time. It does not say that an appeal pauses execution.[S3]

**Operator relevance proved.** A cooperative managing member parcels needs a legal-notice watcher keyed to each municipality and cadastral parcel, not only a PEC inbox. It also needs an explicit `stay_granted` fact. `appeal_filed` must never imply `measure_suspended`.

**Failure modes:** stale PEC in the farm file; notice published against a cadastral owner rather than the practical holder; member assumes no personal letter means no clock; cooperative files an appeal but does not obtain interim relief; silence is mistaken for “pending choice.”

### 3. A guaranteed laboratory second opinion is not proved for Xylella eradication sampling

Regulation (EU) 2017/625 grants an operator a second expert opinion for sampling, testing, or diagnosis **in the context of official controls**.[S1] The same Regulation defines plant-pest detection, prevention, and containment as “other official activities.” Its Article 1(5) lists provisions extended to other official activities and does not include Article 35, the second-opinion article.[S1] Puglia’s delegation act expressly classifies Xylella presence investigations as other official activities.[S2]

**Candidate rejected:** “positive official Xylella test → automatic Article 35 counter-analysis right.” The legal chain found does not support that rule.

**Operator relevance retained.** The operator can seek document access, request reconsideration, and challenge the underlying report and measure through administrative or judicial routes. A 2023 TAR claim specifically sought a new examination, showing that re-test access can itself become disputed litigation, but the accessible copy was paywalled before the court’s reasoning and outcome.[S30]

**Workflow impact:** store `official_sample`, `chain_of_custody`, `laboratory_report`, `test_method`, `access_request`, `retest_requested`, and `retest_authorised`. Do not expose `counteranalysis_entitlement=true` without a measure-specific legal basis.

### 4. Third-party obstruction is a first-class evidentiary event

The removal order distinguishes operator refusal from obstruction by third parties. Third-party obstruction must be documented and recorded under the self-declaration rules; the inspector or ARIF can then request Prefect-supported force. Refusal or failure to start can lead to forced removal, cost charging, an administrative sanction, referral to the prosecutor under criminal-law provisions, and loss of any removal contribution.[S3]

The supervising inspector’s removal/destruction record is described as **propedeutic and indispensable** for recognition of the contribution.[S3]

**Operator relevance proved.** A collective operation can be compliant even when a co-owner, occupier, neighbour, or other person blocks access, but only if the obstruction is captured through the official handoff. A generic contractor note is not equivalent to the inspector/ARIF record.

**New states:** `access_clear | obstructed_by_operator | obstructed_by_third_party | force_requested | force_scheduled | completed`. Evidence includes identity of obstructer, date/time, photos where lawful, declarations, inspector record, Prefect request, and final verbale.

### 5. A collective application does not consolidate beneficiary identity or financial risk

In the demonstrated Article 6 notice, a recognised olive OP or an oil-processing agricultural cooperative may file the collective application. Every participating member must independently hold the substantive eligibility conditions. Ineligible members are removed without necessarily invalidating the rest of the collective application.[S5]

The cooperative or OP needs an approval declaration from participating members, delegation to its legal representative, and a mandate to perform removal and replanting. The FAQ clarifies that the members, not the cooperative, remain beneficiaries; the collective body may select only some valid member adhesions; unselected or incorrectly linked adhesions fall back to individual applications.[S5][S6]

At final balance, the member, technical director, and collective body’s legal representative must sign the completion declaration, and the mandate evidence must be supplied. Missing mandate evidence causes loss and revocation.[S5][S7]

**Operator relevance proved.** Model three identities, not one: `collective_applicant`, `member_beneficiary`, and `parcel_right_holder`. Add `member_selected`, `adhesion_link_valid`, `mandate_scope`, `mandate_effective_from/to`, and `legal_representative_authority`. The cooperative cannot safely pool member aid ceilings, guarantees, land rights, or payment clearances.

### 6. Collective authority can fail through OP recognition or cooperative supervision

Current MASAF guidance requires regional checks of an olive OP’s legal form, democratic control, member base in SIAN, non-duplicate membership, minimum adherence periods, assembly decisions, production value, and other recognition conditions.[S12] Puglia actually suspended an olive OP in December 2024 because its member base did not meet minimum requirements. The OP had ten days to submit counterarguments, did not do so, and was suspended pending corrective action and a later maintenance check; failure to cure exposes it to revocation, and the cited national rule allows suspension for up to twelve months.[S13]

A separate MIMIT regime reviews all cooperatives at least biennially, tests administrative management, internal democracy, mutual character, and entitlement to fiscal and other benefits, and may escalate through extraordinary inspection, government management, dissolution, liquidator substitution, or compulsory administrative liquidation. Interested members and other authorities can trigger extraordinary inspection through reports.[S11][S29]

**Operator relevance proved, with a legal gap.** Loss of OP recognition plainly changes an entity’s recognised-OP status. Cooperative supervision can change who may act and whether the body still exists. The sources do not settle whether a suspension automatically invalidates every already-filed Xylella collective application; that consequence needs the specific aid act and date.

**Workflow impact:** before each collective submission, variant, final balance, and payment, snapshot `legal_form`, `CCIAA_status`, `OP_recognition_status`, `OP_suspension_start`, `corrective_action_due`, `cooperative_revision_status`, `legal_representative`, and `signing_power`. Route status changes to a legal review instead of silently updating the applicant name.

### 7. Death creates two different succession proceedings depending on grant stage

For **pre-concession** death, Puglia created a dedicated process. An heir may enter individually with authority from the other heirs, or through a company formed by them. The successor must possess the original eligibility conditions, acquire lawful conduct of the entire prior holding, assume the original project, have no enforceable public debts, and—where the deceased filed an adhesion—be in the relevant producer body’s member register.[S8]

The deadline is event-dependent: sixty days from portal activation for an older eligible position, from publication of a later ranking scroll, or from death when death follows the scroll. The application uses the EIP/PMA route; portal access itself requires a helpdesk request identifying the deceased CUAA and successor details.[S8][S9]

For **post-concession** change, DDS 44 treats whole-farm transfer, death, and prolonged professional incapacity as a beneficiary variant. The successor assumes all commitments, must take the whole funded holding, preserve eligibility, and keep the project above the funding cut line. The usual ban on variants in the last three months does not apply to a death-based beneficiary change.[S7]

**Operator relevance proved.** `death` is not a generic “change beneficiary” reason. First branch on `concession_effective_at_death`. Track heirs’ assent, whole-holding transfer, successor membership, debts, project assumption, event-specific deadline, and portal enablement.

### 8. Title is a consent graph, not a single land-availability flag

The bando requires a valid title and a current validated farm file. The FAQ adds that a non-owner must obtain the lawful owner’s authorisation for both removal and replanting.[S5][S6] DDS 44 is more granular: an affittuario or comodatario needs owner/co-owner authority; an usufructuary needs the naked owner’s authority; community property can require the spouse’s authorisation. A successor must provide its possession title, and a beneficiary change must cover the entire funded holding.[S7]

**Operator relevance proved.** A collective dossier can pass parcel/title intake but later fail at variant or balance because the correct consenting person was never captured.

**Workflow impact:** replace `has_land_title` with `right_type`, `right_holder`, `owner`, `co_owner_set`, `naked_owner`, `spousal_property_regime`, `consent_required`, `consent_received`, `consent_scope`, `consent_expiry`, and `whole_holding_transfer_confirmed`.

### 9. A grant is not a blanket environmental or plant-health permission

The Article 6 notice requires the necessary environmental, landscape, and hydrogeological permissions at application where they apply. Its landscape shortcut is narrow: resistant/tolerant olive replanting in qualifying infected, non-containment areas can be treated as an ordinary agricultural practice only if the rural landscape features are preserved; otherwise the ordinary Article 146 landscape procedure applies.[S5]

The Constitutional Court invalidated a broader Puglia provision that purported to permit any tree species notwithstanding landscape constraints. The Court emphasised that the national legislature controls landscape-authorisation exemptions and that the emergency cutting derogation did not create a general planting derogation.[S26]

DDS 44 adds a PAI/river-basin compatibility declaration for hydrogeologically constrained parcels. A move to more than 300 plants/ha requires proof of an authorised well abstraction or access to consortium irrigation.[S7]

The general Puglia monumental-olive page requires a territorial-service application and a technical-commission opinion for ordinary exceptional felling.[S27] However, emergency plant-health law and the Xylella order expressly cover official destruction, including monumental trees. The general monument page is therefore **not proved as a second permit gate for an official eradication order**; it remains relevant for voluntary, non-prescribed felling or relocation.

**Workflow impact:** attach a versioned `PermitBundle` to the proposed operation. Each permit has `authority`, `trigger`, `exemption_basis`, `application_date`, `decision`, and `conditions`. Do not infer permits from grant approval or parcel zone alone.

### 10. Some design changes are pre-approved variants; others are post-hoc adaptations that can still fail at balance

DDS 44 permits only two formal variants: beneficiary change and relocation that produces density above 300 plants/ha. Formal variants use the PMA route, are normally barred during the last three months before completion, and are inadmissible if submitted another way. An inadmissible variant does not free the beneficiary from the original project; non-performance can trigger total revocation and recovery.[S7]

A relocation below 300 plants/ha is an adaptation, not a variant. It needs no prior approval, but the beneficiary, technician, and—in a collective case—the legal representative must justify it at final balance. The balance instructor can reject it after implementation, reduce aid for the affected parcels, and must allow counterarguments to a negative result.[S7]

**Operator relevance proved.** The system must classify change requests before work begins: `variant_prior_approval`, `adaptation_post_hoc_review`, or `not_permitted`. “No prior approval required” does not mean “approved.”

### 11. Nursery authorisation and plant movement can reverse a procurement decision

Puglia authorises planting only for specified species/varieties and zone regimes. Plants must carry conforming passports issued by authorised professional operators. For Article 23 movements, the production site must belong to a RUOP operator, pass official annual visual inspection, sampling, and tests, apply vector treatments, and obtain a signed recipient declaration that plants will not leave the allowed zones.[S14]

A failed annual inspection revokes the site authorisation; a law or demarcated-area status change can make it lapse. Operators must request official analyses before sale, retain sent/received lot information for three years, and register cadastral destination information for Leccino and FS-17 in infected zones.[S14] A 2024 Puglia measure also prohibited movement for specified plants across several new and overlapping demarcated areas and assigned movement control to Carabinieri Forestali.[S15]

At EU level, authorised-site movement can be suspended immediately after detection or physical-protection failure. Member States must investigate infection origin and trace host plants moved before an area was demarcated, notifying affected Member States.[S16]

**Operator relevance proved.** A valid purchase order is not a final clearance. Add `supplier_RUOP_status`, `site_authorisation`, `annual_inspection_date/result`, `passport_code`, `lot`, `species/subspecies_scope`, `origin_zone`, `destination_zone`, `recipient_undertaking`, `destination_cadastral_record`, and `movement_clearance_as_of`. Re-evaluate at dispatch and receipt, not only at order date.

### 12. Cross-border plant controls matter only when the cooperative imports or buys through an importer

Regulated plant imports must enter through an authorised border control post. The importer pre-notifies the consignment in TRACES-NT, and regional plant-health inspectors conduct documentary, identity, and physical checks.[S17]

**Conditional relevance proved.** A cooperative importing rootstock, nursery material, or other regulated plants can be blocked before customs release and must connect the TRACES consignment to domestic passport and lot evidence. The branch is rejected for ordinary purchases from a domestic authorised nursery; adding TRACES to every replant dossier would be noise.

### 13. The advance depends on each member’s external guarantor, not the collective body’s pooled promise

The Article 6 proceeding permits a 50% advance only with a bank guarantee or insurance bond for 110% of the advance. Release occurs one year after final inspection.[S5] The FAQ makes the split explicit: because each adherent is the beneficiary, each individual must issue the guarantee even in a collective application.[S6]

**Operator relevance proved.** The bank or authorised insurer has an independent credit/underwriting decision right. A cooperative can have an eligible collective project but fail to mobilise it because one or more members cannot obtain guarantees. Track `advance_requested`, `guarantor`, `underwriting_status`, `guarantee_amount`, `original_received`, `expiry`, `release_eligible_at`, and `released` per member.

ISMEA offers general agricultural-PMI guarantees through a bank or confidi after credit assessment, including fideiussione, co-guarantee, and counter-guarantee.[S25] **Rejected as a dedicated Xylella route:** no live Xylella-specific guarantee or rule making ISMEA automatically acceptable for the Article 6 advance was found.

### 14. Payment clearance is a separate state after technical approval

AGEA instructions for a Xylella measure show distinct payment controls: aid-register/de minimis checks, possible linear reduction, DURC, Article 48-bis public-debt verification, antimafia documentation, and beneficiary-owned IBAN.[S18] A public payment over €5,000 is checked for qualifying tax-collection defaults; if found, payment is not made and the case is referred to the collection agent.[S21]

Puglia later clarified for PSR proceedings that antimafia is checked at the payment request—advance, instalment, or balance—not at the support application, even where older calls said otherwise.[S19]

**Operator relevance proved.** `technically_eligible` and `grant_conceded` do not imply `payable`. Add independent snapshots: `DURC`, `tax_debt_48bis`, `antimafia`, `aid_register`, `IBAN_owner_match`, each with checked-at date, authority, result, expiry, cure route, and payment effect.

### 15. Aid-law expiry or regime change can strand and later reopen otherwise eligible positions

In the Xylella 2018 compensation proceeding, Puglia recorded that late DURC regularisation left 62 otherwise suitable firms unpaid after the relevant aid window. ARIF used Article 10-bis pre-rejection PECs; some firms cured, but official DURC confirmation came too late. Puglia then asked MASAF to move the still-ungranted positions from block-exempt state aid SA.56359 to agricultural de minimis.[S20]

**Operator relevance proved at the “reopening request” level.** Aid-law regime and availability period are not static metadata. A compliance cure may arrive after the legal payment window, and the administration may need a separate regime-transition decision. The source proves the request, not that MASAF approved it or that all 62 positions were paid.

**Workflow impact:** model `aid_regime_proposed`, `aid_regime_authorised`, `legal_availability_end`, `registration_status`, `cumulation_headroom_as_of`, `transition_requested`, `transition_decided`, and `payment_reopened`. Never hard-code one de minimis ceiling across measures or dates.

### 16. Post-payment recovery is a new proceeding with a potentially different court

The Article 6 bando allows ex-post revocation for lost requirements, false declarations, irregularities, refusal of control, police or Guardia di Finanza findings, and voluntary renunciation. Recovery includes interest and a 30-day repayment demand.[S5] DDS 44 confirms that administrative and in-loco checks may occur at any time and can recover previously paid sums.[S7]

The Court of Cassation states that disputes over mandatory agricultural-contribution revocation/recovery and linear reductions fall to the ordinary judge where the authority has no discretion and merely verifies a statutory condition; reduction can occur even after payment.[S22]

**Operator relevance proved.** Do not end the lifecycle at `paid`. Add `audit_open`, `finding`, `pre_recovery_notice`, `revocation`, `recovery_claim`, `interest`, `repayment_due`, `jurisdiction_basis`, `appeal_or_defence`, and `closed`. Determine remedy route from the power exercised, not from the fact that AGEA or a Region issued the letter.

### 17. Accounting status changes eligible cost and evidence even where it does not change eligibility

For Article 6, VAT is eligible only when it is not recoverable under national law.[S5] DDS 44 requires fiscally regular and paid invoices, supplier releases, traceable payments, and the project CUP on every supporting document to prevent double funding.[S7]

**Operator relevance proved.** A cooperative and its member may have different VAT positions; the beneficiary’s recoverability controls its eligible cost. `gross_invoice_amount` is not automatically eligible. Store `VAT_recoverable`, accountant evidence, CUP presence, payment trace, supplier release, and corrected-document status.

**Rejected/bounded branch:** no primary, Xylella-specific tax ruling was found on whether a particular removal indemnity, capital contribution, or income-loss compensation is taxable to each legal form. Do not infer tax treatment from the aid label; route it to an accountant against the specific act and beneficiary regime.

### 18. Annual local vector-control obligations are a sibling compliance proceeding

The 2025 Observatory measure required specified soil operations by a fixed date across listed municipalities. It applied to agricultural land, uncultivated land, public and private green areas, roadsides, canals, and public land. The Observatory used Carabinieri Forestali and aerial surveys for compliance checks.[S28]

A 2025 Prefecture table on the Bisceglie outbreak brought together the Prefect, Province, municipalities, Carabinieri Forestali, Observatory, ARIF, and agricultural associations. Carabinieri committed to checks on tillage and prohibited plant movement.[S29]

**Operator relevance proved.** A cooperative can be fully compliant with a grant project yet separately violate annual vector-control duties. Keep `campaign_obligation` distinct from a parcel’s grant and removal cases. The legal act, not a press release or association message, controls the deadline; communications are discovery and escalation channels.

### 19. Insurance is not one Xylella recovery workflow

AgriCAT is limited to catastrophic weather damage from flood, frost/freeze, and drought; it is not a Xylella compensation route.[S23] The 2024 agricultural risk plan allows listed plant diseases and pest infestations to be added to qualifying weather policies or mutual coverage, subject to technical disease-management practices, but the searched official material did not name Xylella as a standalone product or prove that an insurer currently offers it to Puglia olive cooperatives.[S24]

**Rejected:** “Xylella-positive or Xylella-loss event → AgriCAT claim.”

**Bounded conditional branch:** a private/subsidised policy might use a generic disease category, but the policy wording, crop, municipality, inception date, exclusion, disease-management obligations, and loss trigger must be inspected. Insurance also matters as a cumulation input because the Article 6 grant prevents over-compensation.[S5]

## Workflow redesign impact

### Add proceeding objects

1. **OfficialMeasureCase** — authority, legal basis, target plant/parcel, regime, immediate-execution flag, notice events, response clock, chosen execution route, inspection record, sanctions, costs, contribution consequence.
2. **DelegationAssignment** — competent authority, delegated body, task scope, effective dates, audit status, corrective action, withdrawal, replacement assignee.
3. **NoticeEvent** — publication venue, start/end, legal effect, PEC event, recipient basis, clock derived from the controlling act.
4. **RemedyCase** — access request, reconsideration, pre-rejection observations, administrative appeal, extraordinary appeal, court, interim relief, final decision. Keep `filed`, `admissible`, `stay_granted`, and `decided` separate.
5. **ObstructionEvent** — actor, operator/third-party classification, declaration, official record, Prefect request, police support, execution outcome.
6. **InspectionAndVerbale** — inspector authority, operation observed, findings, document identifier, contribution-gate status.
7. **CollectiveMandate** — collective body, member, scope, project, dates, member/assembly approval, legal representative, final-balance ratification, termination event.
8. **InstitutionalStatus** — OP recognition, OP suspension/cure/revocation, cooperative revision, extraordinary inspection, commissioner, dissolution/liquidation, signing authority.
9. **SuccessionOrTransferCase** — pre/post-concession branch, event date, ranking-scroll date, deadline, heirs, consents, whole-holding transfer, membership, debts, commitments assumed.
10. **PermitBundle** — landscape, PAI/hydrogeological, water abstraction/consortium, title-holder consent, any monument procedure; include exemption basis and conditions.
11. **PlantLotMovement** — supplier RUOP, authorised site, inspection/tests, passport, lot, origin/destination regimes, recipient undertaking, cadastral destination, movement status at dispatch/receipt.
12. **FinancialClearanceSnapshot** — DURC, antimafia, Article 48-bis, aid register/cumulation, IBAN owner, guarantor; each as dated decisions with cure/recheck rules.
13. **AidRegimeCase** — legal regime, availability window, registration, de minimis/cumulation calculation, transition request/decision, linear reduction, payment effect.
14. **RecoveryCase** — audit source, finding, revocation/recovery power, interest, deadline, court/jurisdiction, repayment state.
15. **CampaignObligation** — annual regional/local vector-control act, geography, obligated actor class, task window, inspection method, evidence, violation state.

### Add decision safeguards

- Never derive a response deadline from document date alone. Use the legally effective notice event.
- Never derive suspension from the existence of an appeal.
- Never treat ARIF, an ARIF contractor, and the Observatory as the same authority.
- Never treat a collective applicant as the payee or beneficiary unless the act says so.
- Re-check OP/cooperative authority at submission, variant, balance, and payment.
- Branch succession on whether concession was effective at death/transfer.
- Treat all consents as scoped, signed, dated, and person-specific.
- Re-check plant-lot movement at order, dispatch, and receipt against current zone and site status.
- Treat technical approval, concession, payment clearance, and recovery exposure as different states.
- Require the official inspection verbale where the act makes it a contribution condition.
- Keep press releases and association communications as alerts; derive legal duties from the underlying act.
