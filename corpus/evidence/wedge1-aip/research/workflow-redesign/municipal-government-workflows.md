# Municipal government workflows in Puglia Xylella control and recovery

**Status:** independent workflow research; no product, interface, ontology, Foundry, AIP, or Challenge proposal  
**Research date:** 21 August 2026  
**Scope:** comuni (municipalities), municipal offices and police, and their handoffs to Regione Puglia, the Sezione Osservatorio Fitosanitario, ARIF, Prefetture, police forces, Provinces/Città Metropolitana, consorzi and applicants. The analysis covers eradication foci, the containment/buffer geography, and older infected territory.  
**Primary-source count registered:** 29 official legal, regional, municipal, Prefecture, ARIF and civil-protection URLs. Twenty-eight bodies or files were extracted or directly downloaded and validated. Ruffano's official municipal page was retrieved only through Exa's indexed body after Crawl4AI and direct HTTP failed; it remains explicitly provisional.

## Evidence labels

- **[F — fetched primary/official fact]** means this session extracted an official page or downloaded, MIME-validated and read the signed/official PDF.
- **[I — inference]** means a bounded workflow conclusion derived from cited facts. It is not represented as law.
- **[U — unverified or incomplete]** means search-indexed official material, an official communication without its underlying act, or an acquisition gap.
- **Provenance rule:** sentences that state external legal or administrative facts carry numbered citations. Uncited consequences, handoff designs, failure analyses and recommended minimum evidence in the matrices and detailed workflows are **[I] workflow synthesis** unless expressly marked [U]. The source register separately identifies the one search-indexed case and the official communication whose underlying act is missing.
- **Comune decision** means the municipality can issue, approve, refuse, contract, execute, sanction under its own act, or otherwise determine the stated local outcome.
- **Comune support** means the municipality publishes, communicates, inspects, records, routes or assists without owning the underlying phytosanitary, grant or payment decision.

## Bottom line

A Comune is not a miniature phytosanitary authority.

The Sezione Osservatorio Fitosanitario creates official findings, zones, eradication/containment prescriptions and national phytosanitary sanctions.[2][3]

ARIF performs delegated monitoring, removal and indemnity instruction.[13][14]

The Prefect can arrange force-public support when the competent phytosanitary service asks.[2][10][12]

Municipalities matter because they can make a regional order legally operative against hard-to-notify landholders, own and execute work on public assets, create local ordinances and enforcement routes, contract or delegate territorial work, decide certain delegated landscape or local-planning proceedings, and create separate local recovery programmes.[5][7][18]

The most consequential municipal act may be only seven days long. In current eradication and containment orders, the Region transmits the act to the competent Comune for seven natural consecutive days of albo pretorio publication. After the seventh day the posting assumes notification effect. That publication anchors the owner-choice and removal clocks even when personal PEC is absent.[10][12] A general municipal news item is not a substitute for the legally specified albo event.

Municipal boundaries do not allocate every road, verge, canal or public parcel to the Comune. The 2026 regional annual-vector act binds the **owner/manager**, public or private, of uncultivated agricultural surfaces, public green, road edges, canals and demanial surfaces. A Comune is responsible only where it owns or manages the asset. Province/Città Metropolitana, a reclamation or irrigation consortium, the State/Region, an ASI consortium or a concessionaire may instead be the responsible body.[5][16]

Municipal variance is real:

- Bitonto and Giovinazzo issued mayoral ordinances that add a local enforcement basis, municipal-police routing and local residue instructions.[7][8]
- Modugno's official pages examined here mainly republish or explain regional acts.[11][23]
- Monopoli and Mesagne do the same in the fetched examples.[20][21] That evidence does not establish a separate local ordinance.
- Ruffano's indexed 2026 page starts a municipal market-exploration/manifestation-of-interest process for work on municipal land, potentially using the agricultural-entrepreneur convention route. The signed act and final award were not acquired.[17]
- Ostuni opened a separate municipal free-plant application for non-professional owners/occupiers. Brindisi announced a protocol and intended municipal call for a similar excluded population. Neither municipal initiative changes eligibility or payment under the regional Article 6 measure.[18][19]

### Rejected search branches at discovery

- Generic web searches for `site:comune.*.it` do not enumerate albi hosted on OpenWeb, Halley or Municipium subdomains.
- Current albo interfaces often expose only active/current records. Their search failure cannot prove that an act was never posted.
- Municipal news pages can mirror regional PDFs but do not prove a local ordinance, a publication certificate, personal contact or compliance.
- Press reports about municipal spending do not prove commitment, procurement award, work completion, liquidation or payment.
- A single Comune's ordinance cannot be generalized to all Puglia municipalities or to another campaign year.

## Detailed workflow 1 — legally effective municipal publication

### Trigger

The Osservatorio transmits a parcel/tree-specific removal act to the Comune in which the affected cadastral land lies.[10][12]

### Actor and decision split

1. **Osservatorio — decision:** issues the immediately executable phytosanitary prescription and selects the publication method.
2. **Comune — procedural execution:** posts the complete act for the exact duration.
3. **Albo system/officer — evidence:** records publication number, start, end and retained copy.
4. **Owner/holder — response:** chooses voluntary removal or ARIF route under the order.
5. **ARIF/Osservatorio — operational handoff:** executes/supervises and records the route.

### Evidence and clock

Modugno DDS 7/2026 orders urgent seven-natural-consecutive-day posting. After the seventh day the posting assumes notification effect. The owner communicates during posting or within three days after the end; voluntary or ARIF removal then carries a maximum ten-day window. Silence after the response period routes to ARIF. The order is immediately executable.[10]

Polignano DDS 167/2024 uses the same seven-day posting, three-day election and ten-day removal pattern for that containment order.[12]

ARIF's older general webpage described a seven-day-after-publication election period instead of three days; the specific later order controls the case.[10][12][13]

### Handoff record

Minimum payload:

- regional act identifier and hash;
- attachments actually posted;
- cadastral Comune/foglio/particella set;
- albo publication number;
- start/end timestamps and certificate;
- legal-effect rule copied from the act;
- PEC events, if present;
- response deadline and route;
- owner choice or silence;
- ARIF/Osservatorio receipt.

### Failure modes and consequences

| Failure | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Wrong Comune or cadastral mismatch | **Land:** wrong legal-notice venue; **Field Work:** access/removal clock unsafe. |
| Attachment with target parcels omitted | **Land:** addressee/target cannot be reconstructed; forced action and contribution are challengeable. |
| News page posted but no legally specified albo event | **Land:** communication exists but notification evidence is missing. |
| Active board expires and no publication certificate is retained | **Applications/Payments:** contribution or challenge file lacks notice evidence; **Field Work:** start clock cannot be audited. |
| Cooperative watches PEC only | Member can miss a clock started by municipal publication. |
| Municipality delays posting | Immediate regional objective is delayed; later owner/ARIF clocks shift or become disputed. |

## Detailed workflow 2 — retention, discovery and operational outreach

### Publication is not retention

Giovinazzo defines the albo as the place where publication-required acts become effective and produce legal effects. Its current board link begins on 12 June 2025, while the April 2025 Xylella ordinance remains available as a dated municipal news page and PDF attachment.[8][9] **[I]** This proves that the active-board interface and durable public retrieval route can be different. It does not prove a universal retention period.

### Outreach

Municipal environment/agriculture offices can publish explanatory pages, answer calls, contact known landholders, coordinate with associations and record failed contact. Modugno exposes named environment/activities contacts alongside the regional order.[11] That support can start operational acknowledgement but cannot change the response deadline or official target.

### Failure consequence by function

- **Land:** missing historical posting proof leaves notice status unresolved.
- **Funding:** indemnity/funding claims may need the order and compliance chronology.
- **Applications:** an owner cannot safely claim that no PEC meant no notice.
- **Field Work:** contractor scheduling must read legal publication, not only a phone acknowledgement.
- **Payments:** retain the act, publication proof and official verbale separately; a municipal news URL is insufficient.

## Detailed workflow 3 — annual vector control on private and public land

### 2026 regional rule

DDS 39/2026 applies juvenile-vector control throughout Puglia. It requires shallow cultivation by ploughing, milling, harrowing or shredding; where mechanical access is difficult or impossible, such as slopes, road edges, shoulders and roundabouts, it prescribes physical means and only then suitable low-impact herbicides. It binds agricultural owners/occupiers and public/private owners/managers of uncultivated agricultural surfaces, public green, road edges, canals and demanial surfaces.[5]

The 2026 clocks are based on average municipal altitude:

- under 200 m: 25 March–30 April 2026;
- over 200 m: 10 April–15 May 2026.[5]

The act says the Osservatorio controls through Carabinieri Forestali, and municipalities may exercise controls under existing competence using appointed or formally delegated personnel.[5] Failure to perform regional phytosanitary measures falls under D.Lgs. 19/2021 art. 55(15), €1,000–€6,000; regional phytosanitary services impose those national sanctions.[3]

### Municipality's two separate roles

1. **Regulator/support on private land:** inform, possibly issue a lawful local ordinance, inspect under existing powers and route evidence.
2. **Obligated owner/manager on its own assets:** plan and complete municipal road/green/public-land work.

### Asset-responsibility handoff

```text
regional annual act
→ identify physical asset
→ identify owner/manager, not merely boundary
→ Comune / Province / Città Metropolitana / Consorzio / Region / State / concessionaire
→ select lawful execution mechanism
→ complete within agronomic clock
→ preserve work evidence
→ inspection/referral
```

### Failure consequences

- **Land:** wrong asset owner creates an unresolved responsible-person state.
- **Funding:** regional contribution allocation does not establish commitment, procurement or completion.
- **Applications:** a farmer convention needs its own lawful municipal procedure.
- **Field Work:** procurement delay can consume the biological window.
- **Payments:** municipal contractor payment needs contract/completion evidence; it is unrelated to a farmer's regional aid payment.

## Detailed workflow 4 — municipal execution, procurement and delegation to farmers

D.Lgs. 228/2001 art. 15 permits public administrations, expressly including reclamation consortia, to make conventions with agricultural entrepreneurs for territorial maintenance, agrarian/forest landscape and hydrogeological care. It also states annual contract limits of €50,000 for a single entrepreneur and €300,000 for associated entrepreneurs.[4] DDS 39/2026 expressly says public subjects may delegate the annual vector work to farmers under that article.[5]

A regional permission does not itself select the farmer, commit municipal budget or award work. The Comune still needs a competent RUP/manager, exact public assets, a convention/contract, eligibility and procurement checks, price, safety/access plan, deadline and completion acceptance.

**Ruffano provisional case.** Exa indexed an official municipal page stating that municipal land needed Xylella, fire-prevention and visibility work; that the Comune opened a manifestation of interest for economic operators; that the D.Lgs. 228 art. 15 farmer route could be used; and that first- and second-phase expressions were due 26 March and 7 April 2026.[17] Crawl4AI returned 500 and direct HTTP returned 403. **[U]** The page supports discovery of the municipal proceeding, but the signed determination, candidates, award, CIG/contract, work orders, completion and payment remain unverified.

Bitonto's 2026 ordinance separately recites the possibility of direct substitute execution at the non-compliant party's expense, potentially through an external party.[7] That is an enforcement mechanism under that ordinance, not evidence that Bitonto actually procured or completed work.

## Detailed workflow 5 — local ordinance, police, regional sanction and escalation

### Local ordinance route

Bitonto Ordinance 218/2026 orders public/private owners, occupiers and managers to perform local vegetation and soil work from 10 April to 15 May, requires in-situ shredding of pruning material, invokes a municipal sanction basis, directs local and other police to enforce, orders online-albo/site publication, and sends copies to Local Police, the agriculture/territory office, Carabinieri, Carabinieri Forestali, Città Metropolitana and Osservatorio.[7]

Giovinazzo Ordinance 14/2025 similarly orders work, requires in-situ shredding of pruning material, invokes TUEL art. 7-bis, charges Local Police, Metropolitan Police, Carabinieri Forestali and other forces with supervision, and makes the appeal clock run from municipal-albo publication.[8]

### Separate enforcement lanes

| Lane | Fact finder | Decision/sanction owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Municipal ordinance | Local police and named forces | Comune/local authority under the ordinance | ordinance, posting, inspection and contestation record |
| Regional/national phytosanitary failure | Osservatorio/Carabinieri Forestali; municipality may refer | Regional phytosanitary service under D.Lgs. 19 | regional act, inspection, responsible person, violation, regional sanction act[3] |
| Parcel-specific removal refusal | Inspector/ARIF | Osservatorio orders coactive removal; Prefect supports force; possible prosecutor referral | notice, obstruction/refusal record, official request, removal verbale[10][12] |

A municipality must not issue a D.Lgs. 19 phytosanitary penalty merely because its police observed weeds, unless an express delegation/competence basis exists. Conversely, a local ordinance violation is not automatically the same violation as the regional act.

## Detailed workflow 6 — owner choice, ARIF, access and public safety

Modugno DDS 7/2026 makes the owner's silence an operational decision: after the seven-day publication and three-day response period, ARIF proceeds within ten days. Refusal leads ARIF to forced removal, with Prefect and police informed and costs charged. The Osservatorio contests the administrative phytosanitary violation.[10]

Third-party obstruction is different from owner refusal. The order requires it to be documented and verbalised; the inspector/ARIF then asks the Prefect for force-public assistance. If the owner fails to concretely start in time, the Osservatorio orders coactive removal through ARIF, may refer to the prosecutor and denies any removal contribution.[10]

The Comune's possible contribution is practical, not dispositive:

- identify municipal roads/traffic hazards and safe access;
- provide local asset or address information;
- have Local Police assist if lawfully tasked;
- preserve incident evidence;
- route the matter to ARIF/Osservatorio/Prefecture.

The Comune cannot convert a contractor's “no access” note into the official obstruction verbale, grant itself forced-entry power, or close the phytosanitary case.

## Detailed workflow 7 — destruction, wood and residue routes

The exact order controls.

Polignano DDS 167/2024 and Modugno DDS 7/2026 require foliage to be destroyed in situ, separated from the trunk, by shredding or by burning **only if** allowed by law and local regulations.

They leave the cut woody part, appropriately sectioned, with the owner/occupier.[10][12]

The indispensable phytosanitary verbale records correct removal/destruction and gates the contribution.[10][12]

Bitonto and Giovinazzo local annual-work ordinances instead require pruning material to be destroyed in situ by shredding.[7][8]

Consequences:

- No fetched act establishes a universal municipal waste-collection or off-site disposal route for Xylella wood.
- Municipal fire/residue regulations can remove burning as an option where the regional order makes burning conditional on local legality.
- Off-site transport, waste classification, commercial reuse or biomass routes require a separate current legal basis and movement-risk check.
- A municipal collection decision cannot replace in-situ destruction where the phytosanitary order requires it.

## Detailed workflow 8 — landscape authority, planning, SUE and SUAP

D.Lgs. 19 art. 33 generally lets official emergency phytosanitary measures operate notwithstanding other constraints within the measure's limits. For removal of a **noninfected monumental or historic plant** within a demarcated removal area, it provides a case-by-case competent-authority decision after Art. 146 landscape authorization, to be issued within 45 days.[2]

Modugno DDS 7/2026 reflects that narrow branch: potentially monumental, laboratory-negative olives in the 50 m area are referred to the regional environmental-authorisation section for possible recognition and an Art. 146 request. The infected trees themselves do not wait for a generic municipal landscape permit.[10]

Puglia's landscape portal says competence varies:

- Region for projects under regional VIA;
- Province for provincial-VIA cases;
- delegated Comune/association where delegation and structural requirements are met;
- Province/Città Metropolitana or Region where municipal delegation conditions are not met.[24]

The live register exposes delegated entities and their acts. More than 200 comuni have historically received delegations, but current competence must be queried for the exact Comune and date.[24][26] Alberobello provides an active municipal landscape/VAS service and routes requests through SUE.[25]

### Workflow

```text
proposed removal/replant/project
→ classify official emergency work vs voluntary/funded work
→ test landscape/planning trigger
→ query competent authority and delegation as of date
→ assemble SUE/landscape/local-planning evidence only where required
→ receive decision/conditions
→ Field Work dispatch or stop
```

### SUAP bound

No fetched Xylella order, annual-vector act, municipal plant call or Article 6 workflow establishes SUAP as a universal gate. SUAP becomes relevant only when the proposed recovery includes an independently regulated productive establishment, building, commercial activity or other SUAP proceeding. Basic agricultural planting may instead encounter landscape, hydrogeological, water, title/consent or SUE issues. Treating “Comune” as “SUAP” would manufacture an application block.

## Detailed workflow 9 — municipal recovery calls and local applications

### Ostuni

The official municipal page opened “L'Ulivo che rinasce” on 3 November 2025. It targets non-professional owners or occupiers of agricultural land in Ostuni, offers 20–200 resistant olive plants, requires hand/PEC applications by noon on 2 December 2025, promises technical assistance and gives under-40 applicants priority.[18]

The Comune therefore creates:

- a distinct local applicant population;
- a local application clock and channel;
- local allocation criteria;
- a plant-supply/assistance handoff.

The page does not prove final ranking, plant procurement/lot trace, allocation, planting, inspection or payment. The signed full notice attachment was not exposed by Crawl4AI. Those are direct-acquisition needs.

### Brindisi

The official 23 December 2025 page records a Comune–Coldiretti protocol to develop support for private non-professional plots and describes an intended municipal free-plant call.[19] It is a policy/protocol event, not yet a verified open application or financial concession.

### Consequence for the five functions

- **Land:** verify territorial boundary, title/occupation and planting legality.
- **Funding:** classify as municipal programme, not regional Article 6.
- **Applications:** use municipal protocol/PEC and local criteria.
- **Field Work:** still require lawful cultivars, nursery/passport/lot, access and planting communication where applicable.
- **Payments:** free in-kind plant allocation is not cash payment; municipal procurement and beneficiary receipt are separate evidence.

## Detailed workflow 10 — cadastral Comune identity and institutional routing

Removal acts list Comune, foglio and particella and send the act to the competent municipality. Modugno's attachment even names an ASI consortium as reported owner for the affected industrial-area parcel, demonstrating that the responsible landholder may be another public body rather than the Comune.[10]

The cadastral Comune field performs four routing functions:

1. selects the legally specified municipal albo;
2. disambiguates foglio/particella numbers;
3. selects possible municipal landscape/planning/SUE authority;
4. helps determine local ordinance and public-asset responsibility.

It does not establish:

- current owner/occupier;
- farm operator or cooperative membership;
- beneficiary/applicant;
- municipal ownership;
- title sufficient for funded work.

If ownership changed, Modugno DDS 7/2026 requires the listed owner to notify Osservatorio/ARIF of the new owner within three days after publication ends.[10] The Comune may route or assist that correction, but the order does not give it authority to adjudicate title.

## Detailed workflow 12 — civil protection and emergency language

Some municipal pages classify Xylella under “response to emergencies,” and mayors retain ordinary local safety/civil-protection functions. But OCDPC 318/2016 returned Xylella activity to ordinary Regione Puglia management from 6 February 2016.[27]

Therefore:

- a current municipal civil-protection role requires a new activation basis or a concrete public-safety incident;
- “emergency” in a page category does not create phytosanitary competence;
- Prefecture/police access support follows the current D.Lgs. 19/order route, not the expired 2015 extraordinary commissioner chain;
- municipal traffic, fire, crowd or worker-safety actions can still condition field execution without altering the plant-health prescription.
