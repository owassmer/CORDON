# Data and geospatial opportunity scan for a Puglia Xylella operating picture

**Research date:** 25 August 2026. **Scope:** public, live-web sources. `VERIFIED` means the source or endpoint was opened or probed in this research pass. `UNVERIFIED` marks a claim, license, cadence, or access path that still needs owner confirmation.

## Executive verdict

1. **The open cadastral map is an identity-and-geometry layer, not an agricultural land-use layer.** Agenzia delle Entrate's live WFS exposes parcel geometry plus `INSPIREID_LOCALID`, `INSPIREID_NAMESPACE`, `LABEL`, `NATIONALCADASTRALREFERENCE`, and `ADMINISTRATIVEUNIT`. It does **not** expose `qualità`, `classe`, `reddito dominicale`, `reddito agrario`, ownership, or an explicit `areaValue` in the live `DescribeFeatureType` response. Area can be computed from geometry. The regional and national ZIPs are genuine bulk downloads under CC BY 4.0. [AdE WFS page](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/cartografia-catastale-wfs) · [WFS endpoint](https://wfs.cartografia.agenziaentrate.gov.it/inspire/wfs/owfs01.php) · [bulk downloads](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/accedi-al-servizio-cartografici).
2. **`Uliveto` lives in the separate censuario record.** The current terrain-supply specification has a three-digit `QUALITA` code, `CLASSE`, hectares/ares/centiares, dominical/agrarian income, portion records, and act dates. The official code table maps `036` to `uliveto`. These fields are available in cadastral views and institutional ASCII/SOAP supplies, but I found no current open, openly licensed, nationwide bulk release of the full censuario. [Current terrain record specification](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/268297/Tracciati_forniture_terreni_31012022.pdf/56afa1d8-c91a-f512-7ee2-e952aac04a78).
3. **A regional-government tool can classify parcels as olive-producing at scale only through one of three routes:** (a) obtain the authoritative censuario through an AdE institutional agreement or a lawful municipal data-sharing arrangement; (b) use the open but historical AGEA/ENEA OpenIACS LPIS 2018 classification and spatially intersect it with current cadastral parcels; or (c) intersect current parcels with Regione Puglia's open 2011 land-use polygons (`uliveti`). Route (a) is authoritative but gated; (b) and (c) are obtainable now but historical and spatially derived, not current cadastral truth.
4. **The richest immediately usable operating-picture data is already public in SIT Puglia ArcGIS REST.** Current 2026 sample points, confirmed positives, demarcated zones, monumental olives, protected areas, public-domain parcels, roads/rail, and orthophotos are queryable without login. The missing operational link is **execution state**: the public record shows findings and prescription acts, but no consolidated parcel-level register of notice → voluntary removal → ARIF removal → verified completion → sanction → indemnity paid.
5. **The most surprising underused sources are:** OpenIACS LPIS/GSAA 2018 national RDF dumps; Regione Puglia's state-domain parcel layer, which already carries `QUALITA` and `CLASSE`; the public DOP Terra di Bari surface workbook; and 20 cm orthophoto time series through 2023.

**Important schema correction.** The generic INSPIRE cadastral-parcel model may describe properties such as `areaValue` and lifespan metadata, but the live Italian `CP:CadastralParcel` schema probed here does not return `areaValue`. Do not design against a generic INSPIRE diagram. Design against the live `DescribeFeatureType`, and compute area in an equal-area or appropriate local projected CRS.

The national parcel reference is deterministic: municipality/cadastral section + sheet + parcel name. AdE documents the construction, including `STRADA###` and `ACQUA###` special parcels, in its [identifier specification](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/d/guest/struttura-degli-identificatori-tematici-nazionali).

### 1.2 Censuario (`.TER`): the authoritative categorical record

The [2022 AdE terrain-supply specification](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/268297/Tracciati_forniture_terreni_31012022.pdf/56afa1d8-c91a-f512-7ee2-e952aac04a78) defines, per parcel situation:

- cadastral municipality/section, sheet, parcel number, denominator, subaltern and edificiality;
- `QUALITA` (3-digit code; `036 = uliveto` in the official code table), `CLASSE`, hectares, ares, centiares;
- flags for calculable income, portions, and deductions;
- dominical and agrarian income in euro, plus legacy lire fields;
- effective and registration dates and the act/note that opened or closed the situation;
- up to 20 parcel portions, each with its own portion ID, quality, class, area, and—through interchange—income.

This is exactly the data needed to say **“the cadastral record classifies all or part of this parcel as uliveto.”** It is not the same as current biological canopy, current farmer declaration, or productive status. A dead, abandoned, cleared, replanted, or mixed parcel may retain a cadastral quality that lags reality.

**Open-data verdict:** No AdE, OpenCoesione, regional mirror, SISTER public endpoint, or “attestati” service found in this pass publishes the **complete current censuario terrain table** as anonymous, openly licensed bulk/API data. OpenCoesione publishes programme/project and context-indicator data, not cadastral qualities. This negative is bounded by the AdE open-data pages, cadastral service pages, dati.gov.it search results, OpenCoesione search, and Puglia CKAN search; it is not proof that no bilateral governmental feed exists.

### 1.4 Recommended authoritative acquisition

Ask AdE for a **privacy-minimised regional terrain-object feed** containing only parcel identity, portion, `QUALITA`, `CLASSE`, area, and validity/registration dates—no `.SOG` owner table. Request current snapshot plus objective incrementals through Sistema di interscambio. That directly answers operational land-use questions while avoiding owner-data diffusion. Until approved, label open-derived parcels `olive_likelihood`, never `cadastral_uliveto`.

## 3. Epidemic surveillance and dashboard precedent

### 3.1 Puglia: more open than the visible portal suggests

The public ArcGIS REST root is [`https://webapps.sit.puglia.it/arcgis/rest/services`](https://webapps.sit.puglia.it/arcgis/rest/services). Query layers with `/MapServer/<id>/query?where=1%3D1&outFields=*&returnGeometry=true&f=json`; paginate by `OBJECTID` because GeoJSON support and record limits vary.

- **Current demarcated geography:** [`Operationals/DatiPubbliciFasceXF`](https://webapps.sit.puglia.it/arcgis/rest/services/Operationals/DatiPubbliciFasceXF/MapServer) exposes 16 polygon layers: infected and buffer areas for Cagnano Varano, Giovinazzo, Modugno, Bisceglie, Minervino Murge, and Bari, plus ex-Salento infected/buffer/containment layers. Each currently returns one geometry. License metadata is blank—**reuse license UNVERIFIED**, although anonymous query is enabled.
- **Current surveillance:** [`MonitoraggioXFSintesiAttuale`](https://webapps.sit.puglia.it/arcgis/rest/services/Operationals2/MonitoraggioXFSintesiAttuale/MapServer) returned, on this probe, 210 confirmed `pauca` positives, 18,974 olive samples, 2,577 fruit samples and 1,014 other-area samples for campaign 2026. Fields include sample ID, species, date, result, typology and geometry; positives add symptoms, team, protocol and confirmation document.
- Separate current services exist for [`fastidiosa`](https://webapps.sit.puglia.it/arcgis/rest/services/Operationals2/MonitoraggioXFFSintesiAttuale/MapServer) and [`multiplex`](https://webapps.sit.puglia.it/arcgis/rest/services/Operationals2/MonitoraggioXFMultiplexSintesiAttuale/MapServer), plus prior-campaign services in `Operationals3`. This is sufficient for a subspecies-aware operating picture.
- **What the existing view lacks:** a unified parcel lifecycle, legal-act version, owner/contact-safe assignment, visit/task queue, removal completion, sanctions, indemnity/payment state, explicit dataset timestamps, and reproducible download controls. A map of positives is not a case-management system.

## 4. Enforcement, removal, sanctions, and payments

### 4.1 What is public now

- **Prescription acts and parcel annexes:** BURP determinations are stable primary PDFs. Recent eradication acts state that InnovaPuglia derives cadastral parcels within 50 m of positives, puts parcel maps in Annex 1/A, and owner lists in Annex 1/B. Example: [DET 24/2025](https://burp.regione.puglia.it/documents/20135/2605848/DET_24_20_2_2025.pdf/575a1cb2-0755-5733-985d-77d8911f93bf?t=1740675033380&version=1.0). These can build an **orders/obligations ledger** keyed by decree, municipality, sheet and parcel. Owner annexes must not be republished into an open operational product.
- **Defined execution workflow:** ARIF's [Abbattimenti e indennizzi page](https://www.arifpuglia.it/attivita/monitoraggio-xylella/abbattimenti-e-indennizzi/) states the notification windows, voluntary-removal route, ARIF removal within 10 days after owner communication or no response, forced removal with Prefecture/police support, administrative-violation contestation, and two-technician verification of ARIF removals. It also states ARIF is responsible for indemnity instruction and liquidation.
- **Funding-admission determinations and aggregate payment news:** ARIF published that determinations 24 and 25/2022 admitted 965 applications for €21.95m, and described the 2018/2019 indemnity queues. [Source](https://www.arifpuglia.it/comunicati-stampa/importante-boccata-dossigeno-per-circa-1-000-aziende-agricole/). Individual determinations may list beneficiary/application/payment facts subject to transparency and privacy rules.

### 4.2 What was not found

No anonymous bulk/API register was found for:

- each ordered tree/parcel's voluntary-removal, ARIF-removal, forced-removal, verification date, contractor or evidence;
- administrative sanctions for non-compliance, amounts, appeal and collection state;
- a parcel-linked indemnity lifecycle from eligibility through paid amount/date;
- felling-operation footprints or daily work orders.

**Do not infer completion from an eradication prescription.** It proves a duty was issued, not that a tree was removed. Likewise, a positive sample is not an order, and an admitted grant is not a payment. These must be separate states.

**High-value government-only request:** export the ARIF case-management table behind the two-technician checks with pseudonymous case ID, decree ID, parcel reference, required action, actor route, scheduled date, completion date, verification status, sanction flag, indemnity state and evidence URI. This is the single missing dataset that converts the public map into an execution picture.

## 6. Minimal data model and confidence rules

Keep five facts separate:

1. `CadastralParcel` — current geometry and national cadastral reference.
2. `LandUseObservation` — source, class (`uliveto`, `uliveti`, LPIS code, RS probability), observation date, geometry, confidence.
3. `DiagnosticSample` — official sample ID/date/species/result/subspecies/protocol; point geometry.
4. `LegalDuty` — decree/version, parcel, legal regime, required action, notice date/deadline.
5. `ExecutionCase` — actor, scheduled/completed/verified timestamps, sanction and indemnity states.

Render labels precisely:

- **Authoritative current cadastral `uliveto`** only from current censuario validity.
- **Declared olive use** only from current GSAA/fascicolo.
- **Mapped olive grove (2011/2018)** from UDS/OpenIACS.
- **Likely current olive canopy** from orthophoto/Sentinel classification.
- **Official infection** only from diagnostic `RISULTATO` or confirmation.
- **Ordered / removed / verified removed / paid** as distinct lifecycle states.

## Findings

- **Direct:** Open cadastral geometry cannot classify olive farmland; `uliveto` is in the censuario. **Second-order:** treating the WFS as a full cadastre would systematically hide the very categorical field the product needs.
- **Direct:** a current authoritative categorical feed is institutionally obtainable but not anonymous open bulk. **Second-order:** the correct procurement is a minimal object-only feed, not owner-heavy cadastral replication.
- **Direct:** two real open substitutes exist now—UDS 2011 and OpenIACS LPIS 2018. **Second-order:** retaining vintage and source confidence permits immediate use without laundering historical land use into current legal fact.
- **Direct:** Puglia already publishes unusually rich live surveillance and public-asset GIS. **Second-order:** product value lies less in drawing another dashboard than in connecting diagnosis → parcel → legal duty → execution.
- **Direct:** execution and sanctions are the largest public-data gap. **Second-order:** a privacy-safe ARIF status export likely produces more operational value than another remote-sensing model.
