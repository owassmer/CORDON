### Government reading of the decision corpus

The corpus supports a regional control room with at least six independent decision streams: **legal geography**, **official monitoring and confirmation**, **parcel/plant prescription**, **execution/enforcement**, **grant/indemnity instruction**, and **liquidation/payment authorization**. None can safely be collapsed into a single case status. The strongest legal caution is the mismatch between a current GIS label and the act that instituted the area: DDS 82 is ex-Salento only, and Valenzano exists legally without a Valenzano-named current layer.

## 4. Enforcement and compliance chains

### 4.1 Canonical notification → execution → verbale → indemnity/substitution chain

The most complete on-disk chain is DET 3/2026:

1. **Official result:** inspectors identify a plant; the laboratory and CNR confirmation retain sample ID, protocol, date, and subspecies.
2. **Parcel resolution:** InnovaPuglia resolves the infected parcel and every parcel in the 50 m geometry.
3. **Prescription:** the Osservatorio issues the signed eradication act, identifies the applicable Article 7 classes, and attaches the cadastral/owner schedule.
4. **Notification:** the Comune posts the act for seven natural consecutive days; the seventh day produces the stated notification effect. PEC is additional where available.
5. **Owner election:** during publication and no later than three days after it, the owner chooses voluntary removal or ARIF execution through the ARIF portal.
6. **Execution:** voluntary or ARIF removal is due within ten days; owner silence defaults to ARIF.
7. **Inspection/acceptance:** voluntary work is checked by inspectors/agents/assistants; ARIF work by two ARIF assistants. At least one phytosanitary official presides over removal/destruction and writes the **verbale**.
8. **Financial consequence:** the verbale is expressly indispensable for contribution recognition under DGR 994/903. The owner then files through ARIF.
9. **Substitution/enforcement:** refusal triggers forced ARIF removal, Prefect/police involvement, cost recovery, administrative sanction, possible Procura referral, and loss of contribution.

Primary evidence: `/Users/owenwassmer/Desktop/Connor/wedge1-aip/data/extracts/burp/DET_3_12_1_2026_FITO.pdf`. ### 5.3 What curation keeps and what it drops

`parcels.py` emits only:

- `qualified_cadastral_ref`;
- title;
- geometry and WKT;
- comune code/name;
- foglio, particella, sezione;
- object ID;
- lon/lat envelope;
- source URL and retrieval time;
- sync and conservation/geometry flags.

Exact implementation: `/Users/owenwassmer/Desktop/Connor/cordon-transforms/transforms-python/src/cordon_ingest/parcels.py`, `OUT_COLS` lines 31–51.

Consequently, accepted curated rows drop or fold away the following useful raw/source attributes:

1. **`INSPIREID_NAMESPACE`** — dropped completely.
2. **GML ID** — dropped. This is correct as a primary key decision because the preflight found reused GML IDs, but retaining it backstage is still useful for source debugging.
3. **Raw source occurrence ID, member path, and feature ordinal** — dropped from accepted rows, although rejects retain a `source_identity`. Government audit benefits from a retained source-occurrence lineage even when the business key is the national reference.
4. **`allegato` and `sviluppo` as first-class columns** — parsed in raw but omitted from curated output. They remain encoded inside the national cadastral reference, but explicit columns would make BURP annex joins, anomaly detection, and government reconciliation safer.
5. **Source `LABEL`** — folded into the title/particella fallback rather than retained verbatim. This matters for `ACQUA###`, `STRADA###`, and other non-ordinary labels.
6. **Explicit source `ADMINISTRATIVEUNIT`** — converted to `nome_comune`; source wording is not retained separately.
7. **FeatureCollection timestamp, `numberMatched`, `numberReturned`, cadastral-unit package, source vintage, and explicit `as_of`** — omitted from accepted rows and left to manifests/source register.
8. **Raw `geometry_epsg6706`** — omitted after normalized geometry is produced.
9. **Accepted-row gate reason and occurrence count** — omitted. `occurrence_n` is used to prove conservation and dissolve duplicates but is not in `OUT_COLS`.
10. **INSPIRE local ID** — retained only indirectly as `objectid`, with fallback to the qualified national reference; the original field name and namespace pairing disappear.
