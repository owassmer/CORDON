# First-wave comparison — web and repository lanes

Status: dated research record; not product or ontology authority
Date: 20 August 2026

## Inputs kept independent

This comparison begins only after the independent outputs were complete.

- `web-formal-institutions.md`: formal EU, Italian, and Puglian institutions and procedures; 36 sources.
- `web-operator-practice.md`: operator practice and less-visible workflow; 50 sources.
- `repo-program-workflows.md`: all 186 tracked `olive-xylella` files inventoried and represented; 168 validated path-line citations.
- `repo-data-and-process-evidence.md`: independent data/source/computational lane; 30 validated path-line citations.

The web reports contain no local repository names or source-register vocabulary. The repository reports contain no external URLs.

## Corroborated findings

### 1. The work has no single end-to-end institutional owner

The formal web lane finds legally distinct authorities for plant-health status, funding eligibility/concession, application preparation, field execution, payment controls, and payment execution (`web-formal-institutions.md:8-16,54-77`).

The repository identifies the same fragmented actor set but cannot establish a complete application-to-payment workflow from its own material (`repo-program-workflows.md:320-337,405-415`).

**Meaning:** CORDON must connect handoffs across organisations. It must not imply that the cooperative, CAA, ARIF, Regione, or AGEA controls the whole chain.

### 2. Land is a junction of different official and operational facts

The formal lane distinguishes cadastral identity, fascicolo/LPIS land, title of conduction, official Xylella geography, parcel orders, aid commitments, and executed-work footprints (`web-formal-institutions.md:10-12,83-115`).

The repository independently shows public cadastral geometry, current and historical Xylella zones, monumental-tree records, official monitoring, and order annexes, while the current farm-to-parcel relationship normally comes from CAA/SIAN records (`repo-data-and-process-evidence.md:42-93`).

**Meaning:** a parcel map is not the entire land decision. The operator needs to know which authority and date support each consequential statement.

### 3. Eligibility, application inclusion, award, and payment are different decisions

The formal lane distinguishes legal eligibility, documentary readiness, selection ranking, budget availability, concession, work readiness, claim admissibility, and payment execution (`web-formal-institutions.md:117-148,206-241`).

The repository likewise distinguishes open measures, queued applications, scorrimenti, concessions, commitments, and cash, but lacks an end-to-end case (`repo-program-workflows.md:355-385,405-443`).

**Meaning:** one `eligible` Boolean or one generic project status cannot describe the operating process.

### 4. A CAA can prepare and transmit, but the beneficiary retains responsibility

The formal lane finds that the CAA acts under mandate and convention, while the beneficiary remains responsible for the application's correctness (`web-formal-institutions.md:20-30,54-81,150-178`).

The practice lane shows preparation distributed across source registers, CAA, technician, member, cooperative, SIAN, regional portals, PEC, signatures, OTP, and sometimes paper originals (`web-operator-practice.md:98-105,292-375`).

**Meaning:** “CORDON prepares; operator authorises” is directionally correct only when the authorised person and external exception owners are distinguished.

### 5. An order or award does not make field work dispatch-ready

The formal lane requires authority validation, scope, access, technical prescriptions, contractor, timing, evidence, supervision, and variant handling before field execution (`web-formal-institutions.md:180-204`).

The practice lane independently adds nursery stock, plant passports, contractor capacity, procurement, cash, route planning, member access, and evidence design (`web-operator-practice.md:377-466`).

The repository contains source-supported duties and geographic inputs but no actual scheduling, dispatch, completion, or verification records (`repo-program-workflows.md:387-403,518-523`).

**Meaning:** an order or award starts field-readiness assembly. It does not automatically create a ready-to-dispatch job.

### 6. Payment is a second evidence dossier and a serial public control process

The formal lane distinguishes advance, SAL, final balance, claim preparation, technical checks, administrative checks, admitted amount, payment authorisation, execution, liquidation, bank reconciliation, audit, and recovery (`web-formal-institutions.md:206-241`).

The practice lane finds that finance must design admissible procurement and evidence before spending, while member-level guarantees and later public checks can split a collective project (`web-operator-practice.md:112-118,468-548`).

The repository identifies payment transparency and aggregate audit sources but no case-level claim-to-cash trace (`repo-data-and-process-evidence.md:148-175`; `repo-program-workflows.md:405-415`).

**Meaning:** cooperative finance can authorise a claim submission. It cannot authorise public payment, and a concession is not a payable amount.

### 7. Feedback loops are normal, not edge cases

Across the lanes, normal operating loops include:

- new official findings changing land status and duties;
- amendments and deadline extensions changing open work;
- ranking scorrimenti reactivating old applications;
- source-data correction regenerating an application;
- authority requests and adverse notices requiring response;
- field variants returning to technical or funding authorities;
- payment controls returning claims for evidence or reduction;
- death, ownership, bank, guarantee, and compliance changes reopening cases (`web-formal-institutions.md:13-16,293-324`; `web-operator-practice.md:608-673`; `repo-program-workflows.md:445-454`).

**Meaning:** the process is asynchronous and reversible. It is not a clean five-stage waterfall.

## Direct challenges to the five starting assertions

### Assertion 1 — Land explains status and duties; Field Work owns execution

**Supported with correction.** Land can assemble and explain source-supported official status and duties. Field Work plans and records execution. Legal compliance may still be determined by the competent inspector or authority, and access/acknowledgement/owner choice can sit between explanation and execution (`web-formal-institutions.md:328-335`; `web-operator-practice.md:188-236`).

### Assertion 2 — Funding evaluates the full population; no arbitrary candidate selection

**Materially challenged.** The web practice lane finds a specific collective Art. 6 pattern:

- individual members submit adhesion applications;
- the OP/cooperative can include only some after negotiation;
- an adhesion not included in the collective application falls back to individual treatment;
- individual beneficiaries retain guarantees and other liabilities (`web-operator-practice.md:90-97,267-290`).

This does not justify arbitrary preference. It establishes a separate inclusion decision after eligibility, potentially based on mandate, technical feasibility, capacity, financing, geographic batching, and negotiated execution.

The repository cannot confirm this mechanism because it lacks the full forms/manuals and real applications (`repo-program-workflows.md:371-385,469-477`).

**Research response:** reconstruct the complete Art. 6 collective process from primary artifacts before revising the assertion.

### Assertion 3 — CORDON prepares; operator resolves exceptions and authorises

**Supported with authority correction.** Preparation can be automated, but source-data repairs may belong to the CAA or farm dossier; legal/geographic conflicts may belong to an authority; and final truth certification belongs to the beneficiary or authorised representative (`web-formal-institutions.md:346-353`; `web-operator-practice.md:292-375`).

### Assertion 4 — Orders/awards prepare field operations; manager schedules and assigns

**Partially challenged.** Orders and awards define authority, scope, and deadlines. They do not prepare procurement, materials, access, technical design, contractor capacity, evidence, or approved variants. The stronger formulation is: validated authority starts field-readiness assembly; the manager dispatches only after dependencies are satisfied (`web-formal-institutions.md:355-362`; `web-operator-practice.md:377-466`).

### Assertion 5 — Award rules prepare payment stages; finance authorises

**Supported only after separating internal and public authority.** Award rules define possible claim stages. Finance engineers admissible expenditure and evidence and may approve internal release. The beneficiary/signatory submits. Public bodies instruct and control. The accredited paying agency authorises and executes CAP payment (`web-formal-institutions.md:364-371`; `web-operator-practice.md:468-548`).

## Web-only discoveries

The repository did not contain sufficient evidence for these findings:

1. Collective Art. 6 membership can involve adhesion applications followed by negotiated cooperative inclusion (`web-operator-practice.md:90-97,267-290`).
2. The collective application remains composed of individual beneficiaries with individual guarantee and compliance constraints (`web-operator-practice.md:94-96,281-290`).
3. SIAN exposes compilation, inconsistency, provisional print, release/protocol, rectification, office monitoring, and Excel export states (`web-operator-practice.md:98-105,314-357`).
4. Real workflows cross SIAN, EIP/PMA, PEC, digital signatures, OTP, and original paper guarantees (`web-operator-practice.md:98-105,292-375`).
5. Expiry of a portal-support contract caused a project extension, while plant scarcity caused a separate supplier-contract extension (`web-operator-practice.md:106-110,399-460`).
6. Field evidence should be designed before procurement and execution because payment is a second dossier (`web-operator-practice.md:468-548,755-757`).
7. Public payment processing is a serial chain rather than one finance approval (`web-formal-institutions.md:206-241`; `web-operator-practice.md:510-540`).
8. Death/subentro, debt offsets, guarantees, bank errors, and recognition lapse are normal long-running case paths (`web-formal-institutions.md:229-241`; `web-operator-practice.md:525-548,665-673`).

## Repository-only strengths

The web lanes did not enumerate the same technical data universe:

1. Public current and historical Xylella polygon services.
2. Two public cadastral geometry routes.
3. The 341,428-record monumental-tree registry.
4. Current and historical official monitoring points.
5. Permanent searchable BURP order PDFs and parcel annexes.
6. DOP, land-use, landscape, authorization, host, procurement, and money-source families.
7. Twelve monitoring workbooks and their changing schema/survey frame.
8. Demonstrated remote-sensing limits that prevent field evidence from being overstated (`repo-data-and-process-evidence.md:42-147`).

**Meaning:** the repository is a strong data/source foundation. The web lanes are stronger on real administrative and operator workflow.

## Conflicts and uncertainties not yet resolved

1. **Selection legitimacy.** The Art. 6 inclusion mechanism appears real, but its exact legal discretion, criteria, documentation, fairness duties, and relationship to individual fallback need primary reconstruction.
2. **Who contracts and pays.** Public sources do not settle whether the cooperative or each beneficiary contracts suppliers and bears overruns across every collective design.
3. **Field authority.** Compulsory removal and grant-funded investment are distinct loops that meet at the same land but differ in owner choice, executor, evidence, and payment.
4. **Payment-stage mechanics.** The exact Puglia Art. 6 advance, SAL/final, technical-control, liquidation, and bank-execution path remains incomplete.
5. **Operator observation.** Public sources infer spreadsheets, off-portal worklists, route planning, and exception queues. No direct cooperative observation or interview confirms local tools, time costs, error rates, or decision heuristics.
6. **Complete legal corpus.** Current calls, forms, annexes, consolidated amendments, and payment rules are not yet frozen for a first-release scope.
7. **Actual end-to-end case.** No public or repository record currently connects one member and parcel through adhesion, collective inclusion, concession, field work, claim, liquidation, and cash receipt.

## Second-wave research opened by this comparison

The first-wave gaps justify three independent web deep dives:

1. reconstruct the complete Article 6 collective application and inclusion mechanism;
2. reconstruct compulsory and funded field delivery, evidence, and variant handling;
3. reconstruct advance, SAL/final claim, public controls, liquidation, payment execution, and recovery.

These deep dives remain part of workflow research. They do not select nouns, relationships, ontology types, capabilities, or screens.
