## Accepted decisions

- Land, Funding, Applications, Field Work and Payments are connected operating responsibilities. They are not sequential stages, screens, tabs, applications or Ontology types.
- Six recurring operator questions are the primary connective structure.
- Public eligibility, member adhesion, cooperative acceptance of the negotiated execution mandate and public concession remain separate decisions.
- Land explains the current official situation. Field Work prepares and executes the work and supplies the required proof. The competent inspector or authority decides official legal compliance. That outcome updates Land and can unlock or block Payments.
- Municipalities remain assigned actors across the five responsibilities. Their role follows the exact event, asset responsibility, effective delegation and date. There is no separate municipal or generic Public Authority lane.
- CORDON maintains a trusted current legal and regulatory corpus. Authority is resolved for the exact proposition and proceeding. Source acquisition, reconciliation and ingestion remain backstage controls; the operator sees the applicable rule, any material conflict and the consequence for active work.
- Funding maintains a complete event-driven opportunity population. A material opportunity or governing change causes a complete in-scope evaluation; later changes recompute only affected work. Member interest and management nomination do not define the evaluation universe.
- Scarce cooperative capacity is allocated separately from public eligibility, concession, legal duty, creditor identity and individual fallback.
- Capacity recommendations use Option A. Non-discretionary rules and cooperative-approved fairness safeguards define the feasible portfolios. CORDON recommends the feasible portfolio with the highest expected realized Xylella-loss reduction, explains uncertainty and alternatives, and uses an approved neutral tie-breaker only for materially equivalent portfolios. The authorized cooperative role commits or overrides.
- A date enters the operator model only when it changes legal applicability, starts or ends a named clock, or records a consequential action or outcome. Internal discovery, ingestion and recording timestamps are not operator workflow unless they independently create such a consequence.
- Wedge 1 continues through bounded biological establishment and any required early replacement or cure. Public payment may settle earlier. Full productive maturity, yield optimization, processing and market realization remain outside Wedge 1.
- Funded and self-funded Xylella recovery share one voluntary route. Public Funding, Application, concession and Payment branches activate only when applicable. Self-funded work is part of the manager’s current recovery picture, not a second product or general orchard-management scope.
- Gate 3 requires real, grain-sufficient evidence. Permissionless public, operational, judicial, procurement, scientific or remote-observation evidence can establish noun grain. Direct private evidence remains mandatory only for current private instances or later relationships that cannot otherwise be established. Contradictory evidence or an unmodelled operator decision selectively reopens the affected Gate 2 boundary.

## Operator objective

The primary user is the cooperative or recognized producer-organization operations manager.

The manager coordinates overlapping work for many members and land situations. The objective is not to maximize applications or mark one global case complete. It is to preserve viable paths toward:

- legal compliance where a compulsory duty applies;
- a valid and executable voluntary recovery or funded investment;
- correctly performed and accepted field work;
- public money that reaches the intended beneficiary where support applies; and
- durable productive restoration rather than installation alone.

The same member and land can simultaneously carry compulsory, funding, application, permit, field, payment, recovery and aftercare work. Those lines can share information and resources without sharing one authority, clock or status.

## One recursive operating grammar

```text
material operator event
→ identify affected decisions and decision owners
→ establish what is required for the named decision or action
→ decide, act or hold within the real authority boundary
→ record the consequential outcome and next handoff
↺ later change reopens only affected work
```

Under this grammar:

- an exception is a missing, conflicting, failed or adverse premise, not another workflow;
- a handoff carries the achieved effect, required evidence, next responsible actor and active clock;
- preparation, decision, execution and acceptance remain separate;
- completion always names a bounded result; and
- CORDON detects, reconciles, calculates, prepares and recommends without taking a reserved member, professional or public decision.

## Six recurring operator questions

### 1. What changed, and what does it affect?

An operator event matters when it can change a member or land situation, legal duty, opportunity, route, readiness decision, field outcome, payment result or recovery exposure.

Examples include a changed member or land fact, official condition, governing rule, opportunity, authority outcome, resource condition, field result or financial result. The exact source and intake mechanics remain below the operator workflow.

CORDON identifies the affected active or dormant work and explains the changed consequence. The authority or actor that owns the underlying fact corrects or decides it.

**Bounded result:** affected work and its next decision owner are known; unaffected work continues.

### 2. What is now required, allowed or blocked?

For each affected line, determine only what the named operator decision needs:

- the current official condition, duty, permission or opportunity;
- the member, land and responsible actor;
- the authority that owns the next public decision;
- the triggered legal, technical, permit, resource, finance or evidence conditions;
- the active clock and consequence of delay; and
- any material disagreement that prevents the named decision.

Conditional dependencies such as water, landscape, Natura 2000, public or civic land, plant movement, certification or processing enter only when the governing route makes them consequential.

#### Municipality rule

A Comune may publish legal notice, perform outreach, manage its own assets, issue and enforce a lawful local ordinance, contract public work, support access, decide a delegated permit or run a local programme. Each role is resolved independently. Municipal boundaries do not establish ownership, phytosanitary competence, grant authority or payment authority.

#### Concurrent obligations

Compulsory and voluntary commitments can apply to the same land. Keep their authority, duty, incentive, evidence and clocks independent. Share physical planning or resources only where the applicable rules permit it. An unresolved compatibility issue holds only the affected discretionary action; continuing legal duties and unaffected work remain active.

**Bounded result:** the applicable routes, responsible actors, material conditions and scoped holds are understood well enough to decide which route continues.

### 3. Who chooses, who decides and which route continues?

#### Voluntary recovery and investment

Keep these decisions separate:

1. the public rules determine whether the member and land qualify for a public route;
2. the member decides whether to participate;
3. the cooperative decides whether to accept the negotiated execution mandate;
4. the cooperative decides whether to pursue the opportunity and allocate its own capacity; and
5. the public authority decides admissibility, ranking and concession.

A valid member route omitted from collective execution continues individually where the programme permits it. Member-specific concession, guarantee, claim, creditor, payment and recovery rights remain member-specific.

Self-funded restoration uses the same legal, readiness, execution, acceptance and aftercare questions. Public application, concession and payment branches simply do not apply.

CORDON includes committed private and alternative recovery resources when explaining the member’s current route and when comparing scarce-capacity portfolios. Existing self-funding is not an automatic positive or negative priority. It may reduce additionality, increase completion probability, protect an existing commitment or complement a missing cooperative resource.

Funding-source election, cumulation, enterprise-crisis, continuity and signing-authority questions are applied only where the governing instrument makes them consequential.

#### Scarce-capacity decision

Public eligibility and route readiness do not reserve cooperative capacity.

The cooperative approves the fairness safeguards, permitted decision inputs, override authority, review rights and neutral tie-breaker. Non-discretionary legal, safety, instrument, firm-commitment, named-action readiness and resource-feasibility conditions apply first.

Within the feasible set, CORDON compares workable portfolios and schedules, identifies binding resources, recommends the portfolio with the highest expected realized Xylella-loss reduction, explains inclusions and deferrals, and shows alternatives and sensitivity to uncertain inputs. It does not hide constraints inside one opaque score or manufacture a unique ordering when the evidence does not support one.

The recommendation assesses incremental expected loss reduction relative to each member’s current recovery path. It does not equate low public funding with low total recovery support, and it does not automatically deprioritize a member merely because private resources are present. Exact inputs and calculation remain deferred to the data and design gates.

The authorized cooperative role commits capacity or overrides the recommendation with a recorded reason. Tentative availability, time-bounded reservation, firm commitment and scheduled capacity remain operationally distinct without fixing final state names or data fields.

#### Compulsory route

The competent plant-health authority establishes the duty. The responsible person chooses the execution route only where the order grants that choice. The actual public-asset manager controls its own work. The competent official owns legal acceptance. Silence, refusal, third-party obstruction, appeal and stay remain different outcomes.

**Bounded result:** each decision has an owner, a recorded consequence and the route or routes that remain open. A negative decision changes only the routes it governs.

### 4. Is the route ready for the named action?

There is no generic ready state.

Preparing a filing, releasing it, responding to an authority, requesting a permit, reserving capacity, making a binding commitment, dispatching field work and releasing a payment claim are different actions with different gates.

For the named action, resolve only the conditions its governing route requires. These can include:

- current facts and authority to act;
- professional design or judgment;
- required public decisions or permits;
- committed materials, services and capacity;
- finance and guarantee conditions;
- compatible legal, administrative, commercial and biological clocks; and
- evidence required by later acceptance or payment.

CORDON prepares known contents, checks route-specific conditions, identifies the actor who can cure a missing premise and exposes the consequence of proceeding or waiting. It does not certify professional judgments, release the member’s filing, grant a permit, concede aid or dispatch work.

Protected-site overlap, for example, triggers competent review rather than automatic clearance or refusal. Non-binding preparation can continue unless the governing rule prohibits it; an affected binding commitment or dispatch waits for the required decision.

**Bounded results remain independent:** a source record can be corrected, filing released, permit decided, aid conceded, capacity reserved or work made dispatchable without implying the others.

### 5. What was done, and who accepted it?

Possible execution routes include voluntary owner delivery, ARIF or forced public execution, municipal or other public-asset work, private funded work, self-funded work and local in-kind recovery.

Preserve the contemporaneous execution, exception and custody evidence required by the controlling act, acceptance procedure or contract. Exact evidence fields belong to later data mapping.

Acceptance powers remain separate:

- the executor evidences its own performance;
- the beneficiary and technician make any required completion assertions;
- the official inspector or authority records compulsory legal completion;
- the assigned public control body determines regular execution and admissible quantities for funded work;
- private parties decide contractual acceptance, defects and warranty; and
- public payment bodies consume accepted evidence without retroactively performing the work.

Aftercare responsibility is assigned before planting. Administrative installation acceptance does not establish biological survival or productive restoration.

**Bounded result:** name the route-specific result and remaining open work without defining one universal completion status.

### 6. Did the intended result land, and what remains open?

A programme-specific entitlement can open an advance or other permitted claim before accepted installation. Accepted installation separately starts or continues aftercare.

```text
route-specific entitlement
├─ permitted claim → public review → liquidation/order → settlement
│                                             └─ audit/revocation/recovery
└─ accepted installation → aftercare → survival/defect review → replacement/rework
```

Keep these outcomes distinct:

- entitlement or award;
- claim and admitted amount;
- public accounting decision and payment order;
- actual cash settlement;
- administrative installation acceptance;
- biological establishment and productive recovery; and
- later audit, revocation or recovery.

Only settlement evidence proves cash. Public payment can settle while aftercare remains active. A later financial or biological event reopens only the affected work.

Wedge 1’s biological endpoint is bounded establishment: aftercare responsibility is assigned, early survival or defect outcome is reviewed, and any required replacement or cure is completed or left with an explicit unresolved owner. It does not extend into full productive maturity, yield optimization, processing or market realization.

**Bounded result:** the manager knows what actually landed, what remains exposed and who owns the next action.

| **Funding, adhesion and collective inclusion** | Questions 2–3; opportunity population, member/cooperative decisions and capacity allocation |

Municipal roles, permits, source support, exceptions, clocks, aftercare, audit and recovery fit inside these responsibilities and questions. None requires a sixth function.

## Legally independent route families

| Route family | Entry | Independent decisions | Bounded result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Compulsory duty** | Official condition or general prescription | Authority duty; responsible actor and any execution choice; actual public-asset manager; official acceptance; local companion enforcement where valid | Compliance, residual duty, enforcement, indemnity or rework |
| **Voluntary recovery and investment** | Member recovery decision, funding opportunity or local programme | Member participation; cooperative mandate and pursuit; capacity allocation; public decisions where applicable; contracting, dispatch and claim release | Accepted work, any cash settlement, aftercare and recovery exposure |

The two families can meet on the same land and share compatible resources. Neither inherits the other’s authority, evidence or financial consequence.

A handoff never transfers decision authority.

## Coherence rules

1. There is no global case status or completion state.
2. Preparation, decision, execution, acceptance and settlement remain separate.
3. Only decision-changing applicability, clocks, actions and outcomes surface in the operator model; source and ingestion mechanics remain backstage.
4. Evidence requirements travel upstream and shape procurement and execution before money is spent.
5. Concurrent commitments remain separate until an express rule reconciles them.
6. Exceptions return to the affected question and actor; they do not enter a generic exception workflow.
7. Later events reopen only affected work and preserve earlier outcomes.
8. Portals, maps, contractors, CAAs, technicians and delegated executors do not inherit authority they were not granted.
9. Every result is named at its real bounded level.
10. Exact fields, status labels, source mappings, Ontology representation and implementation mechanics remain deferred to Gate 5 and later ordered gates. Noun and relationship grain are owned by the accepted Gate 3–4 authorities.

## Direct-observation boundary

Detailed local practice still requires direct evidence from:

- one cooperative mandate, cohort and capacity-allocation walkthrough;
- one CAA case through source repair, filing and an adverse or repaired route;
- one supplier, contractor and technician field package through acceptance;
- one claim through finance, guarantee, public control and bank settlement or repair;
- one aftercare and survival cohort; and
- one municipal or compulsory case involving publication, public assets, enforcement, obstruction or permit routing.

These observations remain mandatory only where permissionless real evidence cannot establish the required current instance or relationship. Public and operational analogues can establish structure but never a target actor’s private fact. Contradictory evidence selectively reopens the model. Nothing justifies invented behavior or further untargeted broad search.

## Plain-language terms

- **Member:** person, farm or business participating in the cooperative relationship.
- **Beneficiary:** person or business holding the public grant or payment right. In collective Article 6 work, the member remains the beneficiary.
- **Cooperative / OP:** body coordinating members. An OP is a formally recognized producer organization; not every cooperative is an OP.
- **CAA:** authorized agricultural assistance centre that maintains the official farm file and releases agricultural filings under mandate.
- **Fascicolo aziendale:** official agricultural holding file containing identity, land, titles, crops, contacts, bank information and other administrative evidence.
- **Comune:** municipality.
- **Osservatorio:** Regione Puglia’s competent plant-health authority.
- **ARIF:** delegated regional body performing specified monitoring, removal and administrative-instruction work; it is not the plant-health authority.
- **Adhesion:** member application to participate through a named cooperative or OP.
- **Concession:** public act granting aid and fixing approved scope, amount, conditions and deadlines.
- **Verbale:** official field record signed by the assigned inspector or official.
- **Payment application:** beneficiary claim for an allowed advance, interim payment or final balance. Article 6 permits an optional 50% advance and final balance, with no SAL.
- **Liquidation:** public accounting decision recognizing an amount as payable. It is not proof of bank receipt.
- **RUOP:** official register of professional plant operators.
- **VIncA:** *Valutazione di Incidenza Ambientale*, the assessment process for potential effects on Natura 2000 sites.
- **Scorrimento:** movement down a ranked list when budget or available positions allow additional applications to enter review.
