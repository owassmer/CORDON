# Puglia Xylella and agricultural-support payment completion

**Research cut-off:** 20 August 2026  
**Scope:** independent public-web reconstruction. This report prioritizes the Puglia Article 6 replant measure, then ARIF Xylella indemnities, then current Puglia CAP/CSR patterns. It does not assume that one regime's rules apply to another.

## Executive finding

Article 6 is a **regional-budget, two-payment-stage investment grant**, not an AGEA-paid PSR claim. The beneficiary may request a 50% advance secured at 110%, then a final balance. No SAL is allowed. ARIF performs the technical-administrative instruction. The Regional Phytosanitary Observatory adopts the concession and liquidation measures. The regional Accounting and Budget Section executes the accounting flow through SAP-RP and bank transfers to each beneficiary's own account. SIAN supplies the original application/farm-file data, while the regional PMA/EIP portal carries the advance, final balance, variant, and supporting documents.[S1][S2][S3][S4]

A collective application does **not** turn the cooperative into the grant beneficiary. Each participating member remains the beneficiary, signs or co-signs the final claim evidence, provides the payment account, and—if requesting an advance—provides an individual guarantee. The cooperative/OP submits the collective aid application and executes uprooting/replanting under the member's mandate. Its legal representative also signs the final completion declaration. The public rules leave the precise allocation of supplier contracting, invoice addressee, and cash pre-financing to the private mandate; they do not establish that the cooperative receives the member's grant.[S1][S5]

A public claim-level exception path is visible. Advance claim **20210300172110143085** passed ARIF's technical instruction but was held out of regional liquidation because SAP found the IBAN invalid. The beneficiary sent the corrected IBAN by PEC on 18 December 2024. The Region then recorded positive DURC, Article 48-bis tax-debt, and Deggendorf checks, included the claim in DDS 14/2025, generated a mass-payment row, and authorized transfer by bank credit. The public record proves repair through **ordered for payment**, but not the receiving bank's settlement confirmation.[S6][S7]

Current CSR/PSP investment measures use a different control spine: dematerialized SIAN claims, regional instruction and regional liquidation lists, then payment by OP AGEA. They commonly support advances up to 50% with a 100% guarantee, measure-specific SAL and final balance rules, line-level invoice/payment evidence or simplified-cost evidence, in-situ/on-the-spot controls, debtor offsets, and AGEA recovery. These are current Puglia patterns, not retroactive Article 6 rules.[S8][S9][S10][S11]

## 1. Terminology and amount-state discipline

| Term | Plain-language meaning | Authority/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **DdS / domanda di sostegno (aid application)** | Request to enter the scheme and obtain a legal commitment. It is not a request to pay cash. | SIAN application, farm file, ranking, ARIF instruction, regional concession.[S1] |
| **Domanda di adesione** | A member's application to join a cooperative/OP collective application. If not selected into the collective application, the 2020 rules treat it as an individual application.[S1] | Member signs; cooperative later selects mandated members in collective DdS. |
| **DdP / domanda di pagamento** | Beneficiary's post-concession claim for advance or final balance. | Article 6 advance/final forms on PMA/EIP; CSR forms on SIAN.[S2][S3][S9] |
| **Anticipazione** | Advance before final physical completion. Article 6: exactly 50% of granted aid and a guarantee equal to 110% of the requested advance.[S2][S3] | Requested amount is not yet liquidated or paid. |
| **SAL** | Acconto on a certified state of work. | **Not available under Article 6.** Available only where a PSR/CSR intervention expressly allows it.[S2][S9] |
| **Saldo** | Final balance after completion and final control, net of any advance. | Article 6 claim is due within 30 days after the completion deadline.[S2] |
| **Spesa ammissibile** | Expenditure or standardized cost that survives eligibility and control. | Article 6 uses unit-cost tables; final proof still includes invoices and traceable payments for plants.[S1][S2] |
| **Variante** | Material approved change. Article 6 allows change of beneficiary or relocation above 300 plants/ha; advance authorization is required.[S2] | Filed only through PMA/EIP and not in the final three months, except death-related succession. |
| **Adattamento tecnico/economico** | Minor change that does not require prior approval. Article 6 relocation below 300 plants/ha within parcels already in DdS quadro S qualifies.[S2] | Beneficiary and technician assume responsibility; ARIF approves or rejects it during final-claim instruction. |
| **Istruttoria** | Administrative and technical examination of eligibility, claim evidence, amount, and controls. | ARIF for Article 6; regional/territorial offices for CSR, under AGEA procedures.[S2][S8] |
| **Collaudo / regolare esecuzione** | Final determination that funded work was completed correctly. | Article 6: final administrative and field control, beneficiary/technical declaration, photos, plant passports, registrations.[S2] |
| **Liquidazione** | Administrative accounting recognition that a defined amount is payable and authorization to the accounting office. | A liquidation act is **not proof that the bank credited the beneficiary**.[S4][S7] |
| **Massivo / partita-liquidazione** | Structured batch file/row sent into SAP-RP. It carries CUP, commitment and gross amount. | Public attachments to DDS 14 and DDS 23 label rows “Liquidazione e Pagamento.”[S7][S12] |
| **Mandato/ordinativo** | Accounting instruction that causes treasury/bank execution. Public Article 6 acts expose the SAP mass-payment input but not treasury return messages. | Do not equate it with settled payment without bank/treasury evidence. |
| **Pagato** | Funds actually credited to the beneficiary's account. | Requires successful treasury/bank execution or a paying-agency payment-status record. A decree alone is insufficient. |
| **Condizione risolutiva antimafia** | Payment may proceed after statutory waiting conditions while the antimafia result is pending, but a later interdiction defeats entitlement and triggers revocation/recovery. | Article 92(3), D.Lgs. 159/2011 as applied in Article 6 liquidation acts.[S4][S7][S12] |
| **DURC** | Official social-contribution compliance document. | Article 6 concession and payment checks; current CSR payment irregularity may be handled by AGEA debtor compensation instead of blocking payment.[S1][S8][S10] |
| **Verifica 48-bis** | Check for qualifying tax-collection defaults before a public payment. | Performed by the Region in Article 6 liquidation batches.[S6][S7] |
| **Visura Deggendorf** | Check whether the beneficiary owes recovery of unlawful/incompatible State aid. | SIAN State Aid section; negative check is recorded before Article 6 liquidation.[S6][S7] |
| **CUP** | Unique public-project code. It ties the grant, invoices, accounting row and creditor together and helps prevent double funding. | Mandatory on Article 6 evidence and mass-payment rows.[S2][S7] |
| **CAA** | Authorized Agricultural Assistance Centre. | May compile/release SIAN forms under mandate; it does not decide eligibility or payment.[S1][S13] |
| **OP AGEA** | Paying agency for current Puglia CSR/PSR operations. | It executes and accounts for CSR payments and recoveries; it is not the Article 6 payer.[S8] |

### The six amount states

1. **Requested (richiesto):** what the applicant/beneficiary asks for in DdS or DdP.
2. **Technically admitted (ammesso):** what ARIF or the competent instructor recognizes after checking scope, evidence and calculations.
3. **Granted/committed (concesso/impegnato):** the legally committed ceiling in the regional concession act. It may be lower than requested.[S14]
4. **Liquidated (liquidato):** the amount the competent authority recognizes as due and authorizes the accounting office to process.
5. **Ordered (ordinato / massivo di spesa):** the creditor line entered into the payment batch, with CUP, commitment and amount.[S7][S12]
6. **Paid (pagato):** the receiving account has been credited. This requires a successful bank/treasury execution status. None of the public Article 6 liquidation PDFs inspected contains the bank's settlement return.

A system should never collapse these states into one “funding amount.” It should store each amount, its date, act/claim identifier, authority, and evidence separately.

## 3. Article 6 replant workflow

### 3.1 Application, ranking and concession

1. **Beneficiary data foundation.** The applicant validates the AGEA farm file, exact olive-plant count by parcel, title of possession, PEC and payment identity.[S1]
2. **Submission.** A CAA or accredited professional compiles, digitally signs/releases the individual DdS or member adhesion on SIAN. The cooperative later files the collective DdS selecting only mandated members. A valid unselected adhesion becomes an individual application.[S1]
3. **Collective mandate.** Each member approves the initiative, delegates the association's legal representative to file the aid application, and mandates uprooting/replanting. Each member must independently satisfy eligibility. An ineligible member is removed without necessarily defeating the rest of the collective project.[S1]
4. **Ranking.** The Region assigns score and publishes ranking. The act is legal notice. Ranking admission is not concession and not a payable claim.[S1]
5. **ARIF instruction.** ARIF checks beneficiary/parcel conditions, declarations, DURC, serious-offence rules, antimafia threshold, intervention coherence, and computes admissible aid from the Article 6 unit-cost table.[S1]
6. **Adverse path.** Negative findings are sent by PEC under Article 10-bis, Law 241/1990. The applicant may submit observations/re-examination material. Confirmed negative outcome causes exclusion.[S1]
7. **Concession.** The Observatory adopts a serial concession act and commitment for each beneficiary. A 2025 example admits 78 beneficiaries for €841,483.17 after ARIF determinations 33/2025 and 36/2025, with per-beneficiary CUP and State-aid checks.[S14]

### 3.2 Eligible cost and claim-line basis

Article 6 pays for restoration of pre-damage productive capacity, not lost income. Aid is calculated per cadastral parcel from the admitted number of damaged trees and planting density:

| Density | Standard €/tree | Minimum €/m² | Maximum €/m² |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1–100 trees/ha | 75 | — | 0.75 |
| 101–150 | 60 | 0.75 | 0.90 |
| 151–200 | 50 | 0.90 | 1.00 |
| >201 | 40 | 1.00 | 1.50 |

The parcel calculation is raised to the class minimum and capped where applicable. Only new resistant/tolerant olive plants are eligible, at least equal to admitted uprooted trees. No lost-income or operating aid is eligible. VAT is excluded unless non-recoverable.[S1]

This is a **standard-cost entitlement** with actual implementation evidence. It is not an open reimbursement of every contractor invoice. The final claim still carries plant invoices, proof of payment, supplier releases and passports to prove that the standardized output was real and compliant.[S2]

### 3.3 Advance

1. Beneficiary chooses one 50% advance and completes the regional advance DdP on PMA/EIP.[S3]
2. Claim identifies DdS/adhesion, concession, requested amount, beneficiary-owned IBAN and legal declarations.[S3]
3. The beneficiary obtains a bank/insurance guarantee for **110% of the advance**, uploads it and sends the original to the regional measure manager within 10 days.[S3]
4. For collective projects, the **member beneficiary**, not the cooperative, provides the guarantee.[S5]
5. ARIF instructs the DdP and adopts an outcome determination. It checks guarantee, criminal record/antimafia and claim eligibility.[S4][S6]
6. The Region adds fresh DURC, Article 48-bis, Deggendorf and SAP/IBAN checks; unresolved checks can cause temporary exclusion from that serial batch.[S6]
7. Observatory liquidation act authorizes Accounting and Budget, records chapter/commitment, mode “bank transfers,” and creates the SAP-RP mass file.[S6][S7]
8. Only a successful treasury/bank return makes the amount **paid**.

**Guarantee recovery loop.** On full/partial revocation, Region gives the beneficiary 30 days for voluntary repayment. If unpaid, Region calls the guarantor. The guarantor must pay automatically and unconditionally within 30 days of first written demand. Interest runs as prescribed. The guarantee survives for recovery proceedings opened during its validity.[S3]

### 3.4 Final balance

1. Complete all admitted work by the concession deadline; file final DdP within 30 days after that deadline. There is no SAL.[S2]
2. Upload/provide the evidence packet:
   - electronic, tax-compliant plant invoices;
   - supplier release declarations;
   - traceable payment records (bank transfer, non-transferable cashier's cheque, F24, Ri.Ba., postal payment, or bank cheque plus draw evidence);
   - selected beneficiary-owned SIAN farm-file account, with exceptional reasoned substitution allowed after notice;
   - VAT purchase-register extract where available;
   - RUOP nursery plant passports, CAC or “certified” virus-free;
   - joint beneficiary + qualified agricultural technician/works-director declaration of complete, workmanlike, safe execution and at least the admitted number of trees;
   - DSAN proving registration of Leccino/FS17 planting on emergenzaxylella.it;
   - DSAN proving submission of the territorial completion notice;
   - hydrogeological PAI compatibility adhesion or non-applicability statement;
   - antimafia authorization plus member/cohabitant declarations where required;
   - final/adaptation report, georeferenced photographs and mapped viewpoints.[S2]
3. Collective member adds the cooperative/OP legal representative's signature to the joint declaration and files the original mandate evidence.[S1][S2]
4. ARIF checks every claim administratively and may perform in-loco controls. It records admitted or partially admitted amount, approved adaptation, field findings and legal checks in the instruction determination/checklist.[S2]
5. Region performs supplementary accounting/legal checks and issues a serial final-liquidation act. The act can hold some claims for pending DURC, 48-bis, antimafia waiting period, invalid IBAN, or accounting mismatch, while liquidating the rest.[S4][S12]
6. SAP-RP mass row carries CUP, commitment and gross amount. Transfer is directed to each beneficiary's current account.[S7][S12]
7. Bank return determines settled payment or repair queue.

### 3.5 Variant, adaptation and extension

- **Variant:** beneficiary change or relocation above 300 trees/ha. Filed only in PMA/EIP after concession becomes effective; normally barred during the last three months. Supporting packet includes EIP receipt, company/adviser DSANs, antimafia/family declarations, beneficiary/technician/cooperative report, title/owner consents, PAI material, and authorized irrigation evidence for dense relocation.[S2]
- **Adaptation:** minor change or relocation below 300 trees/ha, only among parcels already in DdS quadro S. Beneficiary and technician decide without prior approval, then justify at final DdP; cooperative representative also signs for collective projects. ARIF records approval in the final-claim checklist. Rejection reduces the corresponding aid line after a reasoned notice and opportunity for counter-observations.[S2]
- **Virus-free extension:** one extension up to 18 months where certified plants are unavailable. It requires a digitally signed supply contract with a RUOP operator for at least the admitted number of plants, filed at least 30 days before deadline. ARIF/Region verifies it and sends outcome by PEC. Failure to use certified plants removes two ranking points and can push the claim out of the funded range, causing revocation and recovery.[S15]
- **Portal-repair extension:** in 2025 the Region reopened EIP for 60 days for older concessions where work had finished on time but no final claim had been filed. This is an administrative repair window, not an automatic waiver of completion conditions.[S18]

### 4.1 Claim 20210300172110143085: invalid IBAN repaired

| Date/act | Publicly provable event | State |

| 10 Feb 2025, DDS 14/2025 | Observatory includes the repaired claim among previously held claims, authorizes liquidation of a €983,970.24 advance batch, and transmits SAP-RP mass-payment data to Accounting and Budget.[S7] | Liquidated and ordered |
| Bank/treasury return | Not published in the inspected act. | **Paid status unresolved** |

This path proves that an invalid account does not necessarily destroy an admitted claim. The administrative repair loop is: **SAP validation failure → beneficiary PEC request → corrected IBAN → rerun DURC/48-bis/Deggendorf → next serial liquidation act → new mass-payment row**.[S6][S7]

### 4.2 Other observable repair patterns

- Pending DURC or Article 48-bis results: hold claim out of one batch; insert in a later batch after positive checks.[S6][S7]
- Antimafia waiting period not yet elapsed: defer until Article 92 timing permits; where permitted, liquidate under resolutory condition.[S4][S12]
- Invalid IBAN at final balance: request corrected account; a different 2025 batch shows the same repair pattern for claim 2021030095613010139923.[S12]
- Accounting-system mismatch: suspend and redetermine exact balance before liquidation.[S12]
- Virus-free score failure: remove points; if ranking falls below funded cut-off, do not liquidate and start revocation/recovery.[S12]
- Missing instruction delegation: ARIF notified claimants by PEC and public portal, gave 10 days, then archived non-responsive DdS for lack of interest. Region later took formal notice by DDS 40/2026.[S16]

### 4.3 What the public path does not prove

The public mass annex links a CUP/commitment/amount to a liquidation row, but the privacy-protected creditor sheet is not published and the bank's execution return is absent. A full claim-to-**paid** proof therefore requires access to one of:

- SAP-RP/treasury payment-order status and return code;
- beneficiary payment receipt/bank statement;
- regional creditor ledger with settlement date;
- access-to-documents production of the non-public anagraphic-accounting sheet and treasury outcome.

## 5. Collective versus member responsibility

### Binding findings

1. Cooperative/OP files the collective DdS; each farmer first files an adhesion.[S1][S5]
2. Member approves the initiative, delegates filing, and mandates the collective body to execute uprooting/replanting.[S1]
3. Every member independently owns eligibility. One member can be removed without defeating remaining members.[S1]
4. The member is the grant beneficiary. FAQ 25 expressly assigns the member its own advance guarantee.[S5]
5. The member files the payment claim. At final balance, member and technician sign; cooperative legal representative also signs for collective work.[S1][S2]
6. Public payment is by transfer to each beneficiary's own account. Cooperative identity in the collective DdS does not establish a right to receive members' grants.[S3][S7]

### Contracting/payment responsibility that remains private

The public rules say the cooperative “contributes to” and is mandated to execute the works under conditions agreed by the parties.[S5] They do not publish the standard mandate or require one universal commercial arrangement. Therefore:

- the cooperative may centralize nursery procurement and works contracts **if the mandate says so**;
- the member may remain invoice addressee and payer, or the mandate may create reimbursement/netting arrangements;
- claim acceptance still requires a coherent chain from the member's admitted trees to invoice, traceable payment, plant passport, completion declaration and public payment account;
- the grant must not be modelled as payable to the cooperative unless the specific creditor sheet or mandate proves an assignment/delegated collection allowed by law.

**Verdict:** “collective application means cooperative is contractor and payee” is **not proved**. “Collective application means member is beneficiary while cooperative executes under mandate” is **proved**.[S1][S2][S5]

## 6. Claim-line evidence model, in plain language

A claim line should answer nine questions without relying on a narrative PDF:

1. **What was granted?** Link the concession act, beneficiary, DdS/adhesion, CUP, parcel, admitted damaged-tree count, density class and granted unit cost.
2. **What happened physically?** Record uprooted trees, replanted resistant/tolerant trees, variety, parcel, planting date, density and any relocation.
3. **Who asserted completion?** Store beneficiary signature, technician/works-director signature, and cooperative legal representative signature where collective.
4. **What was bought?** Capture supplier, invoice ID/date, tree quantity/variety/category and CUP text.
5. **Was it paid traceably?** Link each invoice to bank/Ri.Ba./other permitted payment and supplier release. Store payer and payee names; flag mismatch with beneficiary/mandate.
6. **Is the plant material legal and traceable?** Link RUOP supplier, plant-passport lot, CAC/certified status, virus-free evidence and quantity reconciliation.
7. **Does location match?** Link cadastral parcel, concession quadro S, georeferenced photos, plan, completion notice, PAI/owner consent and emergenzaxylella registration.
8. **What did the instructor admit?** Store ARIF checklist result per line: eligible quantity, rejected quantity/reason, approved variant/adaptation, inspection date and admitted amount.
9. **What happened to money?** Keep requested, admitted, granted, liquidated, ordered and paid amounts/dates separately; attach regional act, mass row, bank return, offset/recovery and appeal status.

### Minimum machine-readable line

`beneficiary → DdS/adhesion → concession/CUP → parcel → admitted trees/density/unit cost → invoice/passport lot → payment evidence → completion signatures/photos → ARIF result → regional liquidation row → treasury execution → recovery/appeal`

A cooperative case adds:

`member mandate → collective DdS → cooperative execution/signature → supplier-contract payer/invoice allocation → member public-payment creditor`

The model must permit many invoices and payment records per claim line, and one invoice/passport lot to allocate across multiple parcels, but every allocation must reconcile to a declared quantity and amount.

## 7. ARIF eradication indemnities

### 7.1 Current 2024–2026 regime

DGR 994/2024 places owner/tenant indemnities for Xylella eradication/control and damage under ARIF management. Eligible owner/tenant businesses are located in delimited areas. Maximum aid is €600,000 per beneficiary, cumulative aid cannot exceed 100%, insurance receipts are deducted, and payment is direct to the affected enterprise. Outstanding EU State-aid recovery orders exclude the enterprise.[S17]

A 2025 eradication order exposes the current operational evidence chain:

1. official monitoring/lab result and cadastral identification;
2. Observatory eradication DDS with owners/parcels;
3. seven-day public notice and PEC where available;
4. owner chooses voluntary removal or ARIF through the SPID portal within the stated window;
5. voluntary work or ARIF work occurs under phytosanitary supervision;
6. inspector/agent/ARIF assistant draws up the removal **verbale**, expressly described as indispensable for the contribution;
7. owner requests contribution through the ARIF SPID portal;
8. ARIF instructs, records State aid and liquidates directly to the enterprise.[S19][S17]

The older public ARIF guide adds the underlying identity/delegation pattern: application identifies beneficiary status, tax code, DDS, identity document and IBAN; voluntary removal uses DDS Annex B, ARIF removal uses Annex C, and a DSAN accompanies the claim. A tenant can delegate an owner; co-owners can delegate one co-owner to sign, deliver documents and collect the whole indemnity; an owner can delegate the tenant where cultivation is held by another person.[S20]

### 7.2 Key distinction from Article 6

| Article 6 replant grant | ARIF eradication indemnity |
|---|---|
| Restores productive capacity through resistant/tolerant replant. | Compensates mandatory eradication/control costs and damage. |
| Region grants and pays; ARIF instructs. | ARIF manages instruction and liquidation for owner/tenant indemnities.[S17] |
| Advance + final balance, no SAL. | Triggered by eradication DDS, supervised removal and verbale; not an investment SAL chain. |
| Unit cost by admitted damaged trees and density. | Current DGR 994 uses species/damage methodology; capped at €600,000 and net of insurance.[S17] |
| Cooperative collective route exists. | Direct-to-enterprise owner/tenant route with co-owner/owner/tenant delegations.[S17][S20] |
| Cannot be double-funded for same material damage. | Cumulation capped at 100%; insurance deducted.[S1][S17] |

The older DGR 2164/2021 expressly denied the **removal-cost** component where ARIF performed removal, while still distinguishing damage compensation.[S21] DGR 994/2024 supersedes the State-aid basis and methodology. The inspected current text does not repeat that sentence in the short procedure article, so current claim implementation should check the ARIF calculation record rather than importing the 2021 rule mechanically.

### 7.3 ARIF payment evidence gaps

No public ARIF determination was found that joins one 2024/2025 SPID claim to a liquidation mandate, IBAN validation, bank return, failed transfer and reissue. ARIF's organizational page confirms that its administration issues payment mandates, but the public Xylella pages expose instruction and eligibility more readily than treasury execution.[S22]

## 8. Current CAP/CSR payment pattern (comparison only)

### 8.1 Common NO-SIGC investment sequence

A current Puglia CSR investment pattern is:

1. beneficiary updates SIAN farm file;
2. CAA/professional compiles; beneficiary signs with OTP; operator releases/protocols DdS;
3. EIP/PMA carries the detailed investment plan and documents;
4. Region ranks and instructs; concession fixes eligible expenditure, grant, CUP, deadlines and conditions;
5. beneficiary submits advance/SAL/final DdP as the intervention permits;
6. regional offices perform administrative, in-situ and selected on-the-spot controls and calculate eligible expenditure/support;
7. Region creates the regional liquidation list;
8. OP AGEA executes/accounting payment;
9. AGEA releases guarantee after regional authorization and final regularity;
10. ex-post control or detected overpayment triggers debtor registration, offset or recovery.[S8][S9][S10][S13]

A 2026 Puglia SRD01 olive-sector concession makes the handoff explicit: DdP is OTP-signed/released on SIAN, guarantee or final evidence is uploaded before release, PEC notifies territorial and intervention officials, BDNA is checked for each DdP, and the operation uses simplified costs while retaining CUP-bearing invoices; OP AGEA is the payer.[S23]

### 8.2 Advance, SAL, guarantee and final balance

- Common maximum advance: 50%.
- Private guarantee: commonly 100% of the requested CSR advance (measure-specific); the SIAN PGI generates the personalized guarantee.[S9]
- SAL: measure-specific. A PSR 4.1.B procedural act allows up to two SALs, requires at least 30% for the first, and reconciles advance + SAL limits.[S9]
- Final balance: due after completion with invoice/payment register, dedicated-account extract, technical accounting, certificates, plans and final regular-execution checks.[S9]
- Public guarantee bridge: Puglia's late-2024 fund temporarily covered certain PSR advance claims lacking private guarantees, but required later replacement; this emergency mechanism is not a general CSR entitlement.[S24]

### 8.3 Procurement and payment evidence

For private beneficiaries, “procurement” usually means reasonableness-of-cost controls, not public procurement law. Current SRD05 requires dematerialized SIAN supplier quotations and a reasoned choice unless the lowest quote is selected. Unsupported quotations make the related cost ineligible.[S10]

Actual-cost claims typically require tax-valid, registered, CUP-bearing invoices or equivalent documents, traceable payments, supplier releases, dedicated-account statements, DDT where relevant, VAT register, and technical completion evidence. Simplified-cost interventions use physical/output evidence to calculate the grant; invoices can remain mandatory for CUP/double-funding control even when invoice value is not the grant base.[S9][S23]

Public beneficiaries follow the public procurement code and must provide tender/award/contract/SAL/final-account and conformity/certificate records. This public-procurement chain should not be imposed on private Article 6 members without a legal basis.

### 8.4 Debt, DURC, antimafia and recovery

Current Puglia CSR acts distinguish concession from payment. At concession, an irregular DURC can trigger a 60-day cure and then exclusion. At DdP stage, negative DURC does not necessarily block payment because OP AGEA may compensate INPS debt recorded in the national debtor register.[S10]

For payments above applicable thresholds, antimafia declarations support an ex officio BDNA request. A later interdiction causes revocation and recovery.[S9][S23]

AGEA must recover undue payments. Its current instructions describe an order-to-repay period up to 60 days, then interest until repayment or deduction; payment-stage controls may also offset INPS debt and check tax-collection default for payments above €5,000.[S11]

### 8.5 Failed-payment repair

AGEA payments require a correct, active beneficiary-owned IBAN not duplicated improperly across farm files. The public AGEA reissue form allows the beneficiary to provide a corrected IBAN for **riemissione del pagamento**, with bank certification and a duty to report later changes.[S25]

This is comparable to, but legally distinct from, Article 6's regional SAP repair path.

## 9. Exception and recovery loops

### Article 6

- **Missing evidence:** ARIF request/negative finding → PEC observations/re-examination → admit, partially admit or exclude.[S1]
- **Variant rejected:** reasoned notice → counter-observations → original project remains binding; failure can revoke and recover advance.[S2]
- **Adaptation rejected:** notice to beneficiary and technician (plus cooperative in collective case) → counter-observations → reduce affected parcel/line.[S2]
- **Fewer trees uprooted:** reduce grant. Fewer resistant/tolerant trees replanted than admitted uprooted trees makes final DdP inadmissible. Extra trees do not increase grant.[S2]
- **Virus-free score lost:** subtract points → rerank → if no longer fundable, revoke and recover.[S2][S15]
- **Pending DURC/48-bis:** defer claim to later serial liquidation; do not label denied.[S6][S7]
- **Pending antimafia:** defer or, once lawful, liquidate under resolutory condition; later interdiction revokes and recovers.[S4][S7]
- **Wrong IBAN:** request corrected IBAN by PEC → rerun checks → next liquidation/mass batch.[S6][S7]
- **Revocation after advance:** beneficiary has 30 days to repay; then guarantor pays on first demand within 30 days; legal interest and recovery costs apply.[S3]
- **Ex-post irregularity:** administrative/in-loco/police finding can revoke and recover principal plus legal interest.[S1][S2]
- **Appeal:** any measure is challengeable administratively or judicially under general law; the bando does not publish a special Article 6 appeal form or fixed internal appeal ladder.[S1]

### ARIF indemnities

- **No owner response:** ARIF removes within the order's deadline; forced removal can charge costs to owner and defeat contribution where the order says so.[S19]
- **Co-owner/owner-tenant conflict:** require explicit delegation to one claimant/collector.[S20]
- **No supervision/verbale:** contribution evidence chain is incomplete.[S19]
- **Prior State-aid recovery order or overcompensation:** exclude/deduct; insurance is subtracted.[S17]

### CSR/AGEA

- **Excess claim:** if requested amount exceeds admitted amount beyond the statutory threshold, a sanction may equal the difference; ineligible lines found in later controls also reduce payment.[S10]
- **DURC debt:** AGEA offset where applicable.[S10][S11]
- **Invalid IBAN:** correct farm file/account and file reissue communication.[S25]
- **Overpayment:** debtor record, order to repay, interest, set-off against later aid, or enforcement.[S11]
- **Post-payment breach:** ex-post control → reduction/revocation → AGEA recovery; current CSR measures often bar applicants with an unrepaid final revocation in the prior five years.[S10]

## 10. Assertion verdicts

| Assertion | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Article 6 has advance, SAL and final balance. | **Contradicted.** | DDS 44 says no SAL; only advance and final balance.[S2] |
| AGEA pays Article 6 beneficiaries. | **Contradicted.** | Region is payer; acts authorize Regional Accounting and SAP-RP bank transfers.[S1][S7] |
| ARIF decides technical admissibility for Article 6. | **Verified, with qualification.** | ARIF instructs and adopts result determinations; Observatory adopts legal concession/liquidation acts.[S2][S4] |
| Cooperative receives all collective grant money. | **Contradicted / unsupported.** | Member remains beneficiary, owns guarantee and own account; cooperative executes under mandate.[S3][S5] |
| Collective member can disappear behind one aggregate cooperative claim. | **Contradicted.** | Member-level adhesion, eligibility, CUP/creditor, signatures and amount remain auditable.[S1][S2] |
| Article 6 reimburses any actual replant invoice amount. | **Contradicted.** | Grant uses parcel/tree density unit costs; actual documents prove implementation, not an uncapped cost reimbursement.[S1][S2] |
| A liquidation decree proves payment. | **Contradicted.** | It proves administrative liquidation and order input; no bank settlement result is published.[S7][S12] |
| Invalid IBAN always causes loss of grant. | **Contradicted.** | Public repair path shows PEC correction and inclusion in next liquidation act.[S6][S7] |
| Pending antimafia always blocks payment. | **Contradicted.** | Article 92(3) resolutory-condition payments appear after statutory timing; later negative result can unwind payment.[S4][S7] |
| Current CSR rules can fill every Article 6 gap. | **Contradicted.** | Different payer, portals, guarantees, SAL and recovery architecture. CSR is only a labeled comparison.[S8][S10] |
| ARIF indemnity and Article 6 replant are the same claim. | **Contradicted.** | Different trigger, authority, amount method and evidence; cumulation/double-funding controls still matter.[S1][S17] |
| Every Article 6 final claim is administratively controlled. | **Verified.** | DDS 44 says all admitted claims receive administrative control; field/in-loco control can also occur.[S2] |
| Public evidence reveals a full claim-to-bank-credit path. | **Not verified.** | It reaches mass payment order, not receiving-bank settlement. |
