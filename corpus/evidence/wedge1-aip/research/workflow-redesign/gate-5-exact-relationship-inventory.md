## Governing rules

- Every fact-bearing atomic predicate uses an exact domain-named object-backed relationship.
- Factless context uses a direct link.
- One object-backed relationship may expose concise endpoint traversal and detailed traversal from one truth owner.
- No family-level Actor Assignment, Instrument Effect, Scope, Proceeding Relationship, Material Relationship or Lot Accountability object exists.
- Consolidation requires materially identical endpoint contract, fact shape, lifecycle, authority, Actions and security meaning.
- Decision-Capable Actor permits Natural Person or Organization only where the six dimensions remain uniform. Concrete identity remains visible.
- Unknown private facts remain unknown.

## Factless direct-link boundary

Direct links remain only where no independent basis, scope, period, quantity, powers, duties, custody or history exists:

- valid Individual Plant → Cultivar;
- Plant Trade Unit / Lot → Cultivar;
- Plant/Lot → originating Organization, conditionally factless;
- Public Proceeding → Public Programme or Measure context;
- Public Proceeding → factless subject;
- Public Proceeding → factless participant;
- Public Proceeding → current deciding Organization pointer;
- Governing Instrument → Programme/Proceeding/Intervention/Official Area context;
- Intervention → Programme/Proceeding context.

A direct link is not stored separately when it is merely the concise traversal of an object-backed relationship.
