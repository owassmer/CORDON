## Decision

The minimum graph contains the nine accepted operator objects, the accepted `Intervention Capacity Commitment` relationship object, and **21 direct semantic links**. Every other useful connection is a derived traversal, a qualified fact or consequential history detail on an accepted endpoint, an external outcome, or backstage evidence.

The reconciliation adds `Cooperative Pursuit`, administrative Area hierarchy/jurisdiction, Instrument and Proceeding lineage/basis links, and replaces the unsafe Programme–Intervention pursuit shortcut. The accepted graph contains 32 direct links. Use the reconciled graph rather than this lane count.

A direct link below states only that its two endpoints have the named current domain connection. Any role, basis, scope, period, quantity, authority, decision, occurrence or outcome remains qualified on the smallest accepted endpoint that owns the operator thread. This preserves the former relationship fact without creating another independently navigable relationship object.

`Individual Plant` and every Plant link remain conditional on external identity preservation or a validated reproducible observation-derived inventory. Arbitrary detections create neither a Plant nor a Plant link.

This artifact selects semantics only. It does not select cardinality, source, keys, platform mechanics, materialization or operator surface.

## Convenience and transitive duplicates rejected

The following are not admitted as direct links or relationship objects:

- **Party controls Parcel through Holding.** The traversal is useful for discovery but proves no tenure, consent or access.
- **Holding belongs to Official Area.** Derive through its current Parcels and the authority-grounded applicability determination.
- **Plant belongs to Official Area.** Derive through its Parcel; preserve tree-specific Proceedings where the Plant itself is the formal subject.
- **Programme applies to Parcel, Holding, Plant or Party.** Derive proposition-specific applicability from Instruments, Official Areas, subject facts and date; do not turn population evaluation into a legal truth.
- **Instrument governs Parcel/Plant/Intervention.** Derive the exact path through Area, Proceeding or Programme and retain the qualified effect; reject one vague `governs` shortcut.
- **Proceeding concerns Official Area.** Derive from basis Instruments or subject Parcels unless a later operator workflow proves the Area itself must be the independently selected formal subject.
- **Holding owns an Intervention.** Derive contextual population membership through Parcel scope; direct responsibility belongs to Operator Party↔Intervention.
- **Party is an Intervention’s capacity provider.** Derive only through `Intervention Capacity Commitment`.
- **Member is beneficiary because a collective Proceeding exists.** Preserve the received member/public outcome and exact Proceeding role; do not infer it transitively.
- **Intervention is eligible, selected, ready, blocked, accepted, paid or complete.** These are derived or bounded outcome projections, never links.
- **Programme-funded Parcel.** A Programme route, public outcome, accepted work, claim and cash remain separate; no one edge may summarize them.
- **Generic Actor Assignment, Governing Instrument Effect, Proceeding Relationship, Intervention Scope, Material Relationship, Case, Task, Status or Handoff object.** Exact endpoint links and qualified owning histories preserve the needed semantics with less operator complexity.

## Coverage of the accepted operator harness

### Six questions

1. **What changed, and what does it affect?** `Determine Affected Decisions` traverses the member/Holding/Parcel/Plant spine, governing bases, Programme/Proceeding/Intervention threads and Capacity Commitments; typed histories identify the changed premise and preserve unaffected work.
2. **What is now required, allowed or blocked?** Governing Instrument–Area–Programme–Proceeding links, Party authority links and derived Parcel/Area applicability preserve proposition, authority, date, clock and scoped hold without treating geometry as law.
3. **Who chooses, who decides, and which route continues?** Party links and qualified role/authority histories distinguish member, representative, cooperative, authority, creditor, executor and next owner; Programme pursuit, public outcome and Capacity Commitment remain separate.
4. **Is the route ready for the named action?** `Assess Named-Action Readiness` traverses exact Parcel/Plant scope, Instrument/Proceeding bases, responsible Parties, Programme route and real Capacity Commitments; a direct link never upgrades context into permission or readiness.
5. **What was done, and who accepted it?** Intervention history preserves dispatch, partial/multiple performance, inspection and separate route-specific acceptance, each attributed through direct Party and scope links.
6. **Did the intended result land, and what remains open?** Proceeding financial history and Intervention acceptance/establishment/remedy history remain independent; direct links identify the affected member, land, undertaking and next owner for selective reopening.

### Four manager Actions

- **Accept Cooperative Execution Mandate:** Party membership, Party–Holding, Party–Parcel standing and Instrument–Party authority links establish the bounded actors and land context; mandate terms and acceptance remain Instrument/Party history.
- **Decide Cooperative Pursuit:** Programme–Intervention, member/cooperative Party, Holding/Parcel scope and fallback facts establish the exact pre-filing route without implying capacity or public approval.
- **Commit or Rebalance Intervention Capacity:** Capacity Commitment is the sole mutable relationship truth between the owning Party and affected Interventions; portfolio recommendation remains read-only.
- **Dispatch Intervention:** Intervention–Party, –Parcel and conditional –Plant links identify dispatcher, executor and target; Governing/Proceeding/Programme traversals plus Capacity Commitments supply readiness premises without any one link claiming dispatchability.

### Three Functions

- **Determine Affected Decisions** uses the direct graph and consequential histories to return only dependent decisions and unaffected work.
- **Assess Named-Action Readiness** evaluates exact authority, target, basis, route, access, design, permit, capacity, finance, clock and evidence premises without creating a generic ready state.
- **Compare Feasible Intervention Portfolios** starts from the complete Programme–Intervention population, traverses member/land context and real Capacity Commitments, and produces a non-binding recommendation rather than a relationship mutation.

### Handoffs

A handoff is not an object or link. It is a qualified consequential history detail on the Governing Instrument, Public Proceeding or Intervention that records the achieved bounded effect, involved member/land/route/Intervention scope, producing actor and authority, minimum evidence reference, next named action, next owner, active clock and selective-reopening trigger. Direct Party links make the current and next owner addressable; the handoff never transfers authority or claims whole-route completion.

## Deletion verdict

The 21 links are the minimum direct semantic adjacency needed to keep the accepted objects operationally connected. Deleting any one causes at least one of these failures: loss of exact member/land identity, loss of legal or formal basis, loss of actor/authority resolution, loss of pre-filing opportunity population, loss of field target or accepted-work traceability, loss of conditional tree sameness, or loss of atomic capacity commitment.

Every omitted shortcut is recoverable by traversal or computation without losing an operator decision.
