# Puglia Article 6 collective replanting: reconstructed A.P.OL. case lifecycle

**Research date:** 20–21 August 2026  
**Scope:** public-web reconstruction of one real collective case under Article 6 of Interministerial Decree (D.I.) 2484/2020, without relying on local repositories or prior research.  
**Case spine:** collective support application (**domanda di aiuto collettiva**) no. **7270094464**, filed by **A.P.OL. Associazione tra Produttori Olivicoli – Società Cooperativa Agricola**, and one of its ten adhesion applications, **Maddalena Accogli, domanda di adesione no. 7260005785**. The public chain reaches a named concession but no public Accogli-specific advance or final-balance payment.

## Bottom line

The load-bearing proposition is **verified, with a legal-boundary qualification**. A member first files an adhesion application naming an OP/cooperative. When the collective window opens, the OP/cooperative sees the adhesions associated with it and can select only some for the collective application. The FAQ expressly says this choice follows the negotiations between the parties and reflects the adhesions for which the collective intends to exercise the mandate. Correctly filed adhesions not selected become individual aid applications automatically.[S1][S2][S3]

This is genuine selection, but the public instruments do **not** create an unlimited, reason-free discretion. Expressly legitimate bases are: (i) the adherent must belong to the collective's membership base; (ii) the parties must have reached terms for the collective to exercise the mandate; and (iii) the €2 million collective cap can force exclusions under the FAQ's prescribed ordering/rework rule. The public corpus does not state whether a collective may reject a member for any other commercial or operational reason, whether reasons must be recorded, or whether the member has a direct challenge against that private selection. Those are unresolved gaps, not permissions.[S2]

The cooperative/OP is the **collective applicant and mandated operator**, but **never the aid beneficiary merely by filing collectively**. Every selected adherent remains the beneficiary; concession, guarantee, payment application, payment, recovery exposure, and most duties attach to the individual. The collective's legal representative remains a mandatory co-signatory/participant for collective operations, variants/adaptations, and final evidence.[S1][S2][S8][S9]

## Terms (Italian → operational meaning)

- **D.I.** — *Decreto interministeriale*, interministerial decree.
- **Avviso / bando** — public call setting the aid rules.
- **Domanda di sostegno / domanda di aiuto (DdS)** — support/aid application.
- **Domanda di adesione** — an individual landholder's adhesion record used to seek participation through a named collective; if not recalled by that collective, it operates as an individual aid application.
- **Domanda di aiuto collettiva** — the collective aid application filed by an eligible producer association and populated by selected adhesions.
- **OP** — *Organizzazione di Produttori*, a recognized producer organisation. In the FAQ, “OP” can also appear in the unrelated plant-passport sense of *operatore professionale*; context distinguishes them.
- **Cooperativa olearia** — olive-oil agricultural cooperative engaged in transforming olives into oil.
- **CUAA** — *Codice Unico di Identificazione Aziendale*, the enterprise's fiscal identifier used in agricultural systems.
- **Fascicolo aziendale** — the official SIAN farm file: identity, land tenure, parcel/crop and payment data.
- **SIAN** — *Sistema Informativo Agricolo Nazionale*, the national agricultural information system.
- **CAA** — authorised agricultural assistance centre.
- **Rilascio** — formal electronic submission; it starts the administrative procedure and assigns protocol.
- **Mandato** — mandate authorising the collective to carry out uprooting/replanting operations; distinct from the CAA/professional filing mandate.
- **Delega** — delegation/authorisation, used in several senses: a professional's authority to access the farm file and file; the member's authority to the collective's legal representative; and later EIP delegation required to let ARIF start an individual file's review.
- **ARIF** — Puglia's Regional Agency for Irrigation and Forestry Activities; delegated technical-administrative reviewer, not the concession authority.
- **Istruttoria tecnico-amministrativa** — eligibility, consistency, contribution and compliance review.
- **Graduatoria** — ranked list.
- **Scorrimento** — progression down a ranked list when resources permit; invitation to review is not itself a grant.
- **Atto di concessione** — regional act admitting a beneficiary and granting a specified amount.
- **DdP** — *domanda di pagamento*, payment application.
- **Anticipo / saldo** — advance / final balance.
- **Fideiussione** — bank or insurance guarantee.
- **Variante** — material post-award change requiring a formal application/approval.
- **Adattamento tecnico/economico** — minor technical/economic adjustment normally reviewed with the final-balance file rather than pre-authorised.
- **Subentro** — substitution of a successor/cessionary into the application or granted project.
- **Rinuncia / recesso** — voluntary withdrawal.
- **Decadenza / revoca** — forfeiture of entitlement / withdrawal of the grant; may trigger recovery with interest.
- **PEC** — certified electronic mail, legally effective notice channel.

## Real case reconstruction: A.P.OL. 7270094464 / Accogli 7260005785

### 1. Formation and adhesion (October–November 2020)

1. The landholder must have a validated farm file and eligible tenure/land. A single adherent files a **domanda di adesione** through SIAN, via a mandated CAA or delegated accredited professional. The official deadline window was 5 October–5 November 2020; release required electronic signature. The later collective window was 6–20 November 2020.[S1]
2. The SIAN manual shows the adhesion record contains the beneficiary's anagraphic/bank/legal-representative data; **Quadro B1** identifies the target association by CUAA and name; **Quadro S** contains eligible farm-file parcels and uproot/replant counts; **Quadro K** contains declarations and commitments. Release changes the record from draft/printed into “Rilasciato” and starts the procedure.[S3]
3. Before an adhesion could name it, A.P.OL. needed at least an anagraphic farm file. The member also had to approve the initiative, delegate the association's legal representative to file the collective application, and grant the mandate to perform uprooting/replanting.[S1]

### 2. The collective's inclusion decision

1. In collective compilation, the OP/cooperative sees the adhesion filers associated with it in **Quadro B1**, with CUAA and name.[S2]
2. It can select only some. The official explanation is: only members of its own social base, and only those for whom, following negotiations, it intends to exercise the received operational mandate.[S2]
3. Non-members cannot be included. For recognised OPs, SIAN blocked release by non-members; for cooperatives, a non-member's adhesion was treated as individual.[S2]
4. Correctly filed but unselected adhesions automatically became individual applications; the same happened for mistaken association CUAA.[S1][S2]
5. A.P.OL. selected ten adhesions. Accogli's no. 7260005785 was among them.[S5]

### 3. Ranking (17–25 February 2021)

The Region ranked the **collective application**, not A.P.OL. as a grant beneficiary. A.P.OL. no. 7270094464 placed **1st of 26**, with 10 adherents, criterion scores 20 + 15 + 42 + 10 + 2 = **89**, requested amount **€659,870**, and cumulative amount €659,870.[S4]

Annex C allocated the ranked collective to its ten adhesion records. Accogli's row showed **€23,960.25 requested**. Other A.P.OL adhesions ranged from €3,225 to €240,444.05.[S5]

**Eligibility timing:** this ranking was based on receivability and declared/scored facts, not final grant eligibility. The call required each adherent to possess the individual conditions; an ineligible adherent could be removed while the rest of the collective survived. Only applications in resource position progressed to technical-administrative review.[S1]

### 4. Review and authority split (2021–2023)

- The Region formally delegated support for technical-administrative review of support and payment files to ARIF in DGR 1397/2021. The Region retained administrative autonomy and issued ranking, concession, payment and revocation decisions.[S6]
- Review checks included eligibility, declarations, DURC contribution regularity, coherence with eligible intervention, and calculation of admissible aid. Negative preliminary outcomes had to be sent by PEC under Article 10-bis of Law 241/1990, with a right to seek reconsideration; confirmed negatives were excluded. Aid could be reduced after review.[S1]
- Published later acts reveal actual rework loops: Regione asked ARIF to resolve contradictory annex placement, verify active-farmer status, supply antimafia results, and correct system-calculation errors before concession.[S10]

### 5. Concession to the individual (22 September 2023)

DDS 104/2023 did **not** grant A.P.OL. a lump sum. It granted 20 named adherents across several collectives. For A.P.OL., Accogli (adhesion 7260005785) received **€12,900**, Covani €6,836 and Mirelli €15,500. Accogli's final concession was therefore **€11,060.25 below** the amount in the 2021 adhesion annex. The act states that admissible aid may be recalculated after review; it does not publish the parcel-level explanation for Accogli's reduction.[S5][S10]

The concession required, within **30 days of PEC communication**, specified EIP documents (uprooting communication declaration, basin-plan adherence or non-pertinence, and applicable owner/spouse authorisations). Missing owner/spouse authorisation caused beneficiary-specific forfeiture/revocation. Works had to finish within **24 months** of communication, subject to later deferrals/extensions. Principle 5 failure could remove two points, rerank and revoke if no longer in a fundable position.[S10]

### 6. Advance, implementation and final balance

- Each individual beneficiary could request a **50% advance**. Each, including a collective adherent, had to provide their own guarantee equal to **110% of the advance**. The original guarantee had to reach the Region within **10 days** after the online advance request. Release occurs one year after final inspection.[S2][S7]
- At final balance, the beneficiary—not A.P.OL.—files within **30 days after the work-completion date** in PMA/EIP. No interim SAL payment is allowed. Evidence includes invoices and traceable payment, plant passports, joint beneficiary/technical-director completion statement, registrations/communications, environmental/antimafia documents and final technical/photo report.[S8]
- For a collective adherent, the collective legal representative also signs the completion and relevant environmental/variant/adaptation documents; the file must prove the uproot/replant mandate. Missing mandate evidence causes forfeiture and revocation.[S1][S8]
- A public 2024 liquidation act confirms the operational sequence: ARIF payment review → regional checks (DURC, tax-collection defaults, state-aid/antimafia) → regional liquidation. Files can be held for missing/ambiguous environmental confirmation, missing liquidation amount, antimafia clock, invalid IBAN or pending DURC; the act says later liquidation follows after cure/positive outcome.[S12]

**Case endpoint:** no indexed public payment act could be tied specifically to Accogli's support/adhesion number or CUP B75B20008010001. Public evidence therefore supports `CONCEDED; payment status unresolved`, not “paid.”

## Inclusion-decision analysis

### Exact sourced facts

1. The collective application recalls the adhesions “for which the collective intends to exercise the mandate.”[S1]
2. The FAQ says the OP/cooperative sees all associated adhesion filers and may select only some—members for whom, after negotiations, it intends to exercise the mandate.[S2]
3. Outsiders cannot be included; their adhesion becomes individual.[S2]
4. Unselected adhesions become individual; they are not simply rejected or deleted.[S1][S2]
5. The individual remains beneficiary even after selection.[S2][S10]
6. If collective aid exceeds €2m, the FAQ prescribes that selected adhesions are treated starting with those having the **lowest score** until the cap is reached; excluded excess adhesions become individual. If all collective requests together do not exhaust the allocation, the Region may ask the collective by PEC to modify the member base to meet the cap; absent correction, the default rule applies.[S2]

### Inference, clearly labelled

- **Inference:** the architecture intentionally separates a private coordination decision (membership/mandate negotiation) from public eligibility and funding decisions. Evidence: the collective chooses the operational cohort, while ARIF/Region independently assess each adherent and issue individual grants.
- **Inference:** the automatic individual fallback is a due-process/safety-valve design: a cooperative's non-selection changes processing lane rather than extinguishing the member's public claim.
- **Inference:** because no public reason taxonomy or appeal against collective non-selection was found, a production workflow should capture a reason/evidence record (non-member, mandate terms not agreed, capacity/scheduling, cap exclusion, duplicate/error) without pretending those unsourced categories are legally exhaustive.

### Unresolved legal/operational gaps

- No public instrument found says whether, among eligible members willing to grant the mandate, the OP/cooperative may decline for any reason or must use objective/equal-treatment criteria.
- No instrument requires a written reason, notice, internal appeal or audit trail for the collective's inclusion choice.
- “Trattative intercorse” is not defined: no mandatory contract terms, pricing, service-level, conflict policy or bargaining procedure is public.
- No public data shows adhesions that were visible but deliberately unselected; published Annex C shows only included adhesions.
- The FAQ's “lowest-score-first” cap wording is counterintuitive and no worked administrative example was found; it should be implemented only after authority confirmation.
- The precise relationship between the initial collective mandate and the later EIP “delega” needed to open ARIF review is not fully documented publicly.

## Confirmation/challenge of the five starting assertions

| Starting assertion | Verdict | Evidence / qualification |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Members submit adhesion applications. | **Confirmed.** | Individual opens/releases adhesion first; collective window follows.[S1][S3] |
| 2. The OP/cooperative can include only some adhesions. | **Confirmed.** | FAQ Q10 expressly says it sees all and may select only some.[S2] |
| 3. Selection follows negotiation. | **Confirmed, narrowly.** | FAQ says selection is for members for whom, “in virtù delle trattative intercorse,” it intends to exercise the mandate. It does not define negotiation rules.[S2] |
| 4. Unselected adhesions revert to individual treatment. | **Confirmed.** | Call and FAQ both say correctly filed/unrecalled adhesions are treated as individual applications.[S1][S2] |
| 5. The cooperative/OP becomes beneficiary for included members. | **Falsified.** | FAQ: beneficiaries are “sempre e solo” the individual adherents; concessions and guarantees are individual.[S2][S10] |

## Design-relevant conclusions without screen design

1. Model **Adhesion**, **CollectiveApplication**, **InclusionDecision**, **Mandate**, **EligibilityReview**, **Concession**, **PaymentApplication** and **AuthorityDecision** as separate records. Collapsing them into one “application” erases who decides what.
2. Preserve two independent decision axes: `collective_selected?` and `publicly_eligible/fundable?`. A “no” on the first routes to individual; a “no” on the second can exclude/reduce after public review.
3. Make beneficiary identity immutable across collective selection unless a formal subentro is approved. The collective is an operator/signatory, not the creditor.
4. Keep decision provenance: private inclusion basis; ARIF review result; Regional final act; and the effective notice/clock. Publication, PEC and portal receipt are not interchangeable.
5. Support rework rather than a single rejection terminal: rectification, Article 10-bis counterarguments, cap-cohort rework, adaptation counterarguments, held liquidation, variant, extension and subentro are all real transitions.
