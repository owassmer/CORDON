# Gate 5 operator graph — adversarial audit

Status: **ADVERSARIAL LANE COMPLETE — bounded corrections integrated into accepted graph**  
Date: 22 August 2026  
Authority tested: `REDESIGN_SEQUENCE.md`; `gate-5-core-ontology.md`; `gate-5-operator-reground.md`; `gate-5-operator-decision-ledger-six-questions.md`; `gate-5-operator-capability-ledger-five-responsibilities.md`; `gate-5-operator-ledger-reconciliation.md`

## Verdict

The accepted nine-object core, `Intervention Capacity Commitment`, history-first representation, four manager-owned Actions and three Functions is a credible compression of the domain, but it does **not yet cover all 41 decisions and 30 capabilities as an executable compact graph**.

The accepted reconciliation adds `Cooperative Pursuit`, `Determine Remaining Exposure`, qualified context ownership, administrative/regulatory Official Area semantics, conditional Plant-scope reopening and explicit Gate 6 operational dependencies. The failure verdict below applies to the pre-correction lower bound and remains the adversarial evidence for those additions.

It has four substantive defects:

1. **`Decide Cooperative Pursuit` has no safe target or fact owner.** A bounded member–Programme route can exist before a `Public Proceeding` or sufficiently designed `Intervention`, and it is neither a public matter nor a physical undertaking. Storing pursuit on `Operator Party`, `Public Programme`, `Public Proceeding` or `Intervention` either creates a multi-valued God Object, misnames the fact, or conflates pursuit with filing/physical work. Q3-D13 therefore fails the Action target, atomic-effect and selective-reopening tests.
2. **Q6-D41 has no corresponding live reconciliation capability.** `Determine Affected Decisions`, `Assess Named-Action Readiness` and `Compare Feasible Intervention Portfolios` do not determine which independent legal, operational, financial and biological results landed and what remains open. A static convenience projection is insufficient when partial/reversed cash, separate acceptances, audit/recovery and establishment/cure must remain independent.
3. **The 30-capability claim silently depends on an unmodeled operational envelope.** Four manager-owned Actions do not prepare, stage, route, release for an authorized principal, or ingest/record received bounded outcomes. Those need not become domain Actions, but the core cannot claim end-to-end coverage unless Gate 6 supplies governed proposal/routing and received-outcome ingestion mechanics with the Gate 5 authority contract intact.
4. **The compact direct-link claim is unsafe without qualified ownership rules.** Direct links cannot by themselves preserve role, authority basis, scope, interval, exclusions, firmness, or phase. If these facts are pushed onto `Operator Party` or global object properties, the design becomes a God Object/last-known-status model and fails Q2-D06, Q3-D12, Q4-D27 and the separate-acceptance/result decisions.

Two conditional failures also matter:

- When `Individual Plant` is instantiated, a bare `Intervention ↔ Individual Plant` link cannot distinguish intended, dispatched, performed, accepted, defective, replaced and cured scope. Q5-D29 and Q6-D39–D40 fail for the same Plant across phases or Interventions unless a conditional fact-bearing scope representation reopens.
- The rough legal map fails if `Official Area` excludes administrative jurisdiction. Comune-as-`Operator Party` is the authority/organization identity; Comune-as-`Official Area` is the territorial identity. They cannot be one object.

The smallest defensible corrected lower bound is therefore:

- the same nine accepted object types, with `Individual Plant` explicitly extension-gated rather than implied to be universally populated;
- `Intervention Capacity Commitment`;
- **one added relationship object: `Cooperative Pursuit`**;
- the same four manager-owned Actions, with `Decide Cooperative Pursuit` targeting that relationship object;
- **one added Function: `Project Remaining Exposure`**;
- history-first representation retained;
- one **conditional** `Intervention Plant Scope` relationship object only when the conditional Plant population is active and phase-grain tree decisions cannot be represented safely by bounded history;
- no generic Case, Task, Event, Evidence, Status, Route or Execute-CORDON object/Action.

## Adversarial tests applied

The review did not reward a low count. Each ledger decision was tested for:

- an unambiguous object or relationship that owns the current fact;
- a bounded history owner for partial, superseded, reversed or repeated outcomes;
- a typed target for any accepted manager Action;
- preservation of actor, authority basis, scope and consequential time;
- selective reopening without a global case reset;
- direct-link safety where the relationship carries no independent operator fact;
- rough jurisdiction/map, legal-applicability, disease/tree and bounded-AIP addressability.

Verdicts below mean:

- **PASS** — the accepted element set can preserve the decision with the stated direct links/properties/history.
- **CONDITIONAL** — coverage exists only if the listed ownership/property contract is made explicit; a bare direct-link graph does not suffice.
- **FAIL** — a named decision lacks an owner, target or live capability and requires the small correction named here.

The 30 capabilities are therefore **semantically coverable**, but not all are executable by the 17 accepted elements alone. This is not a reason to add eleven narrow domain Actions. It is a reason to state the boundary honestly:

- four manager-owned decisions are Ontology Actions;
- read/compare/explain are Functions, derived properties or views;
- external actor work requires governed proposal/routing and received-outcome ingestion;
- exact non-manager Actions reopen only when that actor really transacts through CORDON.

Gate 6 must prove those latter mechanics. Until then, “covers all 30 capabilities” means **semantic coverage subject to an explicit platform-operation dependency**, not a closed executable harness.

No link label may imply authority merely from contact, geography, membership or organizational kind.

### Property placement constraints

**Operator Party is identity-only.** Keep `partyKind`, qualified identifiers, display identity and contact references. Do not add global role, authority, member, beneficiary, creditor, contractor, technician, inspector or municipality-power booleans. Those facts are contextual and basis-bound.

**Governing Instrument owns operative rule and authority basis.** It needs instrument kind, issuer/parties, proposition, jurisdiction/area, effective interval, version/supersession and qualified assignment/authority details: Party, role, subject, bounded scope, powers, exclusions and interval.

**Public Proceeding owns formal matter and authority outcomes.** It needs proceeding kind, authority, applicant/beneficiary/creditor, scope, Programme/Instrument bases and typed filing, receipt, public-decision, accounting, cash-reference, audit and remedy history. A single `status`, `amount`, `paid` or `accepted` property is prohibited.

**Intervention owns the physical undertaking.** It needs route family, target grain, current bounded design/readiness projection, dispatch authority, executor and aftercare basis, plus typed dispatch, performance, inspection, acceptance, defect, establishment, replacement and cure history. A global completion status is prohibited.

**Intervention Capacity Commitment owns independently mutable scarcity facts.** It needs capacity/resource class, quantity, unit, period, firmness, provider/controller, protected status, release/expiry, decision/override basis and affected Intervention links.

**Cooperative Pursuit owns the accepted missing decision grain.** It needs member, cooperative, Programme, Holding/Parcel/optional Intervention scope, member-election reference, mandate Instrument, pursue/defer/refuse outcome, reason, effective time, fallback, reconsideration trigger and exact decision authority. It must not carry public eligibility, concession or capacity as editable pursuit properties; those remain traversed results.

**Official Area must include administrative jurisdiction as well as regulatory/plant-health area kinds.** Use a controlled `areaKind`, authoritative identifier, parent-area link, geometry/version, defining Instrument and effective interval. Comune Party and Comune territorial Area may share a qualified external identifier but are not the same object.

**Current map properties are projections, not source-of-truth verdicts.** Parcel/Plant can expose current official condition, organism controlled identifier, duty/readiness cue and basis references for map filtering. The authoritative occurrence remains on the appropriate Instrument/Proceeding history; an operational observation cannot overwrite it.

## Hidden relationship/event pressure

### Mandatory now: Cooperative Pursuit

This is not a generic Route or Case. It exists solely because Q3-D13 and the accepted `Decide Cooperative Pursuit` Action need one stable, typed, bounded target before public filing and physical execution necessarily exist. Deleting it forces the decision onto a Party, Programme, Proceeding or Intervention at the wrong grain.

### Conditional with Plant: Intervention Plant Scope

Admit only when `Individual Plant` is populated and the manager must answer Q4-D27, Q5-D29 or Q6-D39–D40 for the same tree across phases or Interventions. Minimum facts are Intervention, Plant, scope role/phase, creating occurrence, quantity if cohort-backed, current bounded outcome and supersession/replacement link. Do not instantiate it merely to draw a tree map.

### Conditional settlement occurrence

Do not add a payment event type now if one Proceeding history can preserve stable transaction identity, every partial/return/reversal and exact order/right allocation. Reopen a focused settlement occurrence only when Q6-D37 requires the manager to select one transaction independently or allocate one transaction across multiple Proceedings. That is the accepted history-first reopening rule, not a new normalized payment model.

### No other event type passes now

Notice, filing, execution, inspection, acceptance, accounting and establishment occurrences remain safely owned by Governing Instrument, Public Proceeding or Intervention as typed consequential history, provided the history mechanism supports stable identity, bounded scope, actor, time, outcome, evidence, changed-decision references and reversals. Gate 6 must not assume an opaque mutable array is automatically adequate.

## God Object and misnomer risks

### Operator Party — high risk if the direct-link lane is careless

The consolidation itself is not the defect. The defect appears if every contextual role and authority is added as Party properties because relationship objects were pruned. That would make Party the answer to “who is this?” and “what may this actor do here, under which basis, during which interval?” across every responsibility. It would recreate the rejected Decision-Capable Actor abstraction as a God Object.

Guardrail: identity on Party; contextual power on the controlling Instrument/Proceeding/Intervention/Pursuit/Commitment detail; every resolved owner answer cites basis, subject, scope and interval.

### Intervention — medium/high risk

Using Intervention as the missing member route, application case, resource container, field order, acceptance aggregate and biological result would make it the actual God Object. Intervention must remain a physical undertaking. Cooperative pursuit gets its own relationship object; formal public matters remain Proceedings; capacity remains Commitment; independent result lines remain history and projections.

### Public Proceeding — medium risk

It may cover multiple formal matter kinds, but one instance must remain one formal matter. Application, claim, audit/recovery and permit matters should be separate linked Proceedings when they have different authority, clock or remedy. A universal member dossier disguised as one Proceeding is prohibited.

## Unnecessary-core test

No unconditional object among the other eight can be deleted without a named decision loss:

- Party is required for exact actor/authority/handoff resolution.
- Holding preserves the farm administrative/operating unit across Party and Parcel change.
- Parcel is the recurring legal, filing, dispatch and residual-work land grain.
- Official Area is required for authoritative area identity, applicability and map/legal change propagation—provided jurisdiction is included.
- Programme preserves the enduring opportunity across openings, Instruments and Proceedings.
- Instrument preserves operative legal/contractual/financial basis, version and interval.
- Proceeding preserves a formal matter across filings, outcomes, appeal, accounting and recovery.
- Intervention preserves one physical undertaking across design through bounded cure.

`Individual Plant` is different: it is **not an unconditional core population**. In deployments without stable external identity or a validated reproducible inventory, it buys no manager capability and must have zero instances (or be omitted from the deployed slice). The correction is classification, not deletion: describe the lower bound as **eight unconditional objects plus one extension-gated object**, with the conditional `Intervention Plant Scope` representation reopening alongside it when phase-grain tree decisions demand it.

## Required corrections—no more

1. Add the `Cooperative Pursuit` relationship object and make it the target/fact owner for Q3-D13 and `Decide Cooperative Pursuit`.
2. Add `Project Remaining Exposure` for Q6-D41. It reconciles but does not mutate or manufacture any external result.

4. State that the 30-capability claim depends on Gate 6 proposal/routing and received-outcome ingestion mechanics for non-manager actors; do not add speculative non-manager Actions at Gate 5.
5. Treat `Individual Plant` and `Intervention Plant Scope` as a conditional pair: Plant may exist without the relationship only where phase can be represented safely in bounded history; otherwise both reopen together.
6. Expand `Official Area` explicitly to administrative jurisdictions and regulatory/plant-health areas; keep territorial Area distinct from governmental Party.

Do **not** add a generic Case, Route, Task, Handoff, Evidence, Event, Result, Entitlement, Resource, Authority or Status object. Do **not** split Person and Organization. Do **not** add all seven authorization-envelope operations as domain Actions. Do **not** re-normalize the nine relationship families.

## Final adversarial disposition

The accepted compression is directionally right and substantially better than the 39+10 normalized upper bound. It is not yet a complete lower bound.

With `Cooperative Pursuit`, `Project Remaining Exposure`, explicit qualified-fact ownership and the conditional Plant-scope rule, the graph can cover the named manager decisions without re-normalizing the domain. Without those corrections, the apparent compactness is purchased by hiding one real cooperative decision in the wrong object, omitting one CORDON-owned determination, and pushing scoped authority/history into God Objects or unspecified platform mechanics.
