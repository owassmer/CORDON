# Consultant universe claim adjudication

Status: primary-source adjudication; not product, interface, or ontology authority  
Date: 21 August 2026

## Bottom line

The consultant report correctly breaks the claim that broad web-query saturation proves external-domain completeness. It also identifies useful workflow refinements. It does not establish the proposed ontology, exact source-coverage thresholds, or several claims that it presents as discoveries.

The report conflates three different conditions:

1. **Already known in the repository:** `Operationals2`, `Operationals3`, current *fastidiosa* and *multiplex* geography, historical non-*pauca* monitoring, cadastral monitoring, SIAN, SRD01.01A/B, the €30 million measure, ANAC, planting/movement controls, and act-versus-GIS separation.[S2]
2. **Genuinely new or newly operationalised:** recursive source enumeration, `PuntiStampa`, water-right proceedings, complete landscape decisions, explicit administrative-act states, judicial reactivation, alternative execution mechanisms, and the planting-registration handoff.[S3][S6][S7][S8][S9]
3. **Premature design:** the nine-entity ontology, relationship cardinalities, value-object decisions, and numeric coverage thresholds.[S1]

The report’s proposed “current master plan” and domain-decision ledger are not authority. Owen rejected the old Wedge 1 planning and Ontology premises. `REDESIGN_SEQUENCE.md` remains the process authority.[S19]

## Adjudication labels

- **ALREADY_KNOWN:** verified, but already present in current research evidence.
- **VERIFIED_NEW:** verified and changes either workflow or acquisition method.
- **PARTLY_TRUE:** directionally correct but overstated, incomplete, or status-dependent.
- **UNSUPPORTED:** no sufficient evidence for the exact claim or threshold.
- **CONTRADICTED:** conflicts with current evidence or current project authority.
- **OUTSIDE_SCOPE:** relevant implementation concern, not a Wedge 1 workflow organiser.

## Verified changes to the connected operating model

These findings change the workflow model after executive review:

1. **Authoritative-state intake becomes explicit.** Monitoring, laboratory confirmation, act state, GIS publication and source disagreement are separate events.
2. **Act lifecycle becomes explicit.** Signed, protocolled, registered, effective, implemented, amended, superseded and exhausted cannot share one date or status.
3. **Land status becomes organism/subspecies/ST/host/date specific.** One scalar Xylella zone is unsafe.
4. **Programme route becomes instrument specific.** SRD01.01A, SRD01.01B, Article 6 and the €30 million route cannot inherit each other’s geography, authority, payer or claim stages.
5. **Field execution mechanism becomes explicit.** Owner, ARIF/delegated, forced/public, private-beneficiary and procured execution have different decision rights and evidence.
6. **Planting and movement traceability becomes a real handoff.** Plant lot, RUOP, passport, production site, movement route and planting communication are rechecked through dispatch and receipt.
7. **Permit authority routing becomes explicit.** Water, landscape, protected-area, civic-use, forest and heritage exceptions route to the competent entity rather than a generic Region queue.
8. **Judicial/control events can reactivate a case.** Filing, interim stay, final decision, audit, revocation and recovery remain separate.
9. **Money semantics remain scoped.** Demand, programme envelope, broad-plan commitment, liquidation, order and bank receipt never collapse.

## Acquisition changes only

These improve research completeness but do not expand the operator surface:

- recursively enumerate finite official catalogs and registers;
- traverse act citations, amendments, annexes, programme lineages and judicial references;
- record source authority, update method, expected population, crawl date and unexplained gaps;
- use Exa for discovery and Crawl4AI/direct/browser recovery for extraction;
- preserve contradictions and registration/effectiveness gaps;
- obtain private cooperative, CAA, nursery, contractor, member and bank evidence directly.

## Findings not adopted

- the nine-entity ontology;
- any proposed relationship cardinality;
- numeric 99%/100% completeness thresholds;
- the old master plan or domain decisions;
- privacy as a product organiser;
- a research/phenotyping workflow inside Wedge 1;
- the claim that all public unknowns are now known.

## Retrieval log and limitations

- Exa supplied discovery.
- Crawl4AI extracted current HTML and JavaScript services.
- Direct official downloads plus `file` and `pdftotext -layout` recovered PDFs when Crawl4AI returned HTTP 500.
- The Constitutional Court site presented a bot page; the complete judgment was recovered from the official Gazzetta Ufficiale publication.[S16]
- The exact registration/effectiveness of MASAF decree 986098 was not located. The signed decree remains explicit that it was sent for registration.[S10]
- No operative regional call for the national €30 million route was located. Its current application path therefore remains unresolved.[S13]
- Recursive SIT enumeration is complete only for the current ArcGIS root at the retrieval date. It does not enumerate every non-GIS register or private operational source.[S3][S18]
