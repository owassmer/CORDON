## What the research changes

### The five functions are connected operating responsibilities, not a five-stage pipeline

Land, Funding, Applications, Field Work and Payments remain useful functional labels. Real cases move backward, branch and reactivate across them.

Examples include:

- a new official result changes land status and creates a compulsory duty;
- a corrected farm file regenerates an application;
- a ranking scroll reactivates a years-old application;
- a member omitted from a collective application continues individually;
- a field discovery triggers a variant and changes payment eligibility;
- an invalid IBAN holds a technically admitted claim until repaired;
- post-payment control can reopen a completed project through recovery (`web-formal-institutions.md:243-324`; `web-art6-collective-case.md:148-240`; `web-payment-deep-dive.md:322-371`).

### Two real control loops meet on the same land

#### Compulsory duty loop

```text
official finding or general prescription
→ legally effective duty
→ notification and responsible person
→ owner route or public execution route
→ supervised field work
→ official verbale/compliance outcome
→ possible indemnity or enforcement
```

#### Voluntary investment loop

```text
funding opportunity
→ member interest and adhesion
→ bounded collective inclusion or individual route
→ application, ranking and public review
→ concession where required
├─ permitted advance or interim claim where the instrument allows it
└─ field readiness and delivery → final or balance claim
→ public controls, liquidation/order and bank receipt
→ later durability, audit and recovery controls
```

The loops can intersect but do not share one lifecycle. A compulsory removal can exist without funding. A grant does not legalize missed compulsory work. The same land and beneficiary can participate in both (`web-operator-practice.md:690-720`; `web-field-delivery-deep-dive.md:43-171,334-354`).

### Municipalities hold several bounded roles

**Executive decision:** accepted. Municipalities remain assigned actors across the five responsibilities, resolved per event, actual asset responsibility, effective delegation and date. No separate municipal or general Public Authority lane is created.

A Comune is not a miniature phytosanitary authority. Depending on the controlling act, asset and delegation, it can:

- perform the legally effective albo posting that starts a notice clock;
- communicate separately with the actual owner, holder or local community;
- own and execute annual vector work on municipal roads, green areas and land;
- issue a lawful local ordinance, inspect it and apply its separate local sanction route;
- contract work or use the agricultural-entrepreneur convention route;
- support access, public safety and Prefecture escalation without owning the phytosanitary order;
- act as delegated landscape, VIncA or local-planning authority for a specific project/date; or
- run a separate municipal recovery or in-kind programme.

Asset ownership and legal competence control. Municipal boundaries do not make every road, canal, public parcel, permit or Xylella decision municipal (`municipal-government-workflows.md:17-39,88-150,511-545`).

### The cooperative coordinates a federation of member cases

A collective Article 6 application is not one grant to the cooperative.

- Members first file adhesion applications.
- The cooperative sees associated adhesions and can select members for whom it will exercise the negotiated operational mandate.
- Valid unselected adhesions continue as individual applications.
- Each included member remains the beneficiary.
- Eligibility, concession, guarantee, payment claim, creditor account, recovery exposure and long-term obligations remain member-specific (`web-art6-collective-case.md:91-134,136-188`; `web-cooperative-governance-practice.md:78-119`).

The private mandate or service agreement determines execution terms that the public call does not disclose. No public source establishes unlimited reason-free selection discretion (`web-art6-collective-case.md:192-216`; `web-cooperative-governance-practice.md:121-184`).

### Public eligibility and collective inclusion are separate decisions

The evidence establishes at least these distinct questions:

1. Does the member and land satisfy the public rules?
2. Is the evidence complete enough to decide?
3. Does the member choose to adhere?
4. Will the cooperative accept the execution mandate after negotiation?
5. Does the collective remain within cap and ranking rules?
6. Does the public authority admit and concede each member?

A negative collective-inclusion decision changes route; it does not necessarily extinguish public eligibility. This directly corrects the earlier assumption that every eligible case should automatically enter the collective application (`web-art6-collective-case.md:99-134,192-216`).

### A CAA office manages an evidence-bearing case bundle, not one form

The public CAA corpus reveals this recurring sequence:

```text
open case and clocks
→ verify mandates and roles
→ repair and validate fascicolo
→ reconcile technician/cooperative inputs
→ compile procedure
→ run controls and route anomalies
→ prepare legally valid signature
→ release and protocol
→ preserve filed evidence
→ monitor notices and worklists
→ answer authority requests
→ follow payment and recovery
```

Portals prefill, calculate, validate, sign, protocol and expose some worklists. Humans determine date-valid truth, authority, evidence sufficiency, anomaly cause, correction layer and escalation path (`web-caa-office-practice.md:7-24,114-280`).

No public source exposes one complete cross-procedure CAA workbench or a local office’s CRM, spreadsheet, communication or staffing practice (`web-caa-office-practice.md:415-474`).

### A valid duty or work basis starts readiness assembly; it does not dispatch work

Compulsory removal requires exact regime, targets, notification, owner route, monumental status, treatment timing, access, mechanical capacity and official supervision (`web-field-delivery-deep-dive.md:43-123`).

Grant-funded work additionally requires title and consent, technical project, permits, lawful water, actual plant batch, contractor capacity, cash, approved variants and evidence design (`web-field-delivery-deep-dive.md:145-319`). Self-funded voluntary restoration uses the same legal, permit, readiness, delivery, evidence and aftercare checks while public application, concession and payment subroutes remain inapplicable.

The public grant verifies installation and conformity. It does not allocate private defects, warranty or aftercare responsibility. No public programme-wide survival outcome exists (`web-cooperative-governance-practice.md:236-255`; `web-field-capacity-aftercare.md:136-173,221-236`).

### Financial completion starts before procurement

Article 6 has two payment stages:

```text
optional 50% advance
→ final balance
```

It has no SAL. Each member supplies its own advance guarantee. ARIF performs technical-administrative instruction. The Region concedes and liquidates. Regional accounting issues SAP-RP payment orders. Bank credit remains a later fact (`web-payment-deep-dive.md:77-145`).

The research distinguishes six amount states:

1. requested;
2. technically admitted;
3. granted;
4. liquidated;
5. ordered;
6. paid (`web-payment-deep-dive.md:18-54`).

Finance must establish admissible procurement and evidence before money is spent. A payment dossier is not reconstructed safely after field completion (`web-operator-practice.md:468-548`).

## Final residual workflow components

The final saturation pass adds five bounded components:

1. **Compulsory-duty compatibility.** When a phytosanitary duty appears to conflict with a funded land-management commitment, locate the express derogation, compatibility rule or authority decision. Preserve both rule versions. Abstain if none exists.
2. **Site-specific VIncA escalation.** Natura 2000 overlap is a warning, not a verdict. Identify the competent authority and site manager, perform project/site-specific screening, and move uncertainty to appropriate assessment before procurement or dispatch.
3. **Municipal companion enforcement.** A Comune may add a local order, inspection, local sanction, substituted execution, cost recovery and separate remedy clock. That does not transfer phytosanitary competence from the Osservatorio.
4. **Funding-source election.** Overlapping support can be cumulable, reducible or mutually exclusive. Where the same surface/year/operation is eligible under an OP sectoral programme and a corresponding SRA route, the beneficiary chooses the permitted source and the administration enforces non-overlap.
5. **Enterprise-crisis status.** Where a live call makes it consequential, recheck liquidation/continuity status, judicial authorisation or homologation and signing authority. Do not transplant one call’s wording into another.

The 2026 national Xylella emergency plan superseding the 2022 plan confirms that authoritative-state monitoring and selective reactivation are permanent cross-function behavior (`final-domain-coverage-saturation.md:7-25,203-288`).

## Corrected starting assertions

These formulations follow from the evidence. Each remains proposed unless its section records executive acceptance.

### 1. Land, duties and compliance

**Executive decision:** accepted. Land explains current official status and duty; Field Work delivers readiness, execution and proof; the competent authority decides legal compliance; the recorded outcome updates Land and Payments.

**Source-conflict decision:** accepted. Authority is resolved for the exact proposition, competence, scope, source version and date. Conflicting assertions remain visible. An unresolved conflict blocks only the decisions it can change; unaffected work continues.

**Corrected assertion:**

> CORDON assembles and explains source-supported official land status, responsible actor and resulting duties. It keeps municipal legal publication separate from operational awareness. It resolves whether a Comune is acting as publication channel, asset manager, local enforcer, permit authority or local programme manager. Field Work assembles readiness and records execution. The competent inspector or authority determines legal compliance where the procedure requires official acceptance.

Why: spatial overlap alone does not create official status; execution evidence is not always legal closure (`web-formal-institutions.md:328-335`; `web-operator-practice.md:188-236`).

### 2. Funding, adhesion and collective inclusion

**Corrected assertion:**

> CORDON maintains a complete event-driven in-scope member–land–intervention population: it evaluates the complete population when an opportunity or governing fact materially changes, then recomputes only affected work. Member interest and management nomination do not define the evaluation universe. A member chooses whether to adhere. The cooperative separately decides whether to accept that member’s negotiated execution mandate into the collective cohort, within membership, cap, conflict and other validated constraints. Valid non-selected adhesions continue through the individual route. Same-operation source elections and instrument-specific enterprise-status exceptions remain explicit. Public authorities independently determine admissibility, ranking and concession. Under cooperative-approved policy, CORDON computes action-specific readiness, feasible portfolios, binding constraints, a non-binding portfolio recommendation, alternatives and consequences. The authorized cooperative role commits or overrides scarce shared capacity without rewriting eligibility, concession, duty, creditor identity or fallback.

**Funding-population decision:** accepted. Maintain the complete event-driven population and treat scarce shared-capacity allocation as a separate cooperative governance decision.

**Scarce-capacity objective:** Option A accepted. Cooperative-approved fairness safeguards and non-discretionary constraints define the feasible portfolios. CORDON recommends the feasible portfolio with the highest expected realized Xylella-loss reduction, exposes uncertainty and alternatives, and uses an approved neutral tie-breaker only for materially equivalent portfolios. The authorized cooperative role commits or overrides.

Why: eligibility, adhesion, private willingness-to-serve and public funding are separate axes (`web-art6-collective-case.md:91-134,192-216`; `web-cooperative-governance-practice.md:78-154`).

### 3. Application preparation and release

**Corrected assertion:**

> CORDON assembles the current, cited application case and routes exceptions. Source-data stewards such as the CAA repair authoritative records; technicians supply professional project judgments; the case operator coordinates resolution; the beneficiary or authorised representative certifies and releases the application; public authorities resolve exceptions outside those actors’ decision rights. Protected-site overlap triggers site-specific VIncA screening and escalation rather than an automatic permit answer.

Why: preparation crosses fascicolo, technician project, SIAN, regional portals, PEC, signatures, protocol and authority feedback (`web-caa-office-practice.md:114-264`; `web-formal-institutions.md:150-178`).

### 4. Field readiness and delivery

**Corrected assertion:**

> A validated duty, order, concession, approved variant or member-authorised self-funded work basis starts field-readiness assembly. The manager dispatches work only after authority, target, access, technical design, permits, any VIncA route, materials, water, capacity, finance, schedule and evidence dependencies are satisfied for the named action. Any conflict between compulsory work and an aid commitment is resolved from an express rule before the affected binding commitment or dispatch. Execution and public acceptance remain separate, and compulsory, municipal/public, private grant and self-funded delivery are not presumed to use the same actors.

Why: the governing act defines work but does not reserve plants, contractors, inspectors or water (`web-field-delivery-deep-dive.md:305-331`; `web-field-capacity-aftercare.md:175-219`).

### 5. Payment preparation and cash receipt

**Corrected assertion:**

> Award rules define the permitted claim stages and evidence. Finance engineers admissible spend and evidence from procurement onward. The beneficiary or authorised representative releases the claim. The assigned public body instructs and controls it; the competent payer liquidates, orders and executes payment. Requested, admitted, granted, liquidated, ordered and paid remain separate outcomes, and bank settlement or beneficiary receipt proves cash.

Why: internal finance approval is not public payment authority, and a liquidation decree is not proof of bank credit (`web-payment-deep-dive.md:45-73,148-179,355-371`).
