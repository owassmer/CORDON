# Gate 5 cooperative-manager decision ledger — six questions

Status: **CURRENT GATE 5 DERIVATION — operator decision ledger**  
Date: 22 August 2026  
Authority: `REDESIGN_SEQUENCE.md`; `connected-operating-model.md`; `gate-5-operator-reground.md`; `gate-5-core-ontology.md`

## Purpose

This ledger states the minimum decisions the cooperative or recognized producer-organization operations manager must own, supervise, recommend, stage, or route. It is organized by the six accepted recurring questions rather than by Land, Funding, Applications, Field Work, or Payments.

The ledger does not propose Ontology types, properties, links, events, Functions, Interfaces, screens, Foundry implementation, or AIP implementation. An **Action** below is only a bounded business operation that CORDON may execute or stage for the real authorized actor. A public or other reserved decision that CORDON only receives is an **external outcome**, not a CORDON Action.

## Counting and classification

- **41 distinct decisions or consequential determinations** survive cross-responsibility collapse.
- **4 are manager-owned when the manager holds the named cooperative authority:** accept the cooperative execution mandate; pursue or include a route collectively; commit or rebalance scarce capacity; dispatch field work.
- **6 are CORDON-owned read-only determinations or recommendations:** affected decisions; deterministic applicability; exact decision owner; clock consequence; feasible-portfolio recommendation; remaining-result projection.
- **31 belong to another real decision owner and must be staged for or routed to that owner.** Some of those owners can act through a bounded CORDON Action; others return an authoritative external outcome.
- “Manager-owned” never means that the job title supplies authority. The manager must hold the exact governance, mandate, contracting, allocation, or dispatch power for the stated scope and date.

### Mechanical handling classes

| Code | Manager relationship | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **O** | Own | The manager may decide only when the manager holds the exact named cooperative authority. |
| **S** | Supervise | CORDON computes or another actor performs; the manager verifies the bounded result and next handoff. |
| **R** | Recommend | CORDON produces a non-binding recommendation; the manager presents it without converting it into a commitment. |
| **T** | Stage | CORDON can prepare or transact a bounded Action for the real authorized actor. Human authority remains with that actor. |
| **X** | Route | Another actor or authority must decide or correct the premise outside the manager’s authority. |

## Ledger invariants

1. Preparation, decision, execution, acceptance, settlement, and establishment remain separate.
2. Public eligibility, member participation, cooperative mandate, cooperative pursuit, capacity commitment, public concession, and member-specific rights remain separate.
3. A recommendation creates no commitment. A commitment creates no public right. A payment order proves no cash. Installation acceptance proves no establishment.
4. Compulsory and voluntary routes can share compatible physical planning and resources without sharing authority, duty, evidence, clock, acceptance, or remedy.
5. Funded and self-funded recovery share technical, field, acceptance, and aftercare decisions. Public application, concession, claim, accounting, and cash branches activate only when applicable.
6. A Comune holds only the exact publication, asset, enforcement, permit, contracting, or local-programme power granted for the event, asset, delegation, and date.
7. A missing or conflicting premise blocks only the decision it can change. Continuing duties and unaffected work remain active.
8. CORDON never turns a computed answer, prepared filing, staged Action, internal approval, map overlap, executor statement, or public order into a reserved external decision.
9. Every handoff carries the achieved bounded effect, required evidence, next decision owner, active clock, and consequence of delay.
10. No row implies a generic case, route status, ready state, completion state, task, exception workflow, or global timeline.
