# Gate 5 Proceeding scope minimum

**Status:** PRE-CORRECTION UPPER-BOUND EVIDENCE — does not define the operator core  
**Date:** 22 August 2026  
**Scope:** Predicate 9, `Public Proceeding subject or scope`, only  
**Authority:** `REDESIGN_SEQUENCE.md`; `connected-operating-model.md`; `gate-4-relationship-candidates.md`; `gate-5-core-ontology.md`; `gate-5-mece-coverage-audit.md`; `gate-5-relationship-representation-ledger.md`; `gate-5-integrated-candidate-synthesis.md`

## Verdict

This verdict applies the prior domain-fact deletion test. The current Gate 5 operator admission rule supersedes its deployment recommendation. The ten types remain evidence of where claimed versus authority-determined scope can matter, not an instruction to create ten core relationship types.

The pre-correction recommendation was to deploy **10 of the 20 accepted maximum types**:

1. `Proceeding Claimed Scope — Natural Person`
2. `Proceeding Authority-Determined Scope — Natural Person`
3. `Proceeding Claimed Scope — Organization`
4. `Proceeding Authority-Determined Scope — Organization`
5. `Proceeding Claimed Scope — Agricultural Holding`
6. `Proceeding Authority-Determined Scope — Agricultural Holding`
7. `Proceeding Claimed Scope — Cadastral Parcel`
8. `Proceeding Authority-Determined Scope — Cadastral Parcel`
9. `Proceeding Claimed Scope — Intervention`
10. `Proceeding Authority-Determined Scope — Intervention`

Keep **six names closed but selectively reopenable**: both authority classes for `Individual Plant`, `Plant Trade Unit / Lot`, and `Governing Instrument`.

Reject **four current deployments as duplicates**: both authority classes for `Official Area` and `Public Programme or Measure`.

This is a semantic minimum, not an implementation preference. It follows the accepted rule that a fact-bearing relationship must own independently changing scope, quantity, amount, basis, version, authority, or history; a meaningful factless subject remains a direct `concerns` link; and an occurrence, outcome, derived result, or another sole-owned relationship must not be copied into Proceeding scope (`gate-5-core-ontology.md:17-23,29-33`; `gate-5-relationship-representation-ledger.md:28-36,77-88,90-99`).

## Accepted scope grammar applied

- **Claimed Scope** owns what an authorized filer formally places before the competent authority. It is created or superseded through an effective `Public Filing Submission`; the Submission owns the transmitting occurrence and protocolled filing version, not the durable claimed subject scope (`gate-5-core-ontology.md:29`; `gate-5-integrated-candidate-synthesis.md:111-115,260-266`).
- **Authority-Determined Scope** owns what the competent authority reviews, admits, excludes, decides, or permits. The public determination occurrence/outcome and any resulting Governing Instrument remain separate (`gate-5-core-ontology.md:29-31`; `gate-4-relationship-candidates.md:205-234`).
- Requested/claimed versions stay inside Claimed Scope. Reviewed/admitted/excluded/decided/permitted versions stay inside Authority-Determined Scope; stages do not become types (`gate-5-core-ontology.md:30`).
- An independently identified appeal, audit, enforcement, or recovery matter is another `Public Proceeding` and reuses this same split (`gate-5-core-ontology.md:31`).
- A required type is still instantiated only when the real matter preserves independent scope facts. Its concise Proceeding-to-subject traversal is a view of the backing scope fact, not a separately stored direct link (`gate-5-core-ontology.md:19-20`; `gate-5-relationship-representation-ledger.md:36,59,88`).
- Public/private unknowns remain unknown. Real filed matters and authority records can establish public instances; permissioned scope cannot be invented before the actual filed or reviewed record establishes it (`gate-4-relationship-candidates.md:234`).

## Verdict meanings

- **REQUIRED CORE** — a current accepted operator proposition and real workflow loses requested-versus-authority-recognized scope, exclusions, quantities/amounts, versions, basis, or history if the type is absent. A factless link, event, or other relationship owner cannot answer the same question.
- **CONDITIONAL REOPENING** — the endpoint is semantically valid, but the current accepted workflow/evidence does not yet establish a deployable, independently fact-bearing instance class. Reopen only on the stated real matter, evidence, and deletion failure.
- **REJECT AS DUPLICATE** — current accepted workflows are completely answered by a factless context/concerns link, another sole fact owner, and/or an event/outcome. Deploying the scope type now would create a second owner for the same truth.

## Minimum-core boundary checks

### Why the five endpoint pairs are required

The accepted C1 correction identifies the omission that must be repaired: Applications, claims, permits, audits, and recovery otherwise depend on event fragments or an unowned contextual link, specifically for member, Holding, Parcel, Intervention, requested/admitted/excluded scope, claim stage, quantity, amount, period, and basis (`gate-5-mece-coverage-audit.md:212-220`). Natural Person and Organization are both required because the accepted model treats a member/beneficiary as a person or business and preserves concrete actor identity; neither concrete endpoint may be hidden by a generic Proceeding Subject Interface (`connected-operating-model.md:282-284`; `gate-5-core-ontology.md:24-25,32`).

For all five endpoint pairs, deletion loses the comparison operators actually need:

- filed versus currently claimed;
- claimed versus reviewed;
- reviewed versus admitted or excluded;
- admitted versus decided/permitted;
- one subject/quantity/amount versus the other subjects in the same matter; and
- current scope versus the earlier scope selectively reopened by a later event.

A final decision cannot reconstruct requested, reviewed, excluded, or earlier scope, and one Proceeding can carry several member-, Holding-, Parcel-, Plant-, Lot-, or Intervention-specific scopes (`gate-4-relationship-candidates.md:218-223`). The current core retains only the endpoint grains proved necessary now.

### Why events do not replace the required ten

`Public Filing Submission` answers **which filing occurrence/version was externally released and protocolled**. It does not own the durable claimed subject scope across submissions. Public decisions, integration, exclusion, appeal, audit, and recovery are occurrences/outcomes; they can create or change scope but are not scope relationships (`gate-5-integrated-candidate-synthesis.md:111-115`; `gate-4-relationship-candidates.md:225-232`).

Likewise, eligibility, admissibility, ranking, concession, payment instruction, liquidation, payment order, settlement, audit, revocation, and recovery remain separate outcomes. Storing only those outcomes would recreate the exact event-fragment omission identified by C1 and would erase the matter’s independently changing subject scope.

### Why other relationship owners do not replace the required ten

- F1 proceeding participation/decision authority owns **who participates or may decide**, not what subject/quantity/amount the matter claims or recognizes.
- F2 owns Holding–Parcel association and actor–Parcel rights, not filed or decided matter scope.
- F3 Instrument effects own **what the Instrument normatively does**; an Instrument may ground an authority determination without owning that Proceeding’s reviewed/admitted/excluded history.
- F5 owns the Intervention’s physical/material truth across work, not the scope asserted or recognized in one application, permit, claim, audit, or recovery matter.
- F7 `Member Entitlement` and `Creditor Right` own resulting durable rights/debt; they do not reconstruct pre-award requests, reviewed exclusions, or Proceeding-specific admitted scope (`gate-5-mece-coverage-audit.md:234-243`).

### Reopening rule for the six conditional types

A conditional type enters the deployable core only when all of the following are evidenced:

1. an actual Public Proceeding and effective filed or authority record preserve the endpoint-specific scope;
2. the scope has independent quantity, amount, basis, version, effective history, inclusion/exclusion, or authority facts;
3. deleting it loses a current accepted operator answer;
4. a factless `concerns` link is insufficient;
5. no event, endpoint, F1/F3/F5/F7 relationship, or contextual association already owns the fact; and
6. for `Individual Plant`, each endpoint satisfies the accepted stable-identity boundary (`gate-5-core-ontology.md:16,33`).

Until then, do not deploy empty types and do not fabricate private instances.

## Final deployment instruction

The minimum current Predicate 9 deployment is therefore **five endpoint specializations in each of the two accepted authority classes: Natural Person, Organization, Agricultural Holding, Cadastral Parcel, and Intervention**. This is **10 exact types**.

For a genuinely factless matter-subject association, retain one direct `concerns` relationship. When a required object-backed scope instance exists, expose concise subject navigation from that one fact owner rather than maintaining a duplicate direct link. Do not deploy the six conditional or four rejected types merely to complete the 20-cell matrix.
