# Formal institutional workflows connecting Land, Funding, Applications, Field Work, and Payments

**Scope.** EU, Italy, and Puglia agricultural-support and plant-health administration, with emphasis on Xylella fastidiosa, cooperatives/recognised producer organisations (OP/AOP), CAAs, SIAN/fascicolo aziendale, public calls, execution, payment claims, liquidation, controls, and appeals.

**Research date:** 20 August 2026.  
**Method:** exploratory web research in Italian and English. Primary law, official instructions, public acts, portals, and agency pages were used first. Professional and judicial material was used only to discover terms and adjacent processes. Each substantive statement below is labelled **Fact**, **Inference**, or **Unresolved**. A citation points to the source that supports the statement; an inference cites its factual premises.

## 1. Executive findings

1. **There is no single end-to-end administrative owner.** The process is a chain of legally distinct determinations: the plant-health authority determines official status and duties; the managing authority/measure office determines eligibility and concession; the paying agency authorises and executes CAP payment; a CAA may maintain the dossier and transmit applications; ARIF and private operators execute or supervise field work. Delegation does not move final payment authority from the accredited paying agency.[S1][S6][S18]
2. **Land is an evidence-bearing junction, not an autonomous source of truth.** A parcel can simultaneously have cadastral identity, fascicolo/LPIS geometry, title-of-conduction evidence, official Xylella zone status, an order-specific affected-tree set, an aid commitment, and an executed-work footprint. These states come from different authorities and effective dates. Treating one map as all of them is a category error.[S2][S3][S10][S20]
3. **Eligibility is decision-time and evidence-time dependent.** A technically eligible parcel may still fail because the applicant, title, fascicolo validation, mandate, budget rank, deadline, aid-intensity ceiling, procurement trail, or payment evidence fails. A complete population therefore requires at least `eligible`, `ineligible`, `unresolved/missing evidence`, `not yet assessable`, and `eligible but unfunded`.[S4][S7][S14][S25]
4. **The application is legally the beneficiary's assertion even when a CAA prepares/transmits it.** EU IACS rules keep responsibility for correctness with the beneficiary. Italian rules let public bodies delegate dossier maintenance, acceptance, and registration to CAAs, but the CAA acts under mandate and within a convention; it does not become the beneficiary or the deciding administration.[S4][S17][S18]
5. **An award is not field authority.** Field authority may come from a phytosanitary order, a programme approval, or a concession act. Each has a different object, issuer, permitted work, timing, and consequence. Before work, the operator still needs a work package that resolves parcel/tree identity, permissions, technical prescriptions, contractor, timing, and evidence plan.[S2][S9][S10][S11]
6. **A payment claim is a new administrative decision, not a clerical continuation of the award.** The authority checks realised work, eligible expenditure, deadlines, variants, title and identity continuity, controls, debt/IBAN/antimafia/DURC where applicable, and the grant ceiling. Payment is authorised only after those checks. The accredited paying agency executes CAP payments directly even where other tasks are delegated.[S5][S6][S13][S16]
7. **Feedback loops are formal workflow components.** AMS warnings may let a beneficiary amend an area claim; pre-rejection/riesame processes let an applicant contest an adverse eligibility result; monitoring and inspections create new official states and orders; executed work and controls feed next year's prefilled application; variants and force-majeure events can modify execution obligations.[S4][S5][S19][S25]

## 2. Terminology

| Italian term | Working definition and operational significance |
|---|---|
| **Azienda agricola / beneficiario** | The legal or natural person applying for and responsible for the aid claim. The beneficiary remains responsible for application correctness even if another actor prepares it.[S4] |
| **CUAA** | Unique identifier of an agricultural enterprise, commonly the tax code. It is the subject key used by SIAN/fascicolo workflows; it does not identify a parcel or an OP membership relationship.[S25] |
| **Fascicolo aziendale** | The official enterprise dossier containing identity, territorial consistency, holdings, titles, bank/contact and other evidence used across agricultural procedures. A CAA may constitute, update and hold it under a paying-agency convention.[S17][S18] |
| **CAA (Centro di Assistenza Agricola)** | An authorised agricultural assistance centre. Under mandate and conventions it may update the registry/fascicolo, accept/register applications, assist preparation, attest certain technical facts, check documentary completeness, and query SIAN practice status.[S17] |
| **SIAN** | Sistema Informativo Agricolo Nazionale: the national agricultural information environment through which many fascicolo, programme, support and payment functions operate. Individual procedures may instead use a regional portal such as PMA.[S14][S25] |
| **LPIS/SIPA** | Land Parcel Identification System / Sistema di Identificazione delle Parcelle Agricole. The reference spatial layer for area-based CAP administration, distinct from legal cadastral ownership and from Xylella demarcation maps.[S5] |
| **Domanda di sostegno/aiuto (DdS/DA)** | Application requesting admission or a commitment/award. A programme may separate a support application from later annual executive-programme or payment applications.[S14][S25] |
| **Domanda di pagamento (DdP)** | Claim for advance, interim/SAL, or final balance after an award/approved programme. It asserts actual eligible work or expenditure and triggers payment controls.[S6][S13] |
| **Rilascio / protocollazione** | Final electronic submission and receipt/protocol event. A draft saved in a portal is not necessarily a lodged application; a receipt is a key record.[S14][S25] |
| **Istruttoria** | Administrative/technical examination. It may produce `ricevibile`, `ammissibile`, `parzialmente ammissibile`, `non ammissibile`, or `non ricevibile`; programme screens and portals may show separate control outcomes.[S25] |
| **Graduatoria** | Ranked list based on selection criteria and available budget. Placement can be “useful for examination” without yet being a concession.[S8][S12] |
| **Scorrimento** | Movement down a ranking after additional funds, renunciations, corrections, or unavailability. It can reopen a previously dormant application but does not erase later technical-administrative examination.[S8][S20] |
| **Provvedimento di concessione** | Formal grant/commitment act. It fixes the beneficiary, eligible project/expenditure, aid ceiling, obligations, and often deadlines. It is distinct from ranking and payment liquidation.[S8][S20] |
| **SAL** | Stato avanzamento lavori, an interim progress/payment stage. It requires evidence of completed work and expenditure and may be subject to minimum/maximum tranches.[S13] |
| **Saldo** | Final payment claim and final eligible-balance determination. It often closes physical/financial execution but remains subject to later ex-post controls or recovery.[S6][S11] |
| **Liquidazione** | Administrative act determining an amount payable after checks. A public page for the Xylella replant measure separately publishes concession and later liquidation acts, confirming the distinction.[S11][S20] |
| **Variante / adattamento tecnico** | A requested or permitted change to the approved project. Material changes generally require prior authorisation; minor adaptations may follow a lighter procedure. Executing an unapproved material change risks exclusion/reduction.[S11] |
| **Rinuncia / revoca / decadenza** | Applicant withdrawal; authority withdrawal of an award; or loss of entitlement due to breach/time/event. Each must remain a separate state because financial and ranking consequences differ.[S8][S20] |
| **Subentro** | Approved succession/replacement of the applicant/beneficiary, for example after death. Puglia created a dedicated pre-concession death-substitution portal workflow requiring the deceased's CUAA and successor data.[S19] |
| **OP/AOP** | Recognised producer organisation / association of producer organisations. Recognition is a legal status under the CMO, not merely a cooperative label. Only recognised bodies may access specified sectoral operational programmes.[S14][S15] |
| **Programma operativo / programma esecutivo annuale** | Multiannual OP/AOP plan and its annual execution slice. Olive OP/AOP programmes are submitted through SIAN; annual executive plans, operating funds, variants, controls, and balances form a recurring lifecycle.[S14] |
| **VPC** | Valore della produzione commercializzata, a calculated/verified value used to cap Union support to an OP/AOP operational programme.[S14] |
| **Fondo di esercizio** | Separately accounted annual operating fund financing an OP/AOP operational programme, combining Union, national where applicable, and beneficiary/member resources.[S14] |
| **Servizio/Osservatorio fitosanitario regionale** | Competent regional plant-health authority: sets surveillance and phytosanitary prescriptions, adopts official acts/orders, and uses inspectors. It is not the paying agency.[S2][S9][S22] |
| **ARIF** | Agenzia Regionale per le Attività Irrigue e Forestali. In the cited Puglia Xylella workflow it conducts monitoring under the Observatorio's plan, can perform removals, supervises its removals, and instructs/liquidates specified removal indemnities.[S9][S10] |
| **Zona infetta / zona cuscinetto / area delimitata** | Official plant-health geography. Demarcated areas combine infected and buffer zones. Eradication and containment impose different removal/survey duties.[S2][S3] |
| **Atto ingiuntivo / ordine di rimozione** | Official act prescribing removal or other phytosanitary measures for identified plants/land. It creates a compulsory duty independent of whether funding exists.[S9] |
| **Eradicazione / contenimento** | Two distinct regulatory strategies. Eradication removes infected and specified nearby plants; containment, where eradication is no longer possible, removes officially infected plants and intensifies frontier/buffer measures.[S2][S9] |
| **AMS** | Area Monitoring System using regular satellite/other observation. It can generate guiding alerts and amendments before a national cutoff; not every condition is monitorable.[S4][S5] |
| **Riesame / preavviso di rigetto** | Administrative feedback/contest step before or after an adverse result. One SIAN procedure exposes adverse control outcomes and allows a request within ten working days of notice.[S25] |
| **Forza maggiore / circostanze eccezionali** | Recognised events such as severe disaster, plant disease, death, or long incapacity that can excuse or reshape CAP obligations if invoked and evidenced under the applicable procedure.[S6] |

| **Contractor / farm worker** | Executes only assigned works under contract/order and prescriptions. | Timesheets, geotagged evidence, machinery/material records, invoices, waste/destruction records. | Legal interpretation or beneficiary authorisation. |
| **Finance officer** | Checks budget, invoices, traceability, co-finance, bank data, claim pack; internal authorisation according to delegation matrix. | Accounting ledger, payment proofs, reconciliations, claim approval. | Public payment authorisation; eligibility of an unapproved variant. |
| **Beneficiary/owner/occupier** | Decides to apply; supplies and attests evidence; authorises submission; obeys orders; chooses voluntary removal or ARIF route where offered; grants access; remains responsible for claim correctness.[S4][S9] | Declarations, title/tenure, mandate, application signature, work consent, claim evidence. | Official finding, ranking, or payment amount. |
| **Prefect/law enforcement** | Supports forced access/removal where refusal blocks a phytosanitary order.[S9] | Assistance/incident record. | Technical disease or aid decision. |
| **Certification/audit bodies, Commission auditors** | Audit management/control systems and expenditure; identify deficiencies; drive remediation/corrections.[S6] | Audit plan, sample, finding, opinion, action plan. | Routine operational scheduling. |

### Internal decision-rights rule for a cooperative operator

**Inference.** A safe internal RACI follows consequence rather than screen ownership: a domain service may compute and prepare; only the role bearing legal/financial consequence authorises. Thus: legal-status changes require authoritative source/version; pursuit requires board/authorised manager; application release requires beneficiary/signatory; field assignment requires manager after source validation; payment claim requires finance/signatory after evidence and control readiness. This is inferred from the beneficiary-responsibility rule, CAA delegation limits, plant-health authority powers, and paying-agency non-delegable payment execution.[S4][S6][S9][S17]

## 4. Detailed workflow 1 — Land: official status, duties, and evidence

### Trigger

- Scheduled annual/multiannual official surveillance; a report/suspicion by an operator or member of the public; an official positive; a changed EU/national/regional act; or a new cadastral/fascicolo event.[S2][S3][S10]

### Workflow

1. **Register the subject and holding.** The beneficiary/enterprise is identified by CUAA and an active mandate where a CAA acts. The fascicolo is constituted or updated with identity, holdings, titles and territorial data. **Fact:** CAAs may do this only under the relevant conventions and must pursue document dematerialisation.[S17]
2. **Validate the land evidence.** Separate: cadastral reference, title of ownership/conduction, fascicolo agricultural unit, LPIS/reference parcel, declared crop/use, and any map geometry. Preserve source date and validation event. **Inference:** cadastral ownership, fascicolo control, and CAP reference geometry are non-equivalent because they serve different systems and decision purposes.[S5][S17]
3. **Acquire official plant-health geography.** Ingest the current demarcation act and map as an effective-dated legal snapshot. Record infected zone, buffer zone, containment strip/area, outbreak/eradication radius and the instrument that created each.[S2][S3]
4. **Conduct risk-based survey.** The Observatorio defines annual objectives, areas, surveillance, vector monitoring, roles and timetable; ARIF executes monitoring under those directions. Sampling records need location, host, date, operator, chain of custody and method.[S2][S10]
5. **Laboratory result.** Result is positive, negative, or undetermined. EU reporting templates expressly accommodate undetermined samples and require method/sampling information. **Fact:** an undetermined result is not a negative.[S2]
6. **Official legal classification.** The competent authority determines whether the finding creates/updates a demarcated area and whether eradication or containment applies. This is a legal act, not a model prediction.[S2][S3]
7. **Identify affected property and persons.** In the cited Puglia workflow, InnovaPuglia sends the regional authority cadastral and owner data after laboratory results; the authority issues injunctions.[S9]
8. **Notify and publish.** The cited procedure publishes the injunction for seven consecutive days on the municipal/public notice route and also sends it to ARIF by PEC and publishes it in BURP, the Xylella portal and administrative transparency pages.[S9]
9. **Create parcel duties.** Duties may include access, vector treatment, removal of specific plants, destruction method, movement restriction, annual inspection, or land-management action. They depend on legal regime, host/species, result and effective instrument.[S2][S9]
10. **Owner election/handoff.** During publication and at most seven days after, the owner communicates voluntary removal or ARIF execution via Annex B in the cited workflow. Voluntary or ARIF removal then has a cited maximum ten-day execution window; silence routes to ARIF; refusal routes to forced execution with Prefect/law enforcement and cost recovery/sanction.[S9]
11. **Inspect closure.** Regional inspectors supervise voluntary removal; two ARIF phytosanitary technicians supervise ARIF removal. Preserve treatment-before-removal, tree identity, destruction/devitalisation and completion records.[S9]
12. **Feed downstream systems.** Official status/duty becomes an input to funding eligibility, field work, indemnity, and next monitoring cycle. Execution does not automatically erase the historical positive or legal act; it adds a completion state.

### Exceptions and clocks

- Monumental/historic plant derogation requires annual inspection/testing and vector controls and only applies if conditions are satisfied.[S9]
- If roots cannot be removed due to location, Puglia allows branch/foliage destruction plus root devitalisation only after regional authorisation.[S9]
- Vector treatment precedes removal except in the cited December–March no-adult period.[S9]
- Weather can make mandatory land work impracticable. In 2026 Puglia moved deadlines to 15 May below 200 m and 30 May above 200 m after persistent rain and stakeholder/municipal feedback.[S21]
- A parcel can be inside a zone but have no parcel-specific removal order. Conversely, a parcel-specific order can impose a strict duty even if no funding window is open. **Inference** from the separate legal bases and actors.[S2][S9][S20]

### Records and common failures

- **Records:** effective-dated act and map; parcel/title crosswalk; sample and chain-of-custody; lab result; injunction and publication/PEC proof; owner response; access record; work evidence; inspector closure; sanction/cost/indemnity links.
- **Failures:** stale map; wrong decree attributed to a polygon; conflating containment with eradication; treating negative/undetermined/pending alike; owner not located; cadastral-to-fascicolo mismatch; publication without usable contact; inaccessible parcel; missed treatment window; weather; protected-tree exception not evidenced; work done without inspection; completion not written back.

## 5. Detailed workflow 2 — Funding: compute population, then decide pursuit

### Trigger

Publication of a call/notice or sector programme cycle; new/changed budget; ranking scorrimento; new official status; newly validated fascicolo evidence; or a cooperative board request.

### Workflow

1. **Register the funding instrument.** Store official call, attachments, amendment/supersession chain, BURP publication, opening/closing dates, budget, authority, fund, intervention, geography, eligible beneficiaries, aid intensity, selection criteria, required evidence, state-aid basis, and payment model.[S7][S8][S20]
2. **Define the legal decision grain.** Determine whether eligibility is per applicant, holding, parcel, tree, investment, OP programme, member, or expense. Never calculate one level and label another.
3. **Build the candidate population.** Start from all in-scope cooperative members/holdings/parcels, not only people who asked about the call. Join current fascicolo/title, official geography/duty, crop/host, enterprise status, OP recognition/membership where required, prior aid, and programme-specific evidence.[S14][S17]
4. **Evaluate gates in order.** Recommended categories: jurisdiction/time; applicant type; recognition/member status; title/control duration; parcel/location; technical action; expenditure; selection evidence; exclusion/compliance; budget/rank. Each criterion stores citation, input, result, and as-of date.
5. **Produce five outcomes.** `eligible`; `ineligible` with failed criterion; `unresolved` with missing/conflicting evidence; `not-yet-assessable` because an authority/portal/event is pending; `eligible-but-unfunded` after ranking/budget. **Inference:** public ranking and subsequent scorrimenti prove that legal admissibility and funding availability are different states.[S8][S20]
6. **Calculate economics.** Estimate eligible cost, aid intensity/cap, co-finance, cash-flow before reimbursement, implementation cost, evidence burden, and downside of delay/recovery. OP/AOP programmes additionally depend on VPC and operating-fund limits.[S14]
7. **Cooperative pursuit decision.** Board/authorised manager chooses pursue/decline/defer, based on the complete population and economics. Record resolution, delegated signatory, co-finance, member-selection rule, conflicts and capacity.
8. **Freeze a decision snapshot.** Preserve the funding-rule version, population, evidence hashes/links, exceptions and assumptions used. Later corrections should create a new assessment rather than overwrite history.
9. **Monitor change.** Amendments, FAQs, budget increases, rankings, concessions, renunciations, and scorrimenti trigger recomputation. Puglia's replant measure shows repeated ranking correction, admission to examination, concessions, and later reopening for delegation.[S8][S20]

### OP/AOP sibling workflow

1. Maintain recognition status; only recognised OP/AOP may submit the specified olive operational programme.[S14][S15]
2. Establish member/production evidence and VPC; create the separately accounted annual operating fund.[S14]
3. Submit the multiannual programme through SIAN, then annual executive programmes by the instrument's deadline; obtain an electronic receipt.[S14]
4. Regional authority approves OP programmes; MASAF handles AOP/national aspects under the programme architecture; approval lists and financing are published.[S14]
5. Execute annual programme, manage variants, maintain member/beneficiary contributions and expense traceability.
6. Submit advance/partial/balance claims under AGEA instructions; controls may be delegated, but final payment remains paying-agency work.[S6][S14]
7. Recognition status must remain valid through the relevant stage; the cited AGEA instruction requires the recognition act to reach AGEA before final balance.[S14]

### Records and failures

- **Records:** funding-window snapshot; criterion ledger; complete candidate population; board resolution; co-finance and capacity check; OP recognition/VPC/fund records; ranking and concession links.
- **Failures:** call found late; amendment missed; eligibility evaluated only for volunteers; stale fascicolo; cooperative mistaken for OP; wrong decision grain; missing title duration; false “ineligible” when only unresolved; points claimed without proof; budget rank confused with legal merit; no cash to bridge reimbursement; duplicate/cumulated aid; recognition lapses.

## 6. Detailed workflow 3 — Applications: preparation, exception resolution, authorisation

### Trigger

Recorded pursue decision and an open submission window.

### Workflow

1. **Open application case.** Bind applicant CUAA, instrument/version, relevant parcels/actions, mandate, signatory and deadline. Use a checklist derived from the official call, not a generic template.
2. **Fascicolo preflight.** Ensure the controlling fascicolo is current/validated; reconcile identity, PEC, IBAN, titles, land/crop and required registers. A CAA may update/custody the dossier under convention.[S17]
3. **Authority/mandate preflight.** Verify CAA mandate, cooperative member consent, board resolution, signatory power, and any power of attorney. Portal access is not equivalent to legal authority.
4. **Generate draft fields and dossier.** Populate from authoritative sources with provenance. Mark every transformation and every unresolved value. Do not silently choose between conflicting hectares, titles, names or geometries.
5. **Resolve exceptions.** Route land/title conflicts to beneficiary/CAA; official-status conflicts to plant-health authority evidence; financial/cumulation questions to finance/legal; death to subentro; missing delegation to signatory/member. Puglia's replant process provides a dedicated PMA subentro path and a later delegation-reopening path, showing these are formal exceptions, not notes.[S19][S20]
6. **Run application controls.** Completeness, date, applicant/beneficiary identity, active fascicolo, parcel overlap, expenditure limits, supporting documents, declarations, antimafia/DURC/IBAN where applicable, signature, and duplicates. Preserve control result per criterion.
7. **Operator review.** Show source data, calculated fields, unresolved exceptions, and legal declarations. The human must be able to reject or correct machine-prepared content.
8. **Authorise and release.** Beneficiary/authorised signatory approves final submission. **Fact:** EU rules retain beneficiary responsibility for correctness even under an automatic claim system.[S4]
9. **Protocol and receipt.** Submit through SIAN/PMA/other named portal; store release timestamp, application ID, protocol and receipt. Drafting without a receipt is not completion.[S14][S25]
10. **Administrative examination.** Authority checks receipt and admissibility, then technical merit, selection and expenditure. Results may be wholly/partly admissible, inadmissible or not receivable. SIAN examples expose criterion-level outcomes and amounts.[S25]
11. **Feedback and review.** Where the procedure allows, receive pre-rejection or adverse outcome, file observations/riesame with evidence, and preserve authority response. The cited SIAN risk-management procedure allows a request within ten working days after notice; the exact clock varies by instrument.[S25]
12. **Ranking/concession handoff.** If selected/funded, accept concession and obligations. If useful only for future examination, monitor scorrimento. If rejected, distinguish correction/review, appeal, and closed failure.

### AMS loop for area-based claims

National administration pre-fills the application; the beneficiary confirms/corrects/completes it. Administrative checks, AMS and non-monitorable on-site checks follow. AMS warnings can permit amendment before the national cutoff, unless a prohibited state such as notified on-site inspection applies. Findings affect payment and update next year's prefilled information.[S4][S5]

### Records and failures

- **Records:** checklist/version; mandate and authority; field-level provenance; exceptions; final signed payload; submission receipt; communications; criterion results; observations/riesame; ranking/concession.
- **Failures:** portal credential/role mismatch; expired mandate; draft not released; wrong portal; late submission; unreadable/missing attachment; document valid at upload but expired at examination; inconsistent surface/parcel; beneficial-owner/antimafia issue; missing PEC; wrong IBAN; material declaration left to preparer without signatory review; partial admissibility hidden as binary rejection; review clock missed.

## 7. Detailed workflow 4 — Field Work: orders/awards to safe execution

### Trigger

A source-supported phytosanitary duty/order, concession, approved annual programme, or approved variant. A manager must not schedule from an uncited dashboard flag alone.

### Workflow

1. **Validate authority to act.** Identify source type: compulsory injunction; voluntary funded project; OP operational programme; routine mandatory vector control. Confirm issuer, effective dates, appeal/suspension state, owner/occupier, parcel/tree/action and current variant.
2. **Resolve overlaps.** If a parcel has both a compulsory removal and a funded investment, decide which costs/actions are fundable and prevent double payment. Preserve legal duty even where funding is declined.
3. **Create work package.** Include cadastral and operational location; exact plants/area; work method; pre-treatment; timing; protected-tree/root exceptions; waste/destruction; access; contractor; safety; required proof; inspector/witness; cost code.
4. **Manager approval and scheduling.** Check crew/equipment, weather, vector stage, land access, materials/approved cultivar, dependencies and statutory/project deadlines. Mandatory tillage deadlines may be formally changed for weather; only the new act changes the clock.[S21]
5. **Owner/member notice and access.** Record appointment, access consent/refusal, and escalation. For forced Xylella removals, ARIF informs the Prefect and police and charges removal cost to the owner under the cited process.[S9]
6. **Execute prescribed preconditions.** For Xylella removal, perform required vector treatment except the specified seasonal exception; mark protected/historic plants and root-removal exception approvals.[S9]
7. **Perform work.** Contractor/crew records start/end, workers, equipment/material, geolocation, quantities, trees/area, destruction or planting, deviations, and incidents.
8. **Supervise/inspect.** Voluntary removal is checked by regional inspectors; ARIF removal by two ARIF phytosanitary technicians in the cited plan.[S9]
9. **Deviation gate.** Stop and route material scope, species, quantity, design or parcel changes to the awarding/plant-health authority before continuing when prior approval is required. Puglia issued a specific act governing final balance, variants and technical adaptations for the replant measure.[S11]
10. **Close field operation.** Inspector/manager accepts or rejects evidence; create completed/partially completed/failed/inaccessible states and residual duties.
11. **Financial handoff.** Match accepted quantities and invoices/payment proofs to concession cost categories. Send only claim-ready items to finance; retain non-claimable compulsory work separately.
12. **Administrative feedback.** Completion updates order status, programme progress, payment claim, asset/planting register, and future monitoring. Failure can trigger rework, sanction, award revocation or recovery.

### Records and failures

- **Records:** authority snapshot; work order; access; pre-treatment; geotagged before/during/after evidence; inspector record; material/cultivar provenance; invoice and proof of payment; deviation/variant; completion/remaining duty.
- **Failures:** manager schedules before concession/order is effective; wrong parcel/tree; stale zone; no owner access; weather; adult-vector treatment omitted; historic-tree exception mishandled; unapproved variant; contractor invoice does not map to work; work completed after deadline; photos lack time/location; destruction record missing; award work and compulsory work commingled; completion never feeds finance/status.

## 8. Detailed workflow 5 — Payments: award rules to claim, authorisation, liquidation and recovery

### Trigger

Concession/approved programme reaches an advance, SAL or final-balance milestone and sufficient work/expenditure evidence exists.

### Workflow

1. **Instantiate payment stages from the award.** Store permitted advance, SAL and saldo stages, guarantees, tranche limits, eligible dates, completion deadline, claim deadline, retention, co-finance, procurement and durability obligations. **Inference:** “next payment stage” is scheme-specific; it cannot be inferred merely from work being complete.[S6][S11][S14]
2. **Finance preflight.** Reconcile approved budget to actual ledger; beneficiary and bank data; invoice supplier/date/category; payment traceability; tax/VAT treatment; procurement/quotes; asset register; co-finance; prior advances; cumulative aid; debt/offset; required DURC/antimafia. A SIAN procedure explicitly exposes controls for active fascicolo, expense proof, antimafia, payment proof and DURC depending on applicability.[S25]
3. **Physical preflight.** Match claimed quantities to inspector/manager acceptance, parcel/tree/action, approved plan and variant. Exclude unresolved or outside-scope items rather than smoothing them into an aggregate.
4. **Prepare payment application.** Build claim lines with award line, invoice, proof of payment, work evidence, eligible amount and reduction reason. Draft the relevant declaration and attach certificates.
5. **Internal authorisation.** Finance verifies arithmetic and evidence; project manager confirms physical completion; legal/authorised signatory resolves exceptions and signs. Separation of duties reduces self-certification risk.
6. **Submit and receive protocol.** File through the named portal and store receipt. Claims may be not received, suspended, partially admitted or admitted.
7. **Administrative checks.** Delegated body/paying agency checks completeness, legality, regularity, calculations, databases, duplicates, debts and applicable conditions. IACS uses systematic administrative and automated cross-checks.[S5][S6]
8. **Remote/on-site check.** Monitorable area conditions may be checked via AMS; other conditions through sample-based or risk-based on-site checks. Some non-IACS expenditure can face ex-post control after payment.[S6]
9. **Contradictory/review loop.** Authority requests integration, issues adverse result, or opens review. Respond within the procedure-specific deadline. Criterion-level states and evidence should remain visible.[S25]
10. **Determine payable amount.** Apply eligible-cost ceilings, aid intensity, previous payments/advance recovery, reductions/penalties and offsets. Record gross claim, admitted amount, reduction code and net payable.
11. **Authorise and execute.** For CAP expenditure the accredited paying agency authorises after sufficient checks and directly executes payment; delegated bodies cannot execute it in the paying agency's place.[S6]
12. **Liquidation/public act.** Where required, the regional office publishes/adopts a liquidation determination. Puglia's replant measure has separate concession and final-saldo liquidation publications.[S20][S24]
13. **Close and retain.** Reconcile bank receipt, programme accounts and member/beneficiary ledger; retain evidence for audit/durability. Update stage to paid/part-paid/suspended/recovered.
14. **Post-payment control and recovery.** Audit or later evidence can identify irregularity. The paying agency/member state must prevent/detect/correct and recover undue payments, with effective, proportionate and dissuasive penalties where applicable.[S6]

### Exceptions

- **Advance:** may require a bank/insurance guarantee and later clearance against actual expenditure.
- **Partial admission:** preserve rejected lines/reasons and review rights; do not overwrite the original claim.
- **Death/incapacity/disaster/plant disease:** may support force-majeure treatment, but requires procedure-specific notice and evidence.[S6]
- **IBAN change:** permitted only through an authorised procedure and eligible fascicolo bank account; the cited SIAN manual limits CAA users to mandated cases.[S25]
- **Recognition lapse:** OP/AOP final balance can be blocked until valid recognition evidence reaches AGEA.[S14]
- **Debt/irregularity:** payment may be offset, suspended or recovered; financial closure and project closure are distinct.

### Records and failures

- **Records:** award/payment-stage rules; claim-line ledger; invoices/proofs; controls; inspection; review communication; authorised amount; liquidation/payment IDs; bank reconciliation; audit/recovery.
- **Failures:** claiming before payment/physical completion; invoice outside eligibility dates; cash/untraceable payment; supplier/beneficiary mismatch; missing procurement trail; unapproved variant; VAT misclassified; duplicate aid; expired DURC/antimafia; wrong IBAN; guarantee not renewed; final claim late; partial rejection hidden; payment received but not allocated to member/project; retention period breached.

### High-risk “wrong but plausible” states

- `ineligible` when evidence is merely missing;
- `no duty` when there is no parcel-specific order but a general mandatory land-management measure applies;
- `authorised` when there is a ranking but no concession;
- `submitted` when only a draft exists;
- `complete` when work is done but not inspected/evidenced;
- `payable` when a concession exists but no eligible expenditure was proved;
- `paid` when the authority issued liquidation but bank execution/reconciliation is pending;
- `OP` when the entity is only a cooperative;
- `negative` when the sample is undetermined or pending;
- `current` when the cited rule has been amended or the deadline formally moved.

## 11. Assessment of the five starting assertions

### Assertion 1 — “Land explains official status/duties; Field Work owns execution.”

**Verdict: substantially confirmed, but needs two constraints.**

- **Confirming fact:** the plant-health authority establishes official regimes and injunctions, while the owner/ARIF carries out removal and inspectors/ARIF technicians verify execution.[S2][S9]
- **Challenge:** “Land” must not invent status from spatial overlap. Official status is an effective-dated legal determination supported by survey/lab and an act. It must retain cadastral, fascicolo, LPIS and legal-zone identities separately.[S2][S3][S5]
- **Challenge:** Field Work does not “own” legal completion. It owns scheduling/execution evidence; the competent inspector/authority closes or confirms compliance where the procedure requires.[S9]
- **Revised assertion:** **Land assembles and explains source-supported official status and duties; Field Work plans and records execution; the competent authority/inspector determines legal compliance.**

### Assertion 2 — “Funding computes the complete eligible/ineligible/unresolved population; cooperative decides whether to pursue.”

**Verdict: confirmed and strengthened.**

- **Confirming fact:** calls define beneficiary, area, action, timing, selection and budget; ranking and scorrimento separate eligibility from budget availability.[S8][S20]
- **Confirming fact:** OP access depends on recognition and programme/VPC rules, so a cooperative's internal status is not enough.[S14][S15]
- **Challenge:** three states are insufficient. Add `not-yet-assessable` and `eligible-but-unfunded`, and calculate co-finance/cash-flow/capacity before a board decision.[S8][S14]
- **Revised assertion:** **Funding computes the whole in-scope population into at least five states with citation and evidence gaps; the authorised cooperative body decides pursuit and co-finance.**

### Assertion 3 — “CORDON prepares applications; operator resolves exceptions and authorizes.”

**Verdict: confirmed if “operator” means the legally authorised beneficiary/signatory, not any back-office user.**

- **Confirming fact:** CAAs can prepare, accept and register applications and maintain the fascicolo under mandate/convention.[S17]
- **Confirming fact:** EU IACS rules keep responsibility for correctness with the beneficiary.[S4]
- **Challenge:** some exceptions require external authority resolution (official zone conflict, title adjudication, antimafia, recognition, subentro), not merely operator choice.[S14][S19][S25]
- **Revised assertion:** **CORDON may assemble a cited draft and route exceptions; the beneficiary/authorised signatory approves release; competent external actors resolve exceptions outside the beneficiary's decision rights.**

### Assertion 4 — “Source-supported duties/orders/awards prepare field operations; manager schedules/assigns.”

**Verdict: confirmed, with a mandatory variant/access/evidence gate.**

- **Confirming fact:** Xylella work has explicit order, notice, owner election, execution and inspection steps.[S9]
- **Confirming fact:** award execution and final balance have separate rules for variants and technical adaptations.[S11]
- **Challenge:** an award is not always permission to start immediately; conditions precedent, acceptance, access, procurement, treatment season, protected trees and approved variants can intervene.[S9][S11]
- **Revised assertion:** **Validated order/award plus satisfied conditions creates a work package; manager schedules/assigns; material deviations return to the competent authority before work continues.**

### Assertion 5 — “Award rules determine/prep the next payment stage; finance authorizes.”

**Verdict: partly confirmed; public authorisation must be stated more precisely.**

- **Confirming fact:** award/programme rules determine advance/SAL/saldo evidence and ceilings; Puglia separately regulates final-balance and variants.[S11][S14]
- **Challenge:** internal finance can authorise the cooperative's claim submission, but it cannot authorise public CAP payment. The accredited paying agency authorises after checks and executes payment directly.[S6]
- **Challenge:** physical acceptance, compliance certificates, debt and review states can block payment even when finance's dossier is arithmetically complete.[S6][S25]
- **Revised assertion:** **Award rules instantiate claim stages; Field Work and finance prepare evidence; authorised signatory releases the claim; the delegated control body/paying agency checks it; the accredited paying agency authorises and executes CAP payment.**

## 12. Common control design implied by the institutions

**Inference, grounded in the workflows above.**

1. Use immutable effective-dated source snapshots for acts, calls, zone maps, rankings, concessions and payment rules.
2. Represent separate objects for `Holding/Parcel`, `OfficialStatus`, `Duty/Order`, `FundingWindow`, `EligibilityAssessment`, `Application`, `Award`, `WorkOrder`, `WorkEvidence`, `PaymentClaim`, `PaymentDecision`, and `Recovery`.
3. Store provenance at field and criterion level. A URL alone is inadequate; include issuer, act/article/annex, publication/effective date and retrieved timestamp.
4. Model authority explicitly: who prepares, who attests, who decides, who executes, and who verifies.
5. Keep legal, administrative and operational states separate. `official_positive`, `ordered`, `scheduled`, `completed`, `inspected`, `claimed`, `liquidated`, and `paid` are not synonyms.
6. Make exceptions first-class queues with owner and clock. Missing title, portal delegation, pre-rejection, weather, death, variant and compliance certificate each have different decision rights.
7. Never overwrite adverse or earlier states. Append review, correction, supersession and recovery events.
8. Generate deadline tasks from source rules but permit only source-linked amendments/prorogations.
9. Require a receipt/protocol for submission and bank reconciliation for paid status.
10. Surface both false-positive and false-negative costs: pursuing an ineligible case wastes work and risks false declarations; excluding an unresolved or eligible-unfunded case loses aid or a future scorrimento.

6. **Cooperative internal governance.** Public sources establish external authority but not any given cooperative's statutes, delegations, member agreements, procurement thresholds or accounting workflow. Those are client-specific evidence inputs.
7. **CAA convention in the relevant case.** National law permits delegation; the actual AGEA/CAA convention defines the selected CAA's tasks, custody, service levels, conflicts and liability. It must be obtained for implementation.[S17]

10. **Data access and owner privacy.** The injunction workflow uses cadastral owner data and public notice. A cooperative tool needs a lawful basis, purpose limitation, retention, access control and masking policy for owner/member data.
11. **Payment execution IDs and status feeds.** Public portals show acts, but machine-readable status, bank execution and recovery identifiers available to a cooperative/CAA were not established.
12. **Indemnity and grant cumulation.** The precise interaction between ARIF removal indemnity, replant aid, other disaster aid, insurance and OP programme costs needs measure-specific state-aid/cumulation analysis.

## 16. Bottom line for workflow redesign

**Fact:** formal institutions divide preparation, decision, execution and verification among different actors and repeatedly require effective-dated acts, mandates, receipts, inspections and payment controls.[S2][S6][S9][S17][S25]

**Inference:** the safest redesign is not a single “application pipeline.” It is an event-sourced case system with five linked but independently authorised functions:

- **Land** explains authoritative state and duties without collapsing legal sources;
- **Funding** computes the whole population and exposes evidence/budget uncertainty;
- **Applications** prepares a cited dossier and routes exceptions, while the beneficiary/signatory releases it;
- **Field Work** converts effective authority into scheduled, evidenced execution, while inspectors/authorities retain compliance decisions;
- **Payments** instantiates claim stages, reconciles actuals and evidence, and hands the authorised claim to the paying agency, which alone executes CAP payment.

The central control is the handoff record: each transition must identify the source, decision owner, unresolved exceptions, deadline and evidence required for the receiving function to accept responsibility.
