## Bottom line

The handbook contains a strong bank of domain facts. Its account of the current EU containment rule, the distinction between layer 14 and layer 15, subspecies-aware surveillance, per-instrument authority routing, the Article 6 collective route, planting communication, and separated money/act/proceeding semantics substantially survives.

The document nevertheless fails as a **canonical**, **complete**, or **superseding** authority. It imports rejected product premises, a huge unapproved Ontology, a Foundry-native/Workshop architecture, stale D-number doctrine, old onboarding authorities, and a false “full product chain.” It also promotes the signed €81,271,242 reprogramming to a current envelope without locating the registration/control event that the decree itself says is still required. The connected operating model remains provisional and noun identification remains closed.

This review treats the handbook as secondary evidence. Fetched primary text and current research control every factual verdict. “Already known” means verified but already present in the authorized research corpus; “verified new” means a verified change that survives into workflow or acquisition practice.

## Verdict labels

- **ALREADY_KNOWN** — verified, but already present in current research evidence.
- **VERIFIED_NEW** — verified and changes workflow or acquisition method.
- **PARTLY_TRUE** — directionally correct but overstated, incomplete, status-dependent, or mixed with design.
- **UNSUPPORTED** — no sufficient evidence for the exact claim.
- **CONTRADICTED** — conflicts with primary evidence or current project authority.
- **OUTSIDE_SCOPE** — relevant elsewhere but not a Wedge 1 workflow organiser.
- **PREMATURE_DESIGN** — cannot be accepted before the noun, relationship, product, platform, or surface gates.

## Direct quotations from primary evidence

1. **Regional phytosanitary authority.** D.lgs. 19/2021 art. 6: “I Servizi fitosanitari regionali ... rappresentano le autorità deputate” and have competence for “la definizione delle aree delimitate” and “la prescrizione ... di tutte le misure ufficiali ritenute necessarie.”[P3]
2. **ARIF is delegated, not interchangeable with the authority.** DGR 644/2022: ARIF “è da considerarsi ‘organismo delegato’” for monitoring, prevention, removal, communications, and aid instruction; the Osservatorio remains the competent authority and auditor.[P4]
3. **Municipal publication is a notice mechanism.** DDS 167/2024 requires “affissione per 7 giorni nell’albo pretorio del Comune” and separately declares the measure immediately executable.[P5]
4. **Containment scope.** Regulation 2020/1201 art. 12: the authority may apply Articles 13–17 “in an infected zone listed in Annex III.”[P1]
5. **Removal trigger.** Article 13: “shall remove all plants which have been found to be infected ... on the basis of the monitoring referred to in Article 15(2).”[P1]
6. **Two-kilometre minimum.** Article 15(2)(a): monitoring occurs “within an area measuring at least 2 km from the border of the infected zone with the buffer zone.”[P1]
7. **Regional layer/act reconciliation.** DDS 82/2026 calls the strip “zona infetta in cui si applicano misure di contenimento ... di 2 chilometri” and applies Articles 13–17 there, while separately publishing the broad “zona infetta.”[P6]
8. **Official judicial number and construction.** TAR Bari no. 384/2026 says the regional “zona di contenimento” “è, invece, pur sempre una zona infetta” under Chapter V.[P7]
9. **Landscape derogation boundary.** Corte costituzionale 74/2021 declares unconstitutional the regional rule allowing any tree species “in deroga ai vincoli paesaggistico-colturali.”[P8]
10. **The amount and unresolved registration.** Decree 986098 states “stanziamento complessivo pari a euro 81.271.242,00” and then: “Il presente decreto è inviato agli Organi di controllo per la registrazione.”[P9]
11. **Collective applicant versus beneficiary.** Article 6 FAQ: “I beneficiari del contributo sono sempre e solo i singoli soggetti aderenti”; OP/cooperatives submit the collective application and act under mandate.[P11]
12. **Collective selection/fallback.** The FAQ says the OP/cooperative may select only some valid adhesions and unselected/incorrectly linked adhesions “verranno trattate come domande di aiuto individuali.”[P11]
13. **CAA/SIAN role.** The FAQ says applications are compiled/released in SIAN “avvalendosi di un CAA o di un professionista abilitato.”[P11]
14. **Payment stages.** DDS 44/2023: “Non è prevista ... una domanda di pagamento sullo stato di avanzamento dei lavori (SAL). Pertanto, oltre alla DdP dell’anticipo, ... esclusivamente la DdP di saldo finale.”[P12]
15. **Planting communication exists.** Scrivania: “Consente ai proprietari di comunicare all’Osservatorio Fitosanitario regionale l’impianto” of Leccino and FS17.[P13]
16. **Cadastral planting record is scoped.** DDS 48/2024 requires, for Leccino/FS17 in the specified zone/movement context, that destination-site cadastral data “devono essere inserite” in the institutional portal.[P14]
17. **Program-specific geography.** SRD01.01A is for “zona indenne” and SRD01.01B for “zona infetta,” each with €20M.[P16][P17]
18. **Money semantics.** The official 22 July 2024 page says €222M “servirebbero” for 9,186 requests and separately says €181M committed and €137M disbursed.[P26]
19. **GDPR is contextual, not a product definition.** GDPR art. 5 requires purpose limitation and data minimisation; art. 6 requires a lawful basis.[P23]
20. **Legal-advice risk is not solved by a disclaimer.** Law 247/2012 art. 2(6) assigns organised, continuous extra-judicial legal advice connected to judicial activity to lawyers, subject to stated exceptions.[P25]

## Verified workflow changes

These survive the handbook review and change the provisional connected workflow after executive acceptance:

1. **Authority is not “government.”** The Osservatorio owns phytosanitary legal state; ARIF is delegated/instruction/execution capacity; the Comune is a notice/local-implementation actor only where the act says so; contractors do not become authorities.
2. **Authoritative-state intake is eventful.** Sample, lab report, official confirmation, area act, GIS publication, parcel order, notice, awareness, and execution are distinct and can interrupt an active case.
3. **Land state is organism-specific.** Subspecies, ST where officially established, host, date, and multiple legal-area memberships remain explicit. Layer 14 is the 2 km minimum surveillance band; layer 15 is broad published infected geometry; legal scope still comes from Annex III plus the operative act.
4. **Act lifecycle is per act.** Signature, protocol, required control/registration, publication, effectiveness, implementation, amendment, supersession, and exhaustion are separate events. Control requirement is itself a legal fact.
5. **Money, act, proceeding, and settlement do not collapse.** Demand, envelope, concession, liquidation, order, bank receipt, audit, revocation, and recovery remain separately sourced.
6. **Routing is per instrument.** Article 6, SRD01.01A/B, and the national €30M route have different geography, portals, instructors, decision makers, liquidators, and payers.
7. **Collective work has distinct decisions.** Public eligibility, member adhesion, cooperative mandate negotiation/selection, collective inclusion, authority review/concession, member guarantee, member claim, and member payment remain separate.
8. **Planting/movement is a downstream handoff.** Planting authorization, Leccino/FS17 communication/registration, RUOP/site inspection, passport, lot, movement, destination, dispatch, and receipt are rechecked independently.
9. **Execution mechanism changes the workflow.** Owner execution, collective/private execution, ARIF/delegated execution, procurement, in-house assignment, and public enforcement change authority, schedule, evidence, and payment route.
10. **Courts and controls reactivate cases.** Appeal filing, interim stay, final judgment, audit, revocation, recovery, and payment repair are independent events; filing never implies suspension.
11. **Out-of-zone remains in scope for Land.** Region-wide duties, disease-free funding, notification duties, and new-foci risk mean “outside current demarcation” is still a dated legal state.
