## Reading key

### Evidence-state codes

- **VL** — verified live source: a live endpoint/page/service was probed or is current official publication.
- **LA** — verified local artifact: bytes/table/manifest exist at the stated authoritative local path; this does not prove current external state.
- **HE** — historical evidence: valid evidence of an earlier state, workflow or implementation, not current production truth.
- **IM** — inferred mapping: source fields or grain plausibly map to the accepted target, but the exact source→target contract has not been exercised.
- **U** — unknown: no current real record exists in the corpus.
- **P / R / N** in the use column — production-capable after gates / research only / no current decision use. Combined codes preserve conditionality.

### Ownership codes

- **source-backed** — external actor/system owns the fact; CORDON may validate and index it but not edit it into truth.
- **edit-only** — only one of the four accepted manager Actions may create the cooperative-owned fact.
- **derived/backstage** — pipelines, model inputs, dependency edges, candidate geometry and summaries. They are never external authority.
- Every target below uses the Gate 6 physical split: 11 visible fact owners; six hidden occurrence types; hidden Intervention Plant Scope; six hidden contextual relationship types; hidden Capacity Portfolio Proposal. Platform-generated Action logs are excluded.
