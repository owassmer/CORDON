Scope: settle whether the CORDON Wedge 1 eligibility engine should compute a legal state for all 4,935,899 Puglia cadastral parcels or only for parcels inside a demarcated area plus buffer. Read-only research brief. Retrieved 2026-08-18.

Everything below is graded. Class 3 is listed first, as required.

## CLASS 1 — QUOTED FROM A PRIMARY SOURCE I ACTUALLY FETCHED

### 1. Surveillance and duties outside demarcated areas

**Reg. (UE) 2020/1201 consolidated, CELEX 02020R1201-20251124**
URL: `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/IT/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02020R1201-20251124`

Art. 2(1) (as replaced by M5 = Reg. 2024/2507) — surveys are territory-wide, not zone-conditioned:

> "Gli Stati membri svolgono indagini annuali sulle piante ospiti e, in caso di sospetta infezione, su qualsiasi altra specie di pianta per rilevare la presenza dell'organismo nocivo specificato nei rispettivi territori. Tali indagini possono riguardare anche i vettori."

Art. 2(3) — the survey venues are ordinary agricultural land, anywhere:

> "Le suddette indagini sono eseguite in base al livello di rischio. Esse si svolgono all'aperto, incluso nei campi per la coltivazione, nei frutteti, nei vigneti, nonché nei vivai, nei centri per il giardinaggio e/o nei centri di vendita, nelle aree naturali e in altri luoghi pertinenti."

Art. 2(6) — the Regulation expressly contemplates positives outside demarcated areas and sets a **stricter confirmation standard** for them:

> "La presenza dell'organismo nocivo specificato è monitorata mediante una delle analisi molecolari elencate nell'allegato IV. Se si ottengono risultati positivi in aree diverse dalle aree delimitate, la presenza dell'organismo nocivo specificato è confermata dal risultato positivo di un'ulteriore analisi molecolare tra quelle elencate in tale allegato, diretta a parti diverse del genoma."

Art. 2(4 bis) — an infected vector found where presence is not known triggers an immediate 400 m survey:

> "Qualora la presenza dell'organismo nocivo specificato sia confermata in un vettore, in un'area in cui la presenza dell'organismo nocivo specificato non è nota, lo Stato membro interessato effettua senza indugio indagini in un raggio di almeno 400 m attorno al luogo in cui è stato rilevato il vettore infetto e sottopone a campionamento e prove le piante ospiti e, in caso di sospetta infezione, qualsiasi altra specie di pianta."

Art. 3(1) — the contingency plan governs "il suo territorio", not a zone:

> "Ogni Stato membro istituisce un piano di emergenza. Il piano di emergenza definisce le azioni da intraprendere nel suo territorio…"

Art. 4(1) and 4(2) — the out-of-zone parcel is where a demarcated area comes from:

> "Qualora la presenza dell'organismo nocivo specificato sia ufficialmente confermata nelle piante, lo Stato membro interessato stabilisce senza indugio un'area delimitata."
> "L'area delimitata è costituita da una zona infetta e da una zona cuscinetto. La zona infetta ha un raggio di almeno 50 m attorno alla pianta che è risultata infetta… a) almeno 2,5 km quando la zona infetta è stabilita ai fini dell'adozione delle misure di eradicazione di cui agli articoli da 7 a 11."

### 2. Duties that attach to any person, and are switched OFF inside the infected zone

**Reg. (UE) 2016/2031**
URL: `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/IT/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R2031`

Art. 14(1) — professional operators (definition art. 2(9)(c) includes "produzione, incluse la coltivazione", so an olive grower qualifies):

> "Quando un operatore professionale sospetta o constata la presenza di un organismo nocivo da quarantena rilevante per l'Unione… su piante, prodotti vegetali o altri oggetti sotto il controllo di detto operatore, ne dà notifica immediatamente all'autorità competente… l'operatore professionale provvede altresì ad adottare immediatamente misure cautelative per prevenire l'insediamento e la diffusione dell'organismo nocivo in questione."

Art. 15(1) and 15(3) — anyone else:

> "Se una persona diversa da un operatore professionale viene a conoscenza della presenza di un organismo nocivo da quarantena rilevante per l'Unione o ha motivi di sospettarne la presenza, deve notificarla immediatamente all'autorità competente."
> "La persona che ha effettuato la notifica… adotta le misure necessarie per prevenire la diffusione dell'organismo nocivo ed eliminarlo dalle piante, dai prodotti vegetali o dagli altri oggetti interessati e, se del caso, dai siti di sua proprietà."

Art. 16 — the exemption runs the opposite way to intuition:

> "Gli obblighi di notifica di cui agli articoli 14 e 15 non si applicano qualora: a) un organismo nocivo da quarantena rilevante per l'Unione sia stato rilevato nella zona infestata di un'area delimitata istituita per il contenimento…; b) …nella zona infestata di un'area delimitata e oggetto di misure di eradicazione che richiedono otto anni o più, durante il periodo dei primi otto anni."

## CLASS 2 — INFERRED FROM CLASS 1

1. **A parcel outside every demarcated area is not legally silent.** Reg. 2016/2031 artt. 14-15, D.lgs. 19/2021 art. 55 commi 12/13/15, and DDS 39/2026 attach live, sanction-backed duties to the holder of any Puglia parcel, regardless of zone. The engine can state a non-empty legal state for such a parcel from these instruments alone.
2. **The notification duty is, in a precise sense, an out-of-zone duty.** Reg. 2016/2031 art. 16 disapplies artt. 14 and 15 inside the infested zone of a containment demarcated area and inside the infested zone of an eradication area during its first eight years. So the duty to report a suspicion binds hardest exactly where the engine would be silent under a demarcated-only design. This inverts the intuition behind the demarcated-only recommendation.
3. **The 2026 tillage obligation is broader than the Piano's own template.** The Piano 2024-2026 table conditions obligatory tillage on comuni intersecting demarcated areas plus other comuni "su disposizione dell'Osservatorio". DDS 39/2026 exercises that discretion at maximum extent: "nell'intero territorio regionale", with Allegato A enumerating comuni in all five provinces. An out-of-zone parcel in, say, San Severo (FG) carried a hard 25 March–30 April 2026 deadline (extended by DDS 79/2026) with Carabinieri Forestali enforcement and a €1,000–6,000 exposure under D.lgs. 19/2021 art. 55 comma 15. That single fact settles the scope question on its own.
4. **The out-of-zone parcel is the source of new demarcated areas, not an exception to them.** Art. 4(1) requires demarcation "senza indugio" on official confirmation in plants. Every one of the acts instituting the Bari, Minervino delle Murge, Modugno, Bisceglie, Giovinazzo, Cagnano Varano and Valenzano areas is therefore, by construction, a response to a confirmation in territory not previously demarcated for that subspecies. Four of these are documented in the acts' own words ("zona indenne", "area indenne", "aree indenni"); the remaining three follow from Art. 4(1) plus the DET 2/2026 institution register. The user's claim (b) is empirically correct: out-of-zone signal is how the map grows.
5. **Positive-doesn't-redraw-geography still holds, with a sharper edge.** Piano §4.4 says an infected **vector** in the pest-free area is notified "senza procedere alla delimitazione dell'area". Reg. art. 2(4 bis) requires a 400 m survey instead. And art. 2(6) requires a second, different-genome molecular test before a positive outside a demarcated area counts as confirmed. So an out-of-zone signal has three distinct legal states — unconfirmed signal, confirmed vector positive (no demarcation), confirmed plant positive (demarcation without delay) — and the engine must not collapse them.
6. **Funding is not uniformly zone-gated.** Three of the four cash instruments are strictly zone-gated (DI 2484 art. 6, the DM 11.02.2025 €30M line, CSR SRD01.01B). One equal-sized instrument, CSR SRD01.01A at €20M, is gated the other way and admits **only** out-of-zone parcels. A demarcated-only engine cannot answer the SRD01.01A question at all — and that is the question most of a cooperative's membership actually has.
7. **DI 2484 art. 6 eligibility is a historical-map test, not a current-map test.** §4 keys the "zona infetta" to DDS 59 del 21/05/2019. A parcel that entered a demarcated area after 2019 does not thereby become art. 6 eligible. The engine must resolve this measure against the 2019 layer, not the current one — which is a separate reason the current-zone join cannot be the engine's only geometry.
8. **Coverage cost of computing for all parcels is bounded.** The region-wide duties resolve from cheap attributes: comune (for DDS 39/2026 altitude band and window), land-use class (the aree protette / boschi / pinete / giardini privati carve-outs), and the presence of olive/almond/prunoidee (for the "solo sotto la chioma" rule on herbaceous land). No zone geometry is required. The expensive point-in-polygon work stays confined to the zone-conditioned measures.

## THE HONEST ANSWER FOR AN OUT-OF-ZONE PARCEL

This is the exact set of statements a legally correct engine should render for a parcel that intersects no demarcated area of any subspecies, as of the snapshot date.

**What it asserts.**

1. Zone status: "As of `{snapshot_date}`, this parcel intersects no demarcated area established under art. 4 of Reg. (UE) 2020/1201, per the zone layer published by the Osservatorio Fitosanitario. In the Regione Puglia action plan's terminology this is *zona indenne*." Cite the zone-layer act and the Piano definition.
2. No zone-conditioned regime applies: "No eradication measure (artt. 7-11) and no containment measure (artt. 13-17) is prescribed for this parcel. Those measures presuppose a demarcated area."
3. **Region-wide obligations that do apply, each with its own deadline and sanction:**
   - Juvenile-vector tillage. "DDS 39/2026 imposes surface tillage on all owners, possessors and holders of land in the entire regional territory. Your comune `{X}` falls in the `{<200 m | >200 m}` band; the window was `{25 Mar–30 Apr | 10 Apr–15 May}` 2026, extended by DDS 79/2026 to `{15 May | 30 May}`. Exclusions: protected areas under L. 394/91 except olive/fruit/vine plantings, woods, pinewoods, private gardens. On herbaceous crops with olive, almond or other prunoidee present, tillage only under the canopy. Failure: D.lgs. 19/2021 art. 55 comma 15, €1,000–6,000; controls by Carabinieri Forestali."
   - Adult-vector treatment. "Puglia issues a separate annual determina for adult treatments, addressed by comune. This engine has not located the `{year}` act; check emergenzaxylella.it." State this as a dated absence, never as absence of duty.
   - Notification of suspicion. "Reg. (UE) 2016/2031 art. 14(1) obliges a professional operator — which includes a grower, art. 2(9)(c) — to notify the competent authority immediately on suspicion or observation, and to take immediate precautionary measures. Art. 15(1) and (3) impose the same on any other person. Art. 16 disapplies these duties only inside the infested zone of a containment area, or of an eradication area in its first eight years. **This parcel is outside those carve-outs, so the duty applies in full.** Failure: art. 55 comma 12, €3,000–18,000."
   - Wood movement, if applicable. "Moving wood or chips from an infected area into the pest-free area requires prior notification to the Osservatorio and the Carabinieri Forestali, and covered transport between 1 April and 31 October." Surface only when the customer's own logistics data supports it; otherwise present as a conditional.
4. **Funding lines whose territorial test this parcel passes:** CSR SRD01.01A (Comparto Olivicolo in zona indenne), criterio CR09, €20M, currently closed — with the window state and the next expected reopening if any.
5. **Funding lines whose territorial test this parcel fails, and why:** DI 2484/2020 art. 6 (avviso §4: only the 2019 zona infetta, containment zone excluded); DM MASAF/MEF 11.02.2025 (art. 2(1): only zone infette, containment excluded); CSR SRD01.01B (CR09: only area infetta). Render the failure with its citation, not as a bare "not eligible".
6. **What happens if a positive appears here:** "A confirmed positive in plants on or near this parcel obliges the Region to establish a demarcated area without delay (art. 4(1)) — infected zone of at least 50 m radius, buffer of at least 2.5 km for eradication — and eradication under artt. 7-11 would then apply, per Piano §4.2.1."

**What it must abstain on.**

- Any claim about infection status. The engine has no diagnostic result for this parcel and must not read absence of a demarcated area as evidence the parcel is healthy. It states "no official positive is recorded in the published monitoring layer as of `{date}`", never "this parcel is not infected".
- Distinguishing the three out-of-zone signal states. A single positive molecular result outside a demarcated area is **not** an official confirmation: art. 2(6) requires a second molecular assay targeting a different genome region. A confirmed **vector** positive out of zone triggers a 400 m survey (art. 2(4 bis)) but does **not** create a demarcated area (Piano §4.4). Only a confirmed **plant** positive triggers art. 4(1). The engine must label which of these it is, or abstain.
- Prospective demarcation. The engine must never predict that a parcel will be demarcated, or pre-render eradication duties for it.
- Zone status at any date other than the snapshot. Zone maps change by decree. Every zone assertion carries the decree id and the snapshot date.
- Adult-treatment obligations for a campaign year whose determina it has not fetched. Report the gap.

**If this reads thin, say it plainly and completely.** For most out-of-zone member parcels the honest answer is: *no demarcation; no eradication or containment measure; three zone-gated funding lines closed with citations; one €20M funding line open on territorial grounds; two live region-wide duties with dated windows and euro-denominated sanctions; and a defined trigger that would change all of this.* That is six actionable statements and two abstentions. It is not silence, and a cooperative operations manager can act on every line of it.

## VERDICT

**Compute for all parcels.** The strongest single piece of evidence is DDS 39 dell'11/03/2026, whose dispositivo reads "Di applicare obbligatoriamente **nell'intero territorio regionale** le misure di lotta allo stadio giovanile del vettore", with an Allegato A that enumerates comuni across all five provinces and addresses the duty to "proprietari, possessori o conduttori a qualsiasi titolo dei terreni" — a dated, sanction-backed obligation (D.lgs. 19/2021 art. 55 comma 15, €1,000–6,000, Carabinieri Forestali enforcement) that attaches to an out-of-zone parcel and that a demarcated-only engine would render as an empty screen. Three independent findings reinforce it: Reg. (UE) 2016/2031 art. 16 disapplies the notification duties of artt. 14-15 precisely *inside* infested zones, so the reporting obligation is strongest where a demarcated-only engine is silent; CSR SRD01.01A commits €20M whose eligibility criterion CR09 admits **only** investments "in area indenne", the exact mirror of SRD01.01B, so out-of-zone parcels have a live funding question that in-zone parcels do not; and four regional acts — DDS 127/2022 (39 plants in the "area indenne" between Monopoli and Polignano), DDS 8/2024 (subsp. *fastidiosa* ST1 at Triggiano, reached through vector monitoring "nelle aree indenni"), DDS 173/2025 (Cagnano Varano "(zona indenne)") and DDS 2/2026 (Valenzano "(zona indenne)") — record official confirmation of infection outside every demarcated area, which is by construction how each new demarcated area in Puglia since 2021 has come into existence. Connor's original demarcated-only recommendation optimised for the geometry that was expensive to compute rather than for the question the customer asks; the correct design computes a legal state for every parcel, resolves the region-wide duties from comune and land-use attributes at near-zero geometric cost, and reserves point-in-polygon work for the zone-conditioned measures alone.
