### Generalization requested during adjudication

Owen asked that the contextual correction be generalized and tested against earlier remediation cycles. The transferable failure is **substituting representation rules for operative meaning**. It can inflate a source distinction into another duty or gate, but can also erase necessary meaning through rigid minimality, stage boundaries or a demand for an already-held case. The question is not whether two source sentences differ; it is whether their difference changes an in-aperture decision, performance requirement, timing, population, lawful alternative, or evidentiary consequence. One act may discharge several requirements; independently consequential requirements must still survive.

The bounded retrospective read covered the September 4 fresh-review, correction/proposal, interpretation-resolution, post-resolution and approved-repair records, plus selected archived stage-B lean-review assertions. Several instances corroborate the pattern: an extra receipt condition confused proof with required performance; clipped vector-surveillance syntax produced a categorical denial of cadence; survey completion improperly required subsequent reporting; an exclusive sampling note concealed the lawful removal alternative. Relevant current A meanings and source spans were rechecked for reporting, vector cadence and sampling/removal. The corresponding past corrections remove false gates or restore context; they are not proposed for reversal.

The opposite failure appears in an archived cycle-4 lean assertion proposing deletion of 56 expression dispositions because their provisions had another disposition. That is evidence of an overbroad proposed cure, not proof the deletion was implemented: provision coverage cannot establish expression coverage. Likewise, an artifact having no implemented C–G consumer yet does not prove it has no necessary downstream consumer. A prospective, legally reachable decision can establish relevance without a held case.

The present round receives the same scrutiny. Retain the necessary notification/territory/promptness/regularity distinctions without inventing extra workflows, automatic invalidity or numerical schedules. R2.4 is completion precision, not a demonstrated running evaluator failure. No conclusion that all seven restored historical clock/parameter items are invalid is established here, and no history-wide reopening is authorized.

Recommended lean generalization, not yet a constitutional edit: refine the existing minimum-sufficient-structure principle to require deriving the combined operative requirement before allocating representation; preserve only independently consequential distinctions, permit shared performance to satisfy overlapping requirements, and never promote an evidence limitation or a representational convenience into an operator duty.
